# Literature and novelty assessment

Reviewed 2026-11-10. This is a targeted primary-source review, not a systematic proof of priority. The existing README was inspected; its ensemble comparison and hand-selected motif flags are exploratory evidence.

| Primary source | Established contribution | Consequence for this project |
|---|---|---|
| [Meyer et al., PRX Quantum 2023](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.4.010328) | Representation-based gate symmetrization and invariant reuploading architectures. | A commutator or group twirl is a mathematical baseline, not a new discovery. Check embedding, preparation, blocks and readout together. |
| [Nguyen et al., PRX Quantum 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.08566) | General construction of equivariant unitary layers and channels, including continuous groups. | Replacing manual flags with channel equivariance is established methodology; benchmark against constructive methods. |
| [Mansky et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.12764v2) | Permutation-invariant QML for connectedness, bipartiteness and Hamiltonian graph properties, including approximate symmetry. | Connectedness plus improved symmetric ansatz performance is directly occupied territory. |
| [Baumann and Linnhoff-Popien, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.20316v1) | Tic-tac-toe subgroup sharing and task-aligned interactions; symmetry alone does not determine a useful architecture. | Do not predict universal monotonic accuracy versus symmetry. Separate interaction choice from symmetry. |
| [Elesedy and Zaidi, ICML 2021](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/elesedy21a.html) | Averaging-operator function-space decomposition and strict generalization benefit in specified linear settings. | Orthogonal-projection risk identities are known; they provide interpretation, not a claim that trained circuits improve monotonically. |
| [Otto et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.00212) | Framework for enforcing, discovering and promoting symmetry through Lie derivatives. | Learning a continuous symmetry penalty is not in itself novel. |
| [Chen, 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00157v1) | Observable/task distinctions, finite-measurement symmetry inference, task validation, projection bias and symmetry-release geometry. | Particularly close conceptual overlap. Do not claim the first task-level quantum symmetry discovery or first separation of symmetry levels. This source is a preprint, not independently validated here. |
| [Lange et al., ICLR 2026](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/7886b9bafe76c52fd568db10ff9772df-Abstract-Conference.html) | ShinkaEvolve's parent sampling, novelty rejection and bandit ensemble selection. | An LLM evolutionary harness is established. Adaptive model allocation confounds naive model-level success comparisons. |

The strongest defensible candidate contribution is **a verified, intervention-based study of how LLM evolutionary search turns symmetry hypotheses into executable quantum models, and whether a mathematical verifier plus compiler improves discovery and transfer under matched evaluation budgets**. This is a proposed contribution; successful experiments and a final broader priority review are required.

The key distinction is between proposing a correct transformation, implementing it correctly, and obtaining useful task performance. Require proposals to specify explicit input transformations and an output action. Validate those claims independently of natural-language vocabulary, then measure executable channel and predictive residuals. This makes the previous 'say versus build' observation transferable and falsifiable. Structural circuit residuals alone cannot show that the model inferred label symmetry from feedback.

A second promising contribution is a controlled repair experiment: preserve a candidate's interaction family and resource budget while repairing or deliberately breaking its claimed equivariance, then measure paired retraining outcomes. Include a randomly selected incorrect group and task-breaking labels. The proposed verifier must help when symmetry is useful and avoid imposing it when it is wrong. Benchmark against direct analytical symmetrization and non-LLM search, so any benefit can be attributed to discovery or search efficiency.

Readiness criteria: independent held-out tasks; clean test isolation; run-level uncertainty; verified transformations; resource-matched baselines; nontrivial predictors; and negative controls. No current literature finding justifies declaring the existing result publishable or the proposed method novel without these checks.
