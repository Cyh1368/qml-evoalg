<DESCRIPTION>
Add a final layer of independent RY rotations after the skip-2 CRZ entangling layer. This introduces a non-commuting rotation axis after the last entangling layer, increasing expressivity of the output state without adding extra entangling depth. Prior experiments show alternating RY-entangle-RY-entangle-RY patterns tend to improve both train and validation accuracy while keeping the circuit within reasonable depth (small +8 gate addition). This should help push accuracy higher; the shared-parameter skip layer already limits overfitting, and we can further tune sharing if the generalization gap grows.
</DESCRIPTION>
