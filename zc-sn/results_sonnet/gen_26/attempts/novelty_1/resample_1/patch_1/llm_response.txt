add_mid_ry_layer_before_post_rz

<DESCRIPTION>
Adding a mid-circuit RY rotation layer between the entangling CZ chain and the final RZ layer introduces a non-commuting rotation axis (RY) right after entanglement, which increases the expressivity of the circuit (RZ-CZ-RY-RZ pattern) without significantly increasing depth (+8 gates, +8 params). This is inspired by hardware-efficient ansatz designs where alternating rotation axes (Y then Z) around entangling layers improve trainability and expressiveness compared to using only Z rotations post-entanglement. Since RZ gates commute with CZ (both diagonal in computational basis for the control qubits) at least partially, adding an RY layer breaks that commutation and lets the entangling operation contribute more effectively to the loss landscape, which may reduce train-test gap and improve generalization by adding a controlled amount of expressive power. This directly implements the recommended change while staying within the current depth budget.
