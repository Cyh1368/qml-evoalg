tie_symmetric_rz_params

<DESCRIPTION>
Revert the extra RY-mid layer (which regressed validation accuracy to 0.85 and increased params/depth without benefit) back to the original two-RZ-layer structure, and additionally tie the pre- and post-entangler RZ parameters across symmetric qubit pairs (0↔7, 1↔6, 2↔5, 3↔4). This halves the RZ parameter count (from 16 unique RZ params to 8) while keeping the same gate count and depth, following the observation that shared-entangler parameterization previously yielded strong generalization (gap ≈0.04-0.07) and better parameter efficiency. This should recover the 0.85 score baseline while improving parameter_efficiency_mean due to fewer trainable parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
