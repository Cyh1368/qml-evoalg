add_ry_mid_layer_after_cz

<DESCRIPTION>
Inserting an additional RY rotation layer on all eight qubits immediately after the CZ entangling chain and before the post-RZ block introduces a non-commuting RY–RZ–CZ–RY–RZ pattern. This breaks up the pure Z-rotation redundancy that follows entanglement (RZ commutes through CZ diagonally, so having RZ immediately after CZ without an intervening non-commuting rotation limits the expressivity gained from entanglement). Adding RY rotations before the final RZ layer lets the entangling CZ gates actually affect the local basis in a richer way (Z then Y then Z covers a fuller SU(2) each qubit), which should improve trainability and slightly increase expressivity without significantly increasing depth (only 8 more gates for 8 more parameters, keeping the total near the empirically good ~146-154 parameter regime, close to the current depth "sweet spot" of ~99). This matches the recommended edit and prior converged faster in related mid-circuit rotation experiments.
