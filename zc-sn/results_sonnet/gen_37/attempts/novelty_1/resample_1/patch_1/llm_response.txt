<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz has strong nearest-neighbor (ring) and skip-2 entanglement but no long-range (diameter) connectivity, which may limit the model's ability to mix information globally and could contribute to the observed train-validation gap (0.08). Following the recommendation, I append a sparse diameter entanglement block: a single shared-parameter CRZ gate connecting each qubit to its antipodal partner (0-4, 1-5, 2-6, 3-7). This adds only 4 gates and 1 new trainable parameter, injecting minimal but complete long-range connectivity across the ring, which should improve generalization/test mixing without significantly increasing parameter count, depth, or overfitting risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
