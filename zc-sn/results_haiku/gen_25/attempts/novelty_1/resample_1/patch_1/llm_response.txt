<DESCRIPTION>
The current approach uses a simple alternating RY-RZ-entanglement structure with aggressive parameter sharing, but it's underperforming compared to prior candidates (0.88 vs 0.91). The key issue is:

1. **Loss of expressivity**: Removing the parametrized two-qubit gates (CRY, CRZ) in favor of fixed CZ gates reduces the circuit's ability to learn complex entanglement patterns.
2. **Poor gate efficiency**: Two full reverse-chain CZ layers (14 fixed gates) consume circuit depth without adding trainable degrees of freedom.
3. **Shallow parametrization**: Only 9 parameters across 44 gates is inefficient compared to prior 44-parameter solution that achieved 0.91.

The new approach combines:
- **Hierarchical entanglement**: Use a mix of fixed (CZ) and parametrized (CRY, CRZ) gates to balance expressivity and efficiency
- **Selective two-qubit parametrization**: Apply CRY for local cross-talk and CRZ for global phase coherence, with moderate parameter sharing
- **Optimized depth**: Reduce fixed gate overhead by using parametrized gates more strategically
- **Three-layer parametric structure**: Pre-encoding, mid-circuit parametric entanglement, and post-encoding layers with independent parameters per layer

This mimics the structure of the best prior candidate (score 0.91) but with refined parametrization strategy and slightly different gate patterns to explore better local minima.
