<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the individually parameterized RZ layers with two parity-shared RZ sandwiches around alternating CZ matchings. The second matching adds the missing 7–0 bond, turning the chain into a ring for uniform connectivity. This preserves parallelizable brick-wall entanglement while reducing the block from 24 to 16 independent parameters, which should improve parameter efficiency and regularization relative to the current slow-converging candidate.
