<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the two RZ banks into one because RZ rotations commute with every CZ gate, making each pre/post pair equivalent to a single rotation with a summed angle. This removes redundant optimization directions and substantially lowers the parameter count without reducing the represented circuit family. Add the missing CZ edge between qubits 7 and 0 to form a balanced ring, giving every qubit equal entangling connectivity and enabling correlations across the chain boundary. The result should improve parameter efficiency, convergence, and generalization while retaining useful expressivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
