<DESCRIPTION>
Regularize the added pre-entanglement RZ layer by sharing its angles across the two parity sublattices. This preserves distinct even/odd phase responses and all existing gates while reducing the block from 24 to 18 independent parameters. The lower-dimensional model should retain most of the validation gain from the pre-RZ layer while reducing overfitting, improving parameter efficiency, and accelerating convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
