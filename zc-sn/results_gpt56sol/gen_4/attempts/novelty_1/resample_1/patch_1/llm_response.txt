real_amplitude_ring

<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the two costly RZ layers and replace the open CZ chain with a closed, staggered CNOT ring. The existing wire-specific RY rotations retain independent feature adaptation, while repeated blocks can build expressive real-amplitude transformations through directional entanglement. This reduces each block from 24 to 8 trainable parameters, addressing the low parameter efficiency and likely reducing overfitting and convergence time. The staggered ordering also exposes disjoint entanglers that can be scheduled in parallel and the closing edge prevents endpoint qubits from being weakly connected.
</DESCRIPTION>
