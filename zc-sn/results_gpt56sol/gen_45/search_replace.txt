<DESCRIPTION>
Add trainable controlled-Z rotations along the existing nearest-neighbor CZ backbone. Sharing one parameter across even bonds and another across odd bonds adds only two parameter families per ansatz repetition, providing tunable correlation phases without substantially increasing model complexity. This targets the current validation ceiling while retaining parity-based regularization and the strong chain topology.
