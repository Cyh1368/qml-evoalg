<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the three odd-starting CZ interactions with a parity-shared CRY angle while retaining fixed CZ gates on the even matching and preserving the empirically preferred open-chain topology. The noncommuting, trainable CRY interactions can convert phase correlations into population changes earlier in the block, potentially improving optimization and validation accuracy. Sharing one parameter across all odd bonds limits added capacity and overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
