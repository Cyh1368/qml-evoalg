<DESCRIPTION>
Append a parity-shared RX→RY mixer after the final phase layer. The noncommuting RX stage converts phase information into measurable populations, matching the strongest prior architecture, while the subsequent RY stage adds a second mixing axis for greater expressivity. Parity sharing limits the addition to four independent parameters per repeated block, balancing accuracy gains against parameter efficiency and overfitting risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
