Add a parity-shared RX mixer after the final phase sandwich. The current tail consists entirely of commuting RZ/CZ operations, so a noncommuting mixer should convert learned phase correlations into measurable population differences and improve expressivity without substantially increasing parameter count. Sharing one angle across even wires and one across odd wires adds only two parameters per repeated block, preserving regularization and fast convergence.
