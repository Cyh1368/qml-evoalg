<DESCRIPTION>
Share the initial RY angles by wire parity, replacing eight independent parameters with two. The current circuit overfits noticeably (0.96 training versus 0.89 validation accuracy), while the stronger prior programs consistently benefit from parity-shared downstream layers. Extending that symmetry to the input mixer should regularize the model, improve parameter efficiency and convergence, and preserve distinct behavior for even and odd qubit sublattices.
</DESCRIPTION>
