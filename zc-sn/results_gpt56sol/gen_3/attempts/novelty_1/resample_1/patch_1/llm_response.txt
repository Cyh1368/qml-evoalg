<DESCRIPTION>
This ansatz replaces the deep nearest-neighbor CZ chain with a three-stage multiscale butterfly of trainable CRY interactions. The stages connect adjacent, distance-two, and distance-four qubits, allowing information to propagate across all eight qubits with logarithmic interaction depth. Control directions are balanced so each qubit participates as both a control and a target.

Independent RY rotations preserve local adaptability, while two parity-shared RZ parameters provide phase sensitivity with strong regularization. Sharing one interaction parameter across each butterfly scale reduces the unique parameters per block from 24 to 13. The resulting circuit is shallower, uses fewer gates, and should reduce overfitting and convergence cost while retaining global expressivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
