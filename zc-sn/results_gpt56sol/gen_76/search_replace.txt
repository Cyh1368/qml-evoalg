<DESCRIPTION>
Prepend a parity-shared RX layer before the existing independent RY rotations. Because RX and RY do not commute, this introduces an early two-axis mixer that can create richer local states before phase rotations and CZ entanglement. Sharing one angle across even wires and one across odd wires adds only two parameters per repeated block, providing structured regularization while potentially reducing the current train-validation gap and accelerating convergence.
