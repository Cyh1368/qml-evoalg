<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the bridge and final RZ layers to the initial parity-shared phase pair. This reduces six phase parameters per block to two while retaining every gate and the parity structure. The stronger sharing acts as regularization, should improve parameter efficiency and generalization, and may accelerate convergence by removing redundant phase directions from the optimization landscape.
</DESCRIPTION>
