remove_diagonal_redundancy
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The RZ layers commute with every CZ entangler, making the pre/post phase rotations highly redundant and contributing many weakly useful parameters. Remove the pre-entangling RZ layer and convert the post-entangling layer to RY rotations. This yields a standard RY-CZ-RY block that can directly alter measured populations, while reducing each block from 27 to 18 parameters and lowering depth and gate count for faster, more stable convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
