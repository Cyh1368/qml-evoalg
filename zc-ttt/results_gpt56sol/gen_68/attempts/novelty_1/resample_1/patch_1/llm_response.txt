<DESCRIPTION>
Insert a graph-tied RY–RZ layer between the second and third CZ matchings. This breaks the long sequence of commuting diagonal interactions, allowing later CZ gates to transform newly mixed amplitudes rather than merely accumulate phases. Sharing parameters across graph-related qubits limits the increase to 12 unique parameters per block while retaining a full nine-qubit rotation layer, balancing expressivity and regularization.
</DESCRIPTION>
