<DESCRIPTION>
Replace four well-distributed fixed CZ gates with trainable CRZ interactions, allowing the optimizer to tune entanglement strength across the connectivity graph. To offset their added parameters, tie each post-entanglement RZ angle to the corresponding pre-entanglement RZ angle. Because the intervening CZ/CRZ gates are diagonal in the computational basis, the pre/post RZ rotations are otherwise redundantly parameterized; tying them preserves the relevant accumulated local phase while reducing the total parameter count from 162 to 132. This should improve parameter efficiency and convergence while adding useful entanglement flexibility.
</DESCRIPTION>
