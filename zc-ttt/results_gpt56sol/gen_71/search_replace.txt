<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the isolated three-body phase and intermediate RX mixer, which increased capacity and depth but produced a lower combined score and a larger generalization gap than the simpler two-block design. Reallocate that parameter budget to a post-entanglement RY–RZ output-basis layer. The RY parameters are tied across graph-related wires for regularization, while independent RZ phases preserve qubit-specific discrimination. This restores clean rotation–entanglement–rotation bracketing while keeping the parameter count near the previously successful range.
