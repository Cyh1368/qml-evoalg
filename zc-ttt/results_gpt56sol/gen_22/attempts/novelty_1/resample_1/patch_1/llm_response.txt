Its duplicated commuting RZ layers increase depth without adding independent parameters, while the most connected boundary qubits, 0 and 8, receive no noncommuting post-entanglement mixing. The parameter count and gate count remain unchanged, but the new RX parameters can transform entanglement-generated phase information into measurable population differences. Retaining independent rotations elsewhere preserves local expressivity, while the focused boundary configuration avoids increasing model complexity or overfitting risk. Learning rate, iteration count, and convergence thresholds remain fixed by the external training loop.
