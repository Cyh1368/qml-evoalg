<DESCRIPTION>
The two RZ layers commute through every CZ gate, so their per-wire parameters contain exact redundancies that create flat optimization directions. Remove the redundant pre-entanglement RZ layer and tie the remaining RZ rotations to one collective parameter. This preserves a complex-valued phase-mixing degree of freedom while reducing the repeated-block parameter count from 27 to 10, lowering the total from 162 to 60 and improving parameter efficiency, convergence, and regularization.
</DESCRIPTION>
