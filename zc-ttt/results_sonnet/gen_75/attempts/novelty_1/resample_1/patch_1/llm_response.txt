sparse_encoding_param_sharing

<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the encoding layer's parameter count by sharing RY parameters between pairs of qubits that are already connected in the entangling layers: ry_1↔ry_8 (linked via crz_3 on wires [1,8]) and ry_3↔ry_6 (both connect to hub qubits 0/2 through crz_1/crz_4 respectively). This is a structured compression—unlike random parameter tying, these pairs are chosen because they participate in shared entangling gates, so tying their encoding rotations reinforces a symmetry the entangler already exploits, rather than fighting it. This drops encoding params from 9 to 7 (total n_params from 162 to 160), slightly easing the optimization landscape and potentially reducing overfitting (train-test gap) while testing whether expressivity is preserved given the seed's stable 0.72 validation accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
