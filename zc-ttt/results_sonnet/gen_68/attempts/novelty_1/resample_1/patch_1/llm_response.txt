crz_backbone_single_zzz

<DESCRIPTION>
Crossover between the two 0.63-scoring seed variants (RY encoding + CRZ backbone with symmetric parameter sharing + RZ post-rotations) and the ZZZ-augmented variant (0.62) that showed a test-accuracy gain from adding three-qubit correlation terms but suffered validation loss/gap due to the added depth of three separate ZZZ gates (each spanning all 9 qubits, tripling the added depth/gate-count).

This version keeps the proven, low-depth CRZ backbone exactly as in the best-scoring seeds (identical structure and parameter sharing, which reached validation_accuracy 0.72 and the best combined score of 0.63), and reintroduces only a *single* ZZZ three-qubit correlation gate (instead of three), placed on well-separated hub qubits (0, 4, 7) that already participate in multiple two-qubit connections in the backbone. This adds just one extra gate and one extra parameter per repetition, giving the model the extra correlation capacity that boosted test accuracy in the previous experiment, while keeping the depth and gate-count increase to roughly a third of what the 3xZZZ version incurred — aiming to capture the upside (better test/generalization) without the downside (extra depth hurting validation).

Everything else (encoding layer, post-rotation layer, no shared params except within the CRZ backbone as in the original seed) is kept unchanged to preserve the well-performing baseline.
