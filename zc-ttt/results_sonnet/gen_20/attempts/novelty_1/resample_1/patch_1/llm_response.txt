<DESCRIPTION>
Based on the recommendation to reallocate trainable capacity from less-central entangling gates toward single-qubit rotations, I prune the two least-central fixed CZ gates (edges [4,8] and [6,7], which are peripheral connections in the entangling graph) and insert a lightweight mid-circuit RY sublayer on qubits [1,3,5,7,8] with unique parameters (ry_mid_0…ry_mid_4). This keeps overall gate count and depth roughly comparable while giving the circuit more expressive single-qubit capacity right after the first entangling block, which should help it fit richer feature combinations without over-relying on the two weakest entangling links. This builds directly on the proven RY→entangler→RZ sandwich structure already in the ansatz, inserting the new RY layer between the CRZ chain and the final RZ layer.
</DESCRIPTION>
