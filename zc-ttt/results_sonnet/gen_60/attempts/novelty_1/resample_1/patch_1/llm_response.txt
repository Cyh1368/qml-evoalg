add_zzz_correlation_layer

<DESCRIPTION>
The previous mid-layer RY "refresh" addition regressed the combined score (0.61 vs. seed's 0.63), suggesting that a plain single-qubit rotation between the CRZ entangling layer and the final phase layer does not add useful expressivity here — it likely just adds depth/overfitting noise without capturing new correlations.

Instead, I replace that mid-layer RY block with two three-qubit ZZZ interaction gates placed on qubits that are NOT already directly coupled by the existing two-qubit CRZ backbone (0,4,6) and (1,5,8). These three-qubit gates introduce genuine higher-order (three-body) correlations among the encoded features that pairwise CRZ gates cannot capture, while only adding 2 new parameters (with no sharing needed since each triple is independent) and 2 gates — a much smaller footprint than the previous 4-gate RY refresh. This should improve model capacity/train accuracy while keeping the generalization gap controlled, since three-qubit gates concentrated on previously-unconnected qubit triples add complementary information rather than redundant rotations on already-entangled wires.
</DESCRIPTION>
