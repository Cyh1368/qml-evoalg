<DESCRIPTION>
The current single-block ansatz uses only single-qubit RY/RZ rotations plus a fixed set of CZ entanglers on 12 allowed pairs. Train and validation accuracies are both low (0.57/0.55) with a small generalization gap (0.07), which is a classic underfitting signature — the circuit lacks the expressivity to correlate more than 2 qubits at a time. To increase representational power without dramatically increasing parameter count, I insert three parametrized three-qubit ZZZ interaction gates (allowed on any qubit triple) after the existing CZ entangling layer. These gates couple qubits that are not directly linked by the fixed 2-qubit topology (e.g. bridging separate CZ clusters), letting the circuit capture higher-order correlations relevant to the (unknown) data structure. Only 3 extra parameters per repetition are added (18 total across the 6 repetitions implied by n_params=162), keeping the parameter-efficiency cost low while directly targeting the underfitting issue via increased circuit expressivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
