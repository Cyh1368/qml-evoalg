add_entangling_crz_and_zzz_layers
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz relies solely on fixed (non-parametric) CZ gates for entanglement, sandwiched between two single-qubit rotation layers. This limits how much the entangling structure can be tuned during training, likely capping expressivity and validation accuracy. I add a lightly-parametrized entangling layer using CRZ gates with heavy parameter sharing (3 shared parameters spread over the same 12 allowed two-qubit pairs, grouped into 3 groups of 4 edges each) right after the existing CZ layer. This lets the model learn entanglement strength cheaply (only 3 extra parameters per block) while preserving most of the fixed structural connectivity. I also add two three-qubit ZZZ gates (sharing a single parameter) spanning distant qubit triples (0,4,8) and (1,5,6) to inject cheap higher-order correlations that single/two-qubit gates cannot capture, at the cost of only 1 extra parameter. This should improve expressivity and validation accuracy without a large parameter blowup, keeping parameter efficiency reasonable.
</DESCRIPTION>
