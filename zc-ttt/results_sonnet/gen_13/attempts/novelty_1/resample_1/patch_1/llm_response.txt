<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the best-performing prior candidate (score 0.60, 150 params, depth 41), this version keeps the identical gate set and parameter structure (9 RY encoding rotations, 7 uniquely-parametrized CRZ entanglers, 5 fixed CZ entanglers, 9 RZ post-rotations) but reorders the entangling layer so that mutually compatible two-qubit gates are grouped adjacently rather than interleaved. Specifically, the three CZ gates touching the "hub" qubits (0,8), (1,8), (2,6) are placed in a contiguous block right after the qubit-0 CRZ chain, and the remaining CRZ/CZ gates are grouped by their shared-qubit locality (1↔3↔5, 2↔4↔8, 6↔7). This does not change gate count or parameter count (so training dynamics and expressivity stay the same), but reduces circuit depth by letting the transpiler/scheduler collapse independent gates into fewer sequential moments, per the depth-reduction recommendation. Lower depth on the same parameter budget should reduce optimization difficulty and improve validation accuracy / generalization gap without any cost in parameter efficiency.

Rationale for keeping the rest identical: among the three prior variants, the one that converted the (5,7) CZ into a parametrized CRZ (score 0.60) outperformed the version with more shared-CRZ layers, ZZZ interactions and extra depth (score 0.56, depth 88). This confirms that adding parameters/depth carelessly hurts generalization here, while enriching only where it can be exploited without depth blow-up-here achieved purely through better scheduling-improves results. Hence, this candidate isolates the "reorder for parallelism" lever in isolation to test its dedicated benefit.
