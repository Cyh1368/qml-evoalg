<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz (extra ZZZ terms, doubled CZ+CRZ layers) increased depth and parameter count significantly (88 depth, 186 params) but achieved a lower combined score (0.56) than the simpler CRY-based variant (0.60) that used only 180 parameters and depth 41. This suggests the extra complexity is hurting generalization rather than helping expressivity. I revert to the higher-performing CRY-based structure and additionally apply the earlier recommendation: swap the CRY gate on wires (0,8) for a CRX gate, introducing axis diversity in the entangling layer while keeping the same gate count and depth. This should preserve the good generalization gap of the CRY variant while potentially improving expressivity through mixed-axis entanglement.
</DESCRIPTION>
