crz_hub_ccrz_hybrid

<DESCRIPTION>
This crossover keeps the proven high-performing backbone from the two 0.63-scoring seeds (RY encoding → hub-and-cluster CRZ entangling layer with partial parameter sharing on the two 4-connectivity qubits (0-7/0-8) and the two symmetric pairs (1-3/1-8, 2-4/2-6) → independent RZ post-rotation), since removing the earlier pre-entangling RZ layer and de-sharing most CRZ parameters was what drove the score up from 0.54 to 0.63.

On top of that stable backbone, two lightweight additions are introduced to boost expressibility and capture higher-order correlations without materially inflating the parameter/gate budget:

1. A cheap RX "pre-tilt" layer between the RY encoding and the CRZ entangling layer, with qubits grouped into 3 sets of 3 (by index mod 3) sharing one parameter each. This gives every qubit a second rotation axis (Y then X) before entangling, at the cost of only 3 extra parameters per block, which should help the circuit represent richer single-qubit states without adding much overfitting risk.

2. A single three-qubit CCRZ gate on well-separated qubits (0, 4, 8) placed after the pairwise CRZ layer, adding one more parameter per block to let the model capture a genuine three-body correlation that pairwise gates cannot express, again at minimal parameter cost.

The final independent RZ post-rotation layer (one free parameter per qubit) is kept unchanged from the best-performing seed, preserving per-qubit phase flexibility for the readout.

This design tries to combine the empirically best entangling/readout structure with a modest, cheaply-parameterized boost in expressibility (extra rotation axis + one higher-order interaction), aiming to push validation accuracy higher while keeping the train-validation gap and parameter count under control.
