<DESCRIPTION>
This candidate is a crossover between the two best-performing seeds (score 0.63) and the more elaborate cycle/chord/CCRZ variant (score 0.58). It keeps the proven low-depth "hub-qubit-0 + secondary cluster" CRZ entangling topology from the 0.63 seed (which gave the best generalization gap and validation accuracy), since that structure clearly outperformed the higher-depth cycle-based design. On top of that stable backbone we graft two targeted improvements inspired by the second program:

1. Parameter-efficient mirrored final RZ phase layer (rz_post_0..rz_post_4 shared symmetrically across "mirror" qubits by distance from the hub), cutting the number of independent output-phase parameters roughly in half versus the 9 independent RZ's in the top seed, improving parameter efficiency without removing any gates from the circuit (gate count/depth unchanged).
2. A single, cheap three-qubit CCRZ correlation gate (on qubits that are maximally separated in the entangling graph: 0, 4, 7) borrowed from the cycle-based program, added once (not twice) to inject a small amount of higher-order correlation while keeping added depth minimal (only one extra multi-qubit gate instead of two CCRZ + extra CRX chords that hurt the other candidate).

This keeps the low depth/gate-count profile that correlated with the higher score while cautiously adding expressiveness and trimming redundant post-processing parameters.
