ry_sandwich_crz_entangler

<DESCRIPTION>
Built directly on the best-performing prior seed (score 0.63), which used a full 12-gate parametrized CRZ entangling layer over all allowed two-qubit pairs with light, deliberate parameter sharing (9 unique CRZ params instead of 12), sandwiched between an RY encoding layer and an RZ output layer. The earlier attempt to swap some CRZ gates for fixed CZ/CRX gates (current program, score 0.58) hurt performance by reducing the model's entangling expressivity and breaking gate-type symmetry — confirming that fully parametrized, symmetric entangling layers are important here.

This version keeps that proven full-CRZ entangling backbone intact (same 12 gates, same sharing pattern: crz_2 shared on (0,7)/(0,8), crz_3 shared on (1,3)/(1,8), crz_4 shared on (2,4)/(2,6)) but inserts a lightweight mid-layer of RY rotations on the four highest-connectivity/most "central" qubits (1, 3, 5, 7 — each touches 2-3 allowed edges) right after the entangling block and before the final RZ layer. This creates an RY(encode) → CRZ(entangle) → RY(mid-refresh) → RZ(output) sandwich. The intuition: after entanglement, a small local amplitude/phase refresh on the qubits most involved in cross-qubit interactions can help the readout layer extract better-separated features, without significantly increasing depth (+2) or parameter count (+4 unique params per repetition), and without touching the already-effective entangling structure.

This is a conservative, targeted modification that isolates a single new idea (recommended mid-layer RY block) on top of the known-best entangling backbone, rather than combining multiple unproven structural changes at once (as the regressed CZ/CRX variant did).
