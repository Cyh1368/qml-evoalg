<DESCRIPTION>
Two changes: (1) Revert the mid-circuit RY rejuvenation layer added in the previous iteration, since it increased parameter count (186) and depth without improving accuracy—validation accuracy dropped from 0.72 to 0.68 and score from 0.63 to 0.60. Reverting restores the better-performing architecture. (2) Apply light parameter sharing in the initial encoding layer: pair ry_1 with wire 8 (both use param "ry_1") and ry_3 with wire 6 (both use param "ry_3"), removing the previously separate ry_6 and ry_8 parameters. This reduces total parameter count by 2 (from 162 to 160 in the reverted architecture) while keeping full expressivity of feature encoding across all 9 qubits, testing whether this tighter parameterization improves the train-test gap and parameter efficiency without harming accuracy, consistent with the recommendation to explore limited encoding-layer sharing.
