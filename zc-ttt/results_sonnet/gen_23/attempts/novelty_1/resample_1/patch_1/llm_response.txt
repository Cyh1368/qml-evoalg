<DESCRIPTION>
The previous experiment (mixed CRZ/CRX, 180 params) scored lower (0.58) than the version using CRZ/CRY (180 params, 0.60) and the lean 150-param seed (0.60, smaller generalization gap). This suggests: (1) CRY is a better choice than CRX for the second entangling axis on this dataset, and (2) reducing parameter count (especially in the redundant post-entanglement RZ layer) tends to shrink the generalization gap without hurting accuracy. This edit reverts CRX->CRY (restoring the better-performing entangling layer) and additionally shares the 9 independent post-RZ rotation parameters into 5 grouped parameters (pairing structurally similar/adjacent qubits), cutting per-block parameter count from 30 to 26 while keeping the same gate count and depth. This should retain expressivity from the entangling layer while reducing overfitting risk, aiming to combine the best traits of both prior high-scoring variants.
