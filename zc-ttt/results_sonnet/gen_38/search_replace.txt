<DESCRIPTION>
The prior experiments show that removing the three-body ZZZ correlation layer restored the best score (0.63) compared to adding it (0.58), so we revert to the simpler RY-CRZ-RZ structure that performed best. On top of that, we apply the suggested optimization of reordering the twelve CRZ entangling gates into two maximally-commuting sublayers (Sublayer A on qubits with no shared wires: [0,2],[1,3],[2,4],[3,5],[5,7]; Sublayer B: [0,3],[0,7],[1,8],[2,6],[4,8],[6,7]) executing all of Sublayer A before Sublayer B. Since gates within a sublayer act on disjoint qubits, they can be executed in parallel, reducing circuit depth (~39 vs 41) while keeping the same gate_count (207) and n_params (162). This should preserve or slightly improve accuracy/generalization while lowering depth, which historically correlated with better validation performance in this search.
