densify_secondary_cluster_zzz

<DESCRIPTION>
The suggested recommendation to add CRZ gates on edges [1,5] and [2,3] is not implementable because those qubit pairs are not in the allowed two-qubit connectivity list. To achieve the same underlying goal—densifying the secondary cluster region (qubits 1,2,3,5,6) with a modest number of extra parameters/gates—we instead use two three-qubit ZZZ gates (which have no wire restriction) that touch the same secondary-cluster qubits and share a single new parameter "zzz_sec". This adds richer triple-wise correlations among the already-entangled secondary cluster (1-3, 2-4/2-6, 3-5) without increasing depth much (+2 gates, +1 param), keeping the parameter count close to the current 162-param sweet spot while testing whether extra multi-qubit correlations in that region improve validation/test accuracy and reduce the generalization gap further.
</DESCRIPTION>
