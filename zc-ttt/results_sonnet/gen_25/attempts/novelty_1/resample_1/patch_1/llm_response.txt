hierarchical_shared_ccrz_ansatz

<DESCRIPTION>
A structurally different ansatz that replaces the flat "one CRZ/CRY per allowed pair" pattern with a symmetric three-tier design: (1) an RY encoding layer, (2) a fully-shared 4-parameter CRZ entangling layer that groups all 12 allowed two-qubit pairs into four rotationally-symmetric triples (so every pair is used exactly once but only 4 unique angles control the whole entangling layer), (3) a genuinely new component — a three-qubit CCRZ layer applied to three disjoint triples that partition all 9 qubits `(0,4,8),(1,5,7),(2,3,6)`, sharing a single parameter `ccrz_0`, which injects higher-order (triple-wise) correlations that no earlier candidate used, and (4) a final individual RZ layer per qubit for measurement-basis rotation. This design continues the empirical trend observed in the seed-program history (aggressive but structured parameter sharing reduced train/val gap and improved score, 180→162 params raised score 0.60→0.63), while adding a fundamentally different three-body interaction term instead of just re-shuffling two-qubit gate axes (which the CRX swap experiment showed can hurt performance). The three-qubit CCRZ term should let the circuit capture correlations among qubit triples that two-qubit-only entanglers cannot represent, potentially improving accuracy without increasing parameter count much, since parameter efficiency and reduced overfitting seem to correlate with higher score in the observed history.
