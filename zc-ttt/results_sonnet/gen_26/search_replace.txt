<DESCRIPTION>
The previous edit (adding a mid RY sub-layer and replacing some CRZ entanglers with fixed CZ) regressed performance (0.57 vs 0.63 for the shared-CRZ design). Revert the entangling layer back to the proven parameter-shared CRZ scheme (which achieved combined score 0.63 with 162 params), and additionally inject a small amount of genuine three-body correlation via a disjoint-qubit ZZZ layer using a single shared parameter. Because the three ZZZ gates act on completely disjoint qubit triples ((0,4,8),(1,3,6),(2,5,7)), they can be scheduled in parallel, adding only ~1 layer of depth and just 1 new parameter, while giving the ansatz access to third-order correlations across all 9 qubits that pure single/two-qubit rotations cannot capture. This should improve expressivity/accuracy without materially harming the train-test gap or convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
