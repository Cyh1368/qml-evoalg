<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the parameter-heavy final RZ layer with a shared-parameter RY echo after ring entanglement. Reusing each qubit’s initial RY parameter reduces the unique parameter count while ensuring the entangling layer affects the Z readout through a noncommuting post-rotation. Closing the CZ line into a ring removes endpoint asymmetry and gives every qubit equal entangling degree, which should improve parameter efficiency and regularize training.
</DESCRIPTION>
