<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the 16 independently parameterized local rotations with a permutation-equivariant four-parameter block. Complete-graph CZ entanglement directly mirrors all 28 pairwise feature channels, while shared pre/post rotations strongly regularize the six independent block copies and align with mean-Z pooling. A single central CRZ supplies limited symmetry-breaking phase expressivity without restoring the severe overparameterization of the current circuit. This reduces the total parameter count from 98 to 26 and should improve validation accuracy, L2 loss, generalization, and parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
