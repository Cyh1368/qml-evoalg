<DESCRIPTION>
Close the brickwork CZ chain with a [7, 0] coupling. This gives every qubit degree two and removes the representational asymmetry of the endpoint qubits, which is especially relevant because all eight Z expectations contribute equally to the fixed mean readout. The added gate is parameter-free and is disjoint from [1, 2], [3, 4], and [5, 6], so it completes the second parallel entangling layer without increasing the ideal circuit depth or parameter count. This should improve global feature mixing while preserving the current ansatz's parameter efficiency and fast convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
