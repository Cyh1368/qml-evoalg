<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the overparameterized 24-parameter-per-block ring ansatz with a four-parameter symmetric block. Complete CZ pair coverage aligns the circuit connectivity with all 28 IsingZZ feature channels, while two shared central CRZ bridges provide adaptive entanglement without sacrificing parameter efficiency. Globally shared pre/post rotations regularize the six independent block copies and match the permutation-invariant mean-Z readout. A final RX mixer reuses the bridge parameter, adding a noncommuting phase-to-population conversion at zero parameter cost; this combines the stronger accuracy of the two-bridge parent with the faster dual-axis optimization behavior of the RX-mixer parent. The resulting model has 26 total parameters instead of 146.
</DESCRIPTION>
