<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the seed’s separated RY/RZ preparation and post-entangler RZ layer with a complete RZ–RY–RZ Euler rotation on every qubit before the CZ chain. This preserves the same 24 trainable parameters per ansatz block, while giving each qubit full local SU(2) expressivity before entanglement. Keeping the parameter count unchanged should avoid increasing overfitting or training cost while improving optimization flexibility.
</DESCRIPTION>
