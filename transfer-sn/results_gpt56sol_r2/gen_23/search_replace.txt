<DESCRIPTION>
Replace each RY-RZ preparation pair with a full RZ-RY-RZ Euler block to improve local basis adaptation around the diagonal feature map and CZ entanglers. Retain post-entangler RY analysis only on alternating qubits to limit depth and overfitting, and share those angles with the corresponding Euler RY parameters. This preserves exactly 24 independent parameters per block and 146 total parameters while increasing expressivity through the additional independent pre-entangler RZ axis. The alternating post-analysis layer still converts phase correlations into measurable Z populations without increasing the parameter budget.
</DESCRIPTION>
