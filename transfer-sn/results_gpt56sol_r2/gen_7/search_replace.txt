<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the closing CZ edge between qubits 7 and 0. The feature map already couples every qubit pair, so closing the ansatz ring adds correlated depth without adding a new data interaction channel. An open CZ chain avoids the observed degradation from the ring, reduces circuit depth and gate count, and preserves the 24 independent local rotation parameters that previously achieved stronger validation accuracy and convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
