<DESCRIPTION>
The previous ansatz used `RY` and `RZ` single-qubit rotations and a simple linear chain of `CZ` gates, which led to high parameter count (24 parameters per block) and limited entanglement (no wrap-around or strong mixing).
This edit replaces it with a strongly entangling, hardware-efficient ansatz using alternating `RY` and `RX` layers with staggered `CNOT` ring entanglement. This effectively spreads information globally across the 8 qubits, increases expressiveness, and reduces the parameter count from 24 to 16 per block. This reduction directly increases parameter efficiency and helps mitigate overfitting, thereby reducing the train-test gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
