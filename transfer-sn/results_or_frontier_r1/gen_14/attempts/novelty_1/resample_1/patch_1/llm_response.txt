<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the overparameterized wire-specific block with a permutation-equivariant four-parameter block. Shared rotations align with the symmetric mean-Z readout and should reduce the observed train-validation gap, while the complete CZ graph matches the 28 possible feature-pair channels and previously produced substantially stronger validation accuracy than sparse connectivity. A new shared RX layer directly after the collective RY layer provides two-axis input mixing, and the final collective RZ-RX pair keeps the last operation non-diagonal so it can directly affect Z readout. This reduces the model from 194 to 26 total parameters while retaining expressive all-to-all interactions.
