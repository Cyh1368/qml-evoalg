crz_parameterized_matchings_and_mid_circuit_mixer
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replaces the fixed CZ gates in the 7 edge-colored perfect matchings with parameterized CRZ gates, sharing one trainable parameter per matching. This gives the optimizer fine-grained control over entanglement strength while preserving the parallelism and circuit depth. Additionally, inserts a mid-circuit mixer of RX and RY rotations to break the sequence of commuting CRZ (diagonal) operations into two noncommuting stages, allowing for more complex multi-qubit unitaries and building on previous high-performing generations.
</DESCRIPTION>
