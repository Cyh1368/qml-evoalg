<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the redundant all-to-all CZ layer with a degree-three Möbius-ladder graph organized into three disjoint perfect matchings: alternating ring edges, shifted ring edges, and opposite-node chords. This preserves uniform qubit connectivity and matches the collective rotations and mean-Z readout while reducing each block from 28 to 12 entanglers. The total gate count falls from 396 to 300 without changing the 20 trainable parameters. Grouping disjoint edges also substantially lowers depth, while repeated uploads and blocks retain sufficient propagation capacity. The sparser symmetric graph should regularize the model, maintain the small train-test gap, and improve the complexity-adjusted combined score.
</DESCRIPTION>
