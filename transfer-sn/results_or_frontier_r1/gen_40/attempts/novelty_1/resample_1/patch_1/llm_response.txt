<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single shared output RX layer with a shared X-Y-X Euler mixer. The three noncommuting collective angles preserve permutation symmetry and parameter sharing while giving every independent ansatz block a full SU(2) readout basis. This should convert a broader range of phase correlations into mean-Z populations, improving classification boundaries and L2 loss without adding entangling depth. The ansatz uses six parameters per block and 38 total parameters, remaining compact.
