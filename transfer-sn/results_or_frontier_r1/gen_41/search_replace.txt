<DESCRIPTION>
The previous bipartite K4,4 entangler heavily bottlenecked intra-partition mixing, resulting in a significant accuracy drop compared to earlier complete-graph versions. However, the complete K8 graph (28 CZ gates) per block resulted in high gate count and lower parameter efficiency.

This edit constructs a highly expressive and parameter-efficient symmetric block:
1. **4-Regular Circulant Graph**: We replace the K4,4 graph with a combination of distance-1 (nearest-neighbor) and distance-3 (rapid-mixing) ring connections. This requires only 16 CZ gates (the same as K4,4) but guarantees uniform and fast correlation spread across all 8 qubits.
2. **Commutativity Breaking**: Breaking the commutativity of consecutive CZ gates is critical for the generation of non-trivial entanglement. We insert a collective `RX` layer (`mid_rx`) *between* the distance-1 and distance-3 CZ stages. This transforms the 16 commuting CZs into two highly non-commuting entanglement stages, massively boosting expressivity without extra entangling gates.
3. **Parameter Efficiency**: We use exactly 4 parameter strings per block (`in_ry`, `mid_rx`, `out_rz`, `out_rx`). This ensures the model is very parameter efficient (only 26 total parameters), significantly increasing the efficiency component of the combined score while achieving top-tier generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
