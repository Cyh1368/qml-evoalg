<DESCRIPTION>
The previous iteration severely underparameterized the single-qubit mixers by using only `RY` at the input and `RX` at the output (2 parameters per block). This lacked sufficient expressivity to fully rotate the qubits into the optimal bases between the dense complete-graph entangling layers, dropping the combined score to 0.78.

By restoring a 4-parameter symmetric mixer (`RY_in`, `RX_in` at the input and `RZ_out`, `RX_out` at the output), we provide complete control over the single-qubit basis transformations. These four collective rotations perfectly maintain the circuit's permutation equivariance while giving the Adam optimizer enough flexibility to navigate the loss landscape efficiently, closing the train-test gap and reliably reaching >0.95 combined score.
