<DESCRIPTION>
This design uses a brick-wall-inspired structure with two key entanglement patterns: a full ring of CZ gates (nearest-neighbor circular) and cross CZ connections (stride-4, connecting qubit q to q+4) for long-range entanglement. Single-qubit layers alternate RY and RZ rotations to provide full Bloch sphere coverage. The structure is: RY layer → RZ layer → Ring CZ → RY layer → Cross CZ → RZ layer. This gives 32 independent parameters per block. With p=2 repetitions and l=3 data re-uploads, the ansatz has 192 total ansatz parameters plus gain/bias. The ring topology ensures every qubit is entangled with its neighbors while the stride-4 cross connections create non-local correlations across the register, which is important for capturing feature interactions encoded by the IsingZZ feature map across different qubit pairs.
</DESCRIPTION>

ANSATZ_SPEC = []

# First rotation layer: RY + RZ for full single-qubit parametrization
for q in range(8):
    ANSATZ_SPEC.append({"gate": "RY", "wire": q, "param": f"ry0_{q}"})
for q in range(8):
    ANSATZ_SPEC.append({"gate": "RZ", "wire": q, "param": f"rz0_{q}"})

# Ring entanglement: CZ on circular nearest neighbors
for q in range(8):
    ANSATZ_SPEC.append({"gate": "CZ", "wires": [q, (q + 1) % 8]})

# Second rotation layer: RY for mid-block expressibility
for q in range(8):
    ANSATZ_SPEC.append({"gate": "RY", "wire": q, "param": f"ry1_{q}"})

# Cross entanglement: CZ with stride 4 for long-range correlations
for q in range(4):
    ANSATZ_SPEC.append({"gate": "CZ", "wires": [q, q + 4]})

# Final rotation layer: RZ
for q in range(8):
    ANSATZ_SPEC.append({"gate": "RZ", "wire": q, "param": f"rz1_{q}"})
