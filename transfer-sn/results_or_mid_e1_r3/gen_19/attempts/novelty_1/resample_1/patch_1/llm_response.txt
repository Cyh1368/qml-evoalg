Replace the current symmetric preprocessing + CZ ring with the better-performing alternating CRZ ansatz pattern. This restores the earlier 5-parameter-per-block structure that achieved higher validation/test accuracy with fewer total parameters, while keeping the circuit shallow and compatible with the fixed training loop. The design uses shared rotations, two shifted CRZ entanglement patterns, and a final shared rotation layer to maintain parameter efficiency and improve generalization.
