<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the dense per-qubit RY/RZ stack with a much more parameter-efficient brickwork mixer.

Why this should help:
- Fewer parameters per block reduces overfitting and improves the parameter-efficiency metric.
- Alternating even/odd shared rotations preserve enough expressivity while dramatically shrinking the search space.
- Mixing CNOT and CZ in a staggered ring encourages stronger amplitude/phase transfer than a pure CZ chain, which is useful for a phase-encoded feature map.
- A final shared RY layer keeps the last block directly expressive at measurement time, helping validation/test accuracy without adding much complexity.

Overall, this is a compact ansatz designed to keep accuracy competitive while lowering depth, gate count, and train-test gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
