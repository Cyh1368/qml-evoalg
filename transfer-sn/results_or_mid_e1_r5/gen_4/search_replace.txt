<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the fully local 24-parameter seed with a lower-parameter, multi-scale ansatz that should generalize better on the binary classification task.

Key ideas:
- Tie parameters across mirrored qubit pairs to reduce overfitting and improve parameter efficiency.
- Mix RX, RY, and RZ rotations so each block can change both amplitudes and phases.
- Use disjoint CZ layers at three scales: long-range, staggered, and local. This creates a butterfly-style entangling pattern that is better suited to the fixed data-reuploading feature map than a simple nearest-neighbor chain.
- Reuse the same phase parameters in a final Z layer to sharpen decision boundaries without increasing parameter count.

Overall, this should reduce train-test gap, lower the total number of trainable circuit parameters, and preserve enough expressivity for strong validation/test accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
