<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the overparameterized mixed single-qubit/entangler stack with a more structured, pair-symmetric bottleneck ansatz. The new block keeps expressive power by combining:
- mirrored local RY/RZ feature extraction across qubit pairs,
- two complementary entangling patterns (local pair CZs and long-range CZs),
- a lightweight trainable CRY bridge on the long-range pairs,
- and a final mirrored RX/RZ refinement layer.

The main goal is to improve generalization and parameter efficiency at the same fixed training budget by enforcing symmetry across the 8-qubit layout and reducing redundant degrees of freedom. This should help validation/test accuracy while lowering the train-test gap and improving combined score through better use of parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
