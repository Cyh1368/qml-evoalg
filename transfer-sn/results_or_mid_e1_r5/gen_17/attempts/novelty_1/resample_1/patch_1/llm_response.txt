<DESCRIPTION>
The previous ansatz used a symmetric pattern but relied heavily on fixed CZ gates and a relatively simple layer structure. The training accuracy was high (0.94), but the test accuracy stalled at 0.88, suggesting a need for better generalization through structured, yet expressive, entanglement.

This modification introduces a more robust ansatz block with the following features:
1.  **Circular CNOT Chain**: Replaces the fixed CZ ladder with a circular CNOT chain to ensure every qubit can interact with its neighbors, facilitating better information flow across the 8-qubit register.
2.  **Increased Non-linearity**: Adds both CRY and CRX parametrized layers. These gates provide controlled non-linearities that are more expressive than fixed CZ gates.
3.  **Strategic Parameter Sharing**: Maintains the 4-qubit symmetric parameter sharing (pairing qubits 0-4, 1-5, 2-6, and 3-7). This keeps the parameter count manageable (20 parameters per block) while allowing the circuit to learn patterns that are distributed across the qubit register.
4.  **Multi-Stage Mixing**: Uses an RY-RZ-CRY-CRX-RY sequence to ensure that each block performs thorough state mixing before and after the entanglement stages.

These changes aim to lower the validation loss further and potentially push the test accuracy beyond the 0.88 plateau by providing a more flexible gradient landscape for the Adam optimizer.
</DESCRIPTION>
