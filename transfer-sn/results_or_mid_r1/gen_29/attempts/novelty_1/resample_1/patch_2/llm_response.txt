<DESCRIPTION>
This edit keeps the compact 74-parameter structure, but replaces the purely phase-based local entanglement with a mixed CZ/CNOT scaffold.

Why this should help:
- The original compact block already reduced overfitting and improved convergence speed.
- CZ-only local mixing can be too diagonal for a readout based on mean Z expectations; inserting CNOTs on nearest-neighbor pairs creates basis-changing correlations that are often easier for Adam to exploit.
- The long-range CZs remain to preserve the useful global parity-like coupling.
- The mirrored shared RY/RZ parameterization is preserved, so parameter efficiency stays high and the train-test gap should remain controlled.

This is a targeted ansatz-only change and stays fully within the allowed schema.
</DESCRIPTION>
