<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the current fully local, over-parameterized block with a more structured low-parameter ansatz that is better matched to the mean-Z readout and the feature-map-induced ZZ phases.

Key ideas:
- Use mirror-symmetric shared single-qubit rotations to reduce redundant degrees of freedom and improve generalization.
- Insert a trainable long-range CRY entangling layer to adaptively mix feature-induced correlations.
- Keep a fixed CZ ring as a cheap second entangling path to preserve expressivity without increasing parameter count.
- Maintain a compact 12-parameter-per-block budget, which should help validation/test stability and convergence speed compared with the current 16-parameter block.

The result keeps the architecture valid, stays within the supported gate set, and should improve the accuracy/gap tradeoff while lowering depth and parameter count.
</DESCRIPTION>
