<DESCRIPTION>
The previous ansatz used a linear CZ chain and fixed RZ/RY rotations, resulting in a validation accuracy of 0.89 and a test accuracy of 0.87 with 146 parameters. 

I propose evolving the ansatz to use parametrized two-qubit gates (`CRZ`) for entanglement, which is well-suited for the phase-based `IsingZZ` feature map. The new block design consists of:
1. A trainable `RY` layer for initial state preparation and basis alignment.
2. A circular `CRZ` entangling layer where each pair's coupling is individually trainable, allowing the circuit to learn specific correlations between adjacent qubits.
3. A final `RX` layer to provide rotation around a different axis, ensuring the ansatz can reach a broader set of states in the SU(2)^N manifold.

This structure maintains the same parameter count (24 per block, 146 total) but increases expressivity by making the entanglement layer trainable. This should lead to better feature extraction and higher accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
