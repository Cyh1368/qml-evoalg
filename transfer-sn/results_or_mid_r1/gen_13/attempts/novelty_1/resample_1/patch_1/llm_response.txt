<DESCRIPTION>
This edit turns the block into a phase-first, symmetry-regularized echo ansatz. It prepends a trainable RZ calibration layer before the entanglers, then uses a compact fixed CZ mixer and a mirrored RY layer, and finally reuses the same RZ parameters as a closing calibration. This directly implements the suggested pre-upload phase adjustment inside the only editable region, while reducing parameter count and encouraging smoother optimization and better generalization through parameter sharing and mirror symmetry.
</DESCRIPTION>
