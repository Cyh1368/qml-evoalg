<DESCRIPTION>
The previous ansatz used an RY, RZ, CZ (linear), RZ structure, containing 24 parameters per block (146 total parameters). The test metrics indicated minor overfitting with a noticeable generalization gap.
This edit simplifies and strengthens the ansatz block by:
1. Using an `RX` and `RY` rotation pair per qubit. `RX` and `RY` rotations are well suited to interact with the Z-basis `IsingZZ` feature map, fully navigating single-qubit states and rotating amplitudes effectively.
2. Replacing the linear `CZ` chain with an even-odd `CNOT` ring topology. `CNOT` is highly expressive, and the periodic wrap-around directly connects qubit 7 to 0, which aids deeper entanglement across all registers while reducing circuit depth due to parallelism in the brick-layer structure.
3. Lowering the parameter count per block from 24 to 16, decreasing the total parameters to 98. This addresses the train-test gap and improves parameter efficiency without sacrificing expressivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
