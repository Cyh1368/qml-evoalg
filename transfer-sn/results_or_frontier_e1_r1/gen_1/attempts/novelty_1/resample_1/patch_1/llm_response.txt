- Only RY/RZ rotations (missing RX axis)

1. All three rotation axes (RY→RX→RZ) for full SU(2) coverage

3. Long-range cross connections (0↔4, 1↔5, 2↔6, 3↔7)
4. CNOT gates instead of CZ (creates X-basis entanglement, complementing Z-basis encoding)

Same parameter count (24/block, 146 total) but much higher expressivity.
