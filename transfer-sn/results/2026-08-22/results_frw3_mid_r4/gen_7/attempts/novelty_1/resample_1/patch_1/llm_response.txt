<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz achieves excellent results (test accuracy 0.90, generalization gap 0.01) through extreme parameter sharing with only 4 unique parameters per block. To improve further, I propose adding per-qubit phase tuning via individual RZ rotations after the collective phase layer. 

Rationale:
1. The collective approach maintains symmetry and efficiency but limits fine-grained expressivity
2. Adding 8 individual RZ parameters allows each qubit to learn its own phase offset while preserving the structured collective input/output mixers
3. This increases expressivity (12 unique params per block vs. current 4) while remaining highly parameter-efficient (74 total params vs. previous 146-194)
4. Per-qubit phases are computationally cheap but can capture subtle qubit-specific effects from the feature map encoding
5. The pattern fits naturally between the collective phase and entanglement, allowing phase information to influence the interaction structure

Expected impact:
- Modest increase in test accuracy (targeting 0.91-0.92)
- Maintained small generalization gap
- Fast convergence due to low parameter count
- Improved parameter efficiency through targeted expressivity
</DESCRIPTION>
