<DESCRIPTION>
This evolution proposes an ansatz block that leverages three key architectural principles to improve the combined score:

1. **Collective Parameter Sharing**: Following the success of prior experiments, all eight qubits share a single parameter for each rotation layer. This serves as a strong regularization prior, significantly reducing the generalization gap (as seen in the 0.01 gap of Seed 2) and increasing parameter efficiency.
2. **Interleaved Entanglement matchings**: Instead of applying all entangling gates in a single cluster, the three perfect matchings of a 3-cube (dimension 0, 1, and 2 edges) are interleaved with parametrized rotation layers. This increases the effective expressivity and mixing of the circuit per unit depth.
3. **Multi-Axis Mixing**: The block utilizes RY, RZ, and RX rotations. Interspersing these non-commuting rotations between the Ising-like entanglement layers allows the model to better navigate the Hilbert space and transform the phase-encoded feature information into the Z-basis for measurement.

The resulting block has 5 unique parameters, leading to a highly efficient total of 32 trainable parameters (including readout) across the full re-uploading circuit.
