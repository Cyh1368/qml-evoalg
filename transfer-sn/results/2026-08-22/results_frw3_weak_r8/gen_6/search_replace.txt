<DESCRIPTION>
This modification enhances the ansatz by incorporating controlled rotations (CRY gates) to create more expressive parameterized entanglement while maintaining the symmetric structure. The key improvements are:

1. Added controlled rotations (CRY) between neighboring qubits in a ring topology to create parameterized entanglement
2. Maintained parameter sharing to keep the parameter count low while increasing expressivity
3. Preserved the symmetric structure that worked well in previous iterations
4. Added intermediate RZ layers to increase expressivity without breaking symmetry

The controlled rotations provide more flexible entanglement patterns compared to fixed CZ gates, while the ring topology ensures all qubits have equal connectivity. This should improve the model's ability to learn complex patterns in the data while maintaining good generalization properties.
</DESCRIPTION>
