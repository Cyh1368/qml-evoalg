Looking at the current design, I notice that all 12 CZ gates are applied in sequence without any intermediate rotations. This is a significant limitation because consecutive diagonal gates (CZ) acting on disjoint qubits without interleaved non-commuting rotations fail to build progressively complex entanglement. The key principle from quantum circuit expressibility research is that **interleaving rotation and entanglement layers** dramatically increases the effective expressivity of the circuit.

By splitting the three CZ matchings (nearest-pair, stride-2, stride-4) and inserting shared RY rotations between them, we create three genuine rounds of entanglement building. Each rotation converts phase information from one CZ layer into amplitude information that the next CZ layer can further correlate. This should push validation accuracy beyond 90% while maintaining the regularization benefits of parameter sharing (keeping total params at only 44).
