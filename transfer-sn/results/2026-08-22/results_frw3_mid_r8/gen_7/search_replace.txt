<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current per-qubit, high-parameter template with a low-dimensional shared-parameter sandwich: a collective RY/RZ input mixer, a cube-style CZ entangler backbone, a sparse long-range CRZ correlation layer tied to the same shared phase parameter, and a collective RY/RX output mixer. This keeps the trainable circuit near the efficient 26-parameter regime, but adds a controlled-phase interaction layer that can capture higher-order correlations from the re-uploaded feature map while still regularizing the model and reducing the train-test gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
