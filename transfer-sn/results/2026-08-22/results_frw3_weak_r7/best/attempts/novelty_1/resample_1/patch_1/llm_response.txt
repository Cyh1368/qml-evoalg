<DESCRIPTION>
This edit introduces controlled-RZ gates to enhance the expressivity of the ansatz while maintaining the symmetric structure. The key improvements are:
1. Adding CRZ gates in a symmetric pattern (similar to the existing CZ gates) to increase entanglement capabilities
2. Maintaining parameter sharing to keep the parameter count low
3. Preserving the overall symmetric structure that worked well in previous iterations
4. The addition of controlled gates should help capture more complex feature correlations while the shared parameters ensure efficient training
</DESCRIPTION>
