Looking at this problem, I need to design an effective ansatz block for an 8-qubit classifier with data re-uploading (l=3) and p=2 repetitions per upload. The key is balancing expressibility with trainability while ensuring good entanglement coverage.

<DESCRIPTION>
This design uses a "brick-layer + cross" entanglement strategy with three rotation layers. The key insights are:

1. **Brick-layer entanglement**: Alternating even (0-1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-7) and odd (1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7-0) CZ pairs create a ring topology through two staggered layers, ensuring all nearest-neighbor pairs interact.

2. **Long-range cross connections**: CZ gates between antipodal qubits (0-4, 2-6) create shortcuts across the ring, reducing the effective diameter of the entanglement graph. This helps information flow between distant qubits that may be connected by the IsingZZ feature map.

3. **Three rotation layers (RY+RZ, RY, RY+RZ)**: The initial and final layers use full Euler-angle coverage (RY+RZ), while the middle layer uses only RY to keep parameters moderate. This gives sufficient single-qubit expressibility while keeping the block at 40 parameters.

4. **Moderate parameter count**: 40 params per block × 6 applications (3 uploads × 2 reps) = 240 total, which is enough for expressibility without overfitting on binary classification.

The brick-layer pattern avoids barren plateaus better than all-to-all connectivity while the cross connections ensure the ansatz can capture long-range correlations in the feature-encoded state.
</DESCRIPTION>

for q in range(8):
    ANSATZ_SPEC.append({"gate": "RY", "wire": q, "param": f"ry0_{q}"})
for q in range(8):
    ANSATZ_SPEC.append({"gate": "RZ", "wire": q, "param": f"rz0_{q}"})

# Layer 6: Final full single-qubit rotations (RY + RZ)
for q in range(8):
