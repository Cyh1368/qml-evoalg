<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current 3-layer CZ “cube entangler” (12 CZ gates per block, all parameter-free) with a more expressive but still parameter-efficient entangling pattern:
- Keep the first 2 matchings as CZ (8 CZ gates total).
- Replace the 3rd matching with CRZ gates that all share a single trainable parameter (“ent_crz”), adding entangling expressivity while adding only 1 new parameter.
This should improve generalization/accuracy while reducing the overall circuit two-qubit gate count (and thus depth-related overhead) versus the all-CZ version.
</DESCRIPTION>
