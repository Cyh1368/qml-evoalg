<collective_crz_entangler_replacement>
<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed CZ entangling layers with a parametrized CRZ entangler block that shares a single trainable parameter across all entangling edges. This increases the ansatz expressive power (introduces nontrivial controlled-phase angles beyond the fixed π/2 phases) while keeping parameter efficiency high via aggressive parameter sharing (only +1 unique parameter per ansatz block).
</DESCRIPTION>
