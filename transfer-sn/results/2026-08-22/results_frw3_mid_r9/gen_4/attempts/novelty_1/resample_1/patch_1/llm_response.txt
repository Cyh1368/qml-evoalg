<DESCRIPTION>
I am replacing the current high-parameter symmetric block with a more compact echoed mixer/entangler design.

The new ansatz keeps the strong inductive bias of shared parameters, but reduces the circuit to three trainable parameters per block:
- one global RY mixer,
- one global RX mixer,
- one shared CRZ bridge strength.

To preserve expressive power while improving generalization and efficiency, I add two complementary entangling patterns:
- local CZ pairings on disjoint nearest-neighbor pairs,
- a shifted CNOT ring to spread parity information across the register.

This should help the model learn phase-encoded binary correlations more efficiently, reduce overfitting, and lower the total parameter count and gate count while keeping the ansatz trainable across the fixed 3×2 re-uploading layout.
</DESCRIPTION>
