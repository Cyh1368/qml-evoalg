<DESCRIPTION>
The previous ansatz was overparameterized with excessive shared RX, CRX, and CRZ layers, leading to significant overfitting (generalization gap increased from ~0.00 to 0.09). I am reverting to a cleaner architecture that performed well in earlier iterations, utilizing shared single-qubit rotations (RY/RZ) combined with robust two-qubit entanglement (two interleaved CZ rings plus additional coupling chords). This reduction in parameter count should improve generalization and test accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
