# GLOBAL INSIGHTS SCRATCHPAD

The following are global insights about optimization approaches and their effectiveness:

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns  
- Coarse-grained single-qubit sharing across halves (wires 0–3 vs 4–7) for RY rotations, paired with merged pre/post entanglement RZ parameters, sustains top raw combined scores:  
  • Gen 21 (26 params) hit raw 0.90;  
  • Gen 24 (38 params) and current best Gen 29 (26 params) both reach raw 0.91.  
- CZ-only entanglement in two interleaved rings plus four opposite-qubit chords boosts expressivity at zero extra params:  
  • Gen 24 added chords to climb from raw 0.90→0.91 and cut convergence steps from ≈270→≈180;  
  • Gen 29 retains that topology (depth 105, gate_count 300) to sustain raw 0.91.  
- The 3-upload × 2-repeat re-upload layout with fixed IsingZZ feature maps consistently lands depth ≈100–105 and gate_count ≈280–300—the empirical “sweet spot” for raw ≥0.90 (Gen 24/29).  
- Reusing identical RZ keys before and after entanglement (merging them into one “rz_h”/“rz_h2” pair) halves per-block parameters and raises parameter efficiency to 0.04 in Gen 29 without loss of accuracy.  

## Ineffective Approaches  
- Adding parameterized two-qubit gates (CRX/CRY/CRZ) inflates params and depth but degrades performance:  
  • Gen 16/28 used ≥56 params, depth >110, yet only raw 0.88 and gen_gap 0.03–0.06.  
- Over-parameterized hierarchical sharing (80 params in Gen 25) yields low parameter efficiency (0.01) and raw 0.87 despite circuit depth 111 and gate_count 348.  
- Omitting cross-bank chords while keeping coarse sharing (Gen 27) caps raw at 0.90 (38 params, depth 99, gate_count 276), confirming chords are key for the extra +0.01.  
- Unguided long-range entanglement (Gen 19) increases depth >115 without exceeding raw 0.88, showing that connectivity must be structured.  

## Implementation Insights  
- ANSATZ_SPEC defined with explicit param keys and validated by validate_ansatz_spec produces a stable PARAMETER_KEYS_PER_BLOCK; apply_ansatz_block uses an offset = block_index × N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK and key_to_position mapping to slice params per block, enabling arbitrary N_UPLOADS×N_REPEATS.  
- Merging pre/post RZ layers in ANSATZ_SPEC by reusing the same param keys simplifies both code and reduces circuit parameters (e.g., Gen 29 achieves 4 params/block vs. 6).  
- qml.queuing.AnnotatedQueue combined with tape.graph.get_depth in circuit_metadata automates depth and gate_count computation, enforcing the optimal corridor (depth ~100–105, gate_count ~280–300).  
- The generic apply_ansatz_block abstraction decouples block content from loop structure, allowing rapid iteration over different sharing and entanglement patterns without refactoring the training loop.  

## Performance Analysis  
- Gen 29 (26 params, raw 0.91, depth 105, gate_count 300, param_eff 0.04, conv_steps 180) matches Gen 24’s raw 0.91 (38 params, param_eff 0.02) but doubles parameter efficiency by cutting params.  
- Only designs with 26–38 params, depth 99–108, and gate_count 276–300 achieve raw ≥0.90; circuits outside that corridor fall to raw 0.87–0.88.  
- Opposite-qubit CZ chords consistently reduce convergence steps from ~270 (no chords) to ~180 (with chords), as shown by Gen 20 vs. Gen 24/29.  
- Higher parameter efficiency (≥0.04) aligns with minimal generalization gaps (0.01 in Gen 29), whereas low-efficiency circuits (~0.01) exhibit gaps up to 0.04 (Gen 25).

# META RECOMMENDATIONS

The following are actionable recommendations for the next program generations:

1. Experiment with alternative upload×repeat splits at fixed depth. Keep six total ansatz blocks (depth ≈105, gate_count ≈300) but restructure as 2 uploads×3 repeats and 1 upload×6 repeats instead of 3×2. This isolates whether concentrating data injections or parameter layering yields faster convergence or higher accuracy while staying in the 26–38-parameter corridor known to hit raw ≥0.90.  

2. Introduce per-upload trainable feature‐scale factors in the fixed IsingZZ map. Add three new scalar keys “fs_u0”, “fs_u1”, “fs_u2” to PARAMETER_KEYS_PER_BLOCK and in feature_map multiply FEATURE_SCALE by fs_ui before upload i. This injects only 3 extra parameters (one per upload) and tests whether adaptive embedding strength further improves expressivity and convergence speed.
