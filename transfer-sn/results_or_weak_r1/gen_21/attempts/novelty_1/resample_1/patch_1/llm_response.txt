<DESCRIPTION>
Following the optimization strategy of reducing parameter count while maintaining expressivity, I am merging the pre-CZ and post-CZ rotation parameters for the RZ layers. By using the same parameter keys ("rz_h" and "rz_h2") for both the pre- and post-CZ RZ layers, we effectively constrain the circuit to a simpler subspace. This reduction in the number of free parameters (from 6 per block to 4 per block) often acts as a regularizer, helping the model generalize better and potentially accelerating convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
