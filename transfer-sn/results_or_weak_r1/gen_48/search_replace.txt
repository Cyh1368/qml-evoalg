<DESCRIPTION>
This change introduces hierarchical single-qubit offset parameters to enhance the expressivity of the ansatz while maintaining parameter efficiency. By adding small per-block offset keys ("Δry_h" and "Δry_h2") to the existing "ry_h" and "ry_h2" parameters, we allow each block to locally adjust its rotation bias. This approach preserves the coarse-grained parameter sharing between the two 4-qubit banks (0-3 and 4-7) while providing more flexibility in the rotations. The hierarchical scheme should help lift performance without significantly increasing the parameter count or risking overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
