<DESCRIPTION>
This change implements the recommendation to make all entanglers learnable via shared CRZ scales. The idea is to replace every CZ gate in ANSATZ_SPEC with CRZ gates, using two new per-block parameters "chi_ring" (for the eight ring edges) and "chi_chord" (for the four opposite-qubit chords). This adds 2 parameters per block (total N_PARAMS=38), retains the exact entanglement topology and corridor depth, and probes whether tuning entangling strengths can push raw scores above 0.91. This approach maintains the proven entanglement structure while adding flexibility to the entangling operations.
