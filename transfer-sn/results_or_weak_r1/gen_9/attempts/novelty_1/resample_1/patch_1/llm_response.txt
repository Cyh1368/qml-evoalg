<improved_expressivity_shared_rx_cr_layers>
<DESCRIPTION>
To improve accuracy/generalization without exploding parameter count, this edit adds (1) an extra shared RX layer (4 parameters per block, pairwise sharing) to increase local expressivity, and (2) a small number of parameterized controlled rotations (shared across multiple edges) to improve the model’s ability to capture nontrivial correlations beyond the fixed CZ rings. Parameter sharing is used aggressively to keep the candidate trainable-parameter budget reasonable while enabling richer ansatz dynamics across the fixed re-uploading/repeat structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
