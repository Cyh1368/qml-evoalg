<DESCRIPTION>
This edit simplifies the ansatz architecture to increase parameter efficiency and reduce overfitting, as indicated by the high generalization gap in the previous run. 

1. **Parameter Sharing**: We merge the pre- and post-rotational RZ layers into shared parameter groups (`rz_h`, `rz_h2`). This enforces a structural constraint that regularizes the training, reducing the parameters-per-block from 12 to 4.
2. **Structural Simplification**: We remove the auxiliary "ladder" CZ gates, reverting to the proven interleaved ring CZ topology. This reduces circuit depth and complexity, focusing the model's expressivity on the data-sensitive RY/RZ rotations.
3. **Efficiency**: By tying the parameters across the pre- and post-CZ layers, we allow the optimizer to find a stable configuration for the Z-axis rotations without doubling the parameter space, which is expected to improve generalizability.
</DESCRIPTION>
