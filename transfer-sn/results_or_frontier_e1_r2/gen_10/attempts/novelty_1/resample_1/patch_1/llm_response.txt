<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the redundant pre/post-RY structure with an RX→RZ_pre→CZ-chain→RZ_post block. RX supplies transverse mixing against the diagonal IsingZZ feature map, while distinct pre/post RZ layers preserve trainable phase diversity. A connected seven-edge CZ chain regularizes the over-entangling current block and is ordered as two commuting brickwork sublayers to reduce circuit depth. The edit retains 24 parameters per block (146 total), lowers the gate count, and prevents adjacent same-axis RY layers from collapsing across repeated blocks.
