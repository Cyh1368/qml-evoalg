<DESCRIPTION>
Insert a permutation-symmetric all-to-all CZ layer between the shared RX and RY mixers. The current repeated blocks contain only global single-qubit rotations, so adjacent blocks can largely collapse into another local rotation and provide limited additional expressivity. The complete-graph CZ layer prevents this collapse and supplies a collective nonlinear phase depending on excitation structure, which can expose higher-order correlations from the pairwise IsingZZ feature map. Sharing the mixers globally and treating all 28 qubit pairs equally preserves the strong generalization bias of the current ansatz, while adding no trainable parameters, keeping N_PARAMS at 14. The CZ gates are ordered as seven disjoint perfect matchings to minimize circuit depth.
</DESCRIPTION>
