<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the independently parameterized, line-entangled block with permutation-equivariant collective RX/RY mixers. Since the 28 pair features correspond to all qubit pairs, the feature map already supplies data-dependent entanglement; the fixed CZ line adds an arbitrary ordering bias that can increase overfitting. Sharing each mixer angle across all eight qubits preserves vertex-permutation symmetry, strengthens aggregated gradients, and lets the two repeated blocks form a flexible collective rotation between uploads. This reduces the model from 146 to 14 total parameters and lowers gate count and depth while retaining noncommuting rotations that convert encoded phases into measurable Z populations.
