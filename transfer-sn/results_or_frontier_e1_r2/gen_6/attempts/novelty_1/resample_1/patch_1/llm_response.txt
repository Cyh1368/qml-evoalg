Looking at the current design, I see several opportunities for improvement:

1. **The CZ entanglement is only a line (0-1, 1-2, ..., 6-7)** — missing the ring closure (7-0) and any long-range connections. This limits information flow between distant qubits.

2. **The final rotation layer is RZ** — but since we measure in the Z basis, RZ after entanglement only adjusts phases and cannot directly change measurement probabilities. A post-entanglement **RY** layer is far more useful because it converts entanglement-induced phase correlations into amplitude differences detectable by Z measurements.

3. **No cross-qubit connections** — qubits like 0 and 4 can only communicate through a chain of nearest-neighbor gates, requiring many layers to mix. Adding CZ cross-connections (0-4, 1-5, 2-6, 3-7) enables direct long-range correlations.

This design keeps the same 24 parameters per block (146 total) but dramatically improves entanglement structure and rotation composition.
