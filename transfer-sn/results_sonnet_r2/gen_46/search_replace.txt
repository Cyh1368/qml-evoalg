<DESCRIPTION>
The current dual-ring ansatz has generalization_gap_mean=0.05 (train 0.97 vs test 0.92), indicating mild overfitting relative to the seed's simpler single-ring version (gap 0.02). The distance-2 ring adds significant expressivity (4 extra shared params per block, 24 extra params total across all blocks) that may be driving overfitting without proportional gains in test accuracy. I propose collapsing the distance-2 ring's four shared parameters (crz_a/b/c/d for the second ring) down to two shared parameters, mirroring the parameter-sharing style of the distance-1 ring but reducing redundant capacity. This cuts total parameter count while preserving the long-range entangling topology, aiming to shrink the generalization gap and improve parameter efficiency, per the recommendation about regularizing entanglement without increasing gate count.
