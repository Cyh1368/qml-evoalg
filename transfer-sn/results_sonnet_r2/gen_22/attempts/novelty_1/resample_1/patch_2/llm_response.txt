This restores the higher generalization behavior. Since block-level odd/even alternation cannot be added without editing the fixed apply_ansatz_block function, I instead diversify long-range coupling by adding one extra fixed CZ gate directly between distant qubits (3 and 7) within the single ANSATZ_SPEC block definition, which applies identically in every block but provides persistent long-range correlation capture, complementing the local ring closure. This is a minimal, static structural change fully expressible within ANSATZ_SPEC.
