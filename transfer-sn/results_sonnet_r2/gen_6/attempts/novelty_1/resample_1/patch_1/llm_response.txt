add_ring_entanglement_and_crz_coupling

<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses a linear chain of fixed CZ gates plus RY/RZ rotations, giving limited entangling expressivity and no trainable inter-qubit correlation beyond the feature map. I propose:

1. Adding a closing CZ gate (7,0) to make the entangling chain a ring instead of an open chain, improving connectivity without adding parameters.
2. Replacing the fixed CZ layer with parametrized CRZ gates (with shared "shared" style unique params per edge) that let the ansatz learn how strongly to entangle each pair, increasing expressivity while still keeping parameter count modest.
3. Adding a second layer of RY rotations after entanglement to allow more expressive per-qubit rotations before the final RZ layer, which should help capture more complex decision boundaries and reduce train-test gap by giving the optimizer more expressive but structured mixing.

This should improve accuracy and reduce the generalization gap by increasing entangling expressivity while keeping the added parameter count reasonable (8 extra CRZ params + 8 extra RY params per block, well within budget given current 146 total params across 6 blocks).
