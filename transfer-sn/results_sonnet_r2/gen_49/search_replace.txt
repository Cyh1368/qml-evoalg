<DESCRIPTION>
Since only the ANSATZ_SPEC list itself is editable (not surrounding code), I cannot implement stochastic dropout logic outside the block. Instead, I apply a static structural improvement within ANSATZ_SPEC: replace the fixed alternating crz_a/crz_b distance-1 ring parameterization with a slightly richer parameter sharing scheme that uses 4 independent nearest-neighbor angles instead of 2, giving the nearest-neighbor ring more expressive power per block (similar param budget growth is offset by removing redundancy in the post-rotation RZ layer, reusing pre-rotation angles instead of independent ones to keep total parameter count similar). This targets better fitting capacity on the primary (most informationally local) coupling ring while keeping overall parameter count close to previous best (98), aiming to improve accuracy/gap tradeoff.
