Since only ANSATZ_SPEC is editable, I focus improvements there. The current spec (170 params) has full independent RY/RZ pre-rotation and post-rotation layers on all 8 wires (16 params) plus 4 CRZ params, totaling 20 params/block x 6 blocks + readout, leading to a larger generalization gap and higher validation/test loss than the better-scoring seed variant (110 params, score 0.86) which shared RY/RZ params by pair and only had 2 unique CRZ angles.

This edit reduces parameter count by sharing single-qubit rotation angles across symmetric qubit pairs (mirroring the higher-scoring seed's design) while keeping a richer entangling ring than the original 4-param seed: I use 4 distinct CRZ angles (one per ring edge pair, reused twice around the 8-cycle) instead of 8 independent ones, and share RY/RZ pre and post rotations per adjacent pair (4 params each layer instead of 8). This roughly halves parameters per block (from 20 to 12: 4 RY + 4 RZ_pre + 4 CRZ... let's recompute: I'll use 4 RY-shared + 4 CRZ (2 distinct reused) + 4 RZ-shared = 12 params/block), reducing total params substantially (12*6+2=74) while retaining full connectivity via the CZ/CRZ ring and enough tunable freedom via distinct pair-groupings to fit the task, aiming to shrink the generalization gap and total parameter count similar to the well-performing 110-param seed, but with even more compact parameter sharing for better efficiency.
