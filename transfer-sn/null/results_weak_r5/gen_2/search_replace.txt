<DESCRIPTION>
Improves the ansatz by introducing alternating entangling layers with CRZ gates to enhance expressivity and parameter efficiency. The modification adds controlled rotations between neighboring qubits in alternating patterns, which helps create more complex correlations while maintaining the same number of parameters. This change should improve both training and test accuracy by allowing the circuit to learn more intricate decision boundaries.
</DESCRIPTION>
