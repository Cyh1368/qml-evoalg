<DESCRIPTION>
To improve on the existing ansatz, I've redesigned the block to include a richer sequence of operations. This structure incorporates:
1. Two layers of single-qubit rotations (RY and RZ) for enhanced state space exploration.
2. A two-layer entangling structure: a set of parametric CRZ gates followed by fixed CZ gates to provide both linear and non-linear interactions.
3. A final layer of RZ rotations to allow the circuit to refine the phase of the encoded state before the next data upload or final measurement.

This structure balances expressivity and parameter count, aiming to improve upon the 1.39 combined score by allowing for more complex decision boundaries while keeping the parameter space manageable.
</DESCRIPTION>
