<DESCRIPTION>
I'm enhancing the ansatz block to improve expressivity while maintaining parameter efficiency. The modifications include:

1. Adding a second RY layer before the CZ ring to increase expressivity
2. Adding parametrized CRZ gates crossing the ring (connecting opposite qubits) to enhance entanglement
3. Adding a final RX layer to break symmetry and provide additional expressivity

This new structure maintains the efficient parameter sharing while providing better entanglement and expressivity, which should improve convergence and reduce loss.
