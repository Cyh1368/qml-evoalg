<DESCRIPTION>
Following the provided recommendations, we boost the expressivity of the ansatz by introducing a pre-entanglement layer of RZ rotations and parameterized two-qubit rotations (CRX) immediately after every second CZ gate. The addition of the RZ layer before entanglement gives full single-qubit expressivity (since it forms an arbitrary rotation when combined with the initial RY), while the CRX gates introduce parameterized, non-commuting entanglement into the CZ chains. This enriched coupling profile allows the circuit to map complex, non-local data interactions more effectively and converge to lower loss faster.
</DESCRIPTION>
