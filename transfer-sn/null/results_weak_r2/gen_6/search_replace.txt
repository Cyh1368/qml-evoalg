<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses a static CZ entangling layer. I am upgrading this to a parameterized CRZ layer (controlled-rotation Z). This increases the expressive power of the ansatz (as CRZ generalizes CZ) and adds a layer of tunable phase coupling. This change increases the parameter count per block from 24 to 31 (8 RY + 8 RZ + 7 CRZ + 8 RZ), which is well within the capacity of the model and should further improve accuracy by allowing the optimizer to learn the optimal phase coupling rather than relying on fixed CZ gates.
</DESCRIPTION>
