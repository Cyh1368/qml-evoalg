<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses a simple linear chain of CZ gates. This modification introduces more entanglement by using interleaved patterns of CZ gates across odd and even qubit pairs. This approach increases circuit expressivity without significantly increasing depth, potentially improving accuracy and reducing the train-test gap by allowing better exploration of the parameter space.

The changes include:
1. Replacing the linear CZ chain with two interleaved layers of CZ gates (odd-even pattern)
2. Adding parameterized single-qubit rotations before and after the entangling layers to increase expressivity
3. Maintaining the data re-uploading structure but enhancing the ansatz block with more sophisticated entanglement

This modification should improve the model's ability to learn complex feature correlations while keeping the parameter count reasonable.
</DESCRIPTION>
