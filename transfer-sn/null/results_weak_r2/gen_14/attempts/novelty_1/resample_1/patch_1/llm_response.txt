<DESCRIPTION>
This modification enhances the ansatz by implementing a more structured entanglement pattern using CRZ gates. Instead of the previous CRX ring configuration, we use a pairwise entanglement strategy that creates stronger correlations between qubits. The design uses a pre- and post-RZ layer to maximize the effectiveness of the CRZ entanglement, and reduces the overall parameter count compared to the baseline while maintaining high performance.
