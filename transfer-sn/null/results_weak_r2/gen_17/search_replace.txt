<DESCRIPTION>
We adopt a hybrid entanglement strategy within the ansatz blocks to balance expressivity and parameter count. We replace every even-indexed CRX gate in the ring topology (0-1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-7) with a fixed CZ gate, maintaining the ring connectivity while reducing the parameter overhead. Additionally, we enforce parameter sharing for the RZ rotation layer, utilizing a single 'rz_shared' parameter for all qubits. This design follows the recommendation to mix fixed-gate scaffolds with parameterized gates, aiming to optimize the trade-off between model capacity and training efficiency.
