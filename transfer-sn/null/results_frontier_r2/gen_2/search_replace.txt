Looking at the current ansatz, I notice a key inefficiency: the RZ layers before and after the CZ chain are redundant because CZ gates commute with RZ gates (both are diagonal in the Z basis). This means 8 parameters per block are wasted. Additionally, the linear CZ chain provides limited connectivity.

My proposed design:
1. **Removes redundant RZ parameters** - replaces the RZ-CZ-RZ sandwich with just CNOT-RX, saving 8 params/block
2. **Uses CNOT ring topology** instead of linear CZ - provides full circular connectivity (including qubit 7↔0), enabling faster information propagation
3. **Uses RX instead of RZ** after entanglement - RX doesn't commute with CNOT or the IsingZZ feature map, maximizing expressivity per parameter
4. **RY + RX combination** spans general single-qubit rotations when interleaved with entanglement

Result: 16 params/block × 6 blocks + 2 readout = 98 total params (vs 146), with better entanglement.
