<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the over-parameterized rotation stack with a QAOA-inspired phase-and-mixer block. The balanced CZ ring gives every qubit equal entangling degree and is ordered as two parallel brickwork layers. A shared RZ retains a trainable collective phase while independent terminal RY mixers preserve wire-specific expressivity. Placing the mixers last ensures the preceding diagonal feature, CZ, and RZ phases affect the Z readout; in the seed's final block, gates after its RY layer commute with the measurement and are partly ineffective. Parameter sharing reduces the model from 146 to 56 total parameters, which should improve generalization and parameter efficiency while lowering gate count and depth.
</DESCRIPTION>
