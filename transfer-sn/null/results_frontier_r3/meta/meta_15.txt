# GLOBAL INSIGHTS SCRATCHPAD

The following are global insights about optimization approaches and their effectiveness:

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns

- Three data re-uploads interleaved with two repeats of a rotation–CZ–rotation ansatz block, as in the current best “8-Qubit Repeated Upload Ansatz Classifier” (Gen 14, patch residual_cross_crz), consistently achieves combined_raw = 0.99 with 100% train/validation/test accuracy and zero generalization gap.  
- Augmenting a fixed IsingZZ feature map with dense CZ entanglement—first nearest-neighbor bricks, then ring closures, then fixed long-range links—followed by small-angle CRZ residuals on those long-range pairs (4 disjoint CRZ gates) sharpens decision boundaries and lifts raw score from 0.98 (e.g. Gen 7 & Gen 11 at 252 gates) to 0.99 (276 gates).  
- The alternating RY–CZ–RZ layering motif drives robust convergence in ~180–300 gradient steps across top performers (Gen 6, Gen 10, Gen 14), confirming that sandwiching single-qubit rotations around entanglers stabilizes training.  
- A two-parameter readout calibration (gain and bias on mean Pauli-Z) is present in every top program (Gen 4–Gen 14), consistently reducing the generalization gap to ≤ 0.01.

## Ineffective Approaches

- Restricting entanglement to ring-only CZ layers (Gen 13 pure_ry_mixer_cz_ring: 50 params, 180 gates) dropped combined_raw to 0.97, demonstrating that long-range couplings are essential for full expressivity.  
- Omitting trainable residual couplings on long-range links (e.g. pure CZ only in Gen 12 rz_even_odd_split_interleave) maintained zero gap but yielded lower raw accuracy (0.98) despite fewer gates/params, indicating that small-angle two-qubit variational phases add critical flexibility.  
- Merely stacking more ansatz repeats without structural enrichment (early Gen 0’s 144-param design) stalled at combined_raw = 0.97, showing that unstructured repeat counts cannot substitute for targeted connectivity.  
- Swapping CZ for CNOT entanglers (Generation 2’s RY–CNOT–RZ bricks) degraded combined_raw by ≈ 0.03, confirming CZ’s superior alignment with binary feature correlations.

## Implementation Insights

- Defining the ansatz via a declarative ANSATZ_SPEC and a dynamic key-to-index lookup allows quick insertion of gates (e.g. adding the four CRZ residuals in Gen 14 required only appending entries).  
- Using Pennylane’s `AnnotatedQueue` + `QuantumScript` to extract depth and gate_count on the fly guided cost/benefit trade-offs: Gen 14 trimmed 24 gates and 24 parameters from Gen 6 (300 → 276 gates, 146 → 122 params) without sacrificing accuracy.  
- Freezing the feature map (fixed `qml.IsingZZ`) focuses variational learning solely in the ansatz block, controlling parameter explosion and ensuring stable convergence.  
- The modular `apply_ansatz_block` with centralized SPEC_ERRORS validation prevents mismatches in parameter indexing across uploads/repeats, reducing debugging overhead when experimenting with new connectivity patterns.

## Performance Analysis

- Increasing gates from 252 (Gen 7 & Gen 11) to 276 (Gen 14) and parameters from 98 to 122 yielded a raw combined gain of 0.01 (0.98 → 0.99), illustrating diminishing but tangible returns for enriched long-range entanglement.  
- A depth increase from 81 (Gen 12) to 93 (Gen 14) correlates with a 0.01 jump in combined_raw, at the cost of lowering parameter efficiency from 0.02 to 0.01—highlighting a trade-off between resource overhead and marginal accuracy gains.  
- All top-scoring variants converge within 180–300 Adam steps at learning rate ≈ 0.03; beyond ~300 global steps, additional training yields negligible improvements, suggesting a practical cap on training budgets.  
- Zero generalization gaps first appeared at combined_raw = 0.99 in Gen 6 and persist through Gen 14, indicating that once a minimal connectivity threshold is met (ring + cross links + CRZ residuals), further entanglement richness no longer risks overfitting.

# META RECOMMENDATIONS

The following are actionable recommendations for the next program generations:

1. Implement parameter sharing across all RY–RZ rotation layers by reusing the same `ry_i` and `rz_i` keys in every of the six ansatz blocks. This will cut N_PARAMS by ~40% (toward ~70 parameters) while preserving the proven alternating rotation–CZ–rotation motif that underlies stable convergence. Measure whether the lower parameter count sustains the 0.99 combined_raw and zero gap, boosting parameter efficiency.

2. Fuse each RY→RZ sandwich into a single `qml.U3` gate per qubit in both pre- and post-entanglement sections. In your `ANSATZ_SPEC`, replace each `{"gate":"RY","param":"ry_i"}` + `{"gate":"RZ","param":"rz_i"}` pair with one `{"gate":"U3","wires":[i],"param":("u3_theta_i","u3_phi_i","u3_lambda_i")}` entry. This fusion slashes depth and gate_count by ~25% while retaining the noncommuting rotation–entanglement–rotation structure, which should speed up convergence without accuracy loss.

3. Inject sparse trainable CRZ couplings into the nearest-neighbor brick layers by swapping every second CZ in both the first and second brick layers for a parameterized `qml.CRZ` initialized at π/4. Extend your `ANSATZ_SPEC` with keys like `crz_nn_01`, `crz_nn_23`, etc., so that local entanglement strengths can be fine-tuned without changing overall depth. This mirrors the success of the long-range CRZ residuals that lifted raw accuracy from 0.98 to 0.99.

4. Experiment with an alternative upload schedule of N_UPLOADS=2 and N_REPEATS=3 (six total blocks) while keeping the block design intact. Successful programs converge robustly in ~180–300 steps regardless of depth distribution, so shifting more ansatz layers per feature upload may accelerate learning or improve generalization. Compare convergence_epoch, combined_raw, and parameter_efficiency against the baseline 3×2 layout.

5. Augment long-range mixing by inserting a star-topology CZ layer on qubit 0 immediately before the residual CRZs: in `ANSATZ_SPEC`, append `{"gate":"CZ","wires":[0,i]}` for i=1…7. This densifies cross-block connectivity using the same CZ primitive that consistently drove combined_raw to 0.99. Evaluate whether the extra entanglers yield marginal accuracy gains or faster convergence under the existing training budget.
