<DESCRIPTION>
This ansatz combines high-expressivity single-qubit layers with a multi-topology entangling strategy to maximize information flow and convergence speed. It employs independent RY and RZ rotations on all 8 qubits to ensure a high-dimensional search space within each block. To ensure efficient communication across the 8-qubit register, the entanglement layer uses a circular CZ chain for nearest-neighbor connectivity, complemented by long-range CRX gates that bridge qubits with a stride of 4 (e.g., 0-4, 1-5). By sharing the CRX parameters in two distinct groups, the model achieves a strong trade-off between expressive power and parameter efficiency, aiming to match the fast convergence of heavy models while maintaining a leaner total parameter count of 110.
