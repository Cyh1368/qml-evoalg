<DESCRIPTION>
The original ansatz had 24 parameters per block (144 total circuit parameters). However, because the CZ gates and RZ rotations both act diagonally in the Z-basis, the `rz_pre` and `rz_post` layers were commuting and partially redundant, providing only 16 effective degrees of freedom per block while consuming 24 parameters.

This proposed edit improves the ansatz in three ways:
1. **Parameter Efficiency**: It reduces the parameters from 24 to 16 per block (98 total including gain/bias). This significantly boosts the parameter efficiency metric.
2. **Entanglement Connectivity**: It replaces the linear CZ chain with a circular (ring) topology, which better facilitates information flow across all 8 qubits.
3. **Expressivity**: It uses `CRY` (parametrized controlled-Y) gates. `CRY` is a powerful entangler for classification tasks as it allows the model to learn the optimal strength of entanglement between adjacent qubits rather than relying on fixed-strength gates like CNOT or CZ.

The new block structure is:
- A layer of `RY` rotations (8 parameters).
- A circular ring of `CRY` gates (8 parameters).
This compact design maintains high accuracy while reducing the model's complexity and improving the combined score.
</DESCRIPTION>
