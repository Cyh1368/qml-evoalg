This edit replaces the dense per-qubit RY/RZ stack with a more parameter-efficient, symmetry-breaking hardware-efficient block.

Key ideas:
- Cut the per-block parameter count from 24 to 12 by sharing angles across structured qubit pairs.
- Use three different pairing patterns across RX, RY, and RZ layers to avoid overly rigid symmetry.
- Add a fuller CZ entangling pattern: a ring plus long-range cross links, which better spreads feature-map correlations across the 8-qubit register.
- Keep only supported gates and preserve the fixed training loop, while improving generalization and parameter efficiency.

This should help reduce overfitting, lower gate count/depth, and improve the combined score without changing the fixed data pipeline.
