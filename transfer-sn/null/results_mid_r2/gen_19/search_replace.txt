<DESCRIPTION>
The current program performs significantly worse (1.26 vs 1.45) despite having MORE parameters (146 vs 98). This indicates overparameterization and poor parameter efficiency.

Analysis:
- Prior top performers used 98 parameters: 8 RY + 8 CRX/CRY ring + 8 fixed CZ gates = 16 params/block × 6 blocks
- Current uses 146 parameters: adds 8 RZ post-gates, bringing to 24 params/block × 6 blocks
- Despite 50% more parameters, accuracy dropped from 1.00 to 0.98 and loss increased

The redundant RZ post-gates appear to hurt generalization without providing benefit.

Strategy:
1. **Remove the 8 RZ post-gates** to eliminate overparameterization (return to 16 params/block baseline)
2. **Convert fixed CZ gates to trainable CRZ gates** with unique parameters (adds back 8 params/block = 24 total, same as current but higher quality)

This preserves the alternating CRX/CRY ring pattern from top-performing prior program while replacing ineffective RZ post-gates with trainable long-range phase couplings. CRZ gates directly control entanglement strength, complementing the fixed IsingZZ feature map's phase encoding. This should improve optimization landscape and decision boundary tightness.

Net effect: Maintain reasonable parameter count (~114 total) but with better-quality gates, restoring accuracy toward 1.45+ level.
