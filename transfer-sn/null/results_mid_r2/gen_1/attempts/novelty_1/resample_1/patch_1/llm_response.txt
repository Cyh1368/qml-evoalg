<DESCRIPTION>
The seed ansatz uses 24 parameters per block across three rotation layers (RY, RZ, RZ) and a linear CZ chain. While it achieves high accuracy (0.98), it is parameter-heavy (146 total params), resulting in a lower parameter efficiency score.

I am proposing a more compact and expressive ansatz that reduces the parameters per block from 24 to 16. This new design uses:
1. A layer of RY rotations (8 parameters) for state preparation.
2. A circular layer of parametrized CRX gates (8 parameters), which provide both entanglement and controlled X-rotations.
3. A fixed circular CZ layer with a stride of 2 to ensure more global connectivity across the 8-qubit register.

By reducing the parameters per block to 16, the total parameter count drops from 146 to 98 (16 params * 6 blocks + 2 calibration params). This significantly improves the parameter efficiency metric while the increased expressiveness of the CRX gates and circular connectivity aims to maintain or improve the classification accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
