<DESCRIPTION>
The previous results show that while sharing parameters (Current) improves parameter efficiency, it significantly slows down convergence (from 150 to 750 steps) and slightly reduces accuracy. Seed 2 achieved the highest score (1.44) by using unshared RY and CRX gates, which are more expressive and allow for faster gradient-based learning.

This proposed edit implements an ansatz with 12 unique parameters per block (8 unique RY and 4 unique CRX). This strikes a balance between the high expressivity of Seed 2 and the parameter efficiency of the current model. Specifically:
1.  **Unique RY layer**: Applies independent RY rotations to all 8 qubits, providing a flexible starting point for the block.
2.  **Fixed CZ chain**: A circular entanglement layer to provide stable base-level connectivity.
3.  **Long-range Parametric Entanglement**: Applies 4 CRX gates on distant qubits (pairs separated by distance 4, e.g., (0,4)). Using independent parameters for these long-range interactions helps capture correlations across the entire qubit register efficiently, which is important given the all-to-all nature of the 28-dimensional IsingZZ feature map.
4.  **Efficiency**: By reducing the parameters from 16 (Seed 2) to 12 per block, we improve the `parameter_efficiency` metric while maintaining enough degrees of freedom to reach 100% accuracy quickly.
</DESCRIPTION>
