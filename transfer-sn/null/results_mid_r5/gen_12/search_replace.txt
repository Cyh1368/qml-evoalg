<DESCRIPTION>
Evolve the ansatz by incorporating a trainable entangling layer. While the current program uses a fixed CNOT ring, empirical results from Prior 2 suggest that increasing the expressive power of the entangler—even at the cost of more parameters—significantly accelerates convergence (from 390 to 180 steps) and improves accuracy.

This design implements a "Strongly Entangling Layer" inspired structure:
1.  **RY Layer**: Initial trainable rotations to prepare qubit states.
2.  **CNOT Ring**: Fixed base entanglement to provide a starting topology.
3.  **CRY Ring**: Trainable controlled-Y rotations. This allows the circuit to learn the optimal strength and phase of entanglement between adjacent qubits, which is critical for mapping the complex 28-feature IsingZZ couplings to the label output.
4.  **RZ Layer**: Final local phase adjustments before the next re-upload or measurement.

The total unique parameters per block is 24, resulting in 146 total parameters (matching the successful Prior 2). This architecture aims to combine the stability of the ring topology with the high expressivity of trainable conditional gates.
</DESCRIPTION>
