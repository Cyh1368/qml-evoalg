To improve the expressivity of the classifier, we replace the fixed `CZ` gates in the second layer of the ansatz with parameterized `CRZ` gates. This introduces trainable entanglement strengths, allowing the circuit to adaptively modulate the coupling between qubits, which can help minimize the loss function more effectively than fixed entanglement gates.
