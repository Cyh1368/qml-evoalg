<DESCRIPTION>
This modification aims to enhance the expressivity of the ansatz by introducing controlled rotations (CRZ gates) in a layered entanglement structure. The idea is to add more complex entanglement patterns while maintaining the same number of parameters. The CRZ gates are placed between the existing CZ layers to create more varied interactions between qubits, potentially improving the model's ability to learn complex patterns in the data.
</DESCRIPTION>
