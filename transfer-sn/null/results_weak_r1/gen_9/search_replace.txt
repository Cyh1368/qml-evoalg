The previous ansatz was overly parameterized (212 params), leading to potential overfitting or inefficient optimization. I am refactoring the ansatz to use parameter sharing across qubit pairs (reducing the parameter count while maintaining expressivity) and simplifying the gate layout to be more structured, similar to successful prior iterations. Specifically, I am using shared RY and RZ parameters for adjacent qubit pairs, which significantly reduces the parameter count and helps convergence.
