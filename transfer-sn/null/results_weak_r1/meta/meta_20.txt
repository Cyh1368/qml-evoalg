# GLOBAL INSIGHTS SCRATCHPAD

The following are global insights about optimization approaches and their effectiveness:

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns  
- Three-fold re-uploading interleaved with two ansatz repeats (N_UPLOADS=3, N_REPEATS=2) consistently delivers perfect classification.  
  • 8-Qubit Reuploading Quantum Classifier (Gen 17, 122 params; raw 0.99) and Evolved Ring-CRZ Ansatz (Gen 19, 158 params; raw 0.99) both use this 3×2 layout with zero generalization gap.  
- Local CZ backbone augmented by strategically placed long-range CRZ couplings boosts expressivity without excessive depth.  
  • The current best (Gen 19) uses a ring of CZs plus ten CRZ links, reaching raw 0.99 at 136 depth/288 gates. Gen 14’s CZ-CRZ mixed backbone (122 params) also hit raw 0.99.  
- RY rotation layers before and after entanglers stabilize training and accelerate convergence.  
  • Both Gen 12 (158 params; converged in ~210 steps) and Gen 19 (158 params; ~210 steps) sandwich CRZ/CZ layers with RY stacks, outperforming RY–RZ–CZ deep stacks.  
- Mid-range parameter counts (~120–160) strike the optimal balance of expressivity and efficiency.  
  • Gen 14 (122 params) and Gen 19 (158 params) each achieve raw 0.99, whereas over-parameterized Gen 18 (194 params) stalls at raw 0.98.  

## Ineffective Approaches  
- Extreme parameter sharing or aggressive pruning under-parameterizes the circuit.  
  • Shared-Parameter CRZ Ansatz (Gen 15, 56 params) plateaued at raw 0.96 despite depth ~91, indicating loss of expressivity.  
- Over-parameterization beyond ~160 trainable parameters yields diminishing returns.  
  • Hardware-Efficient Classifier (Gen 18, 194 params) and earlier Evolved Ansatz (Gen 11, 206 params) both cap at raw 0.98.  
- Deep hardware-efficient stacks without targeted CRZ positioning add gates but not score.  
  • Gen 18’s six alternating RY/RX/RZ blocks with CZ cycles (324 gates) only achieved raw 0.98, vs Gen 19’s 288 gates hitting raw 0.99.  
- Pruning entanglers to reduce depth below ~80 slows convergence and degrades score.  
  • No evaluated circuit with depth <87 surpassed raw 0.98 in validation pipelines.  

## Implementation Insights  
- Defining ansatz via ANSATZ_SPEC + validate/apply pattern enables rapid swap of entangler topologies (ring vs chain vs backbone).  
  • Evolved Ring-CRZ (Gen 19) simply updates ANSATZ_SPEC with ring CZs and CRZ links, reusing the same apply_ansatz_block logic.  
- Ordered parameter-key extraction ensures consistent mapping across multiple uploads/repeats.  
  • PARAMETER_KEYS_PER_BLOCK from validate_ansatz_spec guarantees correct indexing for both Gen 14 and Gen 19 despite differing gate lists.  
- Standardized training loop (PennyLane default.qubit backprop + Adam + early stopping/patience=75 + best-weight restore) produces zero generalization gap reproducibly.  
  • Gen 12, Gen 14, and Gen 19 all converge stably in ~210–240 steps with this framework.  
- Explicit readout calibration (learnable gain/bias) corrects output scaling, enabling L2 loss stability and perfect ±1 classification.  
  • Every top performer (Gen 14, Gen 17, Gen 19) includes two readout parameters and reports zero sign-mismatch error.  

## Performance Analysis  
- Combined raw score leaps from 0.96 (56 params) to 0.99 as parameter count increases from ~56 → ~122–158.  
  • Shared-Parameter CRZ (Gen 15) vs Evolved Ring-CRZ (Gen 19) demonstrates a +0.03–0.04 raw improvement by adding targeted entanglers.  
- No further score gains beyond ~158 params; Gen 19 (158 params) matches Gen 14 (122 params) at raw 0.99, while Gen 18 (194 params) stops at raw 0.98.  
- Deeper uniform entanglement (depth 136) converges faster (~210 steps) than shallower designs (depth 87 converges in ~240 steps), suggesting richer connectivity accelerates learning.  
- Parameter efficiency (~test_accuracy/n_params ≈ 0.01) plateaus across top circuits; increasing parameters beyond 160 dilutes efficiency without boosting accuracy.

# META RECOMMENDATIONS

The following are actionable recommendations for the next program generations:

1. Introduce per-upload trainable feature-map scales. Replace the fixed FEATURE_SCALE with a small vector FEATURE_SCALE_U = pnp.zeros(3, requires_grad=True), and in feature_map use FEATURE_SCALE_U[upload_index] · records[...,k] instead of π/2. This only adds 3 parameters but gives the optimizer direct control over encoding strength for each of the three uploads—a trick shown to speed convergence without altering circuit depth.  

2. Swap two ring CZs for parameterized CRZs per block to boost local flexibility. In ANSATZ_SPEC, replace the CZs on wires [2,3] and [6,7] with  
   {"gate":"CRZ","wires":[2,3],"param":"crz_back_b0"} and  
   {"gate":"CRZ","wires":[6,7],"param":"crz_back_b1"} (with unique keys per block).  
   This adds just two trainable two-qubit parameters per block, directly tuning backbone entanglement and testing whether targeted local CRZs further raise raw accuracy or accelerate convergence.  

3. Complete single-qubit SU(2) coverage by appending an RZ layer after the final RY in each block. After the existing eight final RY entries, insert  
   {"gate":"RZ","wire":q,"param":"rz_corr_w{q}"} for q in 0…7 (with block-indexed keys).  
   This adds eight parameters per block but remains in the mid-range count (~158–170), and builds on the proven RY–entangler–RY pattern by allowing each qubit to perform full SU(2) rotations, which may sharpen decision boundaries.  

4. Mix two-qubit rotation axes on the long-range links to tighten convergence. Modify ANSATZ_SPEC so that for even block indices the 10 long-range gates remain CRZ, but for odd block indices switch those same wires to CRY (with new param names like cry_02_b1, etc.). Axis alternation has driven zero generalization gap in prior variants and could further edge up test accuracy or reduce steps to converge.  

5. Rewire mid-range correlations by swapping out two existing long-range CRZ links for anti-diagonal pairs per block. For each block, drop e.g. the CRZs on [2,4] and [3,5], and instead add CRZs on [0,7] and [1,6] (with params crz_ad1_bx/crz_ad2_bx). This preserves the total of ten two-qubit rotations per block, rebalances the mid-range entanglement pattern, and probes whether alternative long-range connectivity can push raw score beyond 0.99.
