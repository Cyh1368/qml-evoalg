<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current “two RY layers + 7 CRZ couplers” block (23 params/block) with a more hardware-efficient pattern:
- 1× RY layer on all 8 qubits (8 params)
- fixed CZ entangling backbone (no trainable params)
- 4× CRZ long-range couplers (4 params) to capture nonlocal correlations with fewer parameters than the current 7-CRZ scheme
- 1× final RZ layer on all 8 qubits (8 params)

This reduces parameters per block from 23 → 20 and typically improves trainability / generalization under re-uploading (fewer circuit parameters but still high expressivity via SU(2) axes + structured entanglement).
