<DESCRIPTION>
The candidate improves parameter efficiency and expressivity by replacing the complex combination of long-range CRZ and CZ gates with a simpler, hardware-efficient circular CNOT entanglement layer.

Key changes:
1.  **Simplified Connectivity**: Replaces the linear CZ chain and sparse long-range CRZ gates with a circular CNOT chain (8 gates), providing more robust entanglement across the 8-qubit register.
2.  **Strategic Parameter Reuse**: Implements a weight-sharing scheme within the block by applying an initial RY layer, an RZ layer, the CNOT ring, and then reusing the parameters from the initial RY layer for a second RY rotation. This reduces the number of unique parameters per block from 28 to 16, significantly boosting the `parameter_efficiency` metric while maintaining the model's capacity to transform the basis between feature uploads.
3.  **Circuit Optimization**: By reducing N_PARAMS from 170 to 98, we aim to decrease the generalization gap and improve convergence speed, addressing the high convergence step (1650) seen in the previous iteration.
</DESCRIPTION>
