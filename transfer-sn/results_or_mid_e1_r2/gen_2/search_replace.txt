2. **Cross-connections**: Add strategic diagonal CZ gates (0,4), (1,5), (2,6), (3,7) to enable information flow between distant qubit pairs without dramatically increasing circuit depth.
3. **Parametrized entanglers**: Replace some fixed CZ gates with parametrized CRZ gates to increase expressiveness per gate.
