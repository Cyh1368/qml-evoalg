<DESCRIPTION>
The previous iteration used `ZZZ` (MultiRZ) to capture winning line correlations, which improved parameter efficiency but didn't significantly boost accuracy. This edit introduces `CCRZ` (doubly-controlled RZ) gates targeting the center qubit (8) and corners, conditioned on winning line completions. This mimics a "logical AND" for board patterns. 

Additionally, I am replacing the fixed `CZ` grid with `CRZ` gates. While `CZ` is a strong entangler, `CRZ` allows the optimizer to tune the strength of the grid-based correlations. I also add a final `RX` layer to provide local mixing in a different basis before measurement, which is a common technique in variational circuits to increase expressivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
