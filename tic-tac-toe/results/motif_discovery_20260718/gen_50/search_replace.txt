<DESCRIPTION>
The previous best candidate showed that grid-based CRZ entanglement and CCRZ motifs significantly improved accuracy and reduced loss. To further improve the expressivity of the entanglement layer without increasing the parameter count significantly, I am interleaving CRX gates into the grid-based entanglement layer.

By using the same `crz_grid` and `crz_to_center` parameter names for the CRX gates, we allow the circuit to perform a more complex basis-mixing operation (X and Z rotations) conditioned on the same control-target pairs. This "XY-style" entanglement can capture non-classical correlations more effectively than pure Z-basis entanglement. I am also adding a final set of RY rotations to provide a last trainable basis change before the measurements.
</DESCRIPTION>
