<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce overfitting and improve parameter efficiency by consolidating duplicate RZ parameters (pre/post) into a single symmetry-grouped RZ key used in both positions, and by merging the two CRZ edge-pair parameter keys into a single center-edge CRZ parameter. This reduces the number of unique parameters per block (and thus total parameters) while preserving the geometric symmetries and the motif-discovery ZZZ triple interactions aligned with winning lines. The change aims to lower model capacity slightly (reducing generalization gap) while keeping expressive motifs that capture winning-line correlations.
</DESCRIPTION>
