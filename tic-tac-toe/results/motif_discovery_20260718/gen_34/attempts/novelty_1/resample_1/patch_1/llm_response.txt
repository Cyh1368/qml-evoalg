<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current 14-parameter-per-block ansatz with a compact, symmetry-aware 13-parameter-per-block motif that previously showed stronger validation/test performance (78 total parameters vs 84). The new block groups single-qubit rotations by corner/edge/center (parameter-sharing for regularization and parameter efficiency), uses doubly-controlled RZ (CCRZ) motifs along winning-line triples separated into "line" and "center" roles, applies a shared CRZ grid (with separate center-connecting param), and finishes with grouped RZ and RX rotations for local state refinement. This reduces total parameters, keeps motif-specific controlled-phase structure for line detection, and replaces the CZ backbone with trainable CRZ entanglers to make entanglement amplitudes learnable while lowering fixed gate count/depth — all within the allowed gate and connectivity constraints.
