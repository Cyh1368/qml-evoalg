<DESCRIPTION>
This modification injects a targeted phase layer (RZ) immediately after the high-order `ZZZ` interactions to allow the circuit to better process the non-local correlations generated by the winning-line motifs. By grouping these phases by symmetry (corners, edges, center), we introduce specific degrees of freedom to adjust the interference patterns before the final rotation layer. To maintain the parameter count at 78 (13 unique parameters per block), the final `RZ` and `RX` rotations for edges and center are collapsed into a single `rot_others` parameter. This trade-off tests if local phase adjustments following multi-qubit entanglement are more valuable than independent final rotation angles for non-corner qubits.
</DESCRIPTION>
