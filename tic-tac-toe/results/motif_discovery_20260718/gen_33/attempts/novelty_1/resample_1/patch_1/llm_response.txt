<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce parameter count and introduce symmetry-inspired parameter sharing for three-qubit motifs.
This change groups per-qubit rotations by corner/edge/center, replaces eight independent ZZZ line
parameters with three category parameters (row/col/diag), shares center-edge CRZs, and gives
diagonal CCRZs a dedicated shared parameter. These modifications match a successful motif-driven
design: they preserve expressive winning-line triples while improving parameter efficiency and
regularization, which reduces overfitting and improves validation/test accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
