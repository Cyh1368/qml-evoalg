edit_mix_ring_axes_and_merge_zzz
A mixed-entanglement ring with conserved parameter budget

This edit alternates two-qubit controlled-rotation axes around the outer ring
by replacing every second CRZ with a CRX (shared "crx_ring" parameter). To
keep the total number of unique parameters per block unchanged (preserving
N_PARAMS, training budget, and comparability), I merge the row and column
three-qubit ZZZ parameters into a single "zzz_lines" key that covers both
row and column winning lines. The radial center CRZs remain shared via
"crz_center". This introduces diversity in entangling axes (testing XX-like
vs ZZ-like couplings) while maintaining parameter efficiency and respecting
grid connectivity constraints. The rest of the block is unchanged.
