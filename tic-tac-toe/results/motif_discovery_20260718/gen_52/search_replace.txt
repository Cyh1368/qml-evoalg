<DESCRIPTION>
The previous candidate successfully integrated winning-line motifs (ZZZ and CCRZ) but saw a slight drop in accuracy compared to the seed. This edit introduces a more diverse interaction subspace by alternating the basis of the three-qubit motifs.

Specifically, we keep the ZZZ motifs (which capture parity-like correlations across lines) but replace the CCRZ motifs with a "line-conditioned" rotation motif using CRX/CRY logic (simulated here via symmetry-grouped RX/RY layers and selective ZZZ/CRZ interactions).

To improve expressivity without ballooning parameter count, we:
1.  Group the ZZZ motifs by row, column, and diagonal to allow the model to weight different win-orientations differently.
2.  Introduce a 'mid-block' RY layer to allow the circuit to rotate out of the Z-basis before the final entanglement, improving the non-linearity of the decision boundary.
3.  Maintain parameter efficiency by sharing keys across symmetric board positions.
</DESCRIPTION>
