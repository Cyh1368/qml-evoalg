<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce parameter count via symmetry-inspired sharing (corners/edges/center) to boost parameter efficiency and decrease overfitting, while adding motif-aware three-qubit ZZZ interactions on the eight winning lines and targeted parametric CRZ couplings between center and edge qubits to capture line correlations. The block keeps the original CZ lattice entangling backbone but replaces per-qubit pre/post RZ and RY parameters with shared group parameters (corner/edge/center). This yields far fewer unique parameters per block (improving efficiency) while the ZZZ motif and CRZ center-edge couplings increase expressive power targeted at tic-tac-toe winning patterns.
</DESCRIPTION>
