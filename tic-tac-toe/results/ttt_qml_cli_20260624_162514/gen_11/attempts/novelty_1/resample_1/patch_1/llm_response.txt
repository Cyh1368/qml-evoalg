<DESCRIPTION>
This edit enhances the ansatz by focusing on the 'voting dominance' of the cross/circle/draw observables. 

1. **Strategic Entanglement**: Instead of using CRZ (which is a conditional phase shift), I use **CNOT** gates. CNOTs are more powerful at generating maximal entanglement between the center (wire 8) and the ring, which is crucial since the 'draw' class depends solely on wire 8's Z-expectation value.
2. **Parametric Diversity**: I replace the shared `ry_out_periphery` with individual parameters for corners vs edges. In Tic-Tac-Toe, corners and edges are mathematically distinct (corners are part of 3 win-lines, edges only 2), so separating them allows the circuit to weight their contributions to the "cross" and "circle" sums more effectively.
3. **Axis Mixing**: I keep the RY-RZ-RX-RY structure but ensure that the CNOTs are sandwiched between non-commutation rotations to maximize the expressivity of the SU(2) block while maintaining symmetry-based parameter sharing.
</DESCRIPTION>
