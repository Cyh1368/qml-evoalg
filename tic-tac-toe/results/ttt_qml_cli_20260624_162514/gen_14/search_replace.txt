<DESCRIPTION>
I propose a compact, symmetry-aware ansatz that (1) reduces the number of independent parameters via deliberate sharing across corner/edge/center symmetry classes, (2) replaces purely Z-controlled entanglers with a mix of CRY on the ring and CRX on hub edges to better convert the RX-encoded features into Z-readout space, and (3) retains light single-qubit mixing before measurement. This yields fewer unique parameters (better parameter efficiency and regularization) while adding cross-qubit X/Y mixing that helps map the RX feature-map information into measurable Z expectations. All two-qubit gates remain on allowed adjacent grid edges and only allowed gates are used.
</DESCRIPTION>
