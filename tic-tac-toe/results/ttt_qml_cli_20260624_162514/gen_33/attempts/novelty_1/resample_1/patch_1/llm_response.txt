<DESCRIPTION>
Append a lightweight, shared RZ "sharpening" layer after the existing final RY output rotations.
This adds two small phase parameters per ansatz block: "rz_out_periphery" applied to qubits 0–7
and "rz_out_center" for qubit 8. Small end-of-circuit Z phases often help polarize measurement axes
and improve classification margins with only two extra parameters per block (modest parameter increase).
This follows the recommended periphery-vs-center symmetry and keeps connectivity/gate constraints.
</DESCRIPTION>
