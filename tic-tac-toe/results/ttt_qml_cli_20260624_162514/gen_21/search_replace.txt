<DESCRIPTION>
Refine symmetry and reduce unnecessary entangling to improve generalization and efficiency.

Changes:
- Keep the useful diagonal corner symmetry by splitting corners into two diagonal subclasses: (0,4) and (2,6). This matches the suggested motif to better capture diagonal win patterns.
- Merge edge rotations back into a single shared edge parameter (less fragmentation than prior split) to reduce parameters and regularize.
- Replace the expensive full parametrized ring CRX layer with a sparser fixed-CZ ring (4 symmetric ring edges) to lower depth and gate count, while keeping a small number of parametrized hub CRX gates to preserve tunable centre–edge coupling.
- Remove the redundant per-wire RX layer and instead add a compact shared RX mixing (periphery vs center) to keep expressivity with far fewer parameters.
- Use a small shared output layer (periphery vs center) for sharper decisions.

Expected effect:
- Fewer unique parameters per block (parameter efficiency improved).
- Lower circuit depth and gate count (faster training and less overfitting).
- Retains expressivity for diagonal patterns and center coupling via the hub CRX.
</DESCRIPTION>
