<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce tunable parametrized entangling (CRZ) layers targeted to Z-basis readout, and expand per-class symmetry-aware single-qubit rotations with light symmetry-breaking between corner/edge pairs. Add compact final RY+RZ output adjustments that include separate periphery vs center parameters so the ansatz can align rotations with the Z-observables used for classification. Parameter sharing keeps the block compact while giving the circuit more expressive entangling and Z-phase control (CRZ) which should improve mapping to the measured Z expectations and help both accuracy and generalization.

Key ideas:
- Split corners and edges into two symmetry-related pairs to allow controlled asymmetry without exploding parameters.
- Replace fixed CZ entanglers with parametrized CRZ on ring and hub edges (shared per-class) so the entanglement strength (phase) is trainable and directly affects Z-expectations.
- Keep class-based sharing for single-qubit phases to regularize and keep parameters efficient.
- Add final shared RY and RZ periphery/center adjustments to better shape outputs in the Z-basis.

This balances expressivity and parameter efficiency (moderate increase in N_PARAMS) and respects grid adjacency and allowed gate sets.
</DESCRIPTION>
