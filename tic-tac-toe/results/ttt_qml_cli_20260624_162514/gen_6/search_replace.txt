**Key observation:** Gen 2's symmetry-aware output mixing (shared `ry_out` and `rz_out` across all qubits) achieved the best combined score (0.48) with minimal parameters. The recommendation in the prompt suggests splitting the final output layer into periphery (corners+edges) and center subgroups to sharpen the draw-vs-win decision boundary.

**Proposed improvement:** Adopt Gen 2's compact symmetry structure but enhance it with the recommended split output mixing:
- Keep the symmetry-grouped initial RY rotations (corners/edges/center)
- Keep the symmetry-grouped initial RZ rotations (corners/edges/center)
- Use fixed CZ entanglement on selective edges (as in Gen 2) for efficiency
- **NEW:** Split the final output layer into two parameter pairs:
  - `ry_out_periphery` / `rz_out_periphery` for corners (0,2,4,6) and edges (1,3,5,7)
  - `ry_out_center` / `rz_out_center` for center (8)

This costs only 4 parameters (2 extra vs Gen 2's 2 shared params) but allows the center measurement to have independent fine-tuning for the draw classification, which is critical since draw is encoded on qubit 8. This should improve the draw-vs-win boundary while maintaining Gen 2's parameter efficiency and regularization.

Expected outcome: Better test accuracy and reduced generalization gap by giving the center qubit (which directly measures draw) its own output tuning, while keeping the periphery (which measures cross/circle) shared for regularization.
