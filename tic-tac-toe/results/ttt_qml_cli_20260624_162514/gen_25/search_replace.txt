The current program (score 0.44) has degraded from the baseline (score 0.51) despite adding parametrized entangling gates (CRY, CRX). The issue appears to be:

1. **Over-parameterization**: The current ANSATZ_SPEC has 72 parameters (vs. 54 in the baseline), which increases optimization difficulty without clear benefit. The parametrized controlled rotations add complexity but the model struggles to learn effectively.

2. **Loss of symmetry structure**: The baseline's simple RZ pre-phases (shared periphery vs. center) worked well. The current version splits RZ into corner/edge/center, adding parameters without improving expressivity.

3. **Entanglement pattern mismatch**: The CRY on all 8 ring edges plus CRX on 4 hub edges creates a dense, high-depth circuit (depth 76 vs. 40 baseline) that may suffer from barren plateaus and gradient issues.

**Proposed fix**: Implement the "diagonal symmetry split" recommendation from the problem statement. Replace the uniform corner/edge RZ sharing with two diagonal classes:
- **Main-diagonal corners** (qubits 0 & 4): share `rz_diag_main`
- **Anti-diagonal corners** (qubits 2 & 6): share `rz_diag_anti`
- **Edges** (qubits 1, 3, 5, 7): keep shared `rz_edge`
- **Center** (qubit 8): keep `rz_center`

This adds only 2 parameters per block (58 total vs. 54 baseline) while capturing diagonal win-line structure. Simultaneously, **revert the parametrized entangling gates to fixed CZ gates** (like the baseline) to reduce depth and avoid barren plateaus. The fixed CZ pattern is simpler and the added RZ parameters provide enough expressivity.

This balances parameter efficiency with structural awareness of the tic-tac-toe grid's diagonal symmetries.
