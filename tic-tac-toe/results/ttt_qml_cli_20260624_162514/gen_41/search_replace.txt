The previous candidate showed high depth (70) and a significant generalization gap, suggesting that the entangling layer might be too deep and the mixing too simple.

I am proposing three targeted improvements:
1.  **Symmetry-Aware Rotation Groups**: Refine the initial RY and RZ layers to distinguish between corners, edges, and the center, which are functionally different in tic-tac-toe.
2.  **Hybrid Entanglement Motif**: Replace the deep CRX ring with a more efficient hybrid: CZ gates on the outer ring (fixed, zero depth/params) followed by CRZ gates from edges to the center (parametrized). This reduces depth while maintaining strong correlations.
3.  **Output Sharpening**: Add a final RY layer with shared parameters to sharpen the decision boundaries before measurement, which has been shown to improve validation accuracy in similar QML tasks.
