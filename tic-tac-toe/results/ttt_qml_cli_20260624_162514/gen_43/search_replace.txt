<DESCRIPTION>
The previous candidate used a high-depth entangling layer (depth 76) with low validation accuracy (0.42). I am refining the entangling motif to use CRX gates selectively on both the ring and hub edges but reducing the depth by grouping independent pairs.

Specifically, I'm replacing the `CRY_ring` with `CRX` gates on the grid edges to better match the `RX` feature map encoding. I've also simplified the parameter sharing: instead of separating periphery and center for every single layer, I've returned to the three-class symmetry (corner, edge, center) for the initial and final layers, which helps the model distinguish the 8-fold symmetry of the Tic-Tac-Toe board while maintaining parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
