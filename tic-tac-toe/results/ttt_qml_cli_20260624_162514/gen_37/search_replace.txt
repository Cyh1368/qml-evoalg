<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce parameter count while keeping expressivity by grouping qubits into corner/edge/center symmetry buckets and using shared parametrized controlled rotations for entangling. This replacement:
- Collapses many distinct single-qubit parameters into three RY groups (corners, edges, center) and two RZ groups (periphery vs center).
- Uses shared CRX on ring edges and shared CRZ on the four spokes to the hub to introduce tunable two-qubit interactions with only two entangling parameters.
- Adds a compact RX mixing layer with periphery vs center sharing.
- Reuses the compact single-qubit parameters in the final "sharpen" RY/RZ layers to avoid increasing unique parameter keys.
This yields N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK = 9 and total N_PARAMS = 54 (matching the paper's sweet spot), reducing overparameterization, encouraging regularization, and keeping geometry-aware symmetry that should improve generalization and training efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
