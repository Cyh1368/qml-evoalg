<DESCRIPTION>
I propose a compact, symmetry-aware ansatz that reduces parameter count and circuit depth while keeping expressive entanglement around the central hub. Key ideas:
- Aggressively share parameters across the periphery (all eight outer cells) to encode rotational symmetry and improve generalization.
- Keep a distinct center parameter to allow the hub to behave differently.
- Replace multiple fixed hub-CZs with parameterized CRZ gates sharing a single "ent" parameter so entanglement strength is trainable but parameter-efficient.
- Keep a small RX mixing layer and a final RY+RZ sharpening layer, both using shared periphery/center parameters to reduce parameter dimensionality.
This reduces N_PARAMS (faster training, less overfitting), lowers depth and gate count, and adds a single learnable entangling strength to tune center-periphery coupling — a useful inductive bias for the tic-tac-toe geometry.
</DESCRIPTION>
