diagonal_crx_entangler_motif
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses a very sparse fixed CZ entangler that lacks sufficient mixing between non-adjacent sectors of the board. This proposal introduces a "diagonal" entangling motif using CRX gates on pairs (0,3), (2,5), (4,7), and (6,1). These edges are physically adjacent on the 3x3 grid (corners to adjacent edges) and provide a stronger mixing pathway between the quadrants of the board. 

By sharing a single parameter `ent_rx` across these four CRX gates, we increase the expressive power of the circuit with minimal parameter overhead. This helps the model capture the diagonal and cross-sector correlations essential for identifying winning lines in tic-tac-toe, while maintaining a depth that fits within the successful 40–52 range.
</DESCRIPTION>
