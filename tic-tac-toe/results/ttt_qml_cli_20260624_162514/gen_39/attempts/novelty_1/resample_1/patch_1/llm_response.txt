<DESCRIPTION>
Refine the symmetry-aware parameter sharing by splitting the 'periphery' group into 'corner' and 'edge' buckets for the RX mixing and RZ phase layers. This distinction is motivated by the geometric difference in connectivity and winning line participation between corners (3 lines) and edges (2 lines). By using 'rx_corner', 'rx_edge', 'rz_corner', and 'rz_edge' instead of a single 'periphery' tag, we increase the model's capacity to distinguish spatial features without significantly increasing the total parameter count (staying within the 54-60 parameter range).
</DESCRIPTION>
