<DESCRIPTION>Introduce directional CRX ring parameters to increase expressivity while keeping adjacency constraints. Split the perimeter CRX gates into horizontal and vertical parameter keys (crx_ring_h and crx_ring_v) corresponding to edge orientation. This increases the number of trainable parameters per block from 1 to 2 for the ring portion (and thus raises N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK), potentially improving the model's ability to capture anisotropic correlations on the tic-tac-toe grid. The eight CRX gates around the ring are mapped as: (0,1) H, (1,2) H, (2,3) V, (3,4) V, (4,5) H, (5,6) H, (6,7) V, (7,0) V. This change adheres to adjacency constraints and keeps the overall architecture compatible with the fixed training loop and data handling.</DESCRIPTION>
