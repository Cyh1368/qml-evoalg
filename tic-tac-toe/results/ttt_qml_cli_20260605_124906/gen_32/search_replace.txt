<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the outer-ring CZ entanglement with a ring of CRX gates that share a single parameter (ring_angle) across all ring edges. This reduces depth while increasing expressivity via a compact, symmetry-aligned ring, potentially improving validation/test performance and parameter efficiency. All gates remain on adjacent grid edges to satisfy connectivity. Hub entanglement and post-rotation blocks are kept unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
