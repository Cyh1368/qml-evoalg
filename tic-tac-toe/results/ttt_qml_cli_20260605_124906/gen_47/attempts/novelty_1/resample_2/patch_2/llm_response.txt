<DESCRIPTION>
Improve hub-edge entanglement by giving each edge its own CRZ parameter, increasing expressive power and potentially improving convergence and generalization. This aligns with symmetry-aware design while keeping the overall block structure intact. The CRZ gates from center to each edge will now share distinct parameters crz_hub_edge_1 ... crz_hub_edge_4.
</DESCRIPTION>
