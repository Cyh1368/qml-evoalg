<DESCRIPTION>
Add a symmetry-shared second RY layer (ry2_corner/ry2_edge/ry2_center) between the hub-ring entanglement and the final post-entanglement RZ, increasing expressivity while preserving parameter sharing. This keeps the overall budget modest (adds 3 parameters per block, from 11 to 14) and leverages the existing symmetry groups. The patch inserts the new layer immediately after Layer 4 (CRX hub entanglement) and before the final Layer 5 (rz_post_*), without altering the rest of the training loop or data handling.
