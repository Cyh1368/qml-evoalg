<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce a two-parameter CRX ring around the board perimeter by splitting the ring gates into alternating crx_ring_a and crx_ring_b parameters. This increases expressivity of the ring entanglement without a full parameter explosion, while preserving adjacency and symmetry. The ring now uses alternating parameters across the eight perimeter edges, allowing richer correlations between neighboring qubits. This change is designed to improve validation/test performance by enabling more nuanced entanglement patterns during training.
</DESCRIPTION>
