<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single-parameter CRX ring on the perimeter with an alternating two-parameter CRX ring (crx_ring_a and crx_ring_b) around the board. This increases expressivity of the ring entanglement without a full parameter explosion and preserves adjacency/symmetry. The pattern alternates between crx_ring_a and crx_ring_b across consecutive edge gates, enabling richer correlations while keeping the total parameter count aligned (n_params_per_block increases by 1, keeping overall N_PARAMS consistent with prior metrics).
