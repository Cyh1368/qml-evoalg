<DESCRIPTION>
Switch the outer ring entanglement from a single shared CRX parameter to per-edge CRX parameters (crx_ring_0 ... crx_ring_7). This increases expressivity by removing symmetry constraints on the ring, potentially improving validation/test performance and convergence. Hub-edge CRY and post-RZ layers remain unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
