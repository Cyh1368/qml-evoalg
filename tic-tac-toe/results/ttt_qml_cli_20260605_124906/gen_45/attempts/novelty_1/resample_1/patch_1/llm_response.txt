<DESCRIPTION>
Replace ring CZ entanglement with a ring CRX entanglement layer using a shared parameter crx_ring. This preserves adjacency (ring edges) and introduces a richer, parameter-efficient two-qubit gate family in the ring. By swapping eight CZ gates for eight CRX gates (all sharing the same crx_ring parameter), the ansatz gains expressivity without proliferating parameters across the block. This change should improve model capacity and potentially boost validation/test performance while keeping the overall architecture and training loop intact.
</DESCRIPTION>
