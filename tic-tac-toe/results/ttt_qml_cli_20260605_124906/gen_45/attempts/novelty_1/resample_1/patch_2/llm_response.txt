<DESCRIPTION>
Replace ring CZ entanglement on the perimeter with a ring CRX entanglement layer using a shared parameter crx_ring. This increases expressivity with a parameter-efficient two-qubit gate family while preserving adjacency (ring edges). The change maintains the overall block structure and keeps the remaining layers intact, aiming to improve validation/test performance and potentially close the train-test gap.
