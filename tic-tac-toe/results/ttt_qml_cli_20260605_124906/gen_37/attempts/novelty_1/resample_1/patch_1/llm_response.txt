double_ring_cry_entanglement

<DESCRIPTION>
Enhance expressivity by duplicating the outer-ring CRY entangling layer while keeping parameter sharing intact. The 8 CRY gates around the perimeter already form a ring; by adding a second identical CRY ring that reuses the same cry_ring parameter, we effectively increase the entangling depth without increasing the number of distinct parameters. This can amplify gradient signals and improve optimization dynamics, potentially boosting validation/test performance while preserving parameter efficiency. Note: this keeps the total number of free parameters per block the same (still 11), but increases circuit depth and gate count, which may trade off noise sensitivity for better expressivity.
