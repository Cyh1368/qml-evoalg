<DESCRIPTION>
The current candidate (7 distinct params) underperforms the previous simpler design (5 distinct params, score 0.69) that used a fully-shared ZZ ring and reused the same RZ parameters both before and after entanglement. I revert to that more parameter-efficient structure (RY_a/RY_b, RZ_a/RZ_b shared pre/post, single shared ZZ ring) and add one small refinement recommended: a single shared global RZ rotation applied to all 8 qubits at the very end of the block. This adds only one new parameter but can act as a global phase/bias correction that fine-tunes the decision boundary uniformly across qubits, potentially improving worst-group margin and validation/test loss without needing per-qubit granularity. Net distinct params: 6 (ry_a, ry_b, rz_a, rz_b, zz_ring, rz_global), still fewer than the current 7-param version, while restoring the stronger baseline structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
