<DESCRIPTION>
The 4-distinct-parameter variant (ry_a/ry_b/rz/zz_ring) scored lower (0.70) than the earlier 2-parameter design (ry_a + theta, score 0.74) despite better raw accuracy, because parameter economy is weighted heavily and worst-group margin was actually lower (0.35-0.36 vs 0.36). Since fewer distinct parameters always score higher per the guidance, we revert to the proven 2-parameter structure (single "ry_a" pre-rotation + single shared "theta" driving all RZ/ZZ/XX layers) and extend it with the recommended skip-3 ZZ coupling shell (pairs at hypercube distance 3: [0,3],[1,4],[2,5],[3,6],[4,7],[5,0],[6,1],[7,2]) reusing "theta". This densifies entanglement connectivity further, which previously correlated with improved worst-group margin (0.34->0.35 when skip-2 and XX ring were added), while keeping n_distinct_params at 2 for maximal parameter-economy score.
