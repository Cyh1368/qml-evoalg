<DESCRIPTION>
Merge all trainable parameters into a single shared name "theta" (following the highest-scoring prior seed which used only 2 distinct params and scored 0.73, confirming parameter economy dominates the score). This drops distinct parameter count from 4 to 1, which per the scoring rules should improve combined_score directly through parameter_efficiency and likely also tightens generalization since fewer free parameters restrict overfitting on the small training set. Additionally, append a second zero-parameter CNOT mixing ladder (forward 0→7 then backward 7→0) after the final RZ layer, as recommended, to further mix qubit correlations and break residual correlations without touching the parameter budget. This combines the two most successful zero-cost levers seen across prior generations: aggressive parameter sharing and deeper fixed-gate mixing.
