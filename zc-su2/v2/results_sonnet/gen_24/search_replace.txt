<DESCRIPTION>
Add long-range ZZ entanglers connecting opposite qubits (0-4, 1-5, 2-6, 3-7) to the existing entangling ring, reusing the same shared "zz_ring" parameter (no new trainable parameters). This creates a more expressive, non-local connectivity pattern (a hypercube-like topology) on top of the nearest-neighbor ring, which should help the circuit capture correlations that span across the two symmetric qubit groups (0-3 vs 4-7) that are already treated asymmetrically by ry_a/ry_b. This is expected to increase the worst-group margin by giving the hardest samples more entangling pathways to become linearly separable, while keeping parameter count identical (still 4 distinct params), preserving parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
