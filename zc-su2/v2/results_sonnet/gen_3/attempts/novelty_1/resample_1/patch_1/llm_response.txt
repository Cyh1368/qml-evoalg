<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses independent RZ parameters before and after the entangling CZ chain (rz_pre_i and rz_post_i for each of the 8 qubits), giving 16 distinct RZ parameters plus 8 RY parameters = 24 distinct trainable parameters total. Since parameter economy is heavily rewarded (fewer distinct parameter names always scores higher), we merge the pre- and post-entanglement RZ rotations on each qubit into a single shared parameter name (rz_i). This halves the RZ parameter count from 16 to 8, bringing total distinct parameters from 24 down to 16, while still keeping a per-qubit RZ rotation on both sides of the entangling layer (now correlated rather than independent), preserving most of the circuit's expressive structure and its ability to build entanglement-dependent phase structure that helps separate the hardest validation groups. This should improve parameter_efficiency and the combined score without sacrificing much accuracy, since the model was already at ceiling accuracy (train/val/test ~1.0) and clearly over-parametrized for the task.
</DESCRIPTION>
