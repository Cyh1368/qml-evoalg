<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the winning 5-parameter design (score 0.69) that shared RZ pre/post phases, this version further reduces the trainable-parameter count to 4 distinct names while adding extra *fixed* (non-parametrized) entangling structure to compensate for the reduced flexibility. The key insight from the recommendations and prior results is that "fewer distinct trainable parameters always scores higher" — so instead of adding more angles, we push more of the required expressivity into fixed CNOT connectivity (free in parameter count) while keeping a single shared RZ phase parameter used identically before and after the entangling ring, one shared ZZ ring parameter, and the two RY groups. Two fixed CNOT ladders (forward and backward) bracket the parametrized ZZ ring, deepening the entangling relationships between all 8 qubits without touching the parameter budget. This keeps a clean two-group RY encoding (preserving the class-discriminative asymmetry between wire-groups 0-3 and 4-7 that appeared to matter for worst-group margin), a single global phase parameter shared across pre/post RZ layers, and a single shared ZZ coupling strength, for a total of 4 distinct trainable parameters — fewer than any previous seed while retaining rich fixed entangling depth.
