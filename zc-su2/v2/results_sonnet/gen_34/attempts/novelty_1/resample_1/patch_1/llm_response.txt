cz_interleaved_ring_ansatz

<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the best-performing seed (4 distinct trainable parameters: ry_a, ry_b, rz_p, zz_ring; worst_group_margin 0.36, score 0.71), this version diversifies the fixed entangling ladders by interleaving CZ gates with the CNOT gates at zero parameter cost. Specifically, every other link in both the forward and backward CNOT ladders (wires [1,2], [3,4], [5,6]) is replaced with CZ, introducing an orthogonal (phase-only) entangling interaction alongside the bit-flip-based CNOT interaction. This mixed-basis entangling structure enriches the effective feature map generated by the fixed connectivity without touching the parameter count (still 4 distinct names / 6 total instances), which should improve worst-group separation and convergence speed while preserving the excellent train/val/test accuracy and near-zero generalization gap already achieved. The long-range ZZ couplings (0-4,1-5,2-6,3-7) sharing the ring parameter are retained since they were shown to help worst-group margin in the ablation from single-ring to cross-coupled ring (0.33 -> 0.36).
