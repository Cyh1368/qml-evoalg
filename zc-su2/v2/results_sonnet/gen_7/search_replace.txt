<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the previous reduction (sharing the RZ-pre and RZ-post parameters), we now also share the RY parameter with the RZ parameters on the same qubit. Each qubit gets a single trainable parameter name used for its RY, RZ-pre, and RZ-post rotations. This halves the distinct-parameter count from 16 to 8 while keeping the same circuit depth/structure (same gates, same entangling layer), preserving expressivity through the combination of RY and RZ acting with a shared angle. Since parameter economy is heavily rewarded and validation accuracy currently has slack (0.98-1.00), this aggressive sharing should improve the combined score if accuracy holds, and is a natural extension of the already-successful parameter-sharing strategy from the previous edit.
