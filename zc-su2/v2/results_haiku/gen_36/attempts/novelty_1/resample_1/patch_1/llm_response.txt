<DESCRIPTION>
The structural redesign implements a dual-repetition entanglement motif architecture that replaces the ad-hoc mixing of different gate types with a systematic, reusable block structure. Instead of interleaving XX, YY, and CRZ gates in sequence, the new design explicitly constructs two identical entanglement blocks (XX → CRX → YY → CRZ), each reusing the same three entanglement parameters ("ent_nn", "ent_off", "ent_lr"). This achieves:

1. **Improved expressivity with parameter efficiency**: Doubling the proven seven-parameter substructure enriches representational power while keeping the distinct parameter count at 7, maintaining excellent parameter efficiency.

2. **Structural clarity and modularity**: The repeating motif is immediately recognizable, making the architecture more maintainable and debuggable.

3. **Consistent convergence profile**: Both prior programs converged in ~30 steps with zero generalization gap; the dual motif preserves this while targeting harder samples (worst_group_margin improvement).

4. **Enhanced hardest-sample discrimination**: The second entanglement block processes already-entangled qubits through the full motif again, allowing finer discrimination of difficult boundary cases without adding trainable parameters.

The architecture removes the fixed CZ layer (which added depth without parameters) and replaces it with a second parametrized entanglement motif, letting optimization tune both blocks jointly for maximum separation on worst-case samples.
