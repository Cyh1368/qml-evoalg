<DESCRIPTION>
This design builds on the strong parameter economy and multiscale connectivity of the best prior butterfly circuit. It introduces a recurrent-depth approach: the complete RY–CZ butterfly–RX transformation is applied twice back-to-back while reusing the same three parameters. The second pass increases nonlinear correlation processing and allows the shared RX mixer from the first pass to convert phase structure into populations before re-entanglement. This adds representational depth without increasing the distinct parameter count, preserving strong regularization and parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
