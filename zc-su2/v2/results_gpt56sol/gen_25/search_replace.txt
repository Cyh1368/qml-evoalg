<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the initial RY mixers to one global parameter while retaining the two extra CZ links that improved worst-group margin and eliminated the generalization gap. Prior feedback shows that global tying preserved the same validation margin and accuracy as even/odd tying while improving parameter efficiency and combined score. This change reduces the current ansatz from three distinct parameters to two without removing expressive gates, combining the stronger correlation graph of the current circuit with the better regularization and optimization economy of the best prior mixer scheme.
</DESCRIPTION>
