tie_amplitude_entanglers
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the heterogeneous CRZ/CRY links with two CRY links sharing one angle. The tied noncommuting amplitude entanglers retain trainable corrections at both chain locations while reducing the ansatz from five to four distinct parameters. This should improve parameter efficiency, regularize training on the small dataset, and avoid spending a parameter on a phase interaction that may overlap with the surrounding CZ structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
