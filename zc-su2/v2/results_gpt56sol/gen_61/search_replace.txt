<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the final RX layer back to the same global parameter used by the RY mixers. The current staggered butterfly layout already improves validation margin and worst-group margin, while the second distinct parameter lowers parameter efficiency enough to reduce the combined score. Sharing one angle preserves the noncommuting phase-to-population conversion and current gate topology while restoring the stronger one-parameter regularization prior, reducing overfitting risk and simplifying optimization.
</DESCRIPTION>
