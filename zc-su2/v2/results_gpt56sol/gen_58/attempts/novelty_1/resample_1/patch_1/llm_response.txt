<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the parity-specific RY angles into one globally shared mixer, reducing the ansatz from three distinct parameters to two. Remove the redundant second recurrent pass to halve gate count and depth, matching the more parameter-efficient single-pass structure that previously scored better. Reorder the commuting CZ butterfly layers to distance-2 → distance-4 → distance-1, an untested permutation that preserves multiscale connectivity while changing how correlations interact with surrounding encoding, mixer, and repeated-block boundaries.
</DESCRIPTION>
