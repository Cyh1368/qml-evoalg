<DESCRIPTION>
This design compresses the independent per-wire rotations into parity-shared RY parameters and a single shared post-entanglement RX parameter, substantially reducing the number of distinct trainable parameters. The original nearest-neighbor chain is retained for reliable information propagation, while selected CZ gates are replaced by CRZ and CRY gates to make entanglement trainable. A fixed cross-register CZ matching adds multiscale connectivity without additional parameters, aiming to improve difficult-group separation, generalization, and convergence while preserving a compact hypothesis class.
</DESCRIPTION>
