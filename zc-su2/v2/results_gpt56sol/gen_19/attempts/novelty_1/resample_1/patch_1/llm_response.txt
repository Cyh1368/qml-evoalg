<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the highly parameterized nearest-neighbor circuit with a symmetry-tied multiscale butterfly network. Two shared RY parameters provide limited parity-sensitive adaptation, while fixed CZ layers propagate correlations at distances 1, 2, and 4. Additional (0,7) and (3,4) bridges capture global endpoint and central-boundary interactions. A single shared RX parameter converts phase correlations into measurable populations. This reduces the ansatz from 26 distinct parameters to 3, improving regularization, parameter efficiency, and convergence while retaining global expressive power.
