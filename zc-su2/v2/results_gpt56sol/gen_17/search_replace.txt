<DESCRIPTION>
Compress the 26-parameter ansatz to three shared parameters while replacing the local chain with a fixed multiscale butterfly. Parity-tied RY rotations retain limited spatial flexibility, and a shared RX layer exposes phase correlations without overfitting. Two additional non-local CZ links after the distance-4 layer target global correlations that may improve the worst-group margin without increasing the trainable parameter count.
</DESCRIPTION>
