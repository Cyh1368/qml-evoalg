<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the even- and odd-wire RY rotations into one global parameter. Prior feedback shows that parity-specific local parameters do not improve validation or worst-group margins, while the globally tied variant raises parameter efficiency and the combined score. This reduces the distinct parameter count from three to two, strengthens symmetry-based regularization for the small training set, and preserves the full butterfly entanglement structure and observed convergence speed.
</DESCRIPTION>
