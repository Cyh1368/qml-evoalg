<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the noncommuting RX mixer to the existing globally shared RY angle. This reduces the ansatz from two distinct trainable parameters to one while retaining both rotation axes, full qubit coverage, and the butterfly entanglement structure. Given the already saturated accuracy and the explicit reward for parameter economy at every count, this stronger symmetry constraint should improve the combined score and reduce small-data overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
