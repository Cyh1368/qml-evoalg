<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the four fixed distance-2 CZ gates with CRZ couplings tied to the existing global_phase parameter. This adds no distinct parameters while allowing the medium-range correlations to adapt during training. Keeping all four couplings shared preserves the butterfly symmetry and small-data regularization, while the tunable conditional phase may improve the hardest-group margin without worsening parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
