<DESCRIPTION>
This ansatz replaces the diagonal CZ architecture with a directed CNOT parity transform. Long-range correlations are formed first, followed by progressively finer parity mixing, allowing each external repetition to propagate global information efficiently. The same angle is shared by every RY and RX gate, leaving only one trainable degree of freedom. This strongly regularizes the small-data model, improves parameter efficiency, and retains noncommuting input/output mixing for expressive repeated dynamics.
</DESCRIPTION>
