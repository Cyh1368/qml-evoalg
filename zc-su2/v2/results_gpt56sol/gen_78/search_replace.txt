<DESCRIPTION>
Share the RX angle with the existing RY mixer instead of training a separate global tilt. The prior results show that a single distinct parameter improves parameter efficiency and generalization while retaining strong margins. Reusing one angle across noncommuting axes preserves the circuit’s expressive phase-to-population conversion but adds a strong regularization prior, reducing the small-data train-validation gap and optimization dimension.
</DESCRIPTION>
