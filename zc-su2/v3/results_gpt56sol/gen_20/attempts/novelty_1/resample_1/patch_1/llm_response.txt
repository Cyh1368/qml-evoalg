<DESCRIPTION>
Uses four structured RY parameter groups based on wire index modulo four, providing finer local angular control than even/odd sharing while retaining strong regularization. A parameter-free CZ ring distributes local correlations, and opposite-wire CZ links add global information flow without increasing trainable parameter count.
