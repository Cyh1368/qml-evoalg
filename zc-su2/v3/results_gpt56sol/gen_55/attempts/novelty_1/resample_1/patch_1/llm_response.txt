<DESCRIPTION>
This ansatz replaces phase-only ring entanglement and multiple independent angles with a single shared mixer and a depth-efficient CNOT butterfly. Alternating RY/RX axes preserve heterogeneous feature responses, while the three disjoint butterfly stages propagate information across all eight qubits in logarithmic depth. A tied post-mixer converts the aggregated correlations into measurable population differences. The single distinct parameter strongly regularizes the model, reduces overfitting risk, and improves parameter efficiency and convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
