<DESCRIPTION>
Use complementary rotation axes for the two parity-tied parameters: RY on even wires and RX on odd wires. The prior alternating-axis circuit outperformed the current all-RY design, suggesting that noncommuting parity transformations improve useful expressivity and held-out behavior. Keeping one shared parameter per parity preserves strong regularization while allowing the optimizer to tune the two sublattices independently, which may improve worst-group separation.
</DESCRIPTION>
