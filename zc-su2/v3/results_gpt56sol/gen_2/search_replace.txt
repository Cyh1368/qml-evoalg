<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the highly overparameterized per-wire rotations with parity-shared parameters, reducing the ansatz from 24 distinct parameters to 5 while retaining limited spatial flexibility. Close the CZ chain into a ring to remove endpoint bias, then replace the post-entanglement RZ layer—which commutes with CZ and provides weak basis mixing—with shared RY rotations that convert learned phase correlations into observable population differences. This symmetry-regularized circuit should reduce overfitting, improve convergence, and provide more robust worst-group separation.
</DESCRIPTION>
