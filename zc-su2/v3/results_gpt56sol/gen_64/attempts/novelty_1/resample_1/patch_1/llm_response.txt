<DESCRIPTION>
Use one globally shared RX mixer followed by a parameter-free ring-and-antipodal CZ network. The uniform rotation imposes stronger symmetry than the alternating-axis design, reducing small-sample overfitting while preserving one distinct trainable parameter. Removing the redundant post-entanglement layer also lowers depth and gate count, which should improve optimization and generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
