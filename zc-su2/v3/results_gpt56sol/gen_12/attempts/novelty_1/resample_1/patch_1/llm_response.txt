<DESCRIPTION>
This design combines the better-performing even/odd RY sharing pattern with a denser, symmetry-preserving entanglement graph. It restores the full CZ ring and adds four opposite-qubit CZ chords to expose long-range correlations without introducing trainable parameters. The two RZ layers used previously are consolidated into one globally shared layer because RZ gates commute with the diagonal CZ network, removing a redundant parameter while reducing depth and gate count. The resulting ansatz has only three distinct parameters, stronger connectivity, and an inductive bias intended to improve worst-group separation and few-sample generalization.
