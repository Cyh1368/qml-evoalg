<DESCRIPTION>
Use a strongly regularized, symmetry-aware ansatz with one shared trainable angle instead of separate even/odd parameters. Alternating RY/RX rotations retain axis diversity without increasing parameter count. Antipodal CZ links first establish global correlations, followed by staggered ring links and then nearest-neighbor brickwork; this tests a different information-flow ordering while reducing depth and gate count relative to the current program.
</DESCRIPTION>
