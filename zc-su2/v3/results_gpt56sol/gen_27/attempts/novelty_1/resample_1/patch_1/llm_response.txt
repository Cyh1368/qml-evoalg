<DESCRIPTION>
Prepend a parity-tied RZ layer before the established RY mixers. The two shared phase parameters add controlled phase flexibility while preserving the circuit’s even–odd inductive bias and strong parameter sharing. This can expose phase-sensitive encoded features to the later RY/CZ dynamics, potentially improving the hardest-group margin and validation separation without introducing per-qubit parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
