<DESCRIPTION>
Insert a complementary parity-axis mixer immediately after the antipodal CZ layer. The added RX/RY rotations reuse the existing shared parameter, so the distinct parameter count remains one. Placing this mixer between global and local entangling stages lets the later CZ layers transform newly mixed global correlations into readout-relevant local structure, aiming to improve worst-group margin and reduce overfitting without sacrificing parameter economy.
