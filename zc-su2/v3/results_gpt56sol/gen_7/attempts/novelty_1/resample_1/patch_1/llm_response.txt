<NAME>
shared_zz_regularizer
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the two globally shared RZ layers, which add parameters and depth while commuting with the diagonal entangling structure. Replace the fixed, maximally entangling CZ ring with a single-parameter ZZ ring. This preserves the useful even/odd RY inductive bias while allowing training to select the appropriate entanglement strength. The result uses only three distinct parameters, has substantially fewer gates, and should reduce overfitting while retaining noncommuting local-rotation and correlation dynamics needed for robust margins.
</DESCRIPTION>
