<DESCRIPTION>
Rotate the long-range CZ matching from direct antipodal pairs to offset cross-block pairs. This preserves gate count, depth, and the single shared trainable parameter while changing feature-interaction geometry. The rotated matching may better distribute correlations across parity classes, improving worst-group separation without increasing overfitting risk or parameter cost.
</DESCRIPTION>
