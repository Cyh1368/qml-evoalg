<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the uniform CRZ entangler (single shared "ent" parameter across all four qubit pairs) with a mixed-axis pattern: pairs [0,1] and [4,5] keep CRZ under a renamed shared parameter "ent_z", while pairs [2,3] and [6,7] become CRX under a new shared parameter "ent_x". This injects complementary Z- and X-basis couplings into the entangling layer, which should let the circuit capture correlations that a single rotation axis cannot express, potentially improving the worst-group margin and robustness on held-out data. Gate count and depth are unchanged since we only swap gate type on two of the four entangling pairs and split one shared parameter into two shared parameters (a modest, well-justified increase in trainable parameter count from 6 to 7 distinct parameters, traded for expected gains in separation robustness).
</DESCRIPTION>
