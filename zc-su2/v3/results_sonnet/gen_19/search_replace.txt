merge_split_params_reduce_count

<DESCRIPTION>
The best-performing prior variants used fewer distinct trainable parameters and showed smaller train/validation gap. The current candidate splits the RY and RZ rotation layers into two groups ("ry_a"/"ry_b" and "rz_a"/"rz_b"), doubling the parameter count relative to a fully-shared rotation layer, which likely hurts generalization (train-test gap 0.12 vs 0.01-0.03 for lower-parameter seeds). This edit merges each split pair into a single shared parameter, cutting n_distinct_params from 6 to 4 while keeping identical gate structure (same entangling backbone, same ZZ layer, same CNOT rings), preserving expressivity per qubit (since each qubit still receives an input-dependent rotation) while reducing overfitting risk and improving parameter efficiency, which is heavily rewarded by the scoring function.
</DESCRIPTION>
