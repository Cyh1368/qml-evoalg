<DESCRIPTION>
Add a lightweight, single-parameter global ZZ Ising pre-entangling layer on opposite-qubit pairs (0,4), (1,5), (2,6), (3,7) before the initial RY layer. This introduces long-range correlations early in the circuit at minimal cost (only +1 trainable parameter, +4 gates, +1 depth), which should help the subsequent RY/RZ/CRZ structure exploit non-local feature correlations and sharpen the worst-group margin, while keeping the parameter count still very low (4 distinct params) for strong parameter-efficiency and generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
