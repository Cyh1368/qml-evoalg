mixed_axis_ent_ansatz

<DESCRIPTION>
Redesigned the ansatz around the CNOT-ladder + entangler backbone of the earlier high-scoring seed (program 2), but restructured the parameter allocation to squeeze more expressive power into fewer distinct trainable names while diversifying entangling axes for richer correlations.

Key structural changes:
1. Merged the two RZ-mixing parameters (rz_a, rz_b) used previously into a single shared "rz_a" applied to all 8 wires — same gate count, one fewer distinct parameter, still lets every qubit acquire a data-dependent phase.
2. Split the 4-gate CRZ entangling block into two complementary entangling axes: CRZ on the "inner" pairs [0,1] and [4,5] (param "ent_z") and CRX on the "outer" pairs [2,3] and [6,7] (param "ent_x"). This gives the circuit access to non-commuting entangling generators without increasing total two-qubit gate count, at the cost of one extra distinct parameter — a favorable trade since mixed-axis entanglers empirically improve worst-group margin (as seen in the parity-broken seed) while staying within the parameter-economy target of 6 distinct names, matching the best-performing prior program.
3. Kept the double CNOT ladder (forward then reversed) unchanged, since deep bidirectional entangling connectivity was associated with the highest-scoring prior candidate.
4. Kept simple even/odd RY grouping (ry_a, ry_b) at the front and a single shared closing RY (ry_c) at the end, preserving the proven "encode-entangle-mix-entangle-read" skeleton while trimming parameter count.

Net effect: same total parameter economy (6 distinct names) as the best-scoring seed, but with genuinely mixed-axis two-qubit correlations expected to raise the worst-group margin without adding parameters or depth.
