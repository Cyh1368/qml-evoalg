param_sharing_reduce_overfit

<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses 24 distinct parameters (nearly one per gate), which is expensive per the parameter-economy criterion and also likely drives the large train/validation gap (0.88 vs 0.63) seen in the metrics — the circuit is overfitting the tiny training set. I restructure the ansatz to use aggressive parameter sharing: instead of a unique parameter per qubit for each RY/RZ layer, I group qubits into two interleaved sets (even/odd) and share one parameter per gate-type per group. This cuts the parameter count from 24 distinct names down to 6, while preserving the same gate structure/depth (still one RY layer, two RZ layers, and the CZ entangling chain), which should improve parameter efficiency and reduce the generalization gap since fewer degrees of freedom are available to fit noise in the small training set. The entangling CZ layer stays fixed (no parameters) providing the necessary correlations, while the shared rotation parameters still let each qubit express independent-looking dynamics through phase differences generated by the entangling structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
