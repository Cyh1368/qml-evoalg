<NAME>
shared_phase_entangling_rings

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the two fixed, non-parametrized CNOT entangling rings with parametrized CRZ gates that all share a single new parameter "h". This turns pure bit-flip (CNOT) entanglers into phase-type entanglers (akin to the CZ-ring recommendation, but using CRZ since CZ cannot carry a trainable parameter in this gate set). Sharing one parameter across all 12 gates keeps the parameter-count increase to just +1 distinct parameter while adding meaningful trainable entangling structure, which should improve robustness/generalization (phase interactions are typically less sensitive to bit-flip-like noise and can better shape the decision boundary) without harming the strong parameter-economy priority.
</DESCRIPTION>
