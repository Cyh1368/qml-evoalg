<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the second fixed offset CNOT ring (4 nearest-neighbor gates) with a long-hop CNOT pattern that connects qubits at distance 2 in two interleaved rings ([0→2,2→4,4→6,6→0] and [1→3,3→5,5→7,7→1]). This introduces medium-range entanglement that nearest-neighbor rings miss, potentially capturing correlations relevant to the hardest validation groups, while keeping the same parameter count (no new trainable parameters added) and staying within a reasonable depth/gate budget.
</DESCRIPTION>
