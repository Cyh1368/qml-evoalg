To inject stronger, structured entanglement at minimal parameter cost, I prepend a disjoint-pair XX-Ising layer (wires (0,1),(2,3),(4,5),(6,7)) sharing a single new parameter "xx_a" before the initial RY layer. This creates early two-qubit correlations across all qubits with just +1 distinct parameter (4 total), which should substantially increase the achievable margin/separation while keeping parameter economy strong (this mirrors the recommendation and is a minimal, well-motivated way to add non-Clifford entangling capacity without exploding parameter count).
