skip2_cnot_topology_with_shared_entangler

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the two-parameter CRZ chain (ent1/ent2) with a fixed, parameter-free skip-2 CNOT topology (long-range mixing: 0→2→4→6→0 and 1→3→5→7→1) followed by a single shared-parameter CRZ layer on disjoint pairs. This introduces richer non-nearest-neighbor entanglement (a proven driver of past score gains) while simultaneously reducing the distinct parameter count from 5 to 4, directly improving parameter economy and likely reducing the train-test generalization gap by relying on structural (non-trainable) entanglement rather than more trainable angles.
