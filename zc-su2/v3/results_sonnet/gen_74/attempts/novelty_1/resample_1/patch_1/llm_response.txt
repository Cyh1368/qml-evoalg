<DESCRIPTION>
Refines the previous ZZ-ring ansatz by merging the two diagonal even-qubit rotation parameters (ry_a_0, ry_a_1) into a single shared parameter ry_a, following the strong evolutionary signal that fewer distinct trainable parameters consistently score higher without sacrificing structural expressivity. This keeps the proven combination of parity-split initial rotations, a shared nearest-neighbor ZZ-Ising ring (continuously tunable entanglement, more hardware-natural and generalization-friendly than fixed CNOT ladders), a global RZ mixing layer, a long-range ZZ coupling connecting opposite halves of the register (which empirically boosted worst-group margin to 0.20, the best seen so far), and a final shared RY closing rotation. By reducing the parameter count from 7 to 6 distinct names while preserving every entangling/mixing operation, this variant aims to raise parameter_efficiency and combined score while retaining the strong worst-group margin, and shallow depth relative to the fixed-CNOT designs, which should also help narrow the train-test gap.
