# Analysis

Looking at the three programs:

1. **Seed program**: Score 0.46, 6 distinct params, poor generalization (gap 0.17), worst_group_margin 0.21
2. **Best program**: Score 0.58, 10 distinct params, excellent generalization (gap 0.04), worst_group_margin 0.28
3. **Current program**: Score 0.43, 5 distinct params, but underfitting badly (train 0.69, validation 0.64), worst_group_margin only 0.05

The current program has:
- Too few parameters (5 distinct) leading to underfitting
- Poor training accuracy (0.69) indicates the ansatz lacks expressivity
- Worst group margin is critically low (0.05)
- The entangling structure (CZ + long-range XX/YY) may be ineffective

The recommendation suggests inserting a **second alternating RY layer** before the final RZ block, reusing ry_a and ry_b. This:
1. Adds expressivity without new parameters (parameter economy preserved)
2. Doubles the proven heterogeneous RY alternation pattern
3. Can improve robust separation and worst-group margin
4. Matches the structure that worked well in the best program (alternating ry_0/ry_1)

**Strategy**: Insert the second RY alternation layer after the entanglers but before the final RZ (rz_post) block. This leverages parameter sharing to add expressivity without increasing distinct parameter count.
