1. **Simplified entangler layer**: Replace the problematic long-range CRZ diagonal layer (which appears to hurt generalization) with a cleaner nearest-neighbor + selective long-range XX/YY/ZZ pattern that worked well in prior iterations.

2. **Early parity RX layer**: Introduce a lightweight parity-based RX layer immediately after the pre-interaction RZ, adding orthogonal rotation axis mixing at minimal cost (~8 gates, 2 params). This enhances local expressivity without excessive depth.

3. **Reduced gate depth and count**: Remove redundant mid-circuit RZ layer and streamline the overall structure to match the more successful architecture from the second prior program (score 0.59), which had better worst_group_margin and convergence.

4. **Parameter focus**: Maintain 10 distinct parameters while optimizing gate selection for tighter worst-case separation on hard samples.

The prior program (score 0.59) achieved better worst_group_margin (0.31 vs 0.28) and faster convergence (90 vs 2160 steps) with a simpler structure. This version builds on that success while adding the RX parity layer recommendation for enhanced expressivity.
