# Analysis

Looking at the prior programs and current performance:

**Prior Program 2 (Best):** Score 0.58
- n_distinct_params: 10
- test_accuracy: 0.96
- worst_group_margin: 0.28
- convergence_step: 150
- No mid-circuit structure, clean parity-alternating ry_0/ry_1 pattern

**Current Program:** Score 0.50
- n_distinct_params: 11 (added crz_diag)
- test_accuracy: 0.84 (worse!)
- worst_group_margin: 0.30 (slightly better)
- convergence_step: 510 (much slower!)
- generalization_gap: 0.16 (much worse!)

The current program has **degraded** from the best prior result. The problem is:
1. Added 4 CRZ gates with shared param "crz_diag" → n_distinct increased to 11
2. This hurt generalization (gap went from 0.04 to 0.16)
3. Convergence is 3x slower (510 vs 150 steps)
4. Test accuracy dropped significantly (0.96 to 0.84)

**Key insight from the recommendation:** Insert a mid-circuit parity-swapped RY+shared RZ layer AFTER the XX/YY/ZZ block and BEFORE the final rz_post. This should:
- Add expressivity through a mirrored parity structure
- Add only ONE new distinct parameter (rz_mid)
- Keep distinct parameter count at or near 10
- Improve worst_group_margin by better handling hard samples

The recommendation suggests:
- RY gates with alternating ry_1 (even wires) and ry_0 (odd wires) [parity-swapped from initial]
- Single RZ(rz_mid) on all wires

This is more elegant than the CRZ approach and aligns with what worked in Prior Program 2.
