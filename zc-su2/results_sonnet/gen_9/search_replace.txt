<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the trend that fewer trainable parameters have not hurt accuracy (50→62→74 params all reach 1.0 accuracy with similar scores), this edit drastically cuts parameter count further while preserving both local and long-range entanglement structure. We keep the 4 shared-pair RY rotations (providing per-pair expressivity tied to the input encoding), replace the previous post-entangling RZ layer with a single shared long-range CRZ gate connecting the two halves of the register (0-4,1-5,2-6,3-7), and keep the CZ ring for nearest-neighbor entanglement. This reduces trainable parameters from 50 to just 5 (an order-of-magnitude cut) while retaining full connectivity (local ring + cross-half links), which should further boost parameter efficiency and generalization without sacrificing training accuracy, since the earlier experiments show the underlying task is separable with very few parameters.
