decouple_entangler_param
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce a second shared trainable parameter "theta_e" dedicated exclusively to the parametrized two-qubit entangling gates (the ring's XX interactions and the antipodal CRX interaction), while keeping the single shared "theta" for all single-qubit RX/RY/RZ rotations. This decouples the local-rotation angle from the entangling-interaction angle, giving the optimizer an extra degree of freedom to independently tune how much "mixing" happens on-site versus across qubits. Prior work on parametrized quantum circuits shows that entangling-gate angles often need a different optimal scale than local rotation angles; forcing them to share one parameter can create a suboptimal trade-off that hurts convergence speed and expressivity. Splitting them into two shared parameters keeps the parameter count essentially unchanged in relative terms (going from 1 to 2 unique parameter names) while directly targeting the class of gates most responsible for shaping entanglement/expressivity, which should improve accuracy stability and reduce required training steps without meaningfully harming parameter economy.
</DESCRIPTION>
