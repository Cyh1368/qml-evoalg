global_ry_rz_ring

<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the previous group-shared RY/RZ ansatz (4 shared parameters, score 0.66), this version pushes parameter economy to its logical extreme following the explicit recommendation: collapse all 8 pre-entanglement RY rotations into a single shared parameter `ry_global`, and all 8 post-entanglement RZ rotations into a single shared parameter `rz_global`. This cuts the unique-parameter count per block from 4 to 2 (from n_params=26 down to n_params=14 given the fixed 6x repetition + 2 readout params observed empirically), testing the lower bound of expressivity for the RY-CZ-RZ template.

To compensate for the loss of per-qubit rotation flexibility, the entangling layer is upgraded from an open nearest-neighbor CZ chain to a closed ring (adding the wrap-around CZ between qubit 7 and qubit 0). This costs zero extra parameters but increases the connectivity/expressivity of the entangling layer, helping preserve the ability to correlate all qubits despite the coarser rotation sharing. The insight driving this design: with only 8 qubits, a ring topology gives every qubit exactly two entangling neighbors (versus one for the chain endpoints), evening out the entangling power across all wires while keeping gate count and depth roughly the same as before.

This directly tests whether near-perfect accuracy and zero generalization gap can be sustained with an extreme reduction in trainable parameters, maximizing the parameter-economy term of the score while relying on the fixed repetition structure (independent copies per block) to still supply enough total capacity across repetitions.
