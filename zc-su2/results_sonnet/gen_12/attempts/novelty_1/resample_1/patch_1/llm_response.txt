<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the previous 4-group (2 qubits/group) RY/RZ-shared design that already halved parameters via axis reuse, this edit further compresses the parameter count by regrouping the 8 qubits into 3 groups (3,3,2 qubits) instead of 4 groups of 2. This drops the number of unique shared parameters per ansatz repetition from 4 to 3 (a 25% reduction), while keeping the same fixed nearest-neighbor CZ entangling chain and the RY/RZ same-parameter reuse trick (so pre- and post-entanglement rotations still share the same angle per group). Coarser grouping increases correlation between qubits' rotation angles, testing whether the model can still separate classes with fewer degrees of freedom—directly targeting the parameter-economy objective while hopefully preserving the 1.00 train/val/test accuracy and 0 generalization gap observed previously.
</DESCRIPTION>
