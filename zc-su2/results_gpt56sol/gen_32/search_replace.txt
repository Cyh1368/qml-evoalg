shared_crz_entanglers

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed CZ matching with CRZ gates that all share one entangling angle. This adds only one logical parameter per ansatz copy while allowing training to tune the strength and sign of the entangling phase. The shared angle preserves strong regularization and parameter economy while potentially lowering loss or accelerating convergence beyond the fixed maximal-phase CZ topology.
</DESCRIPTION>
