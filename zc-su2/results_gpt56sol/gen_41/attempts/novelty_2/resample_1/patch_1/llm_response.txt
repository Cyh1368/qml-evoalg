<DESCRIPTION>
This crossover retains the shared collective RY mode from the current circuit and the single parallel CZ matching from the compact inspiration. Removing the redundant RZ, RX, and repeated stages preserves the minimum one trainable mode per repeated block while reducing block depth and gate count. The resulting ansatz uses 12 gates per block instead of 40, with all qubits still receiving trainable control and participating in entanglement.
