<DESCRIPTION>
Split the collective RY layer into two independently trainable four-qubit modes. This modest increase in expressivity allows the two halves of the register to adapt separately while retaining strong parameter sharing and regularization. A parallel CZ matching couples corresponding modes across the partition, keeping circuit depth and gate count low compared with the current CZ ring and potentially improving convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
