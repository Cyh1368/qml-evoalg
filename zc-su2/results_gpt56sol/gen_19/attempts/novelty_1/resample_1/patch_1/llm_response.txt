<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the parameter-tied controlled rotations with a fixed parallel CZ matching. Prior feedback shows this topology preserves perfect validation accuracy and loss while converging in 60 rather than 90 steps. Fixed CZ gates also avoid coupling entangling strength to the collective rotation, yielding a simpler optimization landscape without adding trainable parameters, depth, or gate count.
