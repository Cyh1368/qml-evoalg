<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed CZ matching in the second echo stage with CRZ gates sharing one new parameter, `crz_shared`. This adds only one trainable degree of freedom per repeated block while allowing the second entangling layer’s phase strength to adapt independently. The shared angle preserves symmetry and parameter economy while potentially improving loss and convergence without sacrificing the existing perfect accuracy and generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
