<DESCRIPTION>
The current program uses a symmetric structure: all 8 qubits receive identical shared rotations (RY, then RZ), followed by a linear CZ chain, followed by another shared RZ layer. This achieves perfect accuracy but with high gate depth and parameter count.

The redesigned program introduces **alternating qubit groups** with **independent parameters** to break symmetry while maintaining parameter economy. Instead of one shared RY and one shared RZ across all qubits, the new design uses:
- Two alternating RY parameters (ry_even, ry_odd) applied to even/odd qubits respectively
- Two alternating RZ parameters (rz_even, rz_odd) applied to even/odd qubits respectively
- The same linear CZ chain for entanglement
- Final RZ sandwich with the alternating pattern

This reduces the circuit's inherent symmetry, allowing it to capture more expressive features with minimal parameter increase (14→18 params), while maintaining depth at 35 and potentially improving generalization by distributing learned features more adaptively across qubit pairs. The alternating structure also naturally aligns with nearest-neighbor entanglement, improving information flow during training.
</DESCRIPTION>
