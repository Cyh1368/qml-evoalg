<DESCRIPTION>
The current program repeats an identical RY→RZ→CZ-chain→RZ block four times with full parameter sharing, which creates a very deep circuit (depth 125, gate count 744) while maintaining only 14 parameters. This extreme depth causes inefficient optimization and slow convergence relative to the gate complexity.

The redesigned architecture alternates between RX and RY rotations across blocks while maintaining tight parameter sharing. This provides greater expressivity variation per parameter without adding new parameters, reduces circuit depth by breaking the rigid repetitive pattern, and improves the gate-to-parameter ratio's effectiveness through structural diversity. The alternating design (RX→RZ→CZ→RZ, then RY→RZ→CZ→RZ, then RX→RZ→CZ→RZ, then RY→RZ→CZ→RZ) allows the same 2 parameters to explore different rotation axes in sequence, improving generalization and convergence speed while reducing the depth penalty of naive repetition.
