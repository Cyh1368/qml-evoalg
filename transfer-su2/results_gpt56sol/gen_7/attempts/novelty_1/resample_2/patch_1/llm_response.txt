<DESCRIPTION>
Tie each rotation layer across all eight qubits while retaining the proven CZ chain. This preserves a six-layer noncommuting RY-RZ-entangling circuit but reduces the ansatz from 24 to 2 parameters per block, lowering the total from 146 to 14 trainable parameters. The repeated blocks still receive independent copies, so the circuit remains expressive enough to learn different global basis changes at each depth. Global sharing also regularizes the model toward permutation-consistent state features, should preserve fast convergence, and substantially improves parameter efficiency without adding gates or depth.
</DESCRIPTION>
