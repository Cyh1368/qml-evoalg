<DESCRIPTION>
This block reduces each repetition to only three independent circuit parameters: one shared RY angle and two parity-shared RZ angles. The alternating RZ parameters retain more flexibility than a fully global rotation while strongly reducing overfitting and optimization cost. A fixed CZ ring supplies nearest-neighbor entanglement, including the previously missing wraparound connection between qubits 7 and 0. Placing the CZ ring between noncommuting rotation layers makes all six repetitions useful while keeping the total ansatz parameter count at 18.
</DESCRIPTION>
