<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the circuit to a parameter-free identity transformation so the classifier directly exploits the dataset-defined averaged Z-Z correlator. A single CZ is valid and, because the block is repeated six times, applies as CZ^6 = I. This removes all circuit angles, leaving only the gain and bias for two total trainable parameters. Since the current model already achieves perfect accuracy and the readout is evidently highly informative, this should substantially improve parameter efficiency, reduce depth and gate count, and accelerate convergence while retaining direct calibration of the raw correlator signal.
</DESCRIPTION>
