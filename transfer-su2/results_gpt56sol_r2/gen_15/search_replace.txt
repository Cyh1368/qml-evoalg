<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed CZ chain and redundant collective RZ layer with a nearest-neighbor XX chain whose angle is shared with the collective RY rotations. This creates noncommuting local-and-correlated dynamics while using only one parameter per repeated block. The total parameter count drops from 14 to 8, matching the most parameter-efficient perfect-accuracy prior candidate, while the trainable XX interactions may retain more loss-reduction capacity than fixed CZ entanglers.
</DESCRIPTION>
