<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the collective RY and RZ layers to the same angle within each repeated block. This preserves the useful two-axis transformation and entangling structure of the current ansatz while reducing the circuit parameters from two to one per block, lowering the total from 14 to 8 parameters including readout. The constrained RZ component may retain some of the low-loss benefit of the two-axis model, while matching the stronger parameter efficiency and potentially faster convergence of the proven RY-only model.
</DESCRIPTION>
