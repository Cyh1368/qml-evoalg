<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the collective RZ layer and retain the shared RY–CZ mixing block. The prior results show that this reduced ansatz preserves perfect train, validation, and test accuracy while lowering validation/test loss sufficiently, converging faster, and reducing total parameters from 14 to 8. This improves parameter efficiency from 0.07 to about 0.12 and also reduces circuit depth and gate count. The CZ chain still converts collective RY rotations in successive repetitions into expressive correlated dynamics.
</DESCRIPTION>
