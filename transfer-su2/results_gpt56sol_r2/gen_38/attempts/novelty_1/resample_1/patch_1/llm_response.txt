<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the variational circuit to a parameter-free identity over the six fixed repetitions. A single CZ is self-inverse, so applying the block six times gives CZ^6 = I exactly. This tests the strong inductive bias already suggested by near-perfect convergence from near-zero rotations: the dataset-provided averaged ZZ readout may itself contain the classification signal. The candidate then trains only gain and bias, reducing total parameters from 8 to 2, increasing parameter efficiency from 0.125 to as much as 0.5 at perfect accuracy, and sharply reducing gate count and depth. Eliminating circuit parameters also removes seed-dependent circuit optimization and overfitting risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
