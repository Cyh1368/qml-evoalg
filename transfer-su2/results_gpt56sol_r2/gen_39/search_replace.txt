single_angle_collective_block
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Share one angle across both collective RY and RZ layers. The noncommuting rotation axes and intervening CZ chain preserve expressive repeated dynamics, while reducing the circuit parameters from 12 to 6 and total trainable parameters from 14 to 8. This substantially improves parameter efficiency and may accelerate convergence by optimizing only one coherent direction per repeated block.
</DESCRIPTION>
