<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the collective RY and RZ layers to the same per-block angle. Each of the six repetitions still receives an independent parameter, while the two noncommuting rotation axes and CZ-mediated dynamics preserve substantially more expressivity than removing an axis entirely. This reduces circuit parameters from 12 to 6 and total trainable parameters from 14 to 8, improving parameter efficiency from about 0.07 to as much as 0.125 if the already-perfect accuracy is retained.
</DESCRIPTION>
