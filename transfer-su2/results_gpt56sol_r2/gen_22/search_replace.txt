<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed CZ chain with CRZ entanglers driven by the existing per-block collective angle. This makes the entangling strength trainable without introducing any new parameter keys, so the candidate remains at one circuit parameter per repetition and eight total parameters including readout. The six independent block angles can now jointly tune collective local rotations and correlation propagation, potentially reducing L2 loss while preserving the current parameter efficiency and perfect accuracy.
