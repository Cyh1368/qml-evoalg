<DESCRIPTION>
Remove all circuit parameters and reduce the block to one fixed CZ gate. Because the block is repeated six times and CZ is self-inverse, the six applications cancel exactly, leaving the prepared state unchanged before measurement. The model therefore learns only the gain and bias of the already highly informative fixed correlator mean. This reduces the total parameter count from 8 to 2, raises parameter efficiency substantially, eliminates unnecessary circuit optimization, and should accelerate convergence while preserving the demonstrated perfect classification when the raw readout is linearly separable.
</DESCRIPTION>
