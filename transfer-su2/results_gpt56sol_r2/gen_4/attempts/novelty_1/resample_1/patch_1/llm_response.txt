<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the 24-parameter block with a single fixed CZ gate. Because the identical block is repeated six times and CZ is self-inverse, the six applications cancel exactly, leaving the prepared state unchanged before measurement. This directly exposes the dataset-provided mean Z-Z readout to the trainable gain and bias. It reduces the model from 146 parameters to only 2, removes unnecessary circuit optimization, preserves the most relevant observable, and should substantially improve parameter efficiency and convergence while limiting overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
