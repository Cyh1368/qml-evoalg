<DESCRIPTION>
Remove all circuit parameters and retain a single fixed CZ gate. Because the block is repeated six times, this self-inverse gate cancels exactly, leaving the prepared state unchanged before the fixed Z-Z readout. The existing perfect accuracy and near-identity initialization indicate that the dataset’s averaged correlators already contain the classification signal. The trainable gain and bias can calibrate this signal directly. This reduces the model from 8 to 2 total trainable parameters, cuts the repeated ansatz from 138 to 6 gates, and should improve parameter efficiency and convergence while avoiding unnecessary circuit-induced distortion.
</DESCRIPTION>
