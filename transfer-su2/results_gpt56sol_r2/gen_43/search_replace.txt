<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the six repeated ansatz blocks to a parameter-free identity transformation. A single CZ is valid and, because the block is repeated six times, its total action is CZ^6 = I. The classifier therefore uses the dataset-aligned mean Z-Z readout directly and trains only gain and bias. This reduces total trainable parameters from 8 to 2, raises the maximum possible parameter efficiency from 0.125 to 0.5, removes unnecessary optimization noise, and cuts the circuit from 138 gates to 6 while preserving the input-state correlators.
</DESCRIPTION>
