<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the collective RZ layer and retain one shared global RY angle per block followed by the CZ chain. The prior results show that this one-angle block already achieves perfect train, validation, and test accuracy with faster convergence. Since Z rotations are comparatively weak for a final Z-Z readout and double the circuit-parameter count without improving accuracy, this change reduces total trainable parameters from 14 to 8, raises parameter efficiency from about 0.07 to 0.12, and lowers gate count and depth while preserving the entanglement-assisted global mixing needed by the classifier.
</DESCRIPTION>
