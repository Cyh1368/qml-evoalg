<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the collective RZ layer and retain shared RY rotations interleaved with the fixed CZ chain. RY and CZ already form noncommuting repeated dynamics, while the RZ layer doubles the circuit parameters without improving the already-perfect accuracy or loss. This reduces trainable parameters from 14 to 8 (one circuit angle per repeat plus gain and bias), substantially improving parameter efficiency while also reducing gate count and depth.
</DESCRIPTION>
