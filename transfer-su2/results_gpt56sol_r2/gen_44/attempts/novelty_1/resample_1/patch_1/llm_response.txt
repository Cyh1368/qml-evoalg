tie_collective_axes

<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the collective RY and RZ layers to one angle per repeated block. The six repetitions still receive independent angles, while each block implements noncommuting collective rotations separated by entanglement, preserving a useful six-dimensional circuit family. This reduces total trainable parameters from 14 to 8, substantially improving parameter efficiency while retaining the symmetry bias and expressive dynamics that already achieve perfect accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
