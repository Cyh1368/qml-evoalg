<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the 24 independent parameters per block with two symmetry-shared angles: one collective RY angle and one collective RZ angle. The alternating collective rotations and CZ chain remain expressive across six independently parameterized repetitions, while respecting the permutation-like symmetry induced by averaging Z-Z readouts. The interleaved RY layers change the measured basis, the RZ layers provide phase sensitivity before subsequent repetitions, and the CZ chain supplies nonlocal mixing. This reduces the circuit parameters from 144 to 12 and total parameters from 146 to 14, strongly improving parameter efficiency and reducing overfitting risk while preserving the successful structure of the current ansatz.
</DESCRIPTION>
