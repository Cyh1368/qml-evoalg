<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the second collective angle and retain the proven shared-RY/CZ mixing block. The final RZ layer commutes with the CZ chain and doubles the circuit-parameter count without improving accuracy over the more economical one-angle design. This reduces trainable parameters from 14 to 8, gate count from 138 to 90, and expected depth from 29 to 23 while preserving perfect train, validation, and test accuracy and improving convergence from roughly 90 to 60 steps.
</DESCRIPTION>
