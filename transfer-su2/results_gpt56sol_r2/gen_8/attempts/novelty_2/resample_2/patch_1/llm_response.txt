<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the collective RZ layer and retain the trainable collective RY rotations interleaved with the fixed CZ chain. Because each of the six repetitions has an independent copy of the shared block parameter, the circuit still learns a six-angle nonlinear sequence while reducing the total parameter count from 14 to 8. RZ rotations immediately before the next collective RY layer add phase flexibility but double the circuit parameters; the perfect accuracy of the symmetric current model suggests that the lower-dimensional collective subspace captures the dataset structure. This ablation should improve parameter efficiency, reduce gate count and depth, and potentially accelerate convergence while preserving the entangling transformation needed to alter the Z-Z readout.
</DESCRIPTION>
