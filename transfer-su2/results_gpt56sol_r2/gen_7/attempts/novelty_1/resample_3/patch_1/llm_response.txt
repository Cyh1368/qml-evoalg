<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the 24 independent angles per block with two collective angles shared across all eight qubits. The shared RY layer changes the measurement basis and directly exposes non-Z correlations, while the shared RZ layer between repeated RY layers supplies a second noncommuting control axis. The CZ chain retains entangling dynamics, making the six repetitions a compact QAOA-like circuit. This reduces the circuit parameters from 144 to 12, and total trainable parameters from 146 to 14, while preserving a structure well suited to symmetric state-property classification and reducing overfitting risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
