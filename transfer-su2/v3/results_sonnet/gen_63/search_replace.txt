Add a final shared-parameter RZ mixer layer (reusing "theta_z") on all 8 qubits after the three entangling CRZ shells. Since the entangling layer is itself CRZ-based (also using theta_z), a trailing local RZ layer on the same axis lets the circuit re-align single-qubit phases picked up during entanglement before the next repetition/readout, without adding any new trainable parameter (still 2 distinct circuit params). This tests whether an axis-consistent closing rotation improves the worst-group margin and convergence speed relative to leaving the block ending on the antipodal CRZ shell.
