<DESCRIPTION>
Reverts to the higher-scoring multi-scale shared-entangler backbone (global RY, global RZ, three CRZ shells at distances 1, 2, 4 all originally tied to one "theta_ent") which scored 0.60 vs the current 0.51 even/odd variant. On top of that proven structure, apply the targeted recommendation: partially unshare the mid-range (distance-2 "skip") CRZ shell into its own parameter "theta_ent_mid", while keeping the nearest-neighbor (d=1) and antipodal (d=4) shells tied to "theta_ent". This grants the skip-distance connections, which sit at an intermediate entangling scale between local and global correlations, independent expressive capacity to potentially better resolve harder validation samples, at the cost of only one extra parameter (4 distinct circuit params + 2 readout params = 6 total, still far below the 5-circuit-param variant that just regressed). This should recover most of the previous margin/robustness gains while testing whether decoupling the mid-shell helps close the worst-group margin gap further.
</DESCRIPTION>
