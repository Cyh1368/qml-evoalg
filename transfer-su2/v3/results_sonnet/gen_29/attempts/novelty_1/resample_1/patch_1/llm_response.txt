<DESCRIPTION>
The current ANSATZ_SPEC (fully-connected ZZ entangler across all 28 pairs, still sharing one angle) scored dramatically worse (0.41) than the earlier CRZ-based 3-shell design (0.56–0.60). The extra distance-3 ZZ shell apparently pushes the shared-angle entangler into a regime that hurts trainability/generalization rather than helping it, likely because too many simultaneous IsingZZ couplings with one tied angle create a highly non-convex, poorly-conditioned loss landscape for a single scalar parameter to navigate.

This edit reverts the entangling structure to the previously best-performing configuration: CRZ (directed, not symmetric IsingZZ) shells at ring distances 1, 2, and 4 only (skipping distance 3), which was empirically the strongest scoring candidate (0.60) among the prior programs. On top of that proven backbone, it adds the recommended concluding global RY layer with a new shared parameter "theta_ry_out" after the entangling shells (applied once per block, before the block repeats). This final single-qubit rotation layer acts like a QAOA mixer stage, giving the circuit one more degree of freedom to re-orient all qubits' Bloch vectors right before the (repeated) block boundary / final readout, which can sharpen the separation margin on the hardest validation group without a large parameter cost (only +1 parameter versus the 0.60-scoring version, keeping total distinct params at 4).
