<DESCRIPTION>
The previous edit added a second full entangling ring (an "xx" IsingXX ring on top of the "zz" IsingZZ ring), which increased depth from 33->54 and param count from 5->5 (same count but different topology) yet significantly hurt performance (combined score dropped from 0.52 to 0.38). This suggests the extra homogeneous entangling ring adds optimization difficulty / redundant capacity without adding useful correlations, since the ring topology is already covered by "zz" and the readout pairs benefit more from a genuine long-range (antipodal) connection than from a second same-topology ring.

This edit reverts the entangling structure to the empirically best-performing design found so far: even/odd RY sublattice rotations (2 params), a single shared ZZ ring (1 param) matching the ring-like local structure of the readout pairs, a single shared long-range CRZ connecting antipodal qubits (1 param) to give every qubit a short path to the far side of the register, and a single shared final RZ phase layer (1 param). This keeps the total distinct parameter count at 5 (matching the current spec) while restoring the topology that scored 0.52, and removes the redundant XX ring that increased depth without benefit — directly addressing both "fewer distinct trainable parameters" and "reduce train-test gap / converge faster" via a shallower, more structured circuit.
