<DESCRIPTION>
The recommendation suggests reducing N_REPEATS from 3 to 2 and adding a targeted second cross-YY link to maintain expressivity while reducing circuit depth and the generalization gap.

Current metrics show:
- depth_mean: 15.00 (per block × 3 repeats = ~45 total depth)
- generalization_gap_mean: 0.10 (already good but can improve)
- worst_group_margin_mean: 0.31 (hardest samples need better separation)
- n_distinct_params: 12 (room to stay lean)

The strategy:
1. Reduce N_REPEATS to 2: This cuts total circuit depth from ~45 to ~30, reducing overfitting and improving trainability on few samples. The depth was identified as a sweet spot around 8-12 per block in prior analysis.
2. Add a second targeted YY gate between qubits (1, 3) under a new shared key "cross_b": This adds one more expressivity channel without fragmenting parameter count. YY gates have shown strong performance for capturing non-trivial correlations.

Expected impact:
- Reduce total depth by ~33%, improving generalization on held-out samples
- Maintain or improve worst-group margin by introducing targeted cross-coupling that complements the existing (5, 6) YY link
- Keep parameter count reasonable by sharing the new YY gate parameter with existing cross-entanglement
- Converge faster due to simpler landscape
- Improve parameter efficiency and reduce the train-test gap
</DESCRIPTION>
