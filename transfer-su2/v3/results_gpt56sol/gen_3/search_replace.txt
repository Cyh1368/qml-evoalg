<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the highly overparameterized seed with a three-parameter, symmetry-tied ansatz. Global RY and RZ layers learn a common measurement basis, while shared XX interactions on a ring and across opposite qubits provide local and long-range correlation mixing without adding parameters. Repeating this noncommuting block three times yields useful expressivity while preserving a strong permutation/translation-inspired inductive bias. This reduces circuit parameters from 24 to 3 (5 including gain and bias), which should reduce few-shot overfitting, improve parameter efficiency, and make optimization faster and more stable.
</DESCRIPTION>
