<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed-strength CZ circuit and three rotation parameters with a translationally symmetric, two-parameter QAOA-style block. One shared RY parameter learns a collective measurement basis, while one shared ZZ parameter continuously tunes correlation processing across the periodic nearest-neighbor graph. Repeating this same noncommuting mixer–interaction block three times provides useful nonlinear expressivity without qubit-specific parameters. This reduces the circuit parameter count from three to two, improves regularization and parameter efficiency, and allows training to suppress unnecessary entanglement rather than being forced through a maximal CZ layer.
</DESCRIPTION>
