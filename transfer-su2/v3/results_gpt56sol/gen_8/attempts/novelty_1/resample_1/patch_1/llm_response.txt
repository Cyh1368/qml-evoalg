<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the four-parameter pre/post rotation block with a two-parameter, translation-symmetric Floquet block. The collective RY and final RX layers deliberately share one angle, retaining noncommuting basis adaptation while reducing circuit parameters from four to two and total trainable parameters from six to four. The independent collective RZ angle preserves phase sensitivity, which was present in the strongest prior architecture. A periodic CZ ring provides fixed, uniform entanglement without trainable interaction strengths, limiting few-shot overfitting and ensuring that repeated tied blocks propagate information consistently across every qubit and readout pair.
</DESCRIPTION>
