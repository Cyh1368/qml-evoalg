<DESCRIPTION>
Preserve the current one-parameter economy while making the fixed entangler more expressive. The commuting CZ ring retains a regular, low-overfitting inductive bias, while replacing only the four diametric CZ gates with disjoint CNOTs introduces non-diagonal long-range information transport. This hybrid Clifford topology can spread difficult global correlations into the fixed two-qubit readout more effectively than an entirely diagonal CZ network, without adding parameters or gates. It avoids the score penalty observed when splitting the collective angle into two parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
