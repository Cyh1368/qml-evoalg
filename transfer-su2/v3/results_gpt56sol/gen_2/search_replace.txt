cyclic_shared_basis

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace independently parameterized rotations with a cyclically symmetric, strongly shared basis-transform circuit. The two collective rotation layers provide a four-parameter noncommuting local basis change, while the closed CZ ring mixes neighboring correlations without adding trainable parameters or privileging boundary qubits. This reduces circuit parameters from 24 to 4, strongly regularizing the few-shot model, improving parameter efficiency, and limiting the train-validation gap. Ordering the CZ gates in disjoint matchings also keeps the entangling depth low.
</DESCRIPTION>
