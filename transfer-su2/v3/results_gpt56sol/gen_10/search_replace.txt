<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the unentangled collective Euler circuit with a two-parameter, symmetry-preserving entangling block. A shared RY layer changes the measurement basis, while a fixed CZ Möbius-ladder graph combines nearest-neighbor and antipodal correlations without adding trainable parameters. The final shared RZ layer does not commute with the next repetition’s RY layer, so the three tied repetitions remain nontrivial. This retains the topology associated with the strongest prior candidate while removing its trainable interaction parameter, improving parameter economy and reducing few-shot overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
