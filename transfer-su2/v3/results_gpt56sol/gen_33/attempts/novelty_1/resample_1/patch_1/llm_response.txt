<DESCRIPTION>
Restore the parameter-free CZ Möbius ladder that previously generalized better, then append a nearest-neighbour IsingXX ring to add trainable local correlation mixing. All rotations and XX gates share one collective angle, reducing the current three circuit parameters to one while retaining a richer noncommuting trajectory across the three tied repetitions. This targets the current model’s weak validation separation without sacrificing the strong few-shot regularization and parameter efficiency associated with the prior best candidate.
</DESCRIPTION>
