<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the dense XX Möbius layer with a translation-symmetric collective RZ→RY→RZ transformation followed by a nearest-neighbour ZZ ring. The two RZ layers share one parameter, while all ZZ interactions share one parameter, so the circuit retains only three distinct circuit parameters and does not worsen parameter efficiency. The palindromic collective rotations provide a richer measurement-basis transformation than the current single-sided RY/RZ layers, while the ZZ ring supplies controllable entanglement aligned with two-qubit correlation readout. Removing the four long-range interactions also reduces unnecessary few-shot capacity and should improve worst-group robustness and the train-test gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
