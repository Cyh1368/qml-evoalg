<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the underperforming two-parameter hypercube circuit with a one-parameter Möbius ansatz that combines a collective RY basis change, a symmetric CZ ring, and shared IsingXX rotations across antipodal pairs. The trainable nonlocal interactions directly adapt four long-range correlations that fixed CZ gates can only phase, while the ring distributes those features across all wires over the three tied repetitions. Sharing the same angle across every rotation and interaction constrains the model to a strongly regularized one-dimensional trajectory, reducing the total parameter count from four to three including readout calibration. This should improve few-shot generalization and parameter efficiency while retaining noncommuting, globally connected dynamics for hard-group separation.
</DESCRIPTION>
