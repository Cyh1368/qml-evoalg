<DESCRIPTION>
Add a translation-symmetric ring of distance-three CZ couplings to the existing Möbius ladder. The current one-parameter circuit generalizes well but has a weaker worst-group margin, suggesting that its fixed correlation graph may not propagate some difficult nonlocal features sufficiently. These additional parameter-free links complement the nearest-neighbour and antipodal edges, reduce correlation bottlenecks, and preserve the strong dihedral symmetry and single trainable circuit parameter that support few-shot generalization and parameter efficiency.
