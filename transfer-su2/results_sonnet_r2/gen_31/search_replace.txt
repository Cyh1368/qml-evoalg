<DESCRIPTION>
The current best-performing candidate (20 params, 3 params/block: ry_shared, zz_ring, rz_shared) already achieves perfect train/val/test accuracy with minimal parameters. To push parameter economy further while potentially improving convergence speed, I add a single additional globally-shared RX rotation applied to all 8 qubits per block. This introduces a third rotation axis (X) to complement the existing Y (ry_shared) and Z (rz_shared) rotations, enriching the collective single-qubit rotation manifold to cover full SU(2) collective rotations (RX-RY-RZ ~ arbitrary single-qubit unitary applied collectively). This is a cheap way (only +1 param/block, +6 params total) to potentially allow richer state manipulation and faster/more robust convergence, since XYZ collective rotations plus a shared entangler can represent more general symmetric operations than YZ alone. Given the seed shows convergence_step_mean of 90 with 3 params/block, adding tilt_x should not hurt and may reduce that further while keeping total params low (26 vs 20), a reasonable tradeoff for better convergence robustness across seeds.
