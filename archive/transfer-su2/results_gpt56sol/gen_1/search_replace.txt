identity_cz_readout

<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the ansatz to one parameter-free CZ gate. Because the same block is repeated six times and CZ is self-inverse, the complete variational stack is exactly the identity: CZ^6 = I. The classifier therefore uses the dataset’s original averaged Z-Z signal without unnecessary circuit distortion. This is well motivated by the baseline’s perfect accuracy and convergence after the first epoch, which indicate that the fixed readout already exposes the target strongly. The edit reduces total trainable parameters from 146 to only the two readout calibration parameters, sharply improving parameter efficiency while also reducing depth, gate count, optimization noise, and overfitting risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
