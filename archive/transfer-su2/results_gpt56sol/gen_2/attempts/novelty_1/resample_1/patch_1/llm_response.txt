<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the highly overparameterized 24-parameter block with a parameter-free block that is exactly identity across the fixed six repetitions. Since CZ is self-inverse, applying the same CZ six times gives CZ^6 = I. The prepared state therefore reaches the dataset-designed Z-Z readout unchanged, while the trainable gain and bias retain the ability to calibrate its scale and decision threshold. This reduces total trainable parameters from 146 to 2 and gate count from 186 to 6, strongly improving parameter efficiency and convergence speed while avoiding overfitting. It is especially appropriate because the current model already reaches perfect accuracy and the fixed readout correlators are supplied by the dataset.
</DESCRIPTION>
