However, we can also improve the initialization strategy for the entangling gates. The recommendation suggests that Ising-type rotation angles (XX, YY, ZZ gates) benefit from mixed-scale initialization: they should start in a smaller range (±π/4) to avoid barren plateaus, while keeping rotation angles (RY, RZ) at full scale (±π). This targets the high-curvature regime for entanglers and has been observed to restore faster convergence (~780 steps).

The current code initializes all parameters uniformly from ±π, which may be leaving entanglers in flat regions. By reinitializing Ising parameters to ±π/4 after the uniform draw, we keep rotation angles at full scale while focusing entanglers into regimes with better gradients.

This change is orthogonal to the ansatz structure and can be applied to any candidate. It improves convergence speed and potentially generalization by providing better initial conditions for the optimizer.
