**Current strength (score 0.43):**
- High train accuracy (1.00) and reasonable validation accuracy (0.82)
- Good worst_group_margin (0.25), indicating robust separation on hard samples
- Efficient parameter count (9 distinct params)
- Low depth (12) and gate count (66)

**Key insight from prior attempts:**
- The seed (24 params, score 0.29) showed overfitting: train 0.88 but validation 0.63
- The aggressive parameter sharing (5 params, score 0.33) sacrificed capacity too much: validation dropped to 0.52
- The current design (9 params, score 0.43) found a sweet spot with modest sharing

**Proposed improvement strategy:**
The current circuit uses:
1. Alternating RY gates (2 params: ry_a, ry_b)
2. Local XX entanglement (4 params: ent_0/1/2/3)
3. Cross-layer YY entanglement (1 param: cross_a, reused twice)
4. Final RZ gates (2 params: rz_a, rz_b)

To push validation and worst_group_margin higher while controlling generalization gap, I propose:

1. **Add more diverse entanglement patterns**: Include ZZ gates on different qubit pairs to create stronger non-local correlations. The worst_group_margin of 0.25 suggests some hard samples still aren't well-separated; additional diagonal entanglement could help.

2. **Enhance RY parametrization slightly**: Use 3 distinct RY params (ry_a, ry_b, ry_c) instead of 2, applied in a pattern that breaks symmetry while maintaining parameter efficiency. This gives the circuit more expressiveness for distinguishing complex state properties.

3. **Add a single shared ZZ gate on complementary pairs**: ZZ gates commute with Ising XX/YY and provide orthogonal evolution channels. One shared parameter across strategic pairs (0-4, 2-6) bridges the center of the qubit line.

This approach:
- Increases distinct params from 9 to 11 (minimal increase)
- Maintains low depth and gate count
- Provides orthogonal entanglement channels (XX, YY, ZZ) for better expressiveness
- Keeps parameter sharing high (11 params across 22+ gates = ~2x reuse)
- Targets worst_group_margin improvement by enabling finer discrimination
