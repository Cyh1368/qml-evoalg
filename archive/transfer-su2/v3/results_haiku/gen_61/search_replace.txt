## Analysis of Current Performance

The current program scores **0.55** with:
- **worst_group_margin_mean: 0.31** (critical weakness)
- **test_accuracy: 0.89** (degraded from prior 0.96)
- **generalization_gap: 0.11** (increased from prior 0.04)
- **n_params: 10, n_distinct_params: 8** (parameter bloat from the ring-loop YY gates)

The ring-loop YY layer (4 additional gate applications sharing only 1 parameter) introduced too much circuit depth (24 vs 15) without improving worst-group separation.

## Proposed Solution

Following the recommendation and prior Gen 46/52 success patterns, insert **parity-shared mid-circuit rotations** between the entanglement and phase layers. This approach:

1. **Boosts expressivity per parameter**: Injects 4 new tied parameters (ry_mid_even, ry_mid_odd, rz_mid_even, rz_mid_odd) with high sharing—each parameter appears exactly 4 times
2. **Improves worst-group separation**: Mid-circuit rotations break symmetry at the heart of the ansatz, helping distinguish hard samples
3. **Maintains parameter economy**: The 4 new distinct parameters are highly shared
4. **Reduces depth impact**: A single RY+RZ layer is cheaper than 4 redundant YY gates
5. **Addresses generalization**: Tight parameter sharing historically improves held-out behavior

## Implementation

1. Remove the problematic ring-loop YY gates (which have high gate count with insufficient expressivity gain)
2. Add a mid-circuit parity rotation layer after entanglement, before the final RZ phase layer
3. Keep the baseline rotation, nearest-neighbor XX, diagonal YY, and long-range ZZ—all proven working structures

This restores the circuit to lean parameter efficiency while injecting strategic expressivity exactly where prior high-performing circuits succeeded.
