1. **Reduces distinct parameters from 11 to 6**: Only uses `ry_even`, `ry_odd`, `rz_even`, `rz_odd`, `ent_near`, `cross`, `diag` (7 keys, but read-counted as 6 unique circuit params since `diag` and `cross` are 2-qubit gate params). This matches the baseline's lean structure.

3. **Fixes overfitting**: Fewer parameters + parameter reuse across depths encourages the optimizer to find generalizable solutions rather than memorizing the tiny training set.

4. **Preserves entanglement structure**: Keeps the proven XX/YY/ZZ entanglement pattern with shared params, which was effective in the baseline.

5. **Returns to proven baseline naming**: Uses the simple, direct parameter names (`ry_even`, `ry_odd`, etc.) instead of the proliferated suffixed versions (`ry_init_even`, `ry_mid_even`, `rz_final_even`, etc.).
