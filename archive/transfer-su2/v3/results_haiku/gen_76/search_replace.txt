**Analysis:**
- Current entanglement uses: 4 nearest-neighbor XX + 2 cross-layer YY + 2 long-range ZZ = 8 two-qubit gates
- This maintains the lean 7-parameter structure (no new parameters added)
- The long-range ZZ coupling currently only includes [0,3] and [4,7], missing anti-diagonal connectivity

**Proposed improvement:**
Add three complementary anti-diagonal ZZ couplings using the existing "diag" parameter:
- [1,6]: connects qubit 1 (first odd layer) to qubit 6 (second even layer, opposite end)
- [2,5]: symmetric diagonal pairing in the middle layers
- [3,4]: bridges the central layer boundary

This enriches global correlations and long-range entanglement symmetries without increasing parameter count or circuit depth materially. The strategy mirrors Gen 50's anti-diagonal connectivity pattern while maintaining the proven 15/72 sweet spot for depth/gate-count.

**Expected benefit:**
- Stronger worst-group margin via richer global entanglement structure
- No parameter increase (reuses "diag" parameter)
- Improved separation of hard validation samples through complementary long-range couplings
- Maintain parameter efficiency and generalization gap
