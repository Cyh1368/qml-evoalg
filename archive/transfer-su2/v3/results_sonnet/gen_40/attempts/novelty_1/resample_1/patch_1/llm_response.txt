<DESCRIPTION>
The previous edit added a duplicate "mixer-style" RY layer that reuses theta_ry after the entangling shells. Despite keeping the parameter count at 2, this structural change hurt validation/worst-group robustness (score dropped from 0.61 to 0.57), likely because doubling the RY layer's effective rotation angle drove qubits further from the sweet spot needed to align with the fixed two-qubit readout observable, rather than adding useful new degrees of freedom.

This edit reverts that regression: it removes the duplicate closing RY layer, restoring the plain 2-parameter (theta_ry, theta_z) structure that scored 0.61. In addition, it upgrades the entangling shells from controlled-phase (CRZ) gates to symmetric Ising ZZ-type two-qubit rotations (IsingZZ, wired as "ZZ" in the spec) while still sharing the same theta_z parameter. CRZ applies an asymmetric conditional phase (it acts differently on control vs. target and leaves |0><0| on the control untouched), whereas IsingZZ applies a symmetric exp(-i*theta*Z⊗Z/2) rotation to both qubits equally. Since the fixed readout is a symmetric two-qubit Hermitian correlator, a symmetric entangling generator is a better structural match and should produce cleaner, more consistent two-body correlations across the three connectivity shells without adding any new trainable parameters, likely improving worst-group margin and validation/test generalization while keeping the same minimal parameter count of 2.
</DESCRIPTION>
