<DESCRIPTION>
The fully-connected ZZ entangler (distance-1,2,3,4, 28 edges) underperformed badly (score 0.41) compared to the earlier 3-shell CRZ entangler (distance-1,2,4, score 0.60): too much entanglement with a single shared angle apparently washes out the signal and hurts trainability/generalization. Revert the entangling layer to the proven 3-shell CRZ topology (nearest-neighbor ring, skip-2 ring, antipodal pairs) which achieved better validation/test accuracy and margin with the same tiny parameter budget. Additionally, per the recommendation, append a final global RY layer with a new shared parameter "theta_ry_out" after the entangling shells. This acts as a QAOA-style mixer stage that can rotate the Bloch vectors into better alignment with the fixed two-qubit readout observable after the phase/entangling operations, potentially sharpening class separation without adding much parameter risk (only +1 param, keeping total distinct params low at 4).
