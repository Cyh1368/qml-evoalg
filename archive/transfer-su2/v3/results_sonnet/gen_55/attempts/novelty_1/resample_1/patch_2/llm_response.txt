The entangling shells and the CRZ layer operate in the RZ (Z-axis) basis, so a closing rotation axis mismatched from that basis (RY) may be fighting the alignment the entangler already built up, rather than aiding the Hermitian readout alignment. Replace the closing RY("theta_ry") layer with a closing RZ("theta_z") layer instead: this keeps the rotation axis consistent with the CRZ-based entangling shells, giving the optimizer one more use of the already-shared "theta_z" parameter to fine-tune phase alignment before readout, at zero extra parameter cost (still 2 distinct circuit params).
