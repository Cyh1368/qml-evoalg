<DESCRIPTION>
Revert to the higher-scoring multi-scale shared-entangler design (global RY/RZ layers + shared CRZ entangling shells at distances 1, 2, 4 on the 8-qubit ring), which previously scored 0.60 vs the current 0.39 (which used 6 distinct params and underperformed). On top of that proven base, apply the recommended targeted relaxation: keep the nearest-neighbor (d=1) and antipodal (d=4) CRZ shells tied to the shared "theta_ent" parameter, but give the skip-distance (d=2) shell its own independent parameter "theta_ent_mid". This adds only one extra trainable parameter (4 distinct circuit params total instead of 3) while granting the mid-range connectivity shell independent expressivity, which may improve worst-group margin and validation/test generalization without significantly hurting parameter economy.

# Multi-scale shared-entangler ansatz with a partially unshared mid-range
# shell.
#
# Design rationale:
# - A single global RY angle ("theta_ry") and a single global RZ angle
#   ("theta_rz") are applied to every qubit: the minimal single-qubit
#   rotation family (2 params) that still spans a full rotation direction
#   once combined with entangling layers and block repetition.
# - Entangling connectivity is organised into three "shells" on the 8-qubit
#   ring: nearest-neighbor (distance 1), skip (distance 2), and antipodal
#   (distance 4). The distance-1 and distance-4 shells share one angle
#   ("theta_ent"), tying the shortest- and longest-range links together as
#   in the original design. The distance-2 (mid-range) shell is given its
#   own independent angle ("theta_ent_mid"): this is the connectivity that
#   sits "in between" the other two length scales, and granting it
#   independent weight lets the circuit decouple mid-range correlations from
#   the local/global ones at the cost of just one extra parameter.
# - Only 4 distinct circuit parameters total (+2 readout params = 6 total).
