<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the poorly separating ZZ interaction circuit with a translation-symmetric collective RZ→RY→RZ basis transformation. The fixed readout already measures two-qubit correlations, so a shared global SU(2)-like basis change can expose label-relevant correlators without introducing trainable entanglement that may overfit the few-shot set. Three shared circuit parameters provide substantially more directional freedom than the current two-parameter mixer while retaining strong regularization, shallow depth, and parameter economy. Repeating the same Euler block three times yields a nontrivial tied transformation with fast optimization and uniform treatment of every readout pair.
</DESCRIPTION>
