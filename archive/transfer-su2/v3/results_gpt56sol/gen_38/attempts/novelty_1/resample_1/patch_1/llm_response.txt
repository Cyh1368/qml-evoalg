<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the underperforming second collective single-qubit mixer and shifted CZ matching with tied nearest-neighbor ZZ and YY correlation layers. The prior results show that shared ZZ correlations generalize substantially better than the current RX/CZ tail. Adding the orthogonal YY layer increases access to noncommuting correlation features that can improve the hardest-group margin, while reusing `collective_angle` preserves the minimum one-dimensional circuit parameterization and its strong few-shot regularization.
</DESCRIPTION>
