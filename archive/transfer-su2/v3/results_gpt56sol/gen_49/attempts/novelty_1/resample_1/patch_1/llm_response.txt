<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the four trainable diametric ZZ rotations with directional CRZ rotations on the dataset-supplied readout pairs. The current diametric ZZ terms reuse interactions already represented by the fixed Möbius chords and appear to reduce validation and worst-group margins. Readout-aligned CRZ gates instead transform the exact pair features consumed by the classifier and introduce useful noncommuting, directional structure. All gates retain the single shared angle, so the candidate remains at one circuit parameter and three total trainable parameters while recovering the stronger regularized structure seen in prior results.
</DESCRIPTION>
