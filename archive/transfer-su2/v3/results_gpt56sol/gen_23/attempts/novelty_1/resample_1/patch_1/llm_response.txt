<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the underperforming hypercube sequence with the stronger Möbius-ladder baseline, then add a shared CRZ on every dataset-provided readout pair. The symmetric collective rotation remains highly regularized, while the single additional `readout_crz` parameter directly adapts correlations on the measured pairs. Sharing that angle across all pairs and all three repetitions limits overfitting and parameter cost while targeting worst-group margin.
</DESCRIPTION>
