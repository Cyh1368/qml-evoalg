<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the weaker collective RZ trajectory with the previously stronger shared ZZ ring, while retaining the readout-aligned CRZ layer. Crucially, the CRZ gates now reuse the same collective parameter instead of introducing a second circuit parameter. This combines correlation-sensitive processing with readout-pair adaptation while reducing the model from two distinct circuit parameters to one, improving few-shot regularization and parameter efficiency. The terminal CRZ placement also makes it the closest schema-compatible approximation to a shared readout-phase layer without modifying the fixed circuit or measurement code.
