<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the independently parameterized hardware-efficient circuit with a translation-invariant, three-parameter Floquet/quantum-convolution block. A shared global RY field, shared periodic ZZ interaction, and shared global RX field form a noncommuting transverse-Ising layer. Repeating this tied block three times spreads two-qubit information into the fixed correlator readout while retaining a strong symmetry prior. Closing the interaction ring removes boundary-specific behavior, and sharing each parameter across all eight qubits sharply reduces few-shot overfitting and the circuit parameter count from 24 to 3 (5 including gain and bias), improving parameter efficiency and likely reducing the train-test gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
