even_odd_euler
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace fully collective rotations with a semi-collective even/odd Euler pattern. The current ansatz forces all eight qubits into the same learned basis and performs poorly, indicating excessive symmetry and underfitting. Tying parameters within parity groups preserves strong few-shot regularization while allowing two distinct local bases, which can capture staggered or sublattice structure and improve the hardest-group margin. The RZ-RY-RZ sequence supplies complete single-qubit basis control for each group, while sharing keeps the circuit to six distinct quantum parameters despite 24 gates and three repeated blocks.
</DESCRIPTION>
