<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the underexpressive one-parameter kicked-Ising ring with the stronger collective RY/RZ Möbius-ladder architecture, then add a translation-symmetric nearest-neighbor ZZ layer controlled by one shared angle. The fixed CZ ladder supplies parameter-free local and long-range mixing, while the shared ZZ interaction adds tunable correlation processing without introducing per-edge parameters. This retains strong few-shot regularization at only three circuit parameters, while avoiding the current model’s severe underfitting and near-zero validation margins.
