collective_basis_ansatz

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the independently parameterized, entangling circuit with a permutation-symmetric collective SU(2) basis transformation. Each Euler angle is shared across all eight qubits, reducing the circuit parameters from 24 to 3 while retaining the ability to learn a general common measurement basis. Removing the CZ chain avoids converting pairwise readout information into topology-dependent many-body features that can overfit the few-shot training set. The resulting low-dimensional hypothesis class should converge faster, preserve correlations already present in the prepared states, and generalize more uniformly across hidden difficulty groups.
</DESCRIPTION>
