<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the underperforming chord-only trainable layer and tied terminal RZ rotations with the stronger prior Möbius topology: collective RY mixing, a fixed CZ ring plus diametric chords, and trainable ZZ correlations on the ring. The mixer and correlation layers receive separate global angles, allowing Adam to tune basis selection independently from correlation strength while retaining cyclic symmetry and only two circuit parameters. This keeps the current parameter count and gate count unchanged but uses them more effectively, removes the potentially restrictive RY/RZ parameter tying, and restores the topology associated with better validation and worst-group margins.
</DESCRIPTION>
