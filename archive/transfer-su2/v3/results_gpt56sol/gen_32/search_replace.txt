<DESCRIPTION>
Convert the four diametric Möbius-ladder links from fixed CZ gates into ZZ rotations controlled by one shared parameter. At the same time, tie the collective RZ layer to the existing global_y parameter. This keeps the circuit at two distinct ansatz parameters—the same count as the current candidate—while reallocating one degree of freedom from a redundant independent global rotation to direct control of long-range entanglement. The shared cross-chord angle preserves permutation-like regularization, can adapt the strength and sign of all nonlocal correlations coherently, and may improve the hardest-group margin without increasing parameter cost or encouraging wire-specific overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
