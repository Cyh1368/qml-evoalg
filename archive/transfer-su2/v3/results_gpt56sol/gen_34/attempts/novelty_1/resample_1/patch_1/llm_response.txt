<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the weaker two-parameter local-rotation ansatz with a single-parameter correlation ansatz based on the strongest prior candidate. The collective angle is shared by every RY and ZZ gate, reducing the total parameter count from four to three while preserving a trainable noncommuting mixer. Extending the proven nearest-neighbour ZZ ring onto the four diametric Möbius chords makes long-range correlations trainable without adding parameters. This symmetry-preserving inductive bias should improve hard-group separation while limiting few-shot overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
