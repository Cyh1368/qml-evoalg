<DESCRIPTION>
Decouple nearest-neighbor and opposite-wire correlation scales while preserving strong symmetry-based parameter sharing. The ring ZZ gates receive one shared local parameter, while four Möbius cross-chord ZZ gates retain the collective parameter already used by the global RY layer. This replaces the redundant same-edge YY interactions with explicit long-range correlations, keeps the model compact at only two circuit parameters, lowers gate count, and should improve worst-group separation by allowing short- and long-range structure to be optimized independently.
</DESCRIPTION>
