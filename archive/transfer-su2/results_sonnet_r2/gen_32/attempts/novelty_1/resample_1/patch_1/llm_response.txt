add_xx_twist_after_zz_shared

<DESCRIPTION>
Following the Gen 9 one-axis-twisting design (best param-efficient candidate at 20 params, 90 steps convergence), I add an orthogonal IsingXX "twist" term reusing the existing shared "twist" parameter right after each IsingZZ ring gate in that block. This creates a genuine two-axis (ZZ+XX) collective twisting Hamiltonian analogous to two-axis countertwisting spin squeezing, which deepens entanglement nonlinearity without adding any new trainable parameters (same "twist" param is reused for both ZZ and XX), and without increasing per-block parameter count. Gate count roughly doubles the entangling layer (8 extra IsingXX gates per block), but since parameter count and readout are unchanged, this should preserve or improve the parameter efficiency while potentially improving expressivity/convergence speed. I apply this to the current program's block structure (the two-parameter shared-ZZ ring ansatz with ry_even/ry_odd, zz_even/zz_odd, rz_shared), inserting an XX twist reusing zz_even and zz_odd right after their respective ZZ gates.
