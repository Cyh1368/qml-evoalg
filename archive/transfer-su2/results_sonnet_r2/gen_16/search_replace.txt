<DESCRIPTION>
The current best-performing candidate (20 params, test_acc=1.00) used a single shared RY, shared RZ pre-entangler, CZ chain, and shared RZ post-entangler (10 params/block). The current candidate here uses 12 params/block (26 total) with two ZZ entanglers plus RY/RZ, achieving same accuracy but worse parameter_efficiency. To push parameter economy further while retaining full expressivity and entanglement connectivity, I reduce to a single shared ZZ parameter across the entangling ring (merging zz_even/zz_odd into one shared param) and drop the separate RZ layer's redundancy by keeping just one shared RZ. This cuts per-block params from 12 to 8 (8 RY shared as ry_shared, 8 ZZ entanglers shared as zz_shared, 8 RZ shared as rz_shared using only 3 unique keys), significantly improving parameter economy (from 26 to 20 total params) while keeping the same connectivity pattern (ring entanglement covering all pairs), which should preserve the expressivity needed for perfect accuracy but score higher on the efficiency metric.
