<DESCRIPTION>
The current spec uses 4 RY bands + 4 RZ bands + 2 CRZ params = 10 distinct params/block (62 total params), achieving perfect accuracy with score 0.90. Since train/val/test accuracy is already saturated at 1.00, there is clear room for further parameter economy. I reduce the banding granularity from mod-4 to mod-2 (alternating even/odd qubits share the same RY and RZ angles), cutting single-qubit params per block from 8 to 4 (2 RY + 2 RZ), while keeping the same ring CZ topology and the 2 shared CRZ parameters. This drops distinct params per block from 10 to 6, reducing total circuit params from 60 to 36 (plus 2 readout = 38 total), a ~39% reduction. Given the task's strong reward for parameter economy and that the model was already overparameterized for this task (perfect accuracy with large margin), this should improve the combined score through higher parameter efficiency while very likely preserving the perfect accuracy, since entangling layers (CZ ring + CRZ) still provide qubit-specific phase structure necessary for the classification task, and mod-2 banding still breaks qubit symmetry enough given the alternating ring entanglers.
