<DESCRIPTION>
The current spec achieves perfect accuracy (1.00 train/val/test) but with 146 total parameters (16 per block × 6 blocks + 2 readout), giving a low parameter_efficiency score. Since accuracy is already saturated, the biggest lever to increase combined_score is aggressive parameter reduction while preserving expressivity via entanglement.

Key changes:
1. Remove the pre-CZ RZ layer entirely (redundant with RY when combined with CZ entangling structure) - reduces per-block params from 16 to 8.
2. Change the entangling connectivity from a line to a ring (add wraparound CZ 7-0) to improve information mixing per block, allowing fewer repeats worth of expressivity per pass, compensating for fewer parameters.
3. Keep single RY+RZ per qubit (post-entangler) instead of duplicate RZ layers, since RY+RZ already spans a generic single-qubit rotation up to global phase when preceded by entangling gates.

This cuts per-block parameters from 16 to 16 (RY×8 + RZ×8 post) same count actually - so let's instead share parameters across qubits in pairs to cut count while keeping ring connectivity. Specifically, use one RY parameter per qubit (8) and drop the separate pre/post RZ split into a single RZ layer (8), but share params between qubit i and i+4 (opposite qubits) to halve the RZ count to 4, keeping expressivity from RY (8 unique) but reducing RZ to 4 shared. This drops per-block params from 16 to 12, cutting total circuit params from 96 to 72, total params from 146 to 122 — improving parameter_efficiency while the ring connectivity compensates for reduced RZ diversity.
