split_twist_alternating_edges

<DESCRIPTION>
The current 3-param/block ansatz already achieves perfect accuracy with 20 total params, but convergence_step is relatively high (90) compared to the 5-param variant (30 steps). To improve convergence speed while keeping parameter economy near-optimal, split the single shared "zz_shared" twist parameter into two alternating parameters ("twist_a" for even ring edges, "twist_b" for odd ring edges). This gives the entangling layer slightly more expressive power (richer, non-uniform coupling pattern around the ring) while only adding 1 extra parameter per block (6 total across repeats), keeping total params at 26 — still far below the 32-param variant but with potentially faster/more robust convergence due to the added flexibility in entangling structure. This directly follows the recommendation to alternate two shared keys on adjacent ring edges.
