reduce_params_shared_ry_single_entangler_param

<DESCRIPTION>
Further reduce parameter count while retaining full expressivity/entanglement structure. Currently each block has 8 independent RY + 1 shared ZZ + 1 shared RZ = 10 params/block (60 total + 2 readout = 62). We can cut this further by sharing the RY rotation across qubits using a parity pattern (2 distinct RY angles instead of 8), since the ring ZZ+shared RZ already provides permutation-covering entanglement, and per-qubit individuality is largely recovered through repeated blocks (6 reps) acting on different state amplitudes via the ring shifts. This drops per-block params from 10 to 4 (2 RY parity + 1 ZZ + 1 RZ), giving 4*6+2=26 total trainable parameters — a large reduction — while keeping equivalent connectivity (ring CZ-like entangler via ZZ) and enough angle diversity (odd/even) to break symmetry. This should significantly boost parameter_efficiency while the strong entangling ring plus 6 repetitions should preserve the ability to reach high accuracy, as verified empirically by seed prior scores where shared params ansätze already reached full accuracy at 62 params; here we push further towards fewer parameters as the scoring rewards economy strongly.
