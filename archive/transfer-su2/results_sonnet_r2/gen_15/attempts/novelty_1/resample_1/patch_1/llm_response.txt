dual_zz_diagonal_couplers

<DESCRIPTION>
The current spec achieves 100% accuracy already, but has 4 trainable parameters per block (20 total) and no explicit two-qubit entangling rotation—entanglement comes solely from fixed CZ gates. Adding shared ZZ Ising couplers on two orthogonal diagonal pairs ([2,5] and [3,6]) injects data-dependent entanglement strength that can be tuned during training, which typically improves convergence speed and lets the CZ layer's fixed entangling structure be complemented by a trainable one. Since both new ZZ gates share a single parameter name "zz_shared", this only adds ONE new trainable parameter per block (going from 4 to 5 params/block, i.e., 24 total instead of 20), while doubling the entangling connectivity across the diagonal structure. This is a modest parameter increase for a potentially richer entanglement graph, trading a small parameter cost for improved expressivity and possibly faster/more robust convergence, directly following the recommended idea of splitting into two shared ZZ entanglers on wires [2,5] and [3,6].
