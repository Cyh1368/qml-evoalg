<DESCRIPTION>
The current best-scoring candidate (n_params=20, perfect accuracy) uses RY-RZ-CZ-RZ layers but has no explicit parametrized entangling gate, relying only on fixed CZ gates for entanglement. Adding a single shared IsingZZ rotation on one wire pair (e.g., [2,5], a long-range pair not covered by the CZ chain) introduces a genuinely trainable entangling degree of freedom while costing only 1 extra parameter per block (6 total across repeats). This mirrors the recommendation to leverage complementary ZZ coupling expressivity, and since the seed already achieves 1.0 accuracy, this mutation targets faster convergence (fewer steps to reach threshold) and potentially lower loss/variance without harming parameter economy much (20->26 params, still far below the 32-param collective variant that already ties in accuracy).
