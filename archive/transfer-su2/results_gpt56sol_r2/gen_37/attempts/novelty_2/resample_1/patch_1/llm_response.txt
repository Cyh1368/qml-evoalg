<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the tied RZ layer and retain one collective RY angle per repeat. The current shared angle simultaneously drives noncommuting RY and RZ rotations, coupling two distinct optimization directions and likely explaining its higher validation loss relative to the two-parameter design. A pure collective RY layer preserves the minimum achievable parameter count of eight total parameters, keeps direct mixing of the Z-basis correlator readout, and provides a cleaner gradient direction. It also removes 48 gates across six repeats, reducing simulation cost and circuit depth while retaining the CZ entangling line and the observed fast convergence behavior of single-axis designs.
</DESCRIPTION>
