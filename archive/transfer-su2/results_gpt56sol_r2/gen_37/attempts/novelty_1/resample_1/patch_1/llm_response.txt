<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the block to one collective RY angle followed by the CZ chain. This lowers the circuit parameters from 12 to 6 and total trainable parameters from 14 to 8, substantially improving parameter efficiency while preserving the symmetry-aligned transformation that already achieves perfect accuracy. Removing the collective RZ layer also cuts the repeated gate count from 138 to 90 and should improve optimization speed; RY directly changes the Z-basis correlator readout, whereas RZ contributes less directly and adds a redundant optimization direction.
</DESCRIPTION>
