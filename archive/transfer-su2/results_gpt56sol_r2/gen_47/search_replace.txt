collective_qaoa_ring

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the tied RY/RZ rotations and fixed CZ chain with a parameter-neutral, QAOA-like block: collective RY mixing followed by collective trainable ZZ interactions on a periodic ring. Reusing `collective_angle` preserves the current minimum of one circuit parameter per repeat and therefore keeps `n_params` at 8, while making the entangling strength adaptive. The ring removes endpoint asymmetry, directly models pairwise correlations, and reduces the block from 23 to 16 gates, which should improve optimization efficiency and may lower validation loss without sacrificing parameter economy.
</DESCRIPTION>
