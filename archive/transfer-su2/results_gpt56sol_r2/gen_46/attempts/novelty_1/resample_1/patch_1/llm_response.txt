<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the collective RY and RZ layers to one angle within each repeated block. This preserves both noncommuting rotation axes and the proven CZ entangling pattern while reducing the circuit parameters from 12 to 6 and total trainable parameters from 14 to 8. The stronger symmetry constraint should retain the already perfect classification accuracy, improve parameter efficiency from roughly 0.07 to 0.125, and reduce optimization variance without increasing depth or gate count.
</DESCRIPTION>
