<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the RZ rotations tied to the same angle as the RY layer. A shared angle across noncommuting RY and RZ gates constrains every repeat to an unnecessarily coupled rotation axis, which can preserve classification accuracy while limiting L2 calibration quality. A pure collective RY layer directly rotates the measured Z observables, while the CZ chain supplies fixed entangling structure. This retains the minimum achievable parameter count of one circuit parameter per repeat (8 total parameters including readout), reduces the gate count from 138 to 90, lowers circuit depth, and should provide a cleaner optimization landscape for improving validation loss without sacrificing the observed perfect accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
