<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the collective RY and RZ layers to the same angle within each repeated block. This preserves the full-qubit mixing, phase modulation, and CZ entangling structure while reducing the circuit parameters from 12 to 6 and total trainable parameters from 14 to 8. Since the current model already achieves perfect accuracy, this targeted compression should substantially improve parameter efficiency while retaining enough independent freedom across the six repetitions for fast convergence and low loss.
</DESCRIPTION>
