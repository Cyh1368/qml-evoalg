<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the collective RZ layer and retain the proven global RY–CZ mixer. The RZ angles add six circuit parameters across the repeated blocks and slow convergence, while prior results show that the one-angle block preserves perfect train, validation, and test accuracy with zero generalization gap. This reduces total parameters from 14 to 8, improves parameter efficiency from about 0.07 to 0.12, lowers gate count and depth, and previously reached the accuracy threshold in 60 rather than 90 steps.
</DESCRIPTION>
