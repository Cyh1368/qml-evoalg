<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the ineffective parameter-free CZ with a symmetry-preserving, parameter-efficient hardware-efficient block. One shared RY parameter changes the measurement basis on every qubit, while one shared RZ parameter supplies a complementary noncommuting axis. A nearest-neighbor CZ chain between repetitions converts the six independent shared rotation pairs into an expressive QAOA-like sequence rather than a collapsible product of rotations. Parameter sharing reduces the circuit from 146 total parameters to 14 (12 circuit parameters plus gain and bias), which should retain much of the successful seed circuit’s expressivity while improving convergence, regularization, and parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
