<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the collective RY and RZ layers to one angle within each repeated block. This preserves the noncommuting compound rotation and CZ-mediated expressivity that already achieves perfect accuracy, while reducing the circuit parameters from 12 to 6 and total trainable parameters from 14 to 8. The stronger inductive bias should retain generalization, improve parameter efficiency from roughly 0.07 to 0.125 at perfect test accuracy, and reduce optimization dimensionality for potentially faster convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
