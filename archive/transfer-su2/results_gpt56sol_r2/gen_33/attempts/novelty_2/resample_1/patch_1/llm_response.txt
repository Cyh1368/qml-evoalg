<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the trainable Floquet block with a single fixed CZ. Since CZ is diagonal in the computational basis, it commutes with every fixed Z-Z readout observable and therefore leaves their expectations unchanged. This exposes the dataset’s precomputed correlator signal directly to the trainable gain and bias. It reduces the model from 8 to only 2 total trainable parameters, cuts the repeated circuit from 138 to 6 gates, and removes circuit-parameter optimization noise while retaining a valid nonempty ansatz. The six repeated copies also make the fixed CZ product the identity.
</DESCRIPTION>
