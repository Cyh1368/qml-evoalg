<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the collective RY and RZ rotations to one angle within each repeated block. The two noncommuting rotation layers and CZ-mediated dynamics are retained, but circuit parameters fall from 12 to 6, reducing total trainable parameters from 14 to 8. This substantially improves parameter efficiency while preserving the architecture that already achieves perfect accuracy and low loss.
</DESCRIPTION>
