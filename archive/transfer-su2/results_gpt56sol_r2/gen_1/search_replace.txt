<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the identity-equivalent CZ-only block with a symmetry-preserving global RY layer followed by a CZ chain. All eight RY gates share one parameter within each block, so the six repetitions introduce only six circuit parameters and eight total parameters including readout calibration. RY rotations directly transform the measured Z-Z observables, while the interleaved CZ chain makes later rotations generate correlated, nonlinear features. This retains much of the successful seed’s structure while sharply reducing its 146-parameter burden and avoiding RZ layers that are redundant when adjacent to the Z-diagonal entanglers and readout.
</DESCRIPTION>
