<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the collective RY and RZ layers to the same angle within each block. This preserves both noncommuting rotation axes and the full CZ entangling line while reducing the circuit parameters from two to one per repeat. Across six repeats, the model still has six independent angles, but total trainable parameters fall from 14 to 8 including readout calibration. Given the existing perfect accuracy and low loss, this constrained collective trajectory is a strong parameter-economy improvement with less optimization variance and potentially faster convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
