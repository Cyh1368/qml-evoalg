two_global_angles

<DESCRIPTION>
Share one RY angle and one RZ angle across all qubits and both RZ layers while retaining the seed block’s CZ line. This reduces each repetition to only two ansatz parameters, improving parameter economy and limiting overfitting without removing the complementary rotation axes or entangling structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
