<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the 24 independent angles per block with two collective parameters shared across all qubits. A shared RY layer changes the measured Z-basis correlators, while a shared RZ layer preserves access to phase-sensitive information across subsequent RY layers. The CZ chain retains full-register entangling structure. This reduces the model from 146 to 14 total trainable parameters while preserving a symmetry-aligned, expressive six-block circuit, improving parameter efficiency and reducing overparameterization.
</DESCRIPTION>
