<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the tied RZ layer and retain a collective RY rotation followed by the CZ chain. The RZ rotations share the same angle as RY, coupling two physically distinct axes and likely causing the higher validation loss observed in the current candidate. A collective-RY kicked-Ising block preserves noncommuting dynamics across repeats because each RY layer does not commute with the surrounding CZ layers, while keeping the minimum one circuit parameter per repeat. This maintains the favorable 8-parameter count and fast single-axis convergence while reducing the circuit from 138 to 90 gates and eliminating the final RZ layer that commutes with the Z-Z readout.
</DESCRIPTION>
