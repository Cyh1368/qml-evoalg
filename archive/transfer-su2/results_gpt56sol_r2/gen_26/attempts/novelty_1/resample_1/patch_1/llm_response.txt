<DESCRIPTION>
Share the collective RY and RZ angle within each repeated block. This retains both noncommuting rotation axes and the full entangling chain while reducing the circuit parameters from 12 to 6, and total trainable parameters from 14 to 8. The constrained symmetric trajectory acts as regularization, improves parameter efficiency, and should preserve the perfect accuracy of the existing collective ansatz while reducing optimization dimensionality.
</DESCRIPTION>
