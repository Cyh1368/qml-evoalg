<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the trainable collective circuit with a single fixed CZ per block. Because the block is repeated six times, the identical CZ is applied an even number of times and cancels exactly, yielding an identity ansatz. This directly exposes the dataset-provided mean Z-Z feature to the trainable gain and bias. The prior results already show perfect classification near the identity initialization, suggesting that the fixed correlators carry the relevant signal. This reduces total trainable parameters from 8 to the theoretical minimum of 2, reduces gate count from 138 to 6, and avoids unnecessary circuit optimization while retaining calibration of the output scale and threshold.
</DESCRIPTION>
