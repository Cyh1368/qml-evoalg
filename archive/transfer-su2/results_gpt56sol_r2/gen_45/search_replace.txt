<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the tied RZ layer and retain a single collective RY angle followed by the CZ chain. The RZ rotations add no parameters because they share the RY key, but they constrain every repeat to a fixed two-axis trajectory that produced noticeably higher validation loss than the two-parameter baseline. A pure collective RY schedule preserves the current parameter economy of 8 total trainable parameters, retains the fast single-axis convergence behavior, and eliminates 48 gates across six repeats. This should improve optimization conditioning and runtime while reducing circuit depth without sacrificing the already perfect classification accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
