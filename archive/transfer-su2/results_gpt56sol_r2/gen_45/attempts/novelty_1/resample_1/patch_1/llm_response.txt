<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the tied RY/RZ compound rotation with a single collective RY layer. The current shared angle must simultaneously optimize two noncommuting rotation axes, which preserves parameter economy but unnecessarily constrains the effective transformation and likely contributes to the higher validation loss. A collective RY angle retains the minimum one circuit parameter per repeat (8 total parameters including readout), preserves the fast convergence associated with single-axis designs, and directly rotates Z-based correlators into an informative observable basis. Removing the redundant RZ layer also cuts 48 gates across six repeats while retaining the CZ-mediated feature mixing.
</DESCRIPTION>
