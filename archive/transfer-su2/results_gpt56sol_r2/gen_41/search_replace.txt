<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the tied collective RZ layer and retain one shared RY angle per repetition. The current RZ rotations are forced to use the same angle as the feature-changing RY rotations, imposing an unnecessary axis constraint; moreover, Z rotations and CZ gates commute with the Z-diagonal readout and can add optimization complexity without directly improving the measured correlators. This preserves the economy of one circuit parameter per block and eight total trainable parameters, while reducing the circuit from 138 to 90 gates and giving each learned angle a clearer role. The simpler collective-RY/CZ dynamics should improve convergence and L2 loss while maintaining the observed perfect classification accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
