<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the variational circuit to an exact identity and train only the gain and bias. A single CZ repeated six times cancels because CZ is self-inverse, so the prepared states reach the fixed correlator readout unchanged. This directly tests whether the dataset-provided Z-Z observable already contains the classification signal, as the perfect accuracy of highly shared ansätze suggests. The edit reduces total trainable parameters from 8 to 2, gate count from 138 to 6, and depth substantially, maximizing parameter efficiency while avoiding unnecessary circuit optimization and overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
