<DESCRIPTION>
Remove all circuit parameters and retain one valid fixed CZ gate. Because CZ commutes with every fixed Z-Z readout observable, it leaves the measured correlators unchanged; thus the model directly trains only the gain and bias on the dataset’s averaged correlator sufficient statistic. This reduces total trainable parameters from 14 to 2, raises parameter efficiency from 0.07 toward 0.50 if perfect accuracy is retained, and cuts the repeated circuit from 138 gates to 6 without sacrificing the information available to the classifier.
</DESCRIPTION>
