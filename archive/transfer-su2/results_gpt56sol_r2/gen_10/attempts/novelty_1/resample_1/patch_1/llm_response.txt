<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the collective RY and RZ layers to one angle per repetition. The resulting one-parameter Floquet-style block retains noncommuting collective rotations and CZ-mediated interactions while reducing the total parameter count from 14 to 8, including readout calibration. Independent parameters remain available across the six repetitions, preserving temporal expressivity and likely maintaining the already perfect accuracy with substantially better parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
