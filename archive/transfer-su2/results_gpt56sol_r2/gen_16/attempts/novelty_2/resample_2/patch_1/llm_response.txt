<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the collective RY and RZ layers to the same parameter within each block. This preserves the existing noncommuting rotation–entanglement dynamics and circuit depth while reducing the circuit parameters from 12 to 6, and total trainable parameters from 14 to 8. Since the current model already achieves perfect accuracy with a large margin, this constrained trajectory is likely sufficient while substantially improving parameter efficiency and regularization.
</DESCRIPTION>
