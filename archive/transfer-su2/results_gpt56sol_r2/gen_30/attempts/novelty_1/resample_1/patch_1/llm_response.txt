tie_collective_axes

<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the collective RY and RZ layers to one angle per repeated block. This preserves both noncommuting rotation axes and the proven CZ-chain inductive bias while reducing the circuit parameters from 12 to 6 and total trainable parameters from 14 to 8. Independent copies across the six repetitions still provide substantial expressivity, while the stronger sharing should improve parameter efficiency, convergence stability, and resistance to overfitting without increasing depth or gate count.
</DESCRIPTION>
