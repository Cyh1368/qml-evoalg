<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the redundant collective RZ axis and fixed CZ chain with a parameter-economical collective RY–XX mixer. The same angle controls all local RY rotations and nearest-neighbor IsingXX entanglers within each block, leaving only one circuit parameter per repetition (8 total parameters including readout). Unlike RZ rotations, which have limited direct leverage on Z-Z measurements, XX interactions actively transform the measured correlators. Their near-identity initialization also provides a smooth optimization path while retaining correlated many-qubit expressivity across six independently parameterized repetitions.
</DESCRIPTION>
