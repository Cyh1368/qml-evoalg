<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the trainable Floquet circuit with a parameter-free block containing one CZ gate. Because the block is repeated exactly six times and CZ is self-inverse, the total ansatz is CZ^6 = I, so the measured dataset correlators are preserved exactly. Only the gain and bias remain trainable, reducing the model from 8 to 2 parameters while also cutting gate count and depth substantially. Since the existing model already achieves perfect accuracy and begins near the identity, this tests whether the fixed correlator mean already contains the classification signal and should maximize parameter efficiency and convergence speed without introducing an additional generalization gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
