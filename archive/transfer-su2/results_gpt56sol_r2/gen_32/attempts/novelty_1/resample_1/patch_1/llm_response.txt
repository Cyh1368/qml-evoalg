<DESCRIPTION>
Remove all circuit parameters and retain one fixed CZ per block. Since CZ commutes with every Z-Z readout observable, it does not change the measured correlators; moreover, six identical repetitions cancel exactly. The classifier therefore learns only the gain and bias, reducing the total parameter count from 8 to the theoretical minimum of 2. Given the existing perfect accuracy and the dataset-provided correlator readout, this tests whether the variational rotations are unnecessary while sharply improving parameter efficiency, circuit depth, gate count, and optimization speed.
</DESCRIPTION>
