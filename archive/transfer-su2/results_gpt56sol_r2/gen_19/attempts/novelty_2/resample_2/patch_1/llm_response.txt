<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the collective RY and RZ layers to the same angle within each repeated block. This preserves the full collective two-axis rotation and CZ entangling structure while reducing circuit parameters from 12 to 6 and total trainable parameters from 14 to 8. The six repetitions still receive independent angles, retaining useful depth and expressivity. Given the existing perfect accuracy and symmetry of the averaged correlator readout, this targets a substantial parameter-efficiency improvement with minimal architectural disruption.
</DESCRIPTION>
