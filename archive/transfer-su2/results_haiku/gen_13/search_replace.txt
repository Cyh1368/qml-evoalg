Analysis of prior programs:

1. **First program** (hybrid_ry_zz_minimal):
   - Achieved perfect accuracy (1.00) with only **26 parameters** (4 per block)
   - Used selective RY on even qubits, RZ on odd qubits, and only 2 ZZ gates
   - Very efficient: depth=12, gate_count=60, convergence_step=60
   - **This is the gold standard for parameter economy**

2. **Second program** (expanded CZ line):
   - Achieved perfect accuracy (1.00) with 26 parameters
   - But significantly more complex: depth=35, gate_count=186, convergence_step=90
   - Slower convergence due to much higher gate count

3. **Current program** (shared RY/RZ with XX):
   - Perfect accuracy (1.00) but **38 parameters** (6 per block)
   - Moderate complexity: depth=18, gate_count=120
   - Parameter efficiency is lower (0.03 vs 0.04 in the first program)

**Key insight**: The first program's selective RY/RZ + minimal ZZ strategy was optimal. However, the recommendation suggests extending ZZ connectivity while keeping parameter count minimal by using just 2 shared parameters ("zz_even" and "zz_odd").

**Proposed improvement**: Replace the current 6-parameter block (ry_shared, xx_0-3, rz_shared) with a 4-parameter block that:
- Applies selective RY rotations (even qubits only, parameter "ry_even")
- Applies selective RZ rotations (odd qubits only, parameter "rz_odd")
- Replaces the 4 independent XX gates with all 7 nearest-neighbor ZZ interactions
- Uses only 2 unique ZZ parameters:
  - "zz_even": for pairs (0,1), (2,3), (4,5), (6,7)
  - "zz_odd": for pairs (1,2), (3,4), (5,6)

This returns to the 4-parameter-per-block efficiency of the first program while expanding the entanglement topology beyond just two pairs. The increased connectivity (7 ZZ gates vs 2 ZZ gates) may improve expressibility while keeping parameter count at the minimal economy level (26 total parameters).

Expected improvements:
- **Reduce n_params from 38 to 26** (restore parameter economy)
- **Maintain or improve accuracy** (1.00 expected)
- **Improve parameter_efficiency** from 0.03 to 0.04
- **Reduce gate_count** from 120 to a lower value
- **Faster convergence** due to simpler parameter space
