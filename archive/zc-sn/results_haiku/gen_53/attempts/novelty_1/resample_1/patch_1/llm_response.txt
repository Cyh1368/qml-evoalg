Key insights from context programs:
1. Linear chain CZ entanglement (0→1→2→...→7) is highly effective for this problem
2. Parametrized controlled gates (CRZ with alternating parameters) add significant expressivity
3. Alternating parameter sharing on single-qubit rotations (odd/even qubits) is efficient
4. The combination of forward CZ chain + reverse parametrized chain works best

Novel approach: Combine insights by:
1. Restore the proven linear chain CZ topology for forward entanglement

3. Use aggressive parameter sharing with odd/even alternation (4 parameters total for single-qubit gates)
4. Maintain minimal but effective depth while preserving the expressivity of parametrized two-qubit gates

This hybrid approach respects the validated linear chain structure while introducing controlled axis variation through CRX on central qubits, balancing exploration with exploitation of known good patterns.
