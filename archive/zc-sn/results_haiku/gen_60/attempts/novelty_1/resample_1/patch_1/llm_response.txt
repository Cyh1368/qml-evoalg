This redesign introduces targeted structural improvements to the current high-performing ansatz while maintaining its efficient 50-parameter architecture. The key innovation replaces the central reverse-chain CRZ gates (on qubits [4,3] and [3,2]) with CRX gates using a shared parameter. This strategic substitution:

1. **Introduces axis diversity**: The current design relies heavily on Z-axis entanglement. By injecting X-axis parametrized entanglement at the circuit's core (the middle qubits), we add orthogonal expressivity without parameter explosion.

2. **Maintains efficiency**: Uses only one additional unique parameter (crx_rev_c), keeping total parameters at 51—a negligible increase that should not degrade convergence speed.

3. **Targets expressivity bottleneck**: The middle qubits (2, 3, 4) are the most constrained in a linear chain. CRX gates here can capture rotational symmetries that CRZ cannot, potentially improving generalization by reducing the train-test gap.

4. **Preserves proven structure**: All other layers (initial RY/RZ, forward CZ chain, mid-circuit phases, forward/backward CRZ chain, post-entanglement) remain intact, retaining the architecture that achieved 0.91 score.

This minimal, surgical modification tests whether localized axis variation at the circuit core improves performance without architectural overhead.
