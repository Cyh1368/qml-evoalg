<DESCRIPTION>
This redesign merges the pre- and post-entanglement RZ phase schedules into a unified parameter set, reducing unique parameters from 50 to 48 while maintaining expressivity. The key structural change is replacing all `"rz_post_a"` and `"rz_post_b"` references with `"rz_pre_a"` and `"rz_pre_b"` respectively in the final RZ block.

The rationale is that since both pre and post phases operate on the same even/odd qubit pairs, parameter sharing can encode the combined phase rotation more efficiently without sacrificing model capacity. This tighter parameter budget should improve generalization, reduce overfitting risk, and maintain the high validation accuracy (0.93-0.94) achieved in prior runs.

Structural improvements:
- Unified phase modulation: Pre and post RZ phases now share a two-parameter basis (rz_pre_a, rz_pre_b)
- Reduced redundancy: Eliminates the unnecessary distinction between rz_post_* and rz_pre_*
- Cleaner parameter semantics: Even qubits (0,2,4,6) always use the same parameter family; odd qubits (1,3,5,7) use the other
- Expected outcome: Comparable or improved validation accuracy with 4% fewer parameters and potentially faster convergence
</DESCRIPTION>
