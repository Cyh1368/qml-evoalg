The recommendation to "hybridize the forward entanglement block by replacing every other CZ with a shared-parameter CRZ gate" directly aligns with Program 2's successful pattern, but takes it further: instead of pure CZ gates, we'll alternate CZ and CRZ in the forward pass. This provides:
- Minimal parameter overhead (1 additional parameter for crz_fwd vs pure CZ)
- Light tuning of forward entanglement to complement the reverse CRZ chain
- Better parameter efficiency than the current approach
- Maintains the proven lean architecture of Program 2

The strategy:
1. Remove the ineffective CRY bridge (which added parameters without boosting accuracy)
2. Remove the global RX/RY mixing layers (expensive and didn't help)
3. Hybridize the forward CZ chain: CZ, CRZ, CZ, CRZ, CZ, CRZ, CZ pattern
4. Keep the rest of Program 2's lean structure
5. Expected outcome: ~50-52 parameters, better accuracy, faster convergence, and improved parameter efficiency
