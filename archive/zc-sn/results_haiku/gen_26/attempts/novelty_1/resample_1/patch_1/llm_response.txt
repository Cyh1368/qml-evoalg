The current program achieves a strong combined score of 0.91 with excellent generalization (gap of 0.01) and fast convergence (180 steps). However, there is room for improvement in validation accuracy (0.92) and test accuracy (0.91).

The recommendation to add a mid-circuit RZ layer is well-motivated:

1. **Current structure analysis**: The circuit has pre-entanglement RZ (2 parameters, even/odd qubits) and post-entanglement RZ (2 parameters). This two-stage phase structure has proven effective.

2. **Three-stage rationale**: Inserting a mid-circuit RZ layer between the fixed CZ chain and the parametrized CRZ reverse chain creates three independent phase-rotation stages:
   - Pre-entanglement: Initializes qubit phases before any interaction
   - Mid-circuit: Adjusts phases after initial fixed entanglement (CZ) but before parametrized entanglement (CRZ)
   - Post-entanglement: Final phase refinement after all entanglement

3. **Parameter efficiency**: Adding only 2 new shared parameters ("rz_mid_a" on even qubits [0,2,4,6] and "rz_mid_b" on odd qubits [1,3,5,7]) maintains the elegant alternating pattern and keeps total parameters at 46 (minimal increase from 44).

4. **Expressivity boost**: This three-stage structure provides finer control over the quantum state evolution through the circuit, potentially allowing the optimizer to escape local minima and achieve higher accuracy without overfitting (the generalization gap is already excellent at 0.01).

5. **Circuit depth**: While depth increases slightly, the additional RZ gates are shallow single-qubit operations that don't significantly impact convergence or training stability.

The strategy follows the proven pattern of the current design while adding a carefully calibrated refinement that should improve both validation and test accuracy.
