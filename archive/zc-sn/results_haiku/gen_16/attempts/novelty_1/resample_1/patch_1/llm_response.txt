<DESCRIPTION>
This design draws inspiration from the successful parameter sharing patterns in prior programs (even/odd alternation, ab-pairs) but implements a fundamentally different entanglement strategy.

Key insights from prior programs:
1. The second program achieved best convergence (270 steps) with parameter efficiency, using star topology and diagonal connections
2. The current program has excellent generalization (0.01 gap) but lower training accuracy (0.85), suggesting underfitting
3. Parameter sharing (38 params) is efficient, but the linear chain CZ gates may not provide sufficient expressive power

Novel approach:
- Replace the repetitive linear+reverse CZ chains with a more sophisticated 3-layer structure:
  1. **Layer 1**: Shared RY/RZ rotations (feature encoding)
  2. **Layer 2**: Parametrized CRZ gates in a ring topology alternating with fixed CZ cross-links, creating richer entanglement patterns
  3. **Layer 3**: Another shared RZ layer
  4. **Global RX**: A final per-qubit RX rotation shared pairwise (inspired by the recommendation)

This combines:
- The efficiency of parameter sharing from current program (ab-pairs)
- The expressive power of parametrized controlled rotations (CRZ) from first program
- Ring entanglement with cross-links for better connectivity than linear chain
- A global RX finishing layer to enhance Hilbert-space exploration

The CRZ gates provide parameterized control-target interactions that can be tuned during optimization, offering more flexibility than fixed CZ gates while maintaining low parameter count.
</DESCRIPTION>
