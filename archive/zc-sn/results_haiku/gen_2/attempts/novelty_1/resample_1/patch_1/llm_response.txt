## Analysis

Current metrics show:
- Good training accuracy (0.94) but validation (0.89) and test (0.87) are lower → overfitting
- Generalization gap of 0.07 is moderate but could be better
- 146 parameters with depth 99 suggests the circuit may be overly complex
- Convergence at step 750 is reasonable

## Strategy

The circuit has:
1. **Single-qubit rotations (RY + RZ)** on all 8 qubits before entanglement
2. **Linear chain CZ gates** connecting adjacent qubits
3. **Post-rotation RZ gates** on all qubits

Issues identified:
1. **Parameter redundancy**: All 8 qubits have independent parameters throughout. This doesn't encourage good feature extraction and may lead to overfitting.
2. **Limited entanglement pattern**: Only nearest-neighbor CZ gates may not be sufficient for learning complex correlations.
3. **Excessive parameters**: 146 parameters for 8 qubits suggests over-parameterization given the modest accuracy gap.

## Proposed Improvements

I'll propose two complementary changes:

1. **Add strategic parameter sharing** in the rotation layers to reduce parameters and encourage better generalization
2. **Enhance entanglement** with additional two-qubit gates to improve expressiveness while leveraging shared parameters more efficiently
