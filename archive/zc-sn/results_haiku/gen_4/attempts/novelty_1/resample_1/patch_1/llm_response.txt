## Rationale for the changes:

1. **Parameter sharing on RZ gates**: Changed from `rz_pre_i` and `rz_post_i` (32 params) to shared `rz_i` (8 params) - saves 24 parameters while maintaining rotation capability per qubit.

2. **Parametrized entanglement with CRZ**: Replaced fixed CZ gates with CRZ (parametrized controlled-Z rotations). This allows:
   - The model to learn the strength of entanglement
   - Potentially better gradient flow during training
   - More efficient learning of entanglement patterns

3. **Alternating entanglement pattern**: The CRZ gates use two layers:
   - First layer: pairs (0-1), (2-3), (4-5), (6-7) - even/odd coupling
   - Second layer: pairs (1-2), (3-4), (5-6) - odd even coupling
   - This creates a ladder-like entanglement pattern that is commonly effective in VQE/QML

This reduces parameters from 146 to approximately 88 (8 RY + 8 RZ + 7 CRZ = 23 unique parameters × repetitions), significantly improves parameter efficiency, potentially reduces circuit depth through learned entanglement, and should accelerate convergence.
