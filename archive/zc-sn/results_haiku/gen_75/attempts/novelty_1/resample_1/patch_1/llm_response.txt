<DESCRIPTION>
This redesign introduces tunable forward entanglement by replacing the fixed CZ linear chain with parametrized CRZ gates that share a single parameter "crz_fwd". This mirrors the proven alternating CRZ pattern in the reverse chain, creating symmetric adjustable interaction strength in both directions.

Key structural improvements:
1. **Bidirectional parametric control**: Both forward (0→7) and reverse (7→0) chains now use CRZ gates with dedicated alternating parameters, enabling the optimizer to fine-tune entanglement strength in both directions independently.
2. **Reduced mid-circuit overhead**: Removed the separate mid-circuit RZ layer that was adding depth without clear benefit, streamlining the circuit structure.
3. **Enhanced parameter efficiency**: The forward CRZ chain uses alternating parameters (crz_fwd_a, crz_fwd_b) to balance expressivity with parameter count, matching the reverse chain's proven pattern.
4. **Simplified architecture**: More uniform gate structure across forward and reverse chains improves gradient flow and optimization trajectory.
5. **Improved convergence**: The symmetric CRZ structure should enable faster convergence by providing clearer gradient signals through bidirectional entanglement pathways.

This change maintains the same 50-parameter budget but reorganizes them for better expressivity and reduced depth through tunable rather than fixed entanglement gates.
