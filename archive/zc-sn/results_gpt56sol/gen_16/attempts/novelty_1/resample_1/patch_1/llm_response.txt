<DESCRIPTION>
Adds a lightweight, globally shared RY mixing layer directly after the CZ ring and before the post-entanglement RZ layer. Because RY does not commute with the surrounding phase operations, this converts entangled phase information into trainable amplitude changes and increases useful expressivity. Sharing one mixer parameter across all eight qubits limits parameter growth, supports generalization, and preserves the efficient symmetric structure of the current circuit.
</DESCRIPTION>
