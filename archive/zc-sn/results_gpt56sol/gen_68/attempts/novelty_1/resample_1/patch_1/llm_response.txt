<DESCRIPTION>
Share the initial RY angles by wire parity, replacing eight independent parameters with two. The current ansatz already uses parity sharing effectively in its mixer and phase layers, so this aligns the input layer with the circuit’s translational/parity structure. It should substantially reduce the total parameter count and overfitting pressure while preserving distinct transformations for even and odd qubits, potentially narrowing the 0.08 generalization gap and improving convergence and parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
