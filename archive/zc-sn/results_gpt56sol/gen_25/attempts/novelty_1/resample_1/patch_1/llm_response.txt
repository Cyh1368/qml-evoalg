<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the sequential CZ chain with two complementary brick-wall entangling sublayers. The first couples even bonds and the second couples shifted odd bonds, including the 7–0 closure, so every qubit interacts with both neighbors while disjoint CZ gates remain parallelizable. Parity-shared RZ parameters before and after each sublayer retain the sandwich structure, reduce unnecessary parameter freedom, and may improve generalization and convergence while increasing useful ring connectivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
