<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the pre-entanglement RZ layer. RZ gates commute with every CZ gate, so each removed rotation was functionally combinable with the corresponding post-entanglement RZ rotation. This preserves the block’s effective hypothesis class while eliminating redundant optimization directions. Across repetitions, it should substantially reduce parameter count, gate count, depth, overfitting risk, and convergence time without sacrificing expressivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
