dual_phase_sandwich
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Append a second parity-shared RZ→CZ→RZ sandwich after the independent output phases. The extra open-chain interaction stage can transform the learned local phases into richer multi-qubit correlations, while parity sharing limits the added capacity to four parameters per repeated block. This follows the established open-chain topology, avoids a potentially harmful ring closure, and targets improved validation accuracy and faster convergence without introducing unsupported gates.
</DESCRIPTION>
