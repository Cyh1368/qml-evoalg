<DESCRIPTION>
Insert a parity-shared RX mixer between the shifted-ring CZ sandwich and the final full-chain phase sandwich. This noncommuting interleaving converts information accumulated in the first two phase-entangling stages into population differences before the next RZ/CZ stage, increasing useful expressivity beyond a terminal-only mixer. Sharing one angle across even wires and one across odd wires limits the parameter increase to two per ansatz repetition while preserving the circuit’s parity structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
