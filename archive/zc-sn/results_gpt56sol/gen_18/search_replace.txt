<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the shared CRY initializer because it reduced validation accuracy, increased loss, and substantially slowed convergence relative to the simpler baseline. Replace the serial open-chain entangler with two parallel brick-wall CZ sublayers, including the complementary 7–0 bond for uniform connectivity. Each sublayer receives distinct parity-shared pre/post RZ phases, preserving parameter efficiency while regularizing phase transformations and increasing interaction-pattern diversity.
</DESCRIPTION>
