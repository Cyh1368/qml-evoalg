<DESCRIPTION>
Augment each edge of the linear CZ chain with a commuting CRZ rotation. Share one parameter across even-indexed edges and another across odd-indexed edges, adding pairwise-correlation flexibility with only two new parameters per ansatz repetition. Placing each CRZ immediately after its corresponding CZ preserves the chain structure and should improve parameter efficiency relative to independent edge parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
