<DESCRIPTION>
This design preserves the successful independent RY feature transformations and open-chain CZ entanglement while imposing a stronger symmetry on the phase layers. Each post-entanglement RZ now reuses the corresponding even- or odd-parity pre-entanglement parameter. This creates a mirrored phase structure with only two RZ angles per block, substantially reducing parameter count without removing gates or connectivity. The stronger regularization is intended to retain the current validation accuracy while improving parameter efficiency, limiting overfitting, and accelerating convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
