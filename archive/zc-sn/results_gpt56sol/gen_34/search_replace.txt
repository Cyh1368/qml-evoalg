merge_adjacent_rz_layers
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the second pre-entanglement RZ layer because it is directly adjacent to the first post-entanglement RZ layer. On each wire, consecutive RZ gates commute and collapse into a single RZ whose angle is their sum, so the removed parameters add no functional expressivity. This eliminates two independent parameters per repeated block and eight gates per block, reducing depth, optimization redundancy, and overfitting risk while preserving the circuit’s representable transformations.
</DESCRIPTION>
