<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the parity-preserving distance-two CZ ring with a backward nearest-neighbor chain. The current bridge separates even and odd qubits into disconnected entanglement subgraphs, which can limit cross-parity feature mixing and may contribute to slow convergence. Re-entangling adjacent qubits after the noncommuting RY mixer enables local correlations to be refined across both parity classes, while removing the ring-closure gate slightly reduces depth and gate count. The backward ordering also tests the complementary correlation layout suggested by the stronger chain-based prior architecture without adding parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
