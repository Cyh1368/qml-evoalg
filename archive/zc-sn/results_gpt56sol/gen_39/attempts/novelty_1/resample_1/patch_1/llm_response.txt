dual_parity_phase_sandwich
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Share both existing RZ layers by wire parity to reduce redundant parameters and regularize the circuit, then append a second parity-shared RZ→CZ→RZ phase sandwich. This lowers the block parameter count from 24 to 16 while adding a second structured phase-processing stage, aiming to recover the stronger validation score and substantially faster convergence observed for dual-phase designs.
</DESCRIPTION>
