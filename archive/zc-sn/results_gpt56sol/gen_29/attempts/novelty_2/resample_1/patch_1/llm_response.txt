<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the sequential CZ chain with two interleaved brick-wall entangling sublayers. Even and odd bonds can execute in parallel, while the added 7–0 bond closes the ring and removes endpoint asymmetry. Each CZ sublayer is surrounded by parity-shared RZ rotations, providing distinct trainable phase contexts without introducing per-wire parameters. This increases structured expressivity and connectivity while slightly reducing the number of unique ansatz parameters relative to the current independently parameterized post-rotation layer.
</DESCRIPTION>
