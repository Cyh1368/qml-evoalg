<DESCRIPTION>
Use a symmetry-regularized ansatz that preserves independent RY feature mixing while sharing both pre- and post-entanglement phase parameters by wire parity. Compared with pairwise and wire-specific phases, this reduces each repeated block from 20 unique parameters to 12 without removing gates or entangling capacity. The lower-dimensional optimization should converge faster, improve parameter efficiency, and reduce the observed train-validation gap while retaining the proven nearest-neighbor CZ backbone.
</DESCRIPTION>
