<DESCRIPTION>
Regularize the overparameterized phase layers by sharing RZ angles across alternating qubits while retaining independent parameters for the pre- and post-entanglement layers. This reduces the block from 24 to 12 trainable parameters, which should improve parameter efficiency, convergence, and the observed generalization gap without removing gates. Closing the CZ chain into an even ring gives every qubit equal entangling connectivity and adds only one fixed gate.
</DESCRIPTION>
