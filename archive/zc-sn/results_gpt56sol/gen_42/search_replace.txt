<DESCRIPTION>
Add a second parity-shared RZ→CZ→RZ phase-entanglement sandwich after the existing independently parameterized post-rotation layer. This introduces only four parameter identities while giving each repeated block another structured opportunity to transform phase correlations. The shared even/odd parameters regularize the added capacity, aiming to preserve generalization while improving validation accuracy, loss, and convergence.
