<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the two overlapping eight-CZ layers with complementary four-CZ matchings. The first matching couples (0,1), (2,3), (4,5), and (6,7); after the noncommuting RY mixer, the shifted matching couples (1,2), (3,4), (5,6), and (7,0). Together they retain full ring connectivity while every CZ within a matching can execute in parallel. The shifted layer also couples opposite parities instead of preserving the disconnected even/odd sectors of the distance-two layer. This reduces gate count and depth without changing the parameter count, and should improve optimization speed and generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
