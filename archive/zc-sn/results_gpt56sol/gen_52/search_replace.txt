<DESCRIPTION>
Add parity-shared controlled-Z rotations to the seven nearest-neighbor edges of the first entangling chain. The two shared angles refine even- and odd-edge correlation strengths with minimal parameter growth, while retaining the existing CZ backbone and ring closure. This targeted flexibility may improve validation accuracy and reduce the generalization gap without the large parameter cost of independent edge angles.
</DESCRIPTION>
