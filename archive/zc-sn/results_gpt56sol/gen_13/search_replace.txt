<DESCRIPTION>
Add a second phase–entangle–phase layer after the existing post-rotation stage. The new RZ angles are shared by even and odd wires, adding only four independent parameters per ansatz block while allowing the intervening CZ chain to convert additional learned phase structure into useful many-qubit correlations. This preserves parameter efficiency and the successful linear entanglement topology while increasing expressivity without introducing costly controlled-rotation parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
