<DESCRIPTION>
Append a second parity-shared RZ→CZ-chain→RZ sandwich after the independent output phases. This adds a distinct phase-entanglement-phase stage that can transform the first layer’s learned local phases into richer nonlocal correlations. Sharing angles by wire parity limits the increase to four parameters per repeated block, while retaining the proven open-chain topology and avoiding the poorly performing ring closure. The added layer is intended to improve validation accuracy and loss while offering a more direct optimization path than the current initial CRY correlations alone.
</DESCRIPTION>
