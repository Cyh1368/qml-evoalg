<DESCRIPTION>
Append a parity-shared RY layer after the existing RX mixer. The orthogonal RX→RY sequence provides a more expressive final basis transformation, allowing phase correlations generated by the CZ/RZ layers to be converted into measurable populations more effectively. Sharing one RY parameter across even wires and one across odd wires limits parameter growth and preserves the circuit’s parity inductive bias.
</DESCRIPTION>
