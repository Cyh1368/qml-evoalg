<DESCRIPTION>
Add a parameter-efficient parity-shared RY mixer after the existing phase-entangling block, followed by a shifted CZ matching that closes the qubit ring and a final parity-shared RX mixer. The mid-circuit RY layer converts learned phase information into populations before additional entanglement, while the shifted matching propagates correlations across bonds missing from the original linear CZ chain. The orthogonal final RX layer further increases noncommuting expressivity. Parameter sharing limits the addition to four trainable parameters per repeated block, aiming to improve validation accuracy and convergence without excessive overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
