<DESCRIPTION>
Reorder the nearest-neighbor CZ chain into two disjoint matchings and add the missing ring-closing edge. Because CZ gates commute, grouping even bonds followed by odd bonds preserves the existing chain interaction while exposing maximal parallelism. The added (7,0) bond removes boundary asymmetry and gives every qubit equal connectivity, which may improve validation performance and reduce effective entangling depth.
</DESCRIPTION>
