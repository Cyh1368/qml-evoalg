<DESCRIPTION>
Append a two-axis parity-shared RX→RY mixer after the final phase layer. The RX stage converts learned phase information into measurable populations, while the subsequent noncommuting RY stage increases basis flexibility. Sharing one parameter across even wires and one across odd wires on each axis limits the added parameter count and may reduce overfitting relative to wire-independent rotations.
</DESCRIPTION>
