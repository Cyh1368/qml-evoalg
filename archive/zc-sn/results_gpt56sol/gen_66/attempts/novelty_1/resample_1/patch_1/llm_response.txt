<DESCRIPTION>
Append a parameter-efficient, parity-shared RX→RY output mixer after the final phase layer. The current circuit ends with RZ rotations, which may leave learned phase information poorly exposed to a population-based readout. Two noncommuting rotation axes convert those phases into measurable populations more flexibly than a single-axis mixer, while parity sharing adds only four trainable parameters per repeated block. This targets the current validation gap and slow convergence without substantially increasing capacity.
</DESCRIPTION>
