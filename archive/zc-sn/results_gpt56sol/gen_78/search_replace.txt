<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the underperforming initial CRY matching and replace the eight independent input rotations with parity-shared rotations to reduce excess capacity. Then make the open chain trainable with parity-shared CRZ interactions, followed by noncommuting mixing and a distance-two CZ/CRZ ring. This places full-chain propagation before the ring block, improves long-range correlation flow, and reduces the number of unique parameters while preserving expressive controlled interactions.
</DESCRIPTION>
