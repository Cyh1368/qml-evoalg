<DESCRIPTION>
Close the linear nearest-neighbor entangler into an 8-qubit ring by adding CZ(7,0). This gives every qubit equal connectivity and creates a direct path between the chain endpoints, potentially improving long-range feature interactions and validation accuracy. The edit adds no trainable parameters and only a small depth/gate-count cost.
