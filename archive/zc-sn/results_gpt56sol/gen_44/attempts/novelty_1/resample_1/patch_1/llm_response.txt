tie_post_rz_parity
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Tie each post-entanglement RZ angle to the corresponding pre-entanglement even/odd angle. This preserves the full gate layout and entangling depth while reducing the block’s distinct parameters from 18 to 10. The stronger parity symmetry should improve parameter efficiency, limit overfitting, and potentially retain the current validation accuracy with faster convergence and a smaller train-validation gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
