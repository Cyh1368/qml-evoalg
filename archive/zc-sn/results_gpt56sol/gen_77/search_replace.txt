<DESCRIPTION>
Restore the strongest prior circuit’s parameter-efficient inductive bias while reducing entangler depth without changing its unitary. Sharing the initial RY angles by parity cuts six block parameters and should reduce overfitting. The final RX/RY mixer increased parameter count, depth, loss, and convergence time, so it is removed. Since CZ and CRZ are diagonal and mutually commuting, the ring entanglers are reordered into disjoint even- and odd-bond sublayers, preserving expressivity while enabling more parallel execution.
</DESCRIPTION>
