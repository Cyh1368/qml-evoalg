<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the two RZ–CZ–RZ sandwiches into one CZ ring followed by independently parameterized per-wire RZ gates. Because CZ commutes with RZ, the current parity-shared pre/post rotations reduce to only two effective phase combinations while introducing redundant optimization directions and many extra gates. The replacement preserves full ring entanglement, restores eight identifiable local phase degrees of freedom at the same total parameter count, and substantially reduces gate count, which should improve expressivity, conditioning, and convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
