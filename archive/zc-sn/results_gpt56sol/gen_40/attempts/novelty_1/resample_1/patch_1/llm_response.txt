<DESCRIPTION>
Restore parity sharing in the pre-entanglement RZ layer, which previously improved the combined score while reducing parameter count and convergence time. Then add a lightweight parity-shared RY mixing layer immediately after the CZ chain. Unlike the surrounding diagonal RZ/CZ operations, these RY gates change computational-basis populations, increasing useful nonlinearity with only two additional parameters per repeated block. Parity sharing regularizes the circuit and keeps the added expressivity parameter-efficient.
</DESCRIPTION>
