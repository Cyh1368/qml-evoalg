<DESCRIPTION>
Insert a parity-shared RY mixing layer immediately after the CZ chain. Unlike the surrounding diagonal RZ/CZ operations, these noncommuting rotations convert learned phase correlations into population changes that can affect readout and improve expressivity. Sharing one angle across even wires and another across odd wires adds only two parameters per repeated block, limiting overfitting and parameter cost while mixing every qubit.
</DESCRIPTION>
