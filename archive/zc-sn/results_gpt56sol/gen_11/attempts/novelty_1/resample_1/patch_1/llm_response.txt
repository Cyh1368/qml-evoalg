<DESCRIPTION>
This ansatz uses a trainable correlation-injection layer rather than relying solely on fixed CZ gates for entanglement. Four disjoint CRY gates share one parameter, adding pairwise input-dependent interactions with only one extra trainable angle per repeated block. The open CZ chain then propagates these correlations globally, while parity-shared pre-rotations regularize the model and independent post-rotations preserve readout flexibility. This targets improved validation accuracy without a large parameter or depth increase.
</DESCRIPTION>
