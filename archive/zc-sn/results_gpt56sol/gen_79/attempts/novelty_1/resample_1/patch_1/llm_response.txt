<DESCRIPTION>
Regularize the overparameterized output-phase layer by sharing one RZ angle across even wires and another across odd wires. The current candidate has substantially more parameters than the stronger prior programs and exhibits a larger validation gap and loss. This edit removes six unique parameters per repeated block without removing gates or changing depth, preserving phase adaptation while imposing a symmetry-aligned inductive bias that should improve parameter efficiency, generalization, and convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
