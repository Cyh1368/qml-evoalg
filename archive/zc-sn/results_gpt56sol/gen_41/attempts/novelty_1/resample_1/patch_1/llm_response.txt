<DESCRIPTION>
Insert a lightweight noncommuting RY mixing layer between the CZ chain and the post-entanglement RZ layer. Sharing one angle across even wires and another across odd wires adds only two independent parameters per ansatz repetition while allowing the CZ-generated correlations to be transformed into features accessible to later rotations and readout. This controlled capacity increase should improve validation accuracy and loss without substantially harming parameter efficiency or generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
