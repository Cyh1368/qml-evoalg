<DESCRIPTION>
Close the linear entangling chain into a ring by adding a CZ gate between qubit 7 and qubit 0 after the existing chain of CZ gates. This adds one extra two-qubit connection, giving every qubit exactly two entangling neighbors (forming a ring topology) instead of the two endpoint qubits having only one neighbor each. This increases connectivity and information mixing across the whole register with only one additional gate (no new parameters), which can help reduce the optimization landscape's barren regions and improve generalization (smaller train-test gap) while barely increasing depth/gate count and not increasing parameter count at all.
