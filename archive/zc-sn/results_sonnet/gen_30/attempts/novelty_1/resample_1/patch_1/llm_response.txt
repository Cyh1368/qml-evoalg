trainable_ring_plus_readout

<DESCRIPTION>
The current version replaced the previous trainable ring entangler (CRZ) with a fixed, non-parametrized CZ ring. This removes tunable entangling strength from the nearest-neighbour connections, which likely explains the performance drop (0.81 vs 0.86-0.87 in earlier variants). Restore trainable entanglement by using CRZ gates on the ring, but keep the parameter count low by sharing a single "even" angle and a single "odd" angle across alternating ring edges (as validated in the second prior program, which achieved the highest score of 0.87). Additionally, append a final RY readout-preparation layer (one free angle per qubit) after the post-entangling RZ layer, mirroring the seed architecture's successful post-entanglement rotation layer that helps the readout observable align with the encoded correlations. This adds only 2 (ring) + 8 (final RY) = 10 extra parameters while restoring meaningful entangling expressiveness and improving readout flexibility.
</DESCRIPTION>
