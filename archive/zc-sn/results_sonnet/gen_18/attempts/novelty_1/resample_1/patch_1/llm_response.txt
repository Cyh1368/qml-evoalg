<DESCRIPTION>
Building directly on the top-performing seed (combined score 0.85, RY → RZ_pre → linear CZ-chain → RZ_post), this variant closes the entangling chain into a ring by adding a single extra CZ(7,0) gate right before the final RZ_post layer. This creates a cyclic entanglement topology (0-1-2-3-4-5-6-7-0) instead of a purely linear chain, giving qubit 0 and qubit 7 direct correlation and improving information mixing across the whole register without introducing any new parameters. The change adds only +1 gate/+1 depth, keeping parameter count and efficiency unchanged (146 params) while potentially reducing the generalization gap and mitigating barren-plateau-like issues from limited connectivity, consistent with the recommendation. All other structure (RY encoding layer, pre-CZ RZ layer, and post-CZ RZ layer) is left untouched to preserve the proven performance characteristics.
