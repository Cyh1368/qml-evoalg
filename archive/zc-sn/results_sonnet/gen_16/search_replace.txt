<DESCRIPTION>
Insert an RY rotation layer on all eight qubits immediately after the last CZ gate in the entangling chain, before the post-RZ block, using new independent parameters "ry_mid_0"..."ry_mid_7". This creates an RY-RZ-CZ-RY-RZ pattern where the entangling layer is sandwiched between two non-commuting rotation layers (RY then RZ), increasing the expressivity of the local rotations that follow entanglement. Since RY and RZ generate rotations about different axes, this ordering allows the circuit to more effectively rotate the entangled state before the final RZ phase gates are applied, which should improve trainability and lead to faster convergence while only adding 8 parameters (146 -> 154) and 8 gates to the existing depth-99 circuit, staying close to the empirically favorable depth.
