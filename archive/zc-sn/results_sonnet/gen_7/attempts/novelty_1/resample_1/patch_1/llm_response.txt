<DESCRIPTION>
Analysis: since RZ gates and CZ gates are both diagonal in the computational basis, they mutually commute. This means the current block's `rz_pre_i` and `rz_post_i` layers (separated only by a chain of CZ gates) are mathematically equivalent to a *single* combined RZ rotation per qubit (rz_pre_i + rz_post_i) — i.e. half of the 16 RZ parameters are redundant and contribute no extra expressive power, just wasted parameter budget and extra circuit depth.

Fix: remove the redundant duplicate RZ layer, replace the CZ ring entangler with a parametrized CRY ring (which does NOT commute with the surrounding RY/RZ layers, so it contributes genuine entangling expressivity), and close the ring (qubit 7 -> qubit 0) to give a fully connected entangling topology in one pass. This keeps the parameter count roughly the same (8 RY + 8 CRY + 8 RZ = 24 per block, versus 8+8+8=24 previously) while using every parameter meaningfully, which should improve expressivity per parameter, reduce the train/val gap, and potentially speed convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
