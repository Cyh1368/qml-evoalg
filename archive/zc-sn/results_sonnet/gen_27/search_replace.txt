<DESCRIPTION>
The current linear CZ chain (0-1,1-2,...,6-7) leaves qubits 0 and 7 disconnected and uses only CZ gates uniformly. I introduce two improvements: (1) mix in CNOT gates on alternating pairs (odd-indexed links) to diversify the entangling gate set as recommended, which can help error/noise-aware optimization and break the symmetry that CZ-only layers impose; (2) close the chain into a ring by adding a CZ between qubit 7 and qubit 0, giving every qubit two entangling neighbors instead of the two end qubits having only one. This increases connectivity/expressivity at zero parameter cost (CZ/CNOT are fixed, non-parametrized gates) while only mildly increasing depth and gate count (one extra gate), which should improve validation accuracy and reduce the train/validation gap without adding trainable parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
