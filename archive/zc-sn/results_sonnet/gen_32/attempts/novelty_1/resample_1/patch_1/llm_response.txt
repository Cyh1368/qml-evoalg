<DESCRIPTION>
The current candidate (CZ ring + tied RZ pre/post) underperforms (0.79) compared to the earlier RY+CRZ-ring+RX+CRZ-skip2 design (0.87). Revert to that stronger structure (full per-qubit RX parameters, alternating shared CRZ ring, single-parameter skip-2 CRZ layer) and, per the recommendation, append a sparse skip-3 CRZ layer sharing a single parameter `crz_skip3` on wires [0,3],[1,4],[2,5],[3,6],[4,7],[5,0],[6,1],[7,2]. This adds long-range connectivity at minimal parameter cost (only +1 parameter), which should improve validation accuracy and reduce the generalization gap without significantly increasing overfitting risk, building directly on the previously successful sparse CRZ regularization pattern.
