add_sparse_skip2_crz_layer

<DESCRIPTION>
Adding a sparse next-nearest-neighbor entangling layer using a single shared parameter CRZ gate on pairs (0,2),(2,4),(4,6),(6,0) right after the rz_pre stage. This introduces long-range correlations across the ring beyond the existing nearest-neighbor CZ chain, at minimal parameter cost (+1 param, +4 gates). Long-range entanglement can help capture non-local correlations in the data encoding, potentially improving expressivity without significantly increasing depth or parameter count, and may reduce the train-test generalization gap by enriching the entangling structure while keeping the circuit trainable (shared parameter avoids barren-plateau-inducing param blowup).
