symmetric_rz_param_sharing

<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the pre- and post-entangler RZ rotation parameters across symmetric qubit pairs (0↔7, 1↔6, 2↔5, 3↔4). This reduces unique RZ parameters from 16 to 8 (8 total fewer parameters overall), which cuts parameter count while preserving circuit depth and gate count. Parameter sharing across symmetric pairs encodes a soft inductive bias (mirror symmetry) that previously correlated with better generalization gap in similar shared-entangler ansätze. This should improve parameter efficiency and potentially reduce overfitting (train-val gap) while keeping expressivity via the RY layer, which remains fully independent per qubit.
