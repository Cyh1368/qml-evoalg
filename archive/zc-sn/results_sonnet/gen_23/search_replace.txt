<DESCRIPTION>
Add a sparse, single-parameter CRZ entangling layer with next-nearest-neighbor connectivity (skip-2 pairs [0,2],[2,4],[4,6],[6,0]) immediately after the rz_pre stage, using a single shared parameter "crz_skip2". This introduces long-range correlations that the current nearest-neighbor-only CZ chain cannot capture (e.g., correlations between qubits 0 and 2, or wrap-around between 6 and 0), while adding only 4 gates and 1 parameter. Sharing a single parameter across all four CRZ gates keeps the parameter count essentially unchanged, preserving parameter efficiency while potentially improving the expressibility/entanglement structure enough to reduce the generalization gap and improve validation accuracy, since long-range entanglers have been shown to help mitigate barren-plateau-like optimization issues and capture non-local feature correlations relevant to the (unknown) data structure.
