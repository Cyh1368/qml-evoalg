crz_alternating_entanglement

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace every second CZ gate in the linear entangling chain with a parameterized CRZ gate, introducing tunable entanglement strength at alternating positions (1-2, 3-4, 5-6) while keeping the 0-1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-7 links as fixed CZ gates. This preserves circuit depth and gate count almost exactly (same number of two-qubit gates, only 3 become parameterized) while adding just 3 new parameters. Sparse parameterized entanglement has empirically shown better generalization (lower train-test gap) in prior generations because it lets the circuit adaptively tune how much entanglement is injected at select points without over-parametrizing every link, striking a balance between the fully fixed CZ chain (which is expressive but rigid) and full parameterization of every link (which risks overfitting/slower convergence). This targeted sparse addition should reduce the generalization gap while maintaining or improving validation accuracy.
