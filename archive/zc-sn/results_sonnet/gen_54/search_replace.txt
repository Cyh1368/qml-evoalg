<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz (score 0.85) underperforms the earlier variant that used per-qubit RY, alternating-shared ring CRZ, per-qubit RX, and shared skip-2 CRZ entanglers duplicated twice (score 0.89, fast convergence). I revert to that stronger structure and apply the suggested refinement: replace the single shared `crz_skip` parameter with two alternating shared parameters (`crz_skip_even` for even-index edges, `crz_skip_odd` for odd-index edges), mirroring the ring layer's even/odd sharing pattern. This should provide finer-grained control over mid-range entanglement while keeping parameter count identical (still just 2 params for this layer instead of 1, but overall much fewer params than the current 164-param version), likely improving both convergence speed and generalization gap based on Gen35/Gen38 observations.
