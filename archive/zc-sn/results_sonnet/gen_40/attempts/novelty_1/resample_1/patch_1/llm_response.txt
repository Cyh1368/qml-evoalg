<DESCRIPTION>
The current candidate underperforms (score 0.83) compared to the earlier CRZ-ring + skip-2 design (score 0.87) because it replaced the trainable CRZ ring with fixed CZ gates and replaced the long-range skip-2 CRZ block with a second RY rotation layer, losing the beneficial global mixing structure. This edit restores the proven CRZ-ring + RZ-pre/post + skip-2 CRZ structure from the best-performing seed, and additionally appends a single-parameter "diameter" CRZ layer connecting each qubit to its antipodal partner (0-4, 1-5, 2-6, 3-7). This adds long-range (distance-4) entanglement using only one extra trainable parameter, which should improve global feature mixing and generalization (reducing train-test gap) without significantly increasing parameter count or depth, consistent with the sparse/shared-parameter regularization strategy that worked well previously.
