crz_alternate_entangle

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace every second CZ gate in the linear entangling chain (at positions 1-2, 3-4, 5-6) with parameterized CRZ gates. This preserves the overall circuit depth and connectivity pattern while introducing tunable entanglement strength at alternating positions, similar to the successful sparse parameterized approach from Gen 8 that achieved a low generalization gap (~0.04). This adds only 3 new parameters while potentially improving the circuit's ability to learn task-relevant correlations, without significantly increasing parameter count or depth compared to the fully fixed CZ chain.
</DESCRIPTION>
