<DESCRIPTION>
Close the linear entangling chain into a ring topology by adding a single CZ gate between qubit 7 and qubit 0 after the existing chain of CZ gates. This preserves the current RY→RZ(pre)→CZ chain→RZ(post) layering, but adds a long-range connection that lets information flow around the loop, improving expressivity and entanglement uniformity across all qubits without increasing parameter count. This is expected to reduce the optimization barrier and generalization gap by giving qubit 0 and qubit 7 direct correlation, complementing the existing nearest-neighbor connectivity, at the cost of just one extra two-qubit gate (small increase to depth/gate count).
