close_entangling_ring

<DESCRIPTION>
Close the linear CZ chain into a ring by adding a CZ gate between qubit 7 and qubit 0 at the end of the entangling block. This adds one extra two-qubit connection, giving the circuit ring connectivity instead of a linear chain. Ring topology allows information/entanglement to flow between the endpoints directly (qubit 0 and qubit 7 previously only connected through 6 intermediate CZs), which can improve expressivity and gradient flow with minimal added depth/parameter cost (no new parameters, since CZ is a fixed gate), potentially reducing the generalization gap and improving validation accuracy while keeping the parameter count unchanged.
