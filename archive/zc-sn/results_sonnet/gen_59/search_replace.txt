<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz's skip-2 entangling layer only covers 6 of the 8 possible skip-2 edges (missing [6,0] and [7,1]), breaking the full ring topology that performed well in prior generations (0.89 score) versus the current partial version (0.86). Restore full 8-edge skip-2 connectivity and, per the recommendation, split the single shared `crz_skip` parameter into two alternating shared parameters (`crz_skip_even` for edges starting at even indices, `crz_skip_odd` for edges starting at odd indices). This mirrors the proven alternating-strength sharing pattern already used successfully in the nearest-neighbor ring and diameter layers, restoring full long-range connectivity while keeping parameter count controlled through sharing (only 2 params added net, replacing partial coverage with complete coverage plus differentiated strength).
