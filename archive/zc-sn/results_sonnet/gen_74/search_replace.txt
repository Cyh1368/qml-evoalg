<DESCRIPTION>
Extend the shared-parameter "shared depth" trick one step further by applying the ansatz block three times instead of two, reusing the same parameter names across all three copies. This increases circuit depth/expressivity (more entangling layers to mix information across qubits) while keeping the parameter count fixed at 128, testing whether the previous scaling trend (0.78 -> 0.89 when going from 1 to 2 blocks) continues to yield gains in validation accuracy and loss reduction without hurting convergence speed too much. Since deeper circuits with shared parameters acted as an effective regularizer in prior generations (reducing train-test gap), a third repetition may further close the generalization gap and improve test performance, though we monitor convergence_step to ensure training still converges efficiently.
</DESCRIPTION>
