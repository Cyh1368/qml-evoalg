<DESCRIPTION>
Following the recommendation, I insert a mid-circuit RY rotation layer immediately after the CZ entangling chain and before the final RZ post-layer. This creates an RY(pre)-RZ(pre)-CZ-RY(mid)-RZ(post) structure. The added RY layer introduces a non-commuting rotation between the entangling operation and the final phase rotation, increasing the expressibility of the circuit per entangling layer without adding any new two-qubit gates. This should help the circuit build richer correlations from the entanglement while keeping depth/gate-count growth modest (adds only 8 params and 8 gates), potentially improving validation accuracy and convergence speed relative to the pure RY-RZ-CZ-RZ structure.
