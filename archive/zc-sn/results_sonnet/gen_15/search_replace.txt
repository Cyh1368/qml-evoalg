ring_closure_cz

<DESCRIPTION>
Close the linear entangling chain into a ring by adding a CZ gate between qubits 7 and 0 after the existing chain (0-1,1-2,...,6-7). This adds a single extra two-qubit connection, increasing qubit connectivity so long-range correlations between qubit 0 and qubit 7 can be captured directly rather than only through the chain. This typically improves expressivity and can reduce the train-validation generalization gap by allowing more efficient information flow, while adding no new parameters (only one extra fixed CZ gate) and negligible depth increase.
