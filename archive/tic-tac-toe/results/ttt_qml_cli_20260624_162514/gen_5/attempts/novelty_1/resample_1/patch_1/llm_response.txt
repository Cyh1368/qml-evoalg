<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce gate count and parameter count while preserving expressive, symmetry-aware structure.
I replace the previous heavy per-block layout (many single-qubit layers and 12 parameterized CRX edges) with a compact, symmetry-grouped single-qubit layer (shared RY and RZ by corner/edge/center classes) plus a partial entangling layer of CZs (selecting 4 ring edges and 4 hub edges). This halves the entangling operations per block and collapses many per-wire parameters into 6 shared parameters per block, improving parameter efficiency, reducing depth/gate-count, and adding regularization via weight sharing. The smaller parameter set should train faster with Adam and generalize better while keeping enough entanglement to model board structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
