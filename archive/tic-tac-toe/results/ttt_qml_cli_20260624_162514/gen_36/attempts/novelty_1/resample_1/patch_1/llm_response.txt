<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed CZ entanglers and larger per-qubit RX/RZ parametrization with a compact, symmetry-aware block that uses parametrized controlled rotations (CRX on ring edges, CRZ on spokes) and aggressive parameter sharing across symmetric wires. 

Rationale:
- Trainable two-qubit gates (CRX/CRZ) increase expressivity versus fixed CZs while keeping depth similar.
- Sharing parameters by symmetry (corners/edges/center, periphery vs center, ring vs spoke entanglers) keeps the parameter count down to 9 per block, yielding N_PARAMS = 9 * (N_UPLOADS * N_REPEATS) = 54 total. This matches the better-performing prior seed and improves parameter efficiency.
- The design preserves adjacency constraints and keeps entangler count modest (4 ring + 4 spokes) for reasonable depth/gate_count.
- Using a single small shared RZ across wires for parity-breaking reduces parameters while maintaining a global phase bias.

This change should increase expressivity with efficient parameter usage and favor better train/test performance and lower generalization gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
