<DESCRIPTION>
This edit refines the ansatz by introducing two key improvements:
1. **Symmetry-Weighted Entanglement**: Replaces uniform `CRZ` ring/hub parameters with a more granular symmetry-based approach. The ring edges are split into 'corner-to-edge' and 'edge-to-corner' pairs (e.g., [0,1] vs [1,2]) to allow the circuit to learn directional flow on the board.
2. **CZ Cycling**: Adds fixed `CZ` gates that cycle through different subsets of the grid connectivity across the `N_REPEATS` repetitions. By alternating between "ring-heavy" and "hub-heavy" fixed entanglement, we increase the expressive power of the circuit without adding more trainable parameters, helping to reduce the generalization gap and improve accuracy.
3. **Optimized Mixing**: Consolidates the final `RY`/`RZ` layers into a single `RY` layer with more specific symmetry classes (corner, edge, center) to maintain parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
