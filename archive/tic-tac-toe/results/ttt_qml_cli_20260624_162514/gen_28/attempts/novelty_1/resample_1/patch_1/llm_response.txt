<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current heavy parametrized two-qubit CRX/CRZ entangling pattern with a compact, fixed CZ entangling motif that previously gave better performance while keeping symmetry-aware, low-parameter single-qubit layers. This reduces depth and gate count, lowers the per-block parameter count from 10 to 9 (improves parameter efficiency), and preserves useful symmetry-sharing (corners/edges/center and periphery vs center RX/RY groups). The block mirrors the compact, well-performing seed that used CZ on a symmetric subset of ring edges and hub links; it keeps modest pre-phase differences between periphery and center to break parity and retains compact mixing and output sharpening.
</DESCRIPTION>
