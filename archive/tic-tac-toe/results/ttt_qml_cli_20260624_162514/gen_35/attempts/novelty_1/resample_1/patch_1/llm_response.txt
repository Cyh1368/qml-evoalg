<DESCRIPTION>
Refine the periphery parameter sharing into corner vs edge buckets. The previous circuit used a single "rx_periphery" for all eight outer qubits. Based on the grid geometry, corners (0,2,4,6) and edges (1,3,5,7) have different symmetries and connectivity. This edit splits those parameters while maintaining the successful hub-and-spoke entangling structure, aiming to improve accuracy and reduce the generalization gap by capturing board features more precisely.
</DESCRIPTION>
