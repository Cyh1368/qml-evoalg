<DESCRIPTION>
Replace several fixed CZ entanglers with parameterized controlled-rotations that reuse existing shared angle keys.
Rationale:
- Parametrized two-qubit gates (CRX/CRZ) let the optimizer tune entangling strength rather than relying on fixed maximal-phase CZs,
  improving trainability and expressivity.
- Reusing existing shared parameters (rx_periphery, rz_center) keeps the per-block parameter count unchanged (parameter efficiency preserved)
  while injecting nontrivial controllable correlations between ring/periphery qubits and the center hub.
- The new pattern respects adjacency constraints and symmetry (alternating ring CRX edges and hub CRZ spokes), keeping the architecture compact.
This edit only modifies the ANSATZ_SPEC inside the allowed EVOLVE block.
</DESCRIPTION>
