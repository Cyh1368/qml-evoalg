add_output_rz_sharpening
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Append a small shared RZ "sharpening" layer after the existing per-qubit RY_out layer.
This adds only two new parameters per ansatz block: "rz_out_periphery" (shared across qubits 0–7)
and "rz_out_center" (qubit 8). Small end-of-circuit Z phases often improve class separability
with negligible extra depth and very few additional parameters, breaking residual parity
symmetries and sharpening decision boundaries. This is a low-cost, symmetry-aware change
intended to boost validation/test accuracy without dramatically increasing gate count.
</DESCRIPTION>
