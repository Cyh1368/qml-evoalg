<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce parameter count and depth to improve generalization and parameter efficiency while keeping expressive, symmetry-aware structure.

Changes:
- Compress per-block parameterization to 9 shared keys (corners, edges, center, periphery/center Z-phase, RX mixing, and final RY outputs). This lowers N_PARAMS from 72 to 54 (9 params/block * 3 uploads * 2 repeats), matching a previously successful compact design.
- Replace many parametrized controlled rotations (CRX/CRY) with a smaller set of fixed CZ entanglers on a reduced set of grid edges to lower circuit depth and gate count, limiting overfitting.
- Keep symmetry sharing (corners/edges/center) to regularize and exploit board structure.
This should reduce overfitting (smaller train-test gap), improve parameter efficiency, and preserve useful expressivity via interleaved single-qubit rotations and entanglement.
</DESCRIPTION>
