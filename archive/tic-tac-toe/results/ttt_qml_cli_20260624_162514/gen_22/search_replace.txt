<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce parameter count and circuit depth to improve generalization and parameter efficiency by reverting aggressive diagonal splitting and adopting a compact periphery-vs-center sharing motif. This block:

- Shares rotations across all peripheral cells (corners/edges) and the center, cutting unique parameters from 16+ down to 9 per block (N_PARAMS drops from 96 to 54).
- Keeps a small symmetric RZ phase (periphery vs center) to break parity while staying compact.
- Uses a reduced entangling pattern (6 CZs) to lower depth and gate count but retains connectivity to the center for mixing.
- Adds a shared RX mixing across periphery and center plus a small final RY output layer (periphery vs center) to sharpen decision boundaries.

These changes should reduce overfitting (smaller generalization gap), improve parameter efficiency, and often raise test accuracy by focusing capacity and lowering depth.
</DESCRIPTION>
