<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses a single shared parameter `rz_periphery` for all 9 qubits to break parity. While parameter-efficient, this forces the center qubit (which is the sole contributor to the 'draw' expectation value) to rotate identically to the periphery.

By splitting this into `rz_periphery` (qubits 0-7) and `rz_center` (qubit 8), we allow the model to learn a distinct phase for the center cell. This adds only one parameter per block (6 total across the circuit) but significantly improves the model's ability to distinguish between winning states (periphery-heavy) and draws (center-heavy), sharpening the decision boundaries.
</DESCRIPTION>
