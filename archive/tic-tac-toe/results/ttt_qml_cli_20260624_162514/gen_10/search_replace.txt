<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed CZ entangling layer with tunable, shared CRZ entanglers (separate ring/hub parameters) to give the circuit learnable two-qubit phases that align with the Z-basis readout. Add a compact, symmetry-aware RX mixing layer (shared periphery vs center) to increase expressivity and break feature-map-only alignment, while preserving parameter efficiency via symmetry-driven sharing (corners/edges/center and ring/hub shared CRZ). Keep a small final RY output layer shared by periphery/center to sharpen decision boundaries. This balances extra expressivity (learnable entanglement) with parameter efficiency to improve validation/test accuracy and generalization.
