<DESCRIPTION>Introduce alternating ring entanglement axes for the outer perimeter CRX ring using a single shared parameter crx_ring. The alternation is implemented by applying CRX on ring edges for even block_index and CRY for odd block_index, reusing the same parameter. This adds richer correlations without increasing the parameter count. The hub-edge entanglement remains CRY with its own parameter. All changes stay inside the EVOLVE-BLOCK so training remains unchanged.</DESCRIPTION>
