<DESCRIPTION>Introduce alternating ring entanglement axes for the outer perimeter CRX ring using a single shared parameter crx_ring. This adds richer correlations without increasing parameter count, potentially improving validation/test performance and reducing the train-test gap. The alternation is implemented by switching CRX/CRY on ring edges depending on the block_index parity, while hub-edge CRX remain per-edge (no axis alternation) to preserve their distinct parameterization.</DESCRIPTION>
