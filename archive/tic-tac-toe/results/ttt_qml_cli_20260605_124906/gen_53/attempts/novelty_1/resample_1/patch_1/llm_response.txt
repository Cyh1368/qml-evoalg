increase_ring_hub_per_edge_params
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Enhance expressivity by breaking symmetry in the entangling layers. Replace the single shared CRX ring and single shared CRY hub-edge parameter with per-edge parameters. This allows each ring edge and each hub-edge pair to have its own rotation, reducing over-constraint from symmetry and potentially improving validation/test performance and generalization. The change is confined to ANSATZ_SPEC within the EVOLVE-BLOCK, keeping the rest of the training loop, data, and feature map intact.
</DESCRIPTION>
