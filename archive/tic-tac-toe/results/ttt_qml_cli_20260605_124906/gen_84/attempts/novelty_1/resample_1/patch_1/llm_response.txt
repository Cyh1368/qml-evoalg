share_hub_edge_crx_param
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Share the center-to-edge CRX parameter across all four hub-edge connections to reduce per-block parameter count from 4 to 1 for those gates. This lowers N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK (and overall N_PARAMS) while preserving the connectivity pattern, enabling potentially better parameter efficiency and generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>

unify_all_crx_param_crx_ring
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Further unify CRX parameters by using crx_ring for hub-edge gates as well, so all CRX gates across the ring and hub edges share a single parameter. This reduces N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK even more (to 10) and could improve parameter efficiency and generalization, at potential minor cost to expressivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
