<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the outer ring entanglement from CZ gates to a parametric CRX ring gated with a single shared parameter "crx_ring". This introduces trainable, symmetric entanglement around the Tic-Tac-Toe perimeter while keeping the ring structure intact and reducing the total gate depth growth. It increases expressivity with minimal parameter overhead and aligns with parameter-sharing strategies used elsewhere in the ansatz.
</DESCRIPTION>
