<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce two alternating CRX parameters for the ring entanglement (crx_ring_A and crx_ring_B) across the outer ring edges. This increases expressivity of the ring connectivity without drastically changing the overall circuit structure or depth, while preserving adjacency constraints. The ring now alternates parameters around the loop (A, B, A, B, ...), allowing more nuanced coupling between neighboring qubits and potentially reducing training/test gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
