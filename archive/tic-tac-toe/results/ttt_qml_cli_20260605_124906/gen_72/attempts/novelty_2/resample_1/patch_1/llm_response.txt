<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce parameter sharing across ring CRX gates (crx_ring) and hub-edge CRX gates (crx_hub_edge) by replacing the perimeter CZs with CRX gates on the ring and consolidating hub-edge parameters to a single shared key. This reduces the number of free parameters per block (from 13 to 11), increasing parameter efficiency and potentially improving trainability and generalization while preserving the overall hierarchical structure and adjacency constraints.
</DESCRIPTION>
