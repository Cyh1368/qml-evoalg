<DESCRIPTION>
Enhance the ring entanglement by switching the outer CZ ring to a shared-parameter CRX ring. This adds a new trainable parameter crx_ring for the perimeter interactions, increasing expressivity while keeping the overall block structure and parameter sharing scheme. The hub entanglement (center-to-edges) remains unchanged, preserving a compact yet expressive architecture. This change nudges the parameter count to 66 (N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK becomes 11), aligning with previously successful seeds and potentially improving validation/test performance and generalization.
