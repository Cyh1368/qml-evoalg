<NAME>per-edge-crx-ring</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>Increase expressivity of the outer ring entanglement by switching CRX ring from a single shared parameter to per-edge parameters. This allows the ring to capture edge-specific correlations in tic-tac-toe boards, potentially improving validation accuracy and generalization. A moderate increase in parameter count is expected, but the change is localized to the ansatz specification and stays within the EVOLVE-BLOCK scope.</DESCRIPTION>
