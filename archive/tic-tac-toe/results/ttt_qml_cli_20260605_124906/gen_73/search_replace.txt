<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce a diversification of ring entanglement by splitting the outer CRX ring into two parallel parameter groups (crx_ring_1 and crx_ring_2) alternating around the perimeter. This increases expressivity and can improve optimization without changing the overall block depth or data flow. The change is confined to the ANSATZ_SPEC construction inside the EVOLVE-BLOCK so training behavior remains comparable while enabling richer parameter sharing patterns.
</DESCRIPTION>
