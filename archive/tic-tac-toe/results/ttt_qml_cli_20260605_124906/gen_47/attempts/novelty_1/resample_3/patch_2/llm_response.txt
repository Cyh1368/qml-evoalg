<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce a modest increase in expressivity by splitting the ring entanglement CRY gates into two parameter groups across the outer ring. The first half of the ring uses cry_ring_A and the second half uses cry_ring_B, enabling per-edge flexibility while keeping adjacency and overall parameter budget. Hub (CRX) and other layers remain unchanged. This aims to improve validation/test performance with richer entanglement patterns without exploding parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
