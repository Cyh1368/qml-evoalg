<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce a second, symmetry-shared RY layer (ry2_corner/ry2_edge/ry2_center) between the hub-ring entanglement and the final post-entanglement RZ block. This adds expressivity by threading an additional nonlinear rotation per symmetry group while preserving parameter sharing for efficiency. The new layer is placed after Layer 4 (hub entanglement) and before Layer 5 (post-RZ), increasing the per-block parameter count from 11 to 14 and potentially improving validation/test performance and the overall score.
</DESCRIPTION>
