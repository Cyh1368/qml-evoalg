<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce a ring reinforcement by replacing the CZ-only ring with a two-pass CRX ring around the tic-tac-toe perimeter. All ring gates share a single parameter crx_ring to keep the parameter count stable (one additional parameter beyond the baseline, but we maintain a similar total block depth growth). The first pass executes CRX gates in forward order around the ring; the second pass executes the same gates in reverse order. This deepens entanglement with minimal parameter overhead and can improve expressivity and generalization, potentially raising validation/test performance and narrowing train-test gaps on the tic-tac-toe task.
</DESCRIPTION>
