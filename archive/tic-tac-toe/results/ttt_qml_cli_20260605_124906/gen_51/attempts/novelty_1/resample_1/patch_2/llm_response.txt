<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce parameter-efficient ring and hub entanglement by
- replacing the perimeter CZ ring with a shared-parameter CRX ring (crx_ring) around the board,
- replacing per-edge CRX hub gates with a single shared CRY hub-edge parameter (cry_hub_edge).
This reduces the per-block parameter count from 13 to 11, bringing the total parameter count from 78 to 66, which aligns with architectures that previously yielded higher validation/test performance and better generalization with Adam training.
</DESCRIPTION>
