<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the motif-discovery ZZZ triples with oriented doubly-controlled RZ (CCRZ) interactions aligned to the eight winning lines, and augment the center↔edge links by adding lightweight CRX entanglers (shared parameter) in addition to the existing CRZ controls. Rationale: prior candidates that used CCRZ-style winning-line motifs plus diversified two-qubit entanglers performed better than the ZZZ-only variant. CCRZs provide directional, motif-aligned three-body phase interactions that directly target winning-line correlations, while CRX on center-edge pairs adds X-basis two-qubit correlations (complementary to CRZ) without violating grid connectivity and with only one extra shared parameter. Keeping the CZ backbone and symmetry-shared single-qubit rotations preserves stable entanglement and parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
