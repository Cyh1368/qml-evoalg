edit_targeted_crz_and_grouped_zzz
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace redundant center-edge CZs and the single shared CRZ with two symmetry-aware CRZ parameters applied to opposite edge pairs. Also split the collective ZZZ winning-line rotations into three orientation-specific parameters (horizontal, vertical, diagonal) rather than one shared parameter. Rationale: replacing fixed CZs on center edges with parametrized CRZs concentrates conditional phase control where center-edge correlations matter while keeping short-range CZs on the perimeter; splitting ZZZ by geometric orientation permits the circuit to learn distinct three-in-a-row signatures (horizontal vs vertical vs diagonal) without exploding parameter count. This improves expressive power targeted at tic-tac-toe motifs while keeping parameter sharing to respect symmetry and limit parameter growth.
</DESCRIPTION>
