swap_zzz_early_and_parameter_compression
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Move the three-qubit ZZZ "winning-line" motifs earlier in the block (immediately after the symmetry RY layer) to encourage the circuit to form line-correlations before broader ring entanglement. At the same time reduce parameter count and encourage symmetry by merging redundant CRZ/CCRZ/RZ parameter groups:

- Share all grid CRZs (outer ring + center edges) under a single "crz_ring" parameter to compress radial vs. ring coupling into one tunable scale (reduces one parameter).
- Share all CCRZ three-body controls into one "ccrz_center" parameter (reduces one parameter).
- Merge post-entanglement corner+edge RZs into a single "rz_post_outer" shared parameter (reduces one parameter).

These compressions lower N_PARAMS from 96 to 78 (improving parameter efficiency) while the earlier placement of ZZZ tests whether explicit winning-line interactions accelerate learning and tightens generalization. The wiring and gate vocabulary remain valid under connectivity rules.
</DESCRIPTION>
