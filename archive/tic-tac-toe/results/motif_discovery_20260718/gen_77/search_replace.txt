<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the seed ansatz with a motif-aware, symmetry-compressed block that proved effective in prior variants:
- Keep efficient symmetry-grouped RY local rotations (corners/edges/center).
- Use shared CRZ "ring" + shared CRZ center couplings for robust grid entanglement.
- Replace a single shared ZZZ with geometry-aligned ZZZ motifs separated into rows/cols/diags (three parameters). This explicitly encodes the eight winning lines as collective Z rotations, making the ansatz capable of learning line-specific correlations (motif discovery).
- Add CCRZ high-order correlations grouped into center and diagonal families to capture conditional three-qubit effects without exploding parameter count.
- Collapse the final single-qubit RZ+RX into a compact 'rot_*' parameter per symmetry zone (corners/edges/center). Implemented by applying RZ then RX with the same parameter name for each wire in the symmetry group. This reduces effective free-parameter complexity while retaining expressive local rotations.
This edit keeps all gates legal under the schema/constraints and aims to improve validation/test accuracy and parameter efficiency by aligning three-qubit motifs with task structure and compressing redundant single-qubit parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
