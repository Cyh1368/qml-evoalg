<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current monolithic ZZZ/CCRZ motif and shared CRZ grid with a geometry-aware ansatz:
- Keep symmetry-grouped local RY rotations.
- Replace the single shared ZZZ and CCRZ line parameters with separated ZZZ parameters for rows/columns/diagonals and separated CCRZ parameters for edge-center vs diag-center triples. This lets the circuit represent different three-body correlations on winning lines more flexibly.
- Replace the single CRZ grid parameter with a ring-style CRZ ("crz_ring") and a separate center-edge CRZ ("crz_center_edge") to allow the center coupling to differ from outer-ring couplings.
- Retain shared post-RZ rotations by symmetry group for phase flexibility / regularization.

This change increases the per-block parameterization (more targeted triple params and split two-qubit params), which earlier experiments showed improves validation/test accuracy and reduces generalization gap for this task by better matching the problem's geometric motifs (winning lines and center-role).
</DESCRIPTION>
