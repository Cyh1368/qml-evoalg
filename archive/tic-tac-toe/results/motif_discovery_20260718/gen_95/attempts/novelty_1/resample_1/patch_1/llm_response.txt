<DESCRIPTION>
Restore a motif-aligned ansatz that previously yielded stronger performance:
- Reintroduce distinct ZZZ parameters grouped by geometric role (rows, cols, diags)
  instead of a single shared "zzz_line" to let the circuit learn line-specific
  three-body correlations.
- Restore compact symmetry-grouped initial RY rotations and two shared CRZ
  coupling parameters (ring vs center) as in the better-performing seed.
- Add compressed CCRZ groupings (center vs diag) for high-order correlations.
- Replace the single post-RZs with compact RZ+RX tied parameters per symmetry
  region ("rot_*") to increase expressivity while keeping parameter-efficiency.
This increases N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK from 11 -> 13 (N_PARAMS 66 -> 78), matching
the earlier stronger seed and restoring the ordering of motifs that empirically
gave better validation/test accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
