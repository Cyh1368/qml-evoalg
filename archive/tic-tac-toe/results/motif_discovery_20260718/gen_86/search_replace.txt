<DESCRIPTION>
Move the doubly-controlled CCRZ three-qubit interactions immediately after the local RY rotations so high-order motif correlations are planted early (RY → CCRZ → CRZ → ZZZ → compact RZ+RX). This follows the motif-discovery recommendation: seed high-order correlations right after local feature rotations to accelerate learning of winning-line patterns.

Also reduce and reuse parameters by grouping rotations and entanglers by geometric symmetry (corners/edges/center, ring vs center couplings, row/col/diag ZZZ groups) to match a previously strong seed (13 unique keys, 78 total params). Fewer unique parameters improves generalization and parameter efficiency while keeping expressive three-qubit motifs aligned to the eight winning lines. The block preserves allowed gates and connectivity constraints.
</DESCRIPTION>
