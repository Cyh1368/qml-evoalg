<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce orientation-specific doubly-controlled RZ (CCRZ) motifs aligned with the eight winning lines (horizontal/vertical/diagonal), while keeping the ZZZ winning-line MultiRZ motifs split by orientation. Preserve a CZ lattice backbone and a single shared center-edge controlled-phase (CRZ) to focus center modulation. Use symmetry-grouped pre/post RZ and RY rotations (corner/edge/center) for parameter efficiency. This follows the motif-discovery recommendation to subdivide the CCRZ "line" parameter into orientation-specific parameters (ccrz_h, ccrz_v, ccrz_d), enabling the circuit to differentially weight horizontal, vertical and diagonal correlations with only a modest parameter increase. The resulting block uses 14 unique parameters per block (balanced expressivity + efficiency) and should improve line-detection structure and generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
