<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current lightweight, low-parameter block with a symmetry-aware motif-rich block that previously achieved stronger performance. The new ansatz restores:
- grouped RY rotations by corner/edge/center for inductive bias,
- trainable CRZ couplings on outer ring edges (shared) and distinct center-edge CRZs,
- explicit ZZZ three-qubit motifs aligned with the eight winning lines (separated into row/col/diag groups),
- CCRZ doubly-controlled RZ interactions focused on center-involving triples (edge-edge-center and corner-corner-center),
- grouped post-RZ rotations for phase flexibility.

This mirrors a previously successful configuration (13 unique params per block → 78 total parameters given N_UPLOADS=3 and N_REPEATS=2), adding expressive three-qubit motifs that can detect winning-line correlations while keeping parameter efficiency through symmetry sharing. It keeps the existing perimeter CZ entangling structure (unchanged elsewhere) and fits connectivity/validation rules.
