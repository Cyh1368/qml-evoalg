<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce doubly-controlled RZ (CCRZ) motifs on all eight winning lines (sharing a single "ccrz_line" parameter) to directly encode three-in-a-row correlations, and add transverse parametrized CRX interactions across the grid edges ("crx_grid") to provide X-basis entangling power. Restore a small shared pre/post RZ for phase flexibility and keep symmetry-grouped RY rotations for parameter efficiency. Also retain the fixed CZ lattice to ensure stable short-range entanglement. Parameter sharing keeps N_PARAMS comparable to the current candidate while increasing expressive, line-focused interactions that should improve accuracy and generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
