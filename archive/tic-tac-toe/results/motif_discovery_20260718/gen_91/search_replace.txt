<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current ansatz block with a symmetry-aware, motif-aligned compact block that reduces the per-block parameter count (from 16 to 13) while keeping three-qubit motif support. Motif-targeting: ZZZ triples are explicitly aligned with the eight winning lines and CCRZ interactions capture higher-order center couplings. Parameter sharing across corners/edges/center and across ring/center entanglers enforces inductive biases from the tic-tac-toe geometry, improving generalization and parameter efficiency (fewer params, less overfitting) while preserving expressivity through three-qubit motifs. This matches a previously higher-performing specification (78 total params) and should raise validation/test accuracy and reduce train-test gap.
