edit_add_ring_cz_and_center_crx
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce passive CZ entanglement across all outer-ring and radial center edges (no extra parameters), and add a single shared parametrized controlled-X (CRX) coupling from each edge to the center using one shared parameter "crx_center". The CZs give a fixed entangling scaffold that can help triple (ZZZ/CCRZ) motifs act on already-entangled subsystems without increasing parameter count; the shared CRX adds a lightweight, symmetry-respecting controllable non-commuting coupling to strengthen center-edge interactions while keeping parameter efficiency high. All two-qubit gates respect grid adjacency and we keep the winning-line ZZZ and CCRZ motifs plus compact symmetry-shared rotations. This aims to improve expressivity and training speed with minimal parameter overhead.
</DESCRIPTION>
