<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce motif-aware three-qubit ZZZ interactions placed exactly on the eight tic-tac-toe winning lines to allow the circuit to directly detect triple correlations. Reduce parameter count and encourage symmetry/generalization by grouping single-qubit rotations: corners, edges, and center share RY parameters; pre- and post-RZ use a single shared parameter each. Add parametrized CRZ gates between the center (8) and its four adjacent edges to enable controlled phase conditioning of the center on edge qubits; the CRZs share a single parameter. Keep the CZ entangling lattice intact.

Rationale:
- ZZZ on winning lines gives an inductive bias for the task (detect three-in-a-row) without massively increasing parameter count by sharing the same "zzz_win" across lines.
- Parameter sharing across symmetric qubit roles (corners vs edges vs center) reduces N_PARAMS and improves parameter_efficiency and generalization while retaining expressive power via repeated re-uploading and repeated blocks.
- CRZs from center↔edges add useful conditional interactions focused on the central qubit, important for many win patterns.
- Keeps local CZ lattice to maintain short-range entanglement.
This change reduces N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK from 27 -> 7 (N_PARAMS from 162 -> 42), while adding ZZZ motifs aligned to domain structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
