<DESCRIPTION>
Add parameter-efficient controlled rotations that emphasize the center cell's influence and share parameters across symmetric motifs. Specifically:
- Keep the existing symmetry-grouped RY and ZZZ winning-line motifs and the CZ grid entangling layer.
- Add a single shared parametrized CRZ applied on each center-adjacent edge (1-8, 3-8, 5-8, 7-8) to let the center modulate edge phases; all four share one parameter "crz_center_edge" to remain parameter-efficient.
- Add two doubly-controlled RZs (CCRZ) targeting the two diagonal winning lines, but share a single parameter "ccrz_diag_shared" for both diagonals to encode diagonal three-body control with minimal extra parameters.

This increases per-block expressivity in a symmetry-aware and parameter-efficient way (adds 2 unique parameter keys -> modest total parameter increase) to help the circuit learn center-mediated patterns and diagonal triple interactions without introducing many free parameters that raise overfitting risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
