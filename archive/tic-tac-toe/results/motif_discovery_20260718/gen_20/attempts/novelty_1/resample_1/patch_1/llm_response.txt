<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce doubly-controlled RZ (CCRZ) interactions that target the center qubit conditioned on the two outer qubits of each winning line that passes through the center.  Concretely, add four CCRZ gates (one per center-containing winning line) that share a single parameter "ccrz_center".  This implements a low-parameter, geometry-aligned three-qubit conditional phase intended to strengthen the circuit's sensitivity to three-in-a-row patterns involving the center (the high-leverage triples).  The change is minimally invasive (one extra parameter per block) but adds targeted non-linear capacity that often improves validation/test accuracy and reduces the train-test gap for this tic-tac-toe classification task.
</DESCRIPTION>
