add_shared_final_ry_readout
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Append a compact "readout" layer of symmetry-grouped RY rotations at the end of the ansatz block.
To avoid increasing parameter count (which hurts parameter efficiency), the new RY gates reuse the existing
symmetry-grouped parameter keys (ry_corner, ry_edge, ry_center). Re-applying these shared RY rotations
just before the next upload/repetition increases representational flexibility and a simple nonlinear
mapping to the Z expectations without changing the per-block parameter budget. This is a low-risk,
structure-aware modification (matches the board symmetries) intended to nudge validation/test accuracy
and reduce the train-test gap while keeping N_PARAMS unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
