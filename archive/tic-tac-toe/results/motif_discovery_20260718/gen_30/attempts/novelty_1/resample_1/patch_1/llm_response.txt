<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce symmetry-grouped pre/post RZ phases (corners/edges/center), keep shared RY rotations, restore a fixed CZ lattice for robust short-range entanglement, add two shared CRZ parameters for the two orthogonal edge–center axis pairs, and place targeted ZZZ three-qubit motif rotations on the eight winning lines with separate parameters for horizontal/vertical/diagonal lines.  

Rationale:
- The explicit pre/post RZ degrees of freedom let each block adjust local phases before and after entangling layers, increasing expressivity without exploding parameter count.
- The fixed CZ backbone stabilizes short-range correlations (low variance entangling backbone).
- Parametrized CRZ center-edge couplings with two symmetry-aware keys let the circuit learn different center-edge interactions along orthogonal axes while remaining parameter-efficient.
- ZZZ motifs aligned to winning lines provide inductive bias for three-in-a-row structure (motif-discovery), with distinct parameters per orientation so the model can represent orientation-specific signatures.
This combination preserves locality constraints, uses parameters efficiently, and is designed to improve validation/test accuracy and generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
