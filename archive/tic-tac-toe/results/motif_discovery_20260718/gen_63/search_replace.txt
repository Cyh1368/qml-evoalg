<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses ZZZ (MultiRZ) for winning lines and CCRZ for center-related triples. This edit replaces the generic ZZZ motifs with CCRZ (doubly-controlled RZ) gates specifically targeting the third qubit in every winning line. CCRZ is a more powerful non-linear gate for boolean-like logic (if A and B are set, rotate C), which map directly to the "two-in-a-row" logic of Tic-Tac-Toe. I also simplify the parameter sharing to focus on the geometric roles (rows, columns, diagonals) and ensure the center qubit (8) is properly integrated into the line logic.
</DESCRIPTION>
