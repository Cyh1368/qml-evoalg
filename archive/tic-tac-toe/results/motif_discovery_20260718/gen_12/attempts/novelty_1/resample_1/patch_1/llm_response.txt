<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the seed EfficientSU2-style block with a more parameter-efficient, motif-aware ansatz.

Rationale:
- Introduce parametrized three-qubit ZZZ rotations placed exactly on the eight winning Tic-Tac-Toe lines to give the circuit direct capacity to represent "three-in-a-row" correlations (motif discovery).
- Add parametrized CRZ gates between the center and four orthogonal neighbors (1,3,5,7) to emphasize center-edge control correlations (important in gameplay).
- Reduce per-block parameter count by removing many independent pre/post RZs and replacing them with two shared single-qubit RZ parameters ("rz_shared" and "rz_final") that act uniformly across qubits. This reduces N_PARAMS while preserving expressivity via shared global phases.
- Keep independent RY per qubit to preserve local single-qubit expressivity where needed.
- Maintain the CZ entangling lattice (all grid edges) to preserve connectivity and mixing.
- Parameter sharing (single rz_shared, crz_center, zzz_line, rz_final) encourages motif alignment, reduces overfitting risk, and increases parameter efficiency, which should help both test accuracy and generalization.

The new block lowers N_PARAMS per block (from 27 to 13), halving total parameters while adding explicit motif-targeted triple interactions. This aims to improve test accuracy and reduce generalization gap and training time.
</DESCRIPTION>
