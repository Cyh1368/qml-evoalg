<DESCRIPTION>
Augment the existing CCRZ "winning-line" motif with collective three-qubit ZZZ rotations placed on the eight tic-tac-toe winning lines. To avoid increasing the per-block parameter count I reuse the existing CCRZ parameter names ("ccrz_line" and "ccrz_center") so the new collective rotations are parameter-shared with the already-present line-conditioned rotations. This injects an additional, symmetry-aligned nonlinear observable (MultiRZ on triplets) that is well suited to detecting three-in-a-row patterns while remaining parameter-efficient. I place the ZZZ motifs right after the CCRZ layer so they can act together with the conditional rotations before the grid CRZs and the final local rotations.
</DESCRIPTION>
