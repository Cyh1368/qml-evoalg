<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce a symmetry-aware yet slightly richer ansatz: group local rotations by corners/edges/center for parameter efficiency, keep the CZ lattice, add two distinct shared CRZs for vertical/horizontal center-edge couplings, and convert the bottom and right winning-line ZZZ motifs into doubly-controlled RZs (CCRZ) while keeping a shared ZZZ parameter for the remaining winning lines.

Motivation:
- CCRZ on the bottom and right lines tests whether selective controlled three-qubit phases improve detection of those less-central patterns (as suggested).
- Sharing a single zzz parameter across most winning lines biases the circuit to look for three-in-a-row correlations while keeping total parameter count modest.
- Splitting CRZ into vertical/horizontal shared params allows mild asymmetry (helpful for board topology) without exploding parameter count.
Expected effect: improves representational alignment to tic-tac-toe winning triples while retaining parameter efficiency, aiming to raise validation/test accuracy and reduce train-test gap.

</DESCRIPTION>
