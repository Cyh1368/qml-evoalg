symmetry_shared_zzz_crz_spokes
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce parameter count and improve inductive bias by:
- Sharing single-qubit rotation parameters according to board symmetries (corners/edges/center) to regularize and improve generalization.
- Grouping three-qubit ZZZ motif parameters by line type (rows/columns/diagonals) so the ansatz explicitly encodes winning-line motifs but with fewer parameters.
- Replacing the bespoke CCRZ gates with a small set of shared CRZ "spoke" rotations connecting center spokes; CRZs are adjacent and give directional coupling to the center while using one shared parameter.
- Keeping grid CZ entanglement but reordering slightly so motifs and entanglement interact naturally.
Overall this reduces N_PARAMS substantially (from 25->10 per block), improving parameter efficiency and reducing overfitting while preserving motif-discovery capacity by explicitly allocating ZZZs to winning lines.
</DESCRIPTION>
