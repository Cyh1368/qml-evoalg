<DESCRIPTION>
The previous iteration demonstrated that symmetry-shared parameters (corners, edges, center) significantly improve parameter efficiency and generalization. This new candidate refines that approach by:
1. **Subdividing Motif Parameters**: Instead of a single 'zzz_line' parameter, it uses 'zzz_row', 'zzz_col', and 'zzz_diag'. This allows the model to learn that horizontal, vertical, and diagonal winning patterns contribute differently to the final classification (e.g., center-crossing diagonals are often more critical).
2. **Asymmetric Entanglement**: Replaces the uniform 'crz_center_edge' with 'crz_center_edge_v' and 'crz_center_edge_h'. This distinguishes between vertical and horizontal information flow to the center qubit (wire 8), which is the primary output for the 'draw' class.
3. **Structured Rotation Layers**: Maintains the pre- and post-entanglement symmetry groups but ensures independent parameters for the post-layer to allow the circuit to "undo" or "refine" the state after the complex non-local ZZZ interactions.
