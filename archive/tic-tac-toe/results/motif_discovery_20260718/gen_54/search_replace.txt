<DESCRIPTION>
Restore a previously high-performing motif: symmetry-grouped local rotations plus explicit winning-line ZZZ motifs and split CCRZ center interactions, with shared CRZ ring/center-edge couplings. This configuration balances expressive three-qubit line-sensitive interactions (ZZZ) with efficient parameter sharing across geometric symmetry groups and dedicated center-edge CCRZ couplings. It increases per-block parameters back to the effective ~13-key motif (matching prior best runs) to improve validation/test accuracy while keeping parameter efficiency via symmetry sharing.
