Replace the current ansatz block with a motif-aware block that previously showed strong performance: symmetry-grouped single-qubit rotations (RY, RZ, RX) to preserve geometric inductive bias, CCRZ motifs aligned with winning lines to expose three-qubit line structure, and a parameterized CRZ grid with a separate center coupling. The design deliberately keeps parameter sharing by symmetry (corners / edges / center) to remain parameter-efficient (13 unique params per block → 78 total parameters) while adding CCRZ line motifs and local RX/RZ flexibility that improved validation/test accuracy in prior runs. This reduces depth/gate complexity compared with the current CZ-heavy backbone and targets the tic-tac-toe line structure directly to improve generalization.
