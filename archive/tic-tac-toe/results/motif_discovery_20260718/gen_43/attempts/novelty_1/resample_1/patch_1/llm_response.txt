<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed CZ lattice backbone with trainable CRZ couplings on the same grid edges (shared parameter "crz_ring"). This (1) makes the outer-ring entanglement strengths learnable, improving expressivity and optimization, (2) modestly increases the per-block parameter count from 12 to 13 (total N_PARAMS from 72 -> 78), matching previously successful parameter budgets, and (3) preserves the targeted center-edge CRZs (so center edges keep the extra tunable coupling). The change is conservative (only swapping CZ->CRZ with a shared param) and respects adjacency/connectivity constraints.
</DESCRIPTION>
