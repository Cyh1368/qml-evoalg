edit_with_zzz_winning_line_motifs
A reorganized ansatz that reinstantiates explicit ZZZ winning-line motifs and compressed CCRZ symmetry groups inspired by motif-discovery.  This block groups single-qubit rotations by corner/edge/center, applies shared CRZ ring + center couplings, places ZZZ triple rotations aligned with the eight winning lines to directly capture three-in-a-row correlations, and adds compressed CCRZ high-order correlations.  Final compact RZ+RX rotations are shared per symmetry zone to keep parameter count efficient.  This architecture previously improved validation/test accuracy and kept parameter efficiency and depth reasonable.
