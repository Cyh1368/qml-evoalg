<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce parameter count and inject inductive biases that previously improved performance:
- Introduce shared pre- and post-RZ symmetry groups (corners/edges/center) so each block can perform a small phase-twisting before and after entanglement.
- Keep light-weight shared RY rotations per symmetry group.
- Retain the CZ lattice backbone for stable entanglement.
- Add shared parametric CRZ "spokes" between center and its neighbors (efficient center coupling).
- Replace the many line-specific ZZZ parameters with a single shared "zzz_line" motif applied to all eight winning triples to encourage motif discovery while keeping parameter efficiency.
This matches a previously successful motif: lower unique parameter count (improves parameter efficiency and generalization) while preserving expressive, game-aligned three-qubit interactions and center-edge couplings.
</DESCRIPTION>
