Key ideas:
- Increase expressive, domain-aligned three-qubit motifs while keeping per-block parameters moderate by splitting the previous single shared ZZZ parameter into three targeted keys: zzz_row, zzz_col, and zzz_diag. This gives the circuit flexibility to treat horizontal, vertical and diagonal winning triples differently, which should help line detection and class separation.
- Preserve the stable CZ lattice backbone for information propagation and keep lightweight symmetry-grouped local rotations (pre/post RZ and RY) to regularize and reduce overfitting.
- Keep a single shared CRZ coupling from center to edges (crz_center_edge) to let the center modulate surrounding qubits while remaining parameter-efficient.
- Add a separate CCRZ for diagonals (ccrz_diag) to provide controlled diagonal-phase sensitivity.
- Target parameter budget per block to a moderate level (14 parameters per block), giving an overall parameter count that balances expressivity and trainability across the repeated / re-uploaded blocks.

Expected improvement mechanisms:
- Splitting ZZZ into row/col/diag allows the optimizer to emphasize features useful for different winning patterns rather than force all lines to use the same phase.
- CCRZ on diagonals complements ZZZ diagonal rotations, giving a complementary controlled-phase mechanism for diagonals (helps discriminate diagonal wins).
- Maintaining symmetry-grouped local rotations preserves inductive bias for the board geometry while keeping parameter count small to reduce overfitting and improve generalization.
- The CZ lattice keeps entanglement capacity high without excessive additional parameters.

Hyperparameters and training-related choices remain unchanged (Adam stepsize, epochs, early stopping). The ansatz changes are confined to ANSATZ_SPEC.
