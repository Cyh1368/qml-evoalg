<DESCRIPTION>
The existing ansatz uses Z-basis interactions (ZZZ, CCRZ, CRZ) heavily. While these capture board correlations, the classification task might benefit from non-diagonal interactions that better distribute information across the hilbert space for the final observables. 

This change introduces:
1. `CRX` gates along the grid edges to provide a different entanglement basis compared to the uniform `CRZ` ring.
2. An additional symmetry-grouped `RX` layer to allow the circuit to rotate into a more expressive basis before the final Z-measurements.
3. Re-organizes the `CCRZ` and `ZZZ` logic to ensure all eight winning lines are represented by `ZZZ` motifs while `CCRZ` focuses on center-correlated 'fork' patterns.
</DESCRIPTION>
