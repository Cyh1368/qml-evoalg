edit_restore_winning_line_motifs
Aims: restore a geometry-aware, parameter-efficient ansatz that previously showed stronger performance. This edit reintroduces line-aligned three-qubit ZZZ motifs, compressed CCRZ center/diagonal correlations, shared CRZ ring + center couplings, and compact final RX+RZ rotations per symmetry zone. The design balances expressivity (explicit winning-line triples) with parameter sharing (symmetry groups) to improve generalization and accelerate training while keeping the parameter budget at the previously well-performing 78 total parameters (13 block keys × 6 uploads/repeats).
The replacement returns to the earlier, higher-performing ANSATZ_SPEC: grouped RY initial rotations, CRZ grid entanglers, ZZZ winning-line motifs, CCRZ high-order correlations, and compact per-zone final rotations (RX+RZ).
