<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current ansatz block with a symmetry-aware, motif-focused block that matched the stronger prior performer. The new block:

- Groups single-qubit RZ/RY rotations by corner/edge/center symmetry (parameter sharing) to reduce parameter count while preserving expressivity.
- Adds a small shared pre-RZ layer and shared post-RZ layer to give flexible phase adjustment around the entangling backbone.
- Keeps the CZ lattice backbone for stable short-range entanglement.
- Introduces parametric CRZ couplings between center and its four adjacent edges (shared parameter) to emphasize center-edge correlations.
- Places ZZZ collective three-qubit rotations on each of the eight winning lines, all sharing a single parameter to bias the ansatz toward three-in-a-row motifs while keeping parameter count low.

This design balances expressivity (targeted 3-qubit motifs + center-edge controlled phases) with parameter efficiency (symmetry sharing and single shared motif parameter), reproducing a previously successful configuration that improved validation/test accuracy with modest parameter growth (n_params_per_block -> 11, total N_PARAMS -> 66).
