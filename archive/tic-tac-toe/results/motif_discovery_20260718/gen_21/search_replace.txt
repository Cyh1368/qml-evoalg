<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce parameter count while preserving expressive, game-aligned interactions.
This edit:

- Collapses local single-qubit rotations into symmetry groups (corner/edge/center).
- Introduces symmetry-grouped pre- and post-RZ layers (corner/edge/center) to give controllable local phases before/after entanglement.
- Keeps the CZ lattice for stable short-range entanglement.
- Adds a single shared parametric CRZ for center-edge couplings to highlight center role.
- Places motif-discovery ZZZ interactions on all eight winning lines but shares a single parameter ("zzz_line") across them to bias the circuit to detect three-in-a-row structure while keeping parameter count small.

This yields 11 unique parameters per block (-> 11 * N_UPLOADS * N_REPEATS = 66 total params), matching a previously successful motif that improved validation/test accuracy and parameter efficiency while reducing overparameterization relative to the current 84-param block.
</DESCRIPTION>
