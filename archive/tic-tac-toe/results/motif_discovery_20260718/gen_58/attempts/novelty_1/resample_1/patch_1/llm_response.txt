<DESCRIPTION>
This ansatz improves performance by introducing non-commuting X-basis correlations into the grid entanglement layer. While the previous version relied heavily on Z-basis interactions (CRZ, ZZZ, CCRZ), this candidate adds a shared-parameter CRX layer on the same grid edges. This allows the circuit to capture non-trivial basis transformations between the feature map (RX) and the measurement (Z), effectively increasing the expressive power of the entanglement without significantly increasing the parameter count. We also refine the symmetry grouping of the three-qubit ZZZ motifs to better distinguish between orthogonal winning lines (rows vs columns) and diagonals, which are geometrically distinct on the 9-qubit grid.
