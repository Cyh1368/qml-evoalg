# Analysis and Proposed Improvements

## Problem Diagnosis

The current EfficientSU2 ansatz achieves **0.58 combined score** with:
- Test accuracy: 0.59 (low)
- Validation loss: 2.25 (high)
- Generalization gap: 0.05 (acceptable, but losses are high)
- **162 parameters** across 3 uploads × 2 repeats = 27 parameters per block

The high validation loss (2.25) and moderate test accuracy indicate the ansatz struggles to learn effective decision boundaries for tic-tac-toe classification.

## Key Insights

1. **Underutilization of three-qubit gates**: The motif-discovery extension allows ZZZ and CCRZ gates on ANY three qubits, but the seed doesn't use them. Tic-tac-toe has 8 winning lines with 3-qubit correlations—these three-qubit interactions can directly encode win-line patterns.

2. **Limited expressivity**: Pure single-qubit rotations + adjacent two-qubit gates may not capture the ternary decision boundaries well. Three-qubit interactions enable higher-order entanglement and more complex feature spaces.

3. **Parameter sharing opportunity**: Instead of unique parameters for all 27 qubits × pre/post rotations (54 params), we can share parameters across symmetric qubits (corners, edges, center) to reduce parameters while preserving expressivity.

4. **Win-line structure**: Directly encode correlations on the 8 winning lines using ZZZ gates. This is a domain-aware inductive bias.

## Proposed Solution

Replace the EfficientSU2 block with a **hybrid architecture**:
- **Layer 1**: Reduced pre-entanglement RZ with parameter sharing (corners share, edges share, center separate) → 3 unique params
- **Layer 2**: Single-qubit RY (9 params, no sharing to allow flexibility)
- **Layer 3**: Adjacent CZ entanglement (fixed, 12 gates)
- **Layer 4**: Post-entanglement RZ with sharing (3 unique params)
- **Layer 5**: Three-qubit ZZZ interactions on all 8 winning lines (8 params) to directly capture win-line correlations
- **Layer 6**: Optional CCRZ for selective double-controlled rotations on high-value triples (2-3 params)

**Total per block**: ~25-26 params (slight reduction from 27), but with much higher expressivity via three-qubit gates.
