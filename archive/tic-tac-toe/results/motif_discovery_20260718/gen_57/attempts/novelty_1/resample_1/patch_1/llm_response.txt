<DESCRIPTION>
I propose a targeted ansatz modification that modestly increases expressivity where it matters and reduces optimization burden where parametrization is redundant.

Changes:
- Add shared RX rotations (rx_corner, rx_edge, rx_center) alongside the existing symmetry-grouped RY rotations. These give each symmetry group a second-axis rotation and increase single-qubit expressivity with only three extra parameters per block.
- Replace the parametrized outer-ring CRZ entanglers with fixed CZ gates. This removes the shared crz_ring parameter (reducing a potentially noisy global phase parameter) while preserving entangling structure; fixed CZs often stabilize training and reduce parameter redundancy.
- Split the single zzz_row grouped parameter into three separate per-row ZZZ parameters (zzz_row_0/1/2). This allows the ansatz to learn distinct 3-body motifs for each physical row (useful since the three row lines are geometrically distinct in the paper indexing), while keeping columns and diagonals grouped to limit parameter growth.
- Keep the center-edge CRZ, CCRZ motifs, and post-RZ symmetry-grouped rotations as before.

Rationale:
- Adding RX complements existing RY/RZ flexibility and often improves trainability by allowing richer local rotations without exploding parameter counts.
- Replacing ring CRZs with fixed CZs preserves entanglement connectivity but reduces the number of trainable parameters and the optimizer's burden over a repeated ring structure.
- Splitting row ZZZs gives the circuit the ability to specialize to different row-winning motifs, a small, targeted increase in expressivity that is likely useful for tic-tac-toe motif detection.

Net effect: modest increase in per-block parameters (from 13 to 17) and overall parameters (to 102), targeted expressive gains, and better parameter efficiency/stability that should improve validation/test performance without overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
