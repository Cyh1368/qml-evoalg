<DESCRIPTION>
Remove redundant center-edge CRZ entries that were duplicated twice in the block: the crz_ring backbone originally included the four center-edge links ([1,8], [3,8], [5,8], [7,8]) and the block then re-applied separate crz_center_edge gates on the same wires. This produced duplicated two-qubit gates on the same wires (increasing gate_count and depth) while adding little extra representational benefit. The edit removes the center-edge links from the crz_ring backbone so that the four center-edge CRZs appear only once (under crz_center_edge). This reduces circuit depth and gate count, removes redundant entangling that can hurt training stability/generalization, and keeps the parameterization (and therefore N_PARAMS) unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
