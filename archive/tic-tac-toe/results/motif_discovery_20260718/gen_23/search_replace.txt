<DESCRIPTION>
Add lightweight parametric mixing on the center "spokes" (CRX) and introduce orientation-specific doubly-controlled RZ (CCRZ) gates across all eight winning lines. The CCRZ gates act as conditional rotations that target the middle cell of each line (controls = endpoints, target = middle), using three orientation-specific parameters (row/col/diag). This preserves symmetry-sharing within orientations while giving the circuit more expressive, motif-sensitive nonlocal interactions to detect three-in-a-row patterns. CRX on the center spokes complements the existing CRZ spokes by introducing amplitude mixing conditioned on the center, improving trainability. Parameter sharing is used deliberately (one crx_spoke, three ccrz_* keys) to keep per-block parameter count modest while increasing targeted expressive power for motif discovery.
</DESCRIPTION>
