post_motif_cz_add_and_edge_rz_prune
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Add a symmetry-grouped post-motif pairwise entangler layer of CZ gates immediately after the ZZZ motif layer to reinforce pairwise phase coupling following the three-body motifs. To keep parameter and capacity budgets controlled (and reduce overfitting risk), remove the final RZ on the four edge qubits (rz_post_edge). The added CZs are fixed (non-parameterized) so they increase entangling structure without increasing the parameter count; pruning rz_post_edge reduces the per-block parameter count, improving parameter efficiency and helping generalization. This small structural change encourages motif-formed three-body interactions to be consolidated by pairwise phases along the grid edges while slightly lowering N_PARAMS.
</DESCRIPTION>
