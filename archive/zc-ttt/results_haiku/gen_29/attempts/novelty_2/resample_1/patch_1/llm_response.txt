<DESCRIPTION>
This approach draws inspiration from the recommendation to split the global pre-RZ parameter into zone-specific parameters, combined with insights from the prior programs' success with parameter sharing and efficiency.

Key observations from context programs:
1. Gen 1 achieved 0.58 score with 120 params and good generalization (0.09 gap)
2. Gen 2 achieved 0.59 score with only 114 params and excellent generalization (0.06 gap)
3. Current program has 162 params with poor generalization (0.13 gap), suggesting overfitting

The novel approach implements a **dual-zone phase tuning strategy**:
- Split pre-entanglement RZ into two parameters: "rz_pre_a" for qubits 0-4 (lower zone) and "rz_pre_b" for qubits 5-8 (upper zone)
- This mirrors Gen 2's efficiency while providing finer phase control across the circuit
- Reduce parameter count by removing redundant CRZ gates and consolidating three-qubit interactions
- Maintain strategic two-qubit entanglement on allowed pairs only
- Use parameter sharing more aggressively to improve generalization

This design balances expressivity with parameter efficiency, targeting the 0.06 generalization gap seen in Gen 2 while maintaining validation accuracy above 0.63.
