This new approach takes a fundamentally different algorithmic strategy:

1. **Hierarchical Entanglement Structure**: Instead of uniform two-qubit gates, organize the circuit into nested entanglement clusters - local clusters first (adjacent or nearby qubits), then cross-cluster interactions. This reduces redundancy and improves information flow.

2. **Aggressive Symmetric Parameter Tying**: Beyond sharing single parameters globally, tie gate parameters by their role in the circuit architecture. 3. **Basis Rotation Efficiency**: Replace the expensive alternating RX/RY double encoding with a single efficient basis that adapts via shared parameters. This reduces parameter count while maintaining expressivity through strategic parameter reuse.

4. **Controlled Three-Qubit Density**: Rather than fixed three-qubit placements, use CCRZ (controlled-controlled rotations) more strategically on key control qubits, reducing the overall gate count while maintaining non-linear expressivity.

5. **Sparse Entanglement Geometry**: Focus two-qubit entanglement on the connectivity graph's bipartite structure to reduce redundant interactions and improve generalization.

This approach prioritizes parameter efficiency and generalization over raw expressivity, targeting the core weaknesses (gap, parameter count) shown in the current metrics.
