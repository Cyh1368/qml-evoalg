<DESCRIPTION>
This ansatz replaces selected fixed CZ interactions with trainable CRZ gates while retaining a connected fixed-entanglement backbone. Two shared CRZ parameters separately control hub-centered and peripheral interactions, adding adaptive entanglement with only two extra parameters per repeated block. Parameter sharing limits overfitting and optimization cost while allowing the circuit to tune phase correlations that fixed CZ gates cannot adapt to the task.
</DESCRIPTION>
