tie_redundant_rz
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Share each qubit’s pre- and post-entanglement RZ parameter. Because every entangler is CZ, all intermediate RZ gates commute with the entangling layer; the two independent RZ angles on each wire therefore contribute only through their sum and are exactly overparameterized. Tying these pairs preserves the effective circuit family while reducing the block from 27 to 18 independent parameters, improving parameter efficiency and conditioning while lowering overfitting risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
