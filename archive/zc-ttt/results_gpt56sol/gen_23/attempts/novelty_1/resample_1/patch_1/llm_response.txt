<DESCRIPTION>
This ansatz returns to the shallower, fully fixed CZ interaction graph while introducing only moderate parameter sharing. The RY rotations on wires 1 and 7 share one parameter, and wires 2 and 6 share another; all remaining RY and RZ rotations stay independent. This reduces the block from 18 to 16 unique parameters without sacrificing the phase flexibility that was lost under broader RZ sharing. Removing the extra repeated RZ layer and trainable CRZ gates also lowers depth, gate count, and overfitting pressure relative to the current 132-parameter circuit.
