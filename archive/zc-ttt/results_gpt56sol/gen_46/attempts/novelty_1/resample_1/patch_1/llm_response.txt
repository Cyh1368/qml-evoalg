<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed CZ interactions on edges (1,8) and (4,8) with independently trainable CRZ gates. This preserves the complete interaction graph while allowing the optimizer to adjust two midrange phase couplings that connect qubit 8 to distinct branches. The targeted increase in expressivity avoids the larger depth and parameter cost of adding gates.
