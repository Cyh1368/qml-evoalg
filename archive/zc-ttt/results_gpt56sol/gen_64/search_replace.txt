mid_rx_matching_mixer
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Reorder the commuting CZ network into disjoint matchings and insert a symmetry-shared RX layer between the second and third matchings. The improved scheduling lowers entangling depth, while the noncommuting mid-circuit rotations prevent the entire CZ network from behaving like one phase-only layer. Sharing RX parameters across graph-related wires adds only six independent parameters per block, providing a capacity increase smaller than a full independent rotation layer and helping preserve generalization and parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
