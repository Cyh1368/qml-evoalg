<DESCRIPTION>
Remove both RZ layers because they commute with every intervening CZ gate, making the pre/post parameterization redundant and poorly conditioned. A repeated RY-plus-CZ ansatz still provides noncommuting local mixing and full connectivity while reducing the total trainable parameters from 162 to 54. This should improve parameter efficiency, convergence, and generalization while substantially reducing gate count and depth.
</DESCRIPTION>
