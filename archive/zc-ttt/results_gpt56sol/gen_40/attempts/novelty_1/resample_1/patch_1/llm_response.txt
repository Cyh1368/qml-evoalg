<DESCRIPTION>
The editable parameters are the nine independent RY amplitudes, seven effective RZ phase parameters due to sharing on wires (1,4) and (5,6), and the interaction topology. Training-loop settings such as learning rate and iteration count are fixed externally. This version preserves the current 16-parameter budget and gate count but replaces the fixed CZ(3,5) edge with a trainable ZZZ interaction on the symmetry-connected triad (1,3,8). The targeted higher-order correlation increases expressivity without adding depth through an extra gate or worsening parameter efficiency, while the existing phase sharing continues to regularize peripheral qubits.
