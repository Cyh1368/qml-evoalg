remove_commuting_rz_redundancy
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the post-entanglement RZ layer. Every CZ gate is diagonal in the computational basis and therefore commutes with RZ rotations. Consequently, each post RZ can be moved across the CZ network and merged with the corresponding pre RZ, making nine parameters per block functionally redundant. Removing this layer preserves the effective hypothesis class while reducing the total parameter count from 162 to 108, lowering depth and gate count, and likely improving optimization speed, parameter efficiency, and generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
