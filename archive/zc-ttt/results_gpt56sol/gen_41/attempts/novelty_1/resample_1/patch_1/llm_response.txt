<DESCRIPTION>
Uses graph-aware sharing for the RY amplitudes while retaining independent local RZ phases. The over-parameterized controlled interactions and repeated phase layer are replaced by a single CRZ hub interaction at [0,7]. The remaining graph edges are organized into disjoint interaction layers, reducing parameter count, gate count, and effective depth while preserving full graph connectivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
