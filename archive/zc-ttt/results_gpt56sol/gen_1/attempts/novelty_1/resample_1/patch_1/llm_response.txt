remove_commuting_redundancy
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the pre-entanglement RZ layer because every RZ commutes with the intervening CZ network, making the two RZ layers largely redundant and unnecessarily expensive. Convert the remaining post-entanglement rotations to RX so they do not commute with CZ. This yields a compact RY–CZ–RX hardware-efficient block, reducing the independent parameter count by one third while improving useful state mixing, circuit depth, convergence, and regularization.
</DESCRIPTION>
