<DESCRIPTION>
This design keeps the proven full CZ connectivity while correcting the main weakness of the current circuit: the repeated post-entanglement RZ layer commutes with CZ and therefore adds depth without useful noncommuting structure. The replacement uses a tied RY layer after entanglement, reusing the original per-wire RY parameters. This creates a genuinely noncommuting rotation–entanglement–rotation sandwich without increasing parameter count.

The RZ parameters are shared across mirrored wire pairs (0,8), (1,7), (2,6), and (3,5), with an independent center parameter for wire 4. This reduces each block from 18 to 14 independent parameters while retaining wire-specific RY control, targeting better parameter efficiency and generalization without discarding the connectivity that produced the strongest prior validation result.
</DESCRIPTION>
