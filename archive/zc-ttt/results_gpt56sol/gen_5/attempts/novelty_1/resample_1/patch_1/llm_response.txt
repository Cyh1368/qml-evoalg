<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the pre-entanglement RZ layer. Every CZ gate is diagonal and commutes with RZ rotations, so each pre/post RZ pair on a wire is equivalent to a single RZ whose angle is their sum. The removed parameters therefore add no effective expressivity, but increase optimization dimension and depth. This preserves the ansatz’s reachable operations while reducing parameters from 162 to 108 across repetitions, which should improve parameter efficiency, convergence, and generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
