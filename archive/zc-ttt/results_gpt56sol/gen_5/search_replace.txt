<DESCRIPTION>
Share each pre-entanglement RZ parameter with the corresponding post-entanglement RZ parameter. Because RZ commutes with CZ, the two rotations are equivalent to one rotation with twice the shared angle, retaining full angular reach while eliminating nine redundant parameters per block. This reduces optimization dimensionality and should improve parameter efficiency and convergence without removing circuit operations.
</DESCRIPTION>
