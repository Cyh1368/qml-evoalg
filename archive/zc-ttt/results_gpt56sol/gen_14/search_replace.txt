<DESCRIPTION>
Replace four disjoint fixed CZ interactions with independently parametrized CRZ gates. This preserves the successful sparse connectivity and circuit size while allowing the optimizer to tune entanglement strength across eight of the nine qubits. Selecting disjoint pairs limits added depth, and retaining the other eight CZ gates provides a stable entangling backbone. The four additional parameters per block offer a moderate expressivity increase without returning to the overparameterized 162-parameter configuration.
</DESCRIPTION>
