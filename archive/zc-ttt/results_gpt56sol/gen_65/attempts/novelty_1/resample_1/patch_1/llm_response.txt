<DESCRIPTION>
Restructures the ansatz into two entanglement stages separated by a symmetry-tied RY–RZ mixer. The intermediate noncommuting rotations prevent the CZ network from collapsing into one commuting phase layer, increasing expressivity while parameter sharing across graph-related qubits limits capacity and improves parameter efficiency.
