# GLOBAL INSIGHTS SCRATCHPAD

The following are global insights about optimization approaches and their effectiveness:

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns  
- Symmetry-tied phase parameters (Generation 16, “Symmetry-Tied RY-RZ CZ Ansatz,” combined score 0.58):  
  • Reduced RZ parameters from 108 → 90 by assigning the same `param` name to symmetry-related wires (e.g. wires 1&4 share “rz_14”), cutting parameter count by 17% and narrowing the generalization gap to 0.03.  
  • Achieved the highest validation accuracy (0.58) and combined score by preserving expressivity while pruning redundant single-qubit phases.  
- Single-layer RY + single pre-entanglement RZ front end (Gen 2 baseline and Gen 16):  
  • Both hold circuit depth at 41 and gate count at 207, yielding test accuracy ~0.54–0.55. Adding any extra RZ/RY layer (see Gen 10 or Gen 15) pushes depth ≥47 with no accuracy gain.  
- Full allowed 12-edge CZ entanglement graph (Gen 2 & Gen 16):  
  • Retaining all 12 CZ interactions preserves connectivity needed for expressivity. Avoiding extra two-qubit gates or long-range CRZ layers (cf. Gen 14’s sparse CRZ block) keeps depth low.  
- Explicit, unique parameter naming within the EVOLVE-BLOCK:  
  • Uniform labels like `{"gate":"RY","wire":i,"param":"ry_i"}` and tied labels (`"rz_23"`) ensure each declared parameter is trained, preventing silent backend drops seen in overlapped or default-named parameters.  

## Ineffective Approaches  
- Post-entanglement rotation layers (Gen 15 “boundary_rx_post_entanglement,” score 0.54; Gen 18 “boundary_rx_post_entanglement,” score 0.54):  
  • Adding RX/RZ mixers after the CZ block increased depth to 53 or 47 and gate count to ≥219, but test accuracy fell to 0.50–0.53 and the generalization gap widened to ≥0.06.  
- Over-parameterized multi-layer RY-RZ or CRZ entanglers (Gen 14 “trainable_sparse_entanglement,” 132 params, score 0.55):  
  • Despite a depth of only 41, the dense parameterization delivered no accuracy gain over leaner designs and parameter efficiency remained zero.  
- Excessive pruning or sharing of entanglers (Gen 17 “prune_weak_1_8_link,” score 0.55):  
  • Removing long-range CZ edges reduced gate count modestly but yielded only marginal score improvements, indicating a risk of under-entangling.  
- Dense CZ connectivity plus boundary mixers without phase tying (Gen 21 “boundary_post_rx,” score 0.56, depth 42):  
  • While train accuracy rose to 0.64, test accuracy dropped to 0.52 and the generalization gap ballooned to 0.12, showing that extra depth without parameter tying overfits.  

## Implementation Insights  
- Centralized ANSATZ_SPEC in the EVOLVE-BLOCK isolates evolutionary changes from measurement/training logic, ensuring experiments only differ in the variational circuit.  
- Symmetry-aware parameter tying is implemented by assigning identical `param` keys to multiple dict entries, e.g.:  
    • `{"gate":"RZ","wire":1,"param":"rz_14"}`, `{"gate":"RZ","wire":4,"param":"rz_14"}`  
  This leverages graph automorphisms with minimal code overhead.  
- Maintaining a single RY layer followed by a single RZ layer before entanglement, as in the current best program, provides sufficient expressivity without inflating depth.  
- Listing the full CZ edge list explicitly in ANSATZ_SPEC (12 entries) avoids hidden default entanglers and keeps gate count exactly at 207, a sweet spot for this problem scale.  

## Performance Analysis  
- The current best (Gen 16) achieves combined score 0.58, surpassing all others: next best is Gen 21 at 0.56, then Gen 14/17 at 0.55, and Gen 15/18 at 0.54.  
- Circuits with depth = 41 and gate_count = 207 consistently score ≥0.57; increasing depth to 47–53 costs ~0.02–0.04 in combined score.  
- Parameter counts of ~90–108 hit the performance sweet spot: Gen 16 (90 params) edges out Gen 2 (108 params) by 0.01–0.02. Over-parameterization (~132) or aggressive pruning (<84) both degrade combined score.  
- Lower generalization gaps (0.03 in Gen 16 vs. 0.06–0.12 in others) correlate strongly with symmetry-tied parameters and avoidance of post-entanglement rotations.
