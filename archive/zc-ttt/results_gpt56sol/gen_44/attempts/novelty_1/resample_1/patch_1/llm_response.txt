<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed CZ interactions on the two peripheral links into qubit 8 with independently trainable CRZ gates. This preserves the full interaction graph while allowing optimization to tune the phase coupling strengths on [1,8] and [4,8]. The targeted addition of only two parameters per repeated block offers more useful expressivity than the current duplicated local RZ layer, while limiting the parameter and depth increase.
</DESCRIPTION>
