<DESCRIPTION>
Add a second graph-complete entanglement stage after a fresh RY–RZ basis-change layer. Parameters are tied across symmetry-related vertices to limit capacity growth and mitigate the current train-validation gap, while the noncommuting rotations between graph passes let the repeated CZ network generate correlations that cannot be obtained by simply repeating commuting phase gates. This tests whether additional structured depth can improve validation accuracy beyond the shallower variants without using unrestricted per-qubit parameters.
