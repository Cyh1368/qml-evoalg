<DESCRIPTION>
Convert the block into a compact real-amplitude architecture: an independent RY layer, graph-constrained CNOT entanglers, and a second independent RY layer. In the current circuit, every CZ commutes with the surrounding RZ gates, making the two nine-parameter RZ layers substantially redundant. Removing one layer, replacing CZ with noncommuting CNOT interactions, and changing the remaining RZ layer to RY should improve trainability and expressive parameter use while reducing the total parameter count from 162 to 108 and lowering gate count and depth.
</DESCRIPTION>
