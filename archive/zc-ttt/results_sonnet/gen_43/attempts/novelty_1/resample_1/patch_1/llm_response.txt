<DESCRIPTION>
The experiment history shows that adding a 4-gate RY "mid-layer" (ry_mid_0..3) between the CRZ entangling block and the final RZ readout layer increased parameter count (162→186) and depth (41→44) but *decreased* combined score (0.63→0.62) and widened the generalization gap (0.09→0.10). This confirms the mid-layer refresh idea does not pay off here — it adds expressivity that the optimizer cannot use efficiently, likely overfitting.

This edit removes the RY mid-layer entirely, reverting to the leaner 162-parameter backbone that empirically generalizes better. To still get some benefit from an extra "refresh" without ballooning parameter count, it reintroduces a very light entangling touch using heavy parameter-sharing: a single shared-parameter CRY on two previously-unused-for-CRY pairs (4,8) and (5,7) reusing already-touched qubits, added right after the existing CRZ block but before the final RZ layer, using only one extra parameter (crz_share). This nudges correlations between the two mid/late clusters at negligible parameter cost (1 param vs 4), preserving efficiency while giving a small expressivity boost.
</DESCRIPTION>
