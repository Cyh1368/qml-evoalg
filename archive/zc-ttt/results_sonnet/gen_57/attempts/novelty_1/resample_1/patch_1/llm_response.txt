<DESCRIPTION>
The seed ansatz (162 params, depth 41, gate_count 207) achieved the best combined score of 0.63, while later variants that added extra mid-layer rotations or merged final RZ parameters actually hurt validation accuracy and increased depth (44) without benefit. This version returns to the proven seed structure (same RY encoding, same CRZ connectivity/parameter-sharing pattern, same individual final RZ phases) but restructures the 12 CRZ gates into two commuting-friendly sublayers as recommended: Sublayer A operates on qubits {0,2},{1,3},{2,4},{3,5},{5,7} and Sublayer B on {0,3},{0,7},{0,8},{1,8},{2,6},{4,8},{6,7}. This reordering groups gates that share fewer immediate qubit dependencies earlier, allowing the transpiler/simulator to schedule more gates in parallel, reducing circuit depth (~39) while keeping the exact same 162 parameters, 207 gates, and entanglement structure that gave the strongest generalization (0.63 combined, 0.09 gap). The goal is to preserve accuracy while trimming depth, which should improve trainability/convergence without sacrificing expressivity.
