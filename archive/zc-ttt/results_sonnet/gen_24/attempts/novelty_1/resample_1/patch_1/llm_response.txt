mid_ry_sparse_entangler

<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the best-performing seed (RY init → mixed CRZ/CRY entanglers → RZ post-layer, score 0.60, train 0.75/val 0.70/test 0.63), this variant reallocates trainable capacity from two of the least-central controlled-rotation gates to a lightweight mid-circuit single-qubit RY sublayer. Specifically, we drop CRY[4,8] and CRY[6,7] (the two entangling edges most peripheral to the qubit-connectivity graph, each touching low-degree qubits 4 and 6) and insert five independent RY rotations (ry_mid_0..4) on qubits [1,3,5,7,8] between the entangling layer and the final RZ layer. The intuition: these two CRY edges contributed less to information flow through the graph (qubits 4 and 6 have degree 2 in the allowed-pair graph, and their partner edges from CRZ layer already route signal through qubit 2/3), whereas adding local RY rotations on the high-connectivity/output-relevant qubits gives the model extra expressive single-qubit reshaping right before the final RZ readout rotations, at a similar or lower total gate count while nudging total parameter count only modestly (all params still shared per-block-copy across the fixed repetition count). This should preserve the effective depth (~41) while testing whether trainable single-qubit capacity near the readout improves generalization (smaller train/val gap) without sacrificing training accuracy.
