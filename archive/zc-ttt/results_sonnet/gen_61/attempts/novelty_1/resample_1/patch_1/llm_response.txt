crz_backbone_plus_zzz

<DESCRIPTION>
Crossover of the two best-performing seed ansätze (score 0.63) with the mid-layer-refresh variant (score 0.61). Analysis of the metrics shows: (1) the clean seed structure — RY encoding, a CRZ entangling backbone with light, deliberate parameter sharing across symmetric edges, followed by an *individually parametrized* RZ output layer — outperforms the variant that (a) inserted extra mid-circuit RY refresh rotations and (b) shared the final RZ parameters across wire pairs (0.61 vs 0.63, higher depth/gate-count, worse validation accuracy and gap). So we discard the mid-layer RY refresh and the shared post-RZ scheme, restoring the proven backbone.

To still search for improvement (rather than just reproducing the seed), we add a lightweight three-qubit ZZZ interaction layer that captures higher-order correlations across the two loosely-coupled "sub-clusters" of the entangling graph (qubits {0,4,6}, {1,2,5}, {3,7,8}, chosen to be disjoint and to cover every qubit exactly once). All three ZZZ gates share a single parameter, adding just 1 extra parameter (163 total) while giving the circuit access to triple-qubit correlations that pure two-qubit CRZ entanglers cannot represent, potentially improving expressivity/accuracy without notably increasing depth or parameter count (three-qubit gates are unrestricted in wire choice per the schema). The final individually-parametrized RZ output layer (9 unique params) is kept intact, since that was the differentiator that made the seed outperform its variant.

This aims to combine the proven strong structure with a minimal, targeted structural addition to explore for further gains in validation accuracy / generalization gap without materially increasing depth or gate count.
