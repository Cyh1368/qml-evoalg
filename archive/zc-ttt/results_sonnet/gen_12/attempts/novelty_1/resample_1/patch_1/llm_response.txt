crz_grouped_midlayer_ansatz

<DESCRIPTION>
Restructured the entangling block to use grouped/shared CRZ parameters (4 shared params covering all 7 CRZ gates instead of 7 independent ones), freeing parameter budget to introduce a lightweight mid-circuit RY rotation sub-layer on qubits [0,2,4,6] (4 new params) right after the entangler and before the final RZ layer. This follows the "sandwich" pattern (single-qubit → entangler → single-qubit) that stabilized training previously, while keeping full 12-pair connectivity (via CRZ groups + fixed CZ gates) and controlling total parameter growth (~26 unique params per repetition vs 25 previously), aiming to boost expressivity on alternate qubits without materially increasing depth or overfitting.
