sparse_ry_sharing_encoding
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce parameter count in the encoding layer by sharing RY parameters across pairs of qubits that are already coupled downstream in the entangling layer: ry_1↔ry_8 (qubits 1 and 8 are directly connected via crz_3) and ry_3↔ry_6 (qubits 3 and 6 are connected indirectly through the hub structure and share similar connectivity roles). This mirrors the successful parameter-sharing philosophy already used in the CRZ entangling layer, cutting encoding params from 9 to 7 (total n_params from 162 to ~160), which should slightly improve parameter efficiency and possibly reduce overfitting (train-test gap) without significantly harming expressivity, since the shared qubits play structurally symmetric roles in the circuit.
</DESCRIPTION>
