ry_refresh_layer

<DESCRIPTION>
Add a lightweight RY "refresh" layer on the highest-centrality qubits (0, 2, 3, 7 — hub and cluster-connector nodes with 3+ edges in the allowed topology) inserted between the existing CRZ entangling block and the final RZ phase layer. This introduces only 4 new gates and 4 new parameters (ry_mid_0..3), keeping the parameter budget low while adding a mid-circuit local mixing step. The rationale: after the CRZ entanglers act on these hub qubits, their state has accumulated entanglement-dependent phase; inserting an RY rotation lets the model locally re-mix amplitude/phase information on the qubits most involved in information flow before the final RZ readout-phase layer, potentially improving expressivity without significantly increasing overfitting risk (small parameter increase, no added two/three-qubit gates).

</DESCRIPTION>
