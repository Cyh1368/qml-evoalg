Building on the previous best-scoring ansatz (score 0.59, 144 params, depth 41), this version follows the specific recommendation to convert the static CZ gate on the peripheral (5,7) edge into a trainable CRZ gate ("crz_6"). This modestly increases the number of trainable entangling parameters (from 6 to 7 CRZ gates) while leaving the circuit depth and overall topology unchanged. The intuition: CRZ gates on entangling pairs add expressivity for capturing pairwise correlations in the readout, and the (5,7) edge previously carried a fixed (non-trainable) CZ, meaning that interaction could not be tuned during training. Making it trainable lets the optimizer adapt this connection's strength jointly with the rest of the ansatz, which should help push validation accuracy higher (or keep it stable) without increasing depth, since CRZ and CZ have the same gate footprint in this circuit's depth counting. This keeps parameter count low (still efficient) while directly testing whether extra trainable entanglement on a previously fixed edge improves the combined score, generalization gap, and convergence.

- Total distinct params per block: 9 (RY) + 7 (CRZ) + 9 (RZ_post) = 25, vs. previous 24, expected total params ~150 (25*6 reps) assuming same repetition count, still well within efficient regime and depth unchanged at 41.
