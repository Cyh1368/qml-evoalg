<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce parameter count by sharing rotation angles between symmetric leaf-leaf CRZ gates that act on structurally analogous edges of the connectivity graph. Specifically, [4,8] (a leaf pair mirroring [3,5]) now shares "crz_5" instead of its own "crz_9", and [6,7] (a leaf pair mirroring [5,7]) now shares "crz_10" instead of its own "crz_11". This keeps gate ordering, wires, depth, and gate_count identical while cutting n_params from 162 to 160, testing whether tighter parameter sharing preserves or improves the current 0.63 combined score and generalization gap by reducing overfitting risk without losing expressive entangling structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
