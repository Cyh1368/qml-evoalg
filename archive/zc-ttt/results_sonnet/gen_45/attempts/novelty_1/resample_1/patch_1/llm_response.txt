crz_param_reduction_with_shared_mid_ry

<DESCRIPTION>
Based on the two prior results, fewer parameters in the entangling (CRZ) layer correlated with a better combined score and smaller train/val gap (162-param CRZ-only version scored 0.63 vs. 186-param version with an extra RY mid-layer scoring 0.62, and the 156-param CRY-mixed variant scored only 0.59). This suggests: (1) revert to a pure CRZ entangling layer (drop the CRY gates that hurt performance), and (2) push parameter sharing further in the CRZ layer to cut unique parameters from 9 to 6, reducing total trainable parameters and depth pressure while retaining full connectivity across the allowed qubit pairs. To recover some of the expressivity lost from a shallower parameter space, we reinsert a lightweight RY "refresh" layer on qubits [1,3,5,7] right after the CRZ block, but reuse the existing ry_1/ry_3/ry_5/ry_7 parameter names instead of introducing new ones — this adds interference/expressivity from composing rotations around the same generator without any extra parameter cost or significant depth increase (only 4 extra gates, 0 new params).
