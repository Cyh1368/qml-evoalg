<DESCRIPTION>
The 0.63-scoring seed variant (RY layer -> CRZ layer with crz_0/crz_1/crz_2(shared over [0,7],[0,8])/crz_3(shared)/crz_4(shared)/crz_5/crz_9/crz_10/crz_11 -> RZ layer) outperformed the current 0.61-scoring "4-sublayer" variant. I revert the topology to that better-performing structure, and additionally apply the recommended extreme hub-collapse: all four qubit-0-incident CRZ edges ([0,2],[0,3],[0,7],[0,8]) now share a single parameter "crz_hub0" instead of three separate parameters (crz_0, crz_1, crz_2). This further reduces parameter count (from 162 to ~150 given the repetition count) while preserving the entangling connectivity and depth/gate_count, testing whether stronger symmetry around the qubit-0 hub narrows the generalization gap and improves parameter efficiency without hurting accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
