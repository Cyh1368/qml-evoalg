<DESCRIPTION>
The previous variant that replaced several CRZ gates with plain CZ gates degraded performance (score dropped from 0.63 to 0.59), confirming that the parametrized CRZ entanglers carry useful trainable expressivity that fixed CZ gates lack. This edit reverts those CZ gates back to the original shared-parameter CRZ gates (restoring the better-performing entangling structure), and additionally inserts a lightweight RY "refresh" layer on the four highest-connectivity hub qubits (0, 2, 3, 7) between the entangling CRZ block and the final RZ phase layer. This mid-circuit local rotation lets the circuit re-mix amplitude information on the most heavily entangled qubits right after they've accumulated phase from multiple CRZ interactions, before the final RZ readout-phase layer, potentially improving expressivity locally without adding significant depth or parameter overhead (only 4 extra params per repetition block).
</DESCRIPTION>
