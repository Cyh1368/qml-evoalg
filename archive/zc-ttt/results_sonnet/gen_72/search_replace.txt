<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the encoding layer's parameter count by sharing RY feature-rotation parameters across pairs of qubits that are already entangled together downstream (1&8 via crz_3, and 3&6 which are both targets of crz_1/crz_4 chains respectively). This compresses the encoding from 9 independent parameters to 7, lowering total n_params from 162 to 160, while testing whether the shared structure (feature qubits that later interact) preserves expressivity. Since qubits 1/8 and 3/6 already end up correlated through the entangling layer, tying their initial rotation angles is a natural low-risk way to cut parameters without breaking the circuit's information flow, potentially improving parameter efficiency and reducing overfitting (train-test gap) while maintaining accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
