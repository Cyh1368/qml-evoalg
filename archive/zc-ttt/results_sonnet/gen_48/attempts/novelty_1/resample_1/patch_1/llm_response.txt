The current program replaced several CRZ entanglers with plain CZ gates, which hurt performance significantly (score dropped from 0.63 to 0.60, train/val/test accuracy all dropped ~4-8%). This confirms that parametrized CRZ entanglers on qubits (0,8), (1,8), (2,6), (5,7), (6,7) are important for this task and should not be replaced by fixed CZ gates. I'm reverting to the seed's full CRZ entangling structure (with parameter sharing on 0-hub, 1-3-8, and 2-4-6 clusters) which achieved the best score of 0.63.

On top of restoring the entangling layer, I apply the recommended post-rotation parameter sharing on the final RZ layer, merging symmetric qubit pairs (0↔8, 1↔7, 2↔6, 3↔5) while leaving qubit 4 (the center) independent. This reduces RZ params from 9 to 5, cutting total parameter count from 162 to 158 without changing depth or gate count, testing whether compressing redundant end-stage phase parameters improves parameter efficiency and generalization while preserving the accuracy gains from the full CRZ entangling structure.
