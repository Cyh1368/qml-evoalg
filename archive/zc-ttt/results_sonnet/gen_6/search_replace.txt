add_trainable_entanglement_reduce_redundancy
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The original block used RY -> RZ(pre) -> fixed CZ entangling layer -> RZ(post). The pre-CZ RZ layer is largely redundant since RY followed immediately by RZ is just a two-parameter single-qubit rotation that gets undone in expressivity terms by the post-CZ RZ layer anyway (both act before/after a fixed non-parametric entangler). Removing the pre-RZ layer frees up parameter budget without losing much expressivity, since the RY+post-RZ pair already spans the relevant single-qubit rotation axes needed given a subsequent trainable entangling stage.

To compensate and to genuinely improve entangling expressivity (currently entanglement is completely non-parametric, limiting the circuit's ability to learn correlations relevant to the task), I convert half of the fixed CZ gates into trainable CRZ gates. This gives the ansatz the ability to learn the *strength* of entanglement between some qubit pairs while keeping the other half as fixed CZ for computational efficiency and to preserve some inductive bias/structure.

Net effect: parameter count drops from 162 (27 params/rep * 6 reps) to 144 (24 params/rep * 6 reps), and part of that freed budget goes into more expressive trainable entanglement. This should help reduce the train-test gap (fewer redundant single-qubit params, so less overfitting) while improving expressivity via learnable entangling gates, hopefully improving validation/test accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
