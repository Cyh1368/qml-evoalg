mid_ry_sandwich_ansatz

<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the best-performing prior variant (score 0.60, 25 params/block with a balanced mix of parametrized CRZ entanglers on informative edges and cheap fixed CZ entanglers on the remaining edges), this version inserts a lightweight mid-block RY rotation layer on qubits [0, 2, 4, 6] (4 new parameters) between the entangling layer and the final RZ layer. This follows the sandwich pattern (single-qubit → entangler → single-qubit) that stabilized training previously, while keeping the two-qubit gate/parameter count unchanged (avoiding the overfitting seen when all edges were promoted to CRZ, which dropped score to 0.57). The added RY layer increases expressivity cheaply (single-qubit gates, low parameter cost, minimal depth increase) and specifically targets the qubits that are hubs of the entangling structure (0, 2, 4, 6 each participate in multiple two-qubit gates), potentially helping the model separate features more effectively before the final RZ phase layer, without significantly increasing overfitting risk.
