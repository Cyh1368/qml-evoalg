add_shared_ccrz_triples

<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz only captures pairwise correlations between qubits (via CRZ on the 12 allowed edges). To let the circuit model higher‑order (three‑body) correlations that no single two‑qubit gate pair can express, I insert three disjoint CCRZ (controlled‑controlled‑RZ) gates on triples of qubits that are not directly connected by any allowed two‑qubit edge: (0,4,6), (1,5,7), (2,3,8). These three triples partition all 9 qubits and are mutually disjoint in wires, so they can be executed in parallel, adding essentially only one extra layer of depth rather than three. All three CCRZ gates share a single parameter name ("ccrz_0"), so only one new trainable parameter is introduced (162→163), keeping the parameter budget essentially unchanged while enriching the expressivity of the ansatz. This is placed after the two-qubit entangling sublayers and before the final local RZ phases, so the newly created three-body correlations are read out through the following single-qubit rotations. This should help close the generalization gap and lift both validation and test accuracy without meaningfully increasing circuit depth or parameter count.
</DESCRIPTION>
