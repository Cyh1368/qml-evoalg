<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz swapped several CRZ entangling gates for fixed CZ gates and a CRX gate, which increased circuit depth (59 vs 41) without any parameter benefit, and empirically dropped combined score from 0.63 to 0.58 (lower train/val/test accuracy, higher losses). The earlier variant that used only parametrized CRZ gates with deliberate parameter sharing (crz_2 shared for (0,7)/(0,8), crz_3 shared for (1,3)/(1,8), crz_4 shared for (2,4)/(2,6)) achieved the best score so far (0.63) with fewer parameters (162) and shallower depth (41). This edit reverts the entangling block to that better-performing, parameter-efficient, shallower structure, removing the CZ/CRX substitutions and restoring full CRZ coverage with shared parameters, while keeping the same RY encoding and RZ post-rotation layers.
</DESCRIPTION>
