<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the current best-performing "sandwich" layout (RY encoding -> CRZ entanglers -> RZ post-phases), I add a minimal three-qubit correlation block using two ZZZ gates right after the CRZ entangling layer and before the final RZ phases. The two ZZZ gates connect qubits {0,2,4} and {3,5,7}, chosen to bridge across the existing CRZ hub structure (0 is a hub connected to 2,3,7,8; the second triad covers the 3-5 and 5-7 chain already entangled pairwise). This introduces higher-order (three-body) correlations that pairwise CRZ gates cannot capture, potentially improving expressivity without a large parameter cost (+2 params, +2 gates). Since ZZZ gates are unrestricted in wire choice, this is a low-risk, low-cost way to probe if multi-qubit correlations help closesocial the train-test gap and improve validation/test accuracy, consistent with the recommendation given.
