revert_and_add_rx_shared_layer

<DESCRIPTION>
The sublayer-parallelized CRZ decomposition (currently in the file) reduced depth from 41→39 but also reduced validation/test accuracy (0.63→0.61), suggesting that the original strictly-sequential CRZ ordering builds up more useful correlations during training (perhaps due to its particular entangling schedule interacting well with the classical optimizer). We therefore revert the entangling block to the original, better-performing sequential CRZ ordering from the seed (score 0.63) and additionally introduce a lightweight expressivity boost: right after the RY encoding layer, we add an RX rotation on every qubit that reuses the *same* parameter name as the corresponding RY gate (e.g. RX(wire=0, param="ry_0")). This adds no new trainable parameters (parameter-sharing keeps n_params fixed at 162) while giving each qubit's state a combined RY+RX rotation, exploring a richer subspace of single-qubit states before entanglement. This should improve the representational power of the encoding without increasing parameter count or hurting generalization, and it restores the higher-performing entangling schedule.
</DESCRIPTION>
