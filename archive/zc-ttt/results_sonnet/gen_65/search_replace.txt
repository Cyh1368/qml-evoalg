<DESCRIPTION>
Apply symmetric parameter sharing to the final RZ layer: rz_post_i shares its parameter with rz_post_{8-i} for i=0..3, leaving rz_post_4 as the unique center parameter. This reduces the RZ layer from 9 unique parameters to 5, cutting total parameter count by 4 (186→182) while preserving circuit depth and gate count. The idea is that the trailing local phase layer likely encodes some qubit-symmetric structure (mirrored roles of qubit pairs relative to the hub qubit 0), so tying mirrored phases together should reduce overfitting/gap without harming expressivity much, consistent with the recommendation to test if compressing end-stage phases tightens the generalization gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
