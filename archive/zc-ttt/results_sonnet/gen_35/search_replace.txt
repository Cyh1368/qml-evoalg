<DESCRIPTION>
The previous experiment (0.63 score) without the three-body ZZZ layer outperformed the current version (0.58) that added it, indicating the ZZZ layer increases depth/params without benefit and likely causes overfitting/harder optimization. This edit removes the ZZZ correlation layer (reverting to the better-performing entangling structure) and instead inserts a lightweight RY mid-layer on qubits [1,3,5,7] between the CRZ block and the final RZ layer. This "RY→CRZ→RY→RZ" sandwich adds only 4 new parameters/gates, giving a cheap, targeted amplitude refresh on a subset of qubits that may help convergence and expressivity without the depth/parameter cost of the ZZZ interactions.
