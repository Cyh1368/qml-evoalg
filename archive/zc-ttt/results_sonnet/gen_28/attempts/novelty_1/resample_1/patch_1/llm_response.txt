parallel_crz_layers_reorder

<DESCRIPTION>
The 12 CRZ entangling gates are currently issued in the fixed textual order of the allowed-pair list, which creates an artificial serial dependency chain on qubit 0 (used in 4 consecutive gates) and other qubits, inflating circuit depth even though many of these gates act on disjoint qubit pairs and could execute in parallel. I computed a proper edge-coloring of the entangling graph formed by the 12 (control,target) pairs: node 0 has degree 4 (the max degree in the graph), so at least 4 "layers" are needed, and a valid 4-coloring exists that groups gates with completely disjoint qubits into the same layer. Reordering the CRZ gate list to follow this coloring (grouping non-conflicting gates back-to-back: {[0,2],[1,3],[4,8],[5,7]}, then {[0,3],[1,8],[2,4],[6,7]}, then {[0,7],[2,6],[3,5]}, then {[0,8]}) preserves the exact same gate set, parameter names/sharing, and gate count, while giving the ASAP depth-scheduling logic the best chance to compact the circuit into 4 dependency layers for this block instead of a long serial chain. This is a low-risk, structure-preserving change targeting reduced depth (and thus faster/more stable training) without any accuracy trade-off, consistent with the parallelization recommendation.
</DESCRIPTION>
