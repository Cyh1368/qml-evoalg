<DESCRIPTION>
This redesign improves the ansatz architecture by introducing a more diverse and structured entanglement strategy. Key structural changes:

1. **Modular layer composition**: Instead of a single linear chain of CZ gates, the design now uses alternating and cross-coupled entanglement patterns that activate different qubit pairs, maximizing quantum correlations with fewer gates.

2. **Reduced parameter overhead**: By using fixed two-qubit gates (CNOT) in strategic positions and parametrized single-qubit rotations (RY/RZ), we reduce the total parameter count while maintaining expressivity.

3. **Hierarchical qubit connectivity**: The circuit now features both nearest-neighbor and cross-layer entanglement (e.g., qubits 0-4, 1-5, etc.), allowing the circuit to explore richer feature spaces without parameter proliferation.

4. **Optimized parameter sharing**: Parameters are shared across symmetric qubit positions (pairs like 0-7, 1-6, etc.), reducing redundancy and improving generalization on small datasets.

5. **Faster convergence**: The new connectivity pattern and reduced depth should allow the optimizer to find good solutions faster by reducing local minima traps and improving gradient flow.

The target is to maintain perfect accuracy while reducing gate count, parameter count, and convergence steps, thereby improving parameter efficiency and overall training efficiency.
