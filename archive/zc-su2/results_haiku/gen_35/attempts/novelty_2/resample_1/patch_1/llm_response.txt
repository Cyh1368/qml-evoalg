<DESCRIPTION>
The current program achieves perfect accuracy (1.0) but uses 14 parameters with high depth and gate count. The recommendation suggests replacing fixed CZ gates with parametrized CRZ gates to give the optimizer direct control over entanglement angles.

This fundamentally different approach replaces the entire linear chain architecture with a more compact **alternating CRZ ladder** that:
1. Uses parametrized CRZ gates instead of fixed CZ gates, reducing effective parameter count through selective control
2. Employs alternating qubit pairs (0-1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-7 in one layer, then 1-2, 3-4, 5-6 in the next) for better entanglement coverage
3. Minimizes single-qubit rotations by using only strategic RY rotations on even qubits
4. Drastically reduces circuit depth and gate count while maintaining accuracy

The alternating pattern allows fuller connectivity with fewer gates, enabling faster convergence and better parameter efficiency. By making entanglement parametrized rather than fixed, the optimizer gains the flexibility needed for small datasets.
