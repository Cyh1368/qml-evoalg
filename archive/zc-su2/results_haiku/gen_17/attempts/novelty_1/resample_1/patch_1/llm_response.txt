# Analysis

Looking at the performance metrics:

1. **Prior programs**: Both achieved 0.68–0.72 combined score with perfect accuracy (100% train/val/test) and excellent generalization (0.00 gap). They used only **14–20 parameters**.

2. **Current program**: Score is 0.64 (lower), despite perfect accuracy. It has **38 parameters** (nearly 3× more), which hurts parameter efficiency significantly (0.03 vs. 0.07 in the best prior).

The current design introduced alternating `ry_a`/`ry_b` parameters and Ising gates (XX/YY alternating), but this added complexity without improving accuracy—only harming efficiency.

## Strategy

The recommendation suggests testing **RX in place of RY** as the shared single-qubit axis while keeping the proven RZ sandwich and CZ chain structure from the best prior (0.72 score). The second-best prior achieved 0.72 with only **14 parameters** by using a **single shared RZ** pre/post sandwich around CZ gates.

I will:
1. **Restore the minimal, high-efficiency design**: single shared RZ pre-sandwich + CZ chain + single shared RZ post-sandwich
2. **Test RX instead of RY** as the central rotation axis (per the recommendation)
3. Keep total parameter count low (~2 parameters) to maximize efficiency

This balances the proven structural success of the second prior with the axis-choice exploration recommended.
