<DESCRIPTION>
The two previous 1-parameter designs (pure RY/RZ single-scalar and the RX+RY+CRZ+RZ single-"theta" design with ring+antipodal CZ entanglement) both achieved the best combined score (0.80) with only 8 total trainable parameters, while the current 2-parameter design (ry_global/rz_global) regressed to 0.72 with almost double the parameters (14) despite identical accuracy. This confirms that parameter economy dominates the score here and that adding a second independent parameter name hurts rather than helps.

This edit collapses the current ansatz back to a single shared parameter "theta" reused across both the pre-entanglement RY layer and the post-entanglement RZ layer (matching the top-scoring 1-parameter designs), while also upgrading the entangling layer from a plain ring to a ring + antipodal-pair CZ topology. The antipodal edges give every qubit an additional long-range entangling partner at zero parameter cost, which in the second seed reduced convergence_step from 240 to 60 while keeping the same accuracy and score. Combining the single-parameter economy with the faster-converging entangling topology should recover the 0.80 score while also improving convergence speed relative to the original 1-parameter seed.
</DESCRIPTION>
