<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the strongest prior architecture (global RY + ring-CZ entangler + global RZ, which scored 0.72 with only 14 trainable parameters), this edit further reduces parameter count relative to the current 32-parameter candidate while adding two collective single-qubit rotations recommended for capturing global biases: a global RX applied to all qubits before the RY layer, and a second global RZ applied to all qubits after the existing RZ layer. The RX-before-RY pair is not redundant since RX and RY on the same qubit do not commute, giving each qubit an effective 2-parameter single-qubit rotation before entanglement — strictly more expressive than RY alone at a cost of only one extra shared parameter. The extra post-RZ layer models any residual global phase/bias that the measurement stage might be sensitive to, at the cost of one more shared parameter. Total unique parameters per repetition rises from 2 (in the 0.72-scoring seed) to 4, still far fewer than the current 32-parameter design, while retaining the ring entangler that worked well previously.
