<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the 3-parameter design (ry_global, xx_ent, rz_global) with a leaner 2-parameter design as suggested: a single shared rotation parameter "theta1" drives all single-qubit RY (pre) and RZ (post) rotations across all 8 qubits, while a second shared parameter "theta2" drives a fixed-topology entangling layer of parametrized CRZ gates placed on the antipodal (distance-4) and skip-2 (distance-2) links. This reduces the per-block unique-parameter count from 3 to 2 (a ~33% cut), while the CRZ entangler (versus the mixed XX/CZ layer) gives every qubit two non-local parametrized entangling partners plus two additional fixed-free skip-2 CRZ links, preserving expressivity needed to entangle the whole register with minimal parameters. This directly tests whether a slightly richer, but still highly economical, entangling layer can restore the higher combined score (0.80) seen in the 1-parameter seeds while still allowing some flexibility beyond a single global scalar.
</DESCRIPTION>
