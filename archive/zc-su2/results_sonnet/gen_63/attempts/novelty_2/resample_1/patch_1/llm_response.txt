<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce a second shared trainable parameter "theta_e" used across a lightweight skip-2 sub-ring of parametrized CRY gates (i -> i+2 for even i). This adds expressive, non-local, tunable entangling interactions at the cost of only one extra parameter (n_params goes from 8 to 16, since each block still shares theta and theta_e), while keeping the fixed antipodal CZ shortcuts and existing structure intact. The added CRY layer should improve expressivity/generalization beyond the pure-CZ skip topology used previously, and reusing "theta_e" across all skip-2 links keeps parameter count minimal (2 unique names per block).
</DESCRIPTION>
