xx_yy_entangler_ring</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed, zero-parameter CZ gates in the nearest-neighbor entangling ring with a parametrized YY(theta) gate (reusing the same shared "theta" parameter used everywhere else). This turns the ring into an alternating XX(theta)/YY(theta) pattern instead of XX(theta)/CZ. Since XX and YY are non-commuting Ising interactions, alternating them under the same shared parameter enriches the entangling structure and mixing power of the circuit without adding any new trainable parameters (still just 1 parameter, "theta"). This directly extends the successful alternating-entangler motif that gave the best combined score (0.80) and fastest convergence in prior single-block variants, now applied inside the compact block-building function used for both stacked repetitions.
</DESCRIPTION>
