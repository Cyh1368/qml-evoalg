<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the best-performing prior variant (global RY/RZ rotations with a closed CZ ring, score 0.72, 14 params), this edit collapses the current group-based rotations to a single global RY/RZ pair (like the winning seed) and additionally injects tunable two-qubit interaction strength by replacing every second CZ in the ring with an "XX" Ising gate sharing one new parameter "xx_ent". This keeps the parameter count extremely low (3 unique parameters per repetition instead of 3 group params or more), while giving the entangling layer parametric flexibility that a pure CZ ring lacks, which should help both expressivity and generalization from few examples.
</DESCRIPTION>
