<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed, zero-parameter CZ gates on the odd-indexed ring edges with parametrized CRX gates that reuse the same shared "theta" parameter already used everywhere else in the block. This keeps the parameter count fixed at 1 (per repetition) while turning every ring edge into a tunable, non-commuting entangling interaction (XX on even edges, CRX on odd edges), which should enrich the entangling dynamics of the ring without any extra trainable parameters, potentially improving convergence speed and expressivity while preserving parameter economy.
</DESCRIPTION>
