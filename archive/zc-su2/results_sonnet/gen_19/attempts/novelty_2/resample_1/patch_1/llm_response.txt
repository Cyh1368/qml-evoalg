<DESCRIPTION>
Revert to the more parameter-efficient 2-group RY/RZ sharing scheme (matching the best-performing seed: p_g0 for wires 0-3, p_g1 for wires 4-7), which achieved the top combined score (0.72) with only 14 params, shallow depth (29), and fast convergence (30 steps) versus the current 4-group (26-param) version that scored lower (0.66).

On top of that efficient backbone, apply the recommended enhancement: replace every second CZ in the entangling chain (links [0,1],[2,3],[4,5],[6,7]) with a parametrized XX rotation sharing a single new "xx_ent" parameter. This adds only 1 extra trainable parameter (15 total) while injecting tunable two-qubit interaction strength beyond the fixed CZ's fixed-angle entanglement, potentially improving expressivity/accuracy without meaningfully increasing depth or hurting convergence speed. The remaining links [1,2],[3,4],[5,6] stay as fixed CZ gates to preserve full-chain connectivity at zero extra parameter cost.

This combines the best-known parameter-efficient rotation-sharing pattern with a minimal-cost, physically motivated entangler upgrade.
