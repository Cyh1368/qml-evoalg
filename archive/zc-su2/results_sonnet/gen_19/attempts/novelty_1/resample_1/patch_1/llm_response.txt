global_rot_xx_ring_entangler

<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the best-performing global-rotation ring ansatz (score 0.72, 14 params), I inject a small amount of tunable two-qubit interaction strength into the entangling layer while keeping the ultra-low parameter count that drove the parameter-economy score. Instead of an all-fixed CZ ring, every second link (0-1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-7) becomes a parametrized XX(θ) gate sharing a single new parameter "xx_ent", while the remaining ring links (1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7-0) stay as fixed, parameter-free CZ gates. This keeps connectivity, depth and gate count essentially unchanged (just swapping some fixed 2-qubit gates for parametrized ones of the same locality) but adds one extra trainable degree of freedom per block repetition, giving the circuit a tunable entangling strength that can be learned from very few examples rather than being fixed at maximum entanglement. Combined with the two global single-axis rotation parameters (RY before, RZ after entangling), this keeps the total parameter count small (3 unique names per block, ~21 total across repetitions) while allowing the optimizer to modulate both single-qubit phase/amplitude and the strength of two-qubit correlations, which should help match or exceed the 100% train/val/test accuracy of the seed while potentially converging faster and generalizing at least as well, and scoring better on parameter efficiency than schemes with more named parameters.
