<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the ansatz to exactly two shared trainable parameters: "theta1" drives every single-qubit rotation (RX, RY, RZ layers), and "theta2" drives a parametrized entangling layer (CRZ) that is interleaved with a fixed CZ ring. This is a compromise between the 1-parameter seed (score 0.80, but potentially under-expressive) and the 26-parameter current version (score 0.66, over-parametrized). Giving the entangler its own free angle, separate from the single-qubit rotation angle, lets the circuit learn distinct rotation vs. entanglement strengths while keeping parameter count minimal (n_params=2), which should improve parameter_efficiency and maintain high accuracy/generalization, boosting the combined score.
</DESCRIPTION>
