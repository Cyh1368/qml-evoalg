<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the proven 2-parameter group-sharing RY/RZ backbone, we replace the open CZ chain with a closed ring (adding the wrap-around edge 7→0 for full symmetric connectivity) and replace every second edge in that ring with a shared-parameter XX(θ) rotation ("xx_global"). This keeps the entangling layer almost parameter-free (only 1 new trainable parameter added to the existing 2 group parameters) while giving the circuit a tunable, globally shared two-qubit interaction strength that can help it separate classes non-trivially beyond what fixed CZ alone provides. The ring topology also removes the asymmetry between end qubits (0 and 7) and interior qubits present in the open chain, which should aid generalization from very few training examples.
</DESCRIPTION>
