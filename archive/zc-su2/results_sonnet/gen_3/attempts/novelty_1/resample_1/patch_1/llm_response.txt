param_sharing_pairwise_reduction
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses 24 independent parameters per repetition (8 RY + 8 RZ_pre + 8 RZ_post), giving 146 total trainable parameters while already achieving perfect train/val/test accuracy. Since the score heavily rewards parameter economy and the task is apparently easy enough to reach 100% accuracy, we can cut the parameter count roughly in half by sharing rotation angles across adjacent qubit pairs (0,1), (2,3), (4,5), (6,7) for each of the RY, RZ_pre, and RZ_post layers. This preserves the same circuit structure, connectivity, and depth (so expressivity from entanglement is unchanged), but reduces the number of unique trainable parameters per repetition from 24 to 12, which should substantially raise parameter_efficiency while keeping accuracy high due to the apparent simplicity of the underlying task/encoding.
</DESCRIPTION>
