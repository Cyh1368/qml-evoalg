<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz repeats the entire theta-block twice (once globally, once restricted to wires 0-3), which roughly doubles depth and gate count (138 vs 72) without any measurable score improvement (both score 0.80, identical accuracy/convergence). Since parameter economy and low depth/gate-count are explicitly rewarded, this redundant second pass is pure overhead. I remove the extra fractional block to return to the leaner single-block depth (~72), and instead inject the recommended additional non-commuting RY(theta) rotation layer in the middle of the entangling topology (between the skip-2 sub-ring and the antipodal shortcuts) — this adds expressivity via an extra rotation axis at the heart of the entangler stack without introducing any new trainable parameter (it reuses "theta"), and without the depth/gate cost of a full duplicated block. This should preserve the 1.0 accuracy and 8-parameter economy while significantly cutting depth and gate count, improving the efficiency-sensitive part of the score.
