<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses a linear nearest-neighbor CZ chain (0-1,1-2,...,6-7), leaving qubits 0 and 7 disconnected. Closing this into a ring by adding a final CZ(7,0) gate creates a cyclic entangling topology, allowing information to flow between the two ends of the chain without adding any trainable parameters (CZ is parameter-free). This increases circuit expressivity and connectivity at essentially zero parameter cost, which should help maintain or improve accuracy/generalization while keeping the excellent parameter economy (still 26 params) that already scores well.
</DESCRIPTION>
