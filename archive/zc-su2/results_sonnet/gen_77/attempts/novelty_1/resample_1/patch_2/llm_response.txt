<DESCRIPTION>
The previous experiment showed that appending a small localized "mini-block" on qubits 0-3 (reusing the single shared "theta" parameter) after the main block dramatically improved convergence speed (30 steps vs 60) at a modest depth/gate-count cost, while keeping accuracy at 1.0 and parameter count unchanged. The current version dropped this mini-block in favor of a fully tunable ring (XX/CRX), which reduced depth/gate-count but regressed convergence back to 60 steps. This edit re-introduces the localized half-register mini-block (RX, RY, alternating XX/CZ on the 4-cycle 0-1-2-3-0, then RZ, all sharing "theta") appended after the final RZ layer, aiming to recover the faster 30-step convergence while retaining the more expressive full-ring entangler already present, at a smaller depth overhead than a full second block duplication.
