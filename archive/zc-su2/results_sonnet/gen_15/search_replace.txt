xx_entangler_injection
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace half of the fixed CZ entangling gates (the non-overlapping pairs [0,1], [2,3], [4,5], [6,7]) with parametrized XX rotations sharing a single new parameter "xx_ent". This adds only one extra trainable parameter per block repetition while giving the entangler tunable interaction strength instead of a fixed maximally-entangling CZ, which can help the optimizer find a smoother, more generalizable entangling structure without meaningfully increasing parameter count or circuit depth. The remaining CZ gates ([1,2],[3,4],[5,6],[7,0]) keep the ring closed and preserve full connectivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
