<DESCRIPTION>
The previous variant introduced 5 unique parameters per block (4 RY-pair params + 1 shared CRZ), giving n_params=32 and a lower combined score (0.65) than the simpler 2-parameter designs that reached 0.72. This suggests the extra RY granularity added parameter cost without accuracy gain. 

Here we collapse the four RY-pair parameters into a single global RY parameter (as in the best-performing seed), keep the fixed nearest-neighbor CZ chain for full connectivity, retain a single shared CRZ parameter for long-range (cross-register) entanglement to preserve extra expressivity beyond the pure-CZ seed, and add a global RZ layer to mix axes cheaply. This yields only 3 unique parameters per block (down from 5), directly improving parameter economy while keeping an entangling CRZ layer that the 2-parameter seeds lacked, which may help capture correlations with fewer training steps and a smaller train/test gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
