<DESCRIPTION>
Add a second collective RY echo while reusing the existing parameter, so the trainable parameter count remains unchanged. A shifted CZ matching connects the previously disjoint qubit pairs into an eight-qubit ring, increasing useful expressivity and gradient pathways. This may retain perfect generalization while reducing loss and improving convergence, without sacrificing parameter economy.
</DESCRIPTION>
