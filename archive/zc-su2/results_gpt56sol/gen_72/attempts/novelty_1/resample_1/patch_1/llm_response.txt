<DESCRIPTION>
This ansatz replaces disconnected repeated CZ matchings with a symmetry-preserving collective echo coupled through a periodic XX Ising ring. Every parametrized gate shares one mode per repeated block, retaining the minimum trainable parameter count while allowing correlations to propagate across all eight qubits. The RY/RZ basis change around the Ising ring provides richer nonlinear dynamics and should preserve perfect accuracy while potentially reducing loss and improving robustness on the small training set.
</DESCRIPTION>
