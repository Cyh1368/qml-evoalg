<DESCRIPTION>
Compress the block from 24 trainable parameters to two by sharing RY angles across alternating qubits. Independent parameters across repetitions still provide layerwise flexibility, while parity sharing regularizes the model and should preserve enough local variation to maintain the already-perfect accuracy. Remove all RZ gates because they commute with the CZ entanglers and create substantial parameter and depth overhead with limited additional value here. Retaining the full CZ chain preserves information propagation across all eight qubits. This substantially improves parameter efficiency, gate count, depth, convergence, and resistance to overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
