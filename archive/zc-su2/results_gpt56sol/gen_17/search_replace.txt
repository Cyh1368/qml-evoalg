<DESCRIPTION>
Extend the shared RY mode to the four previously unrotated qubits. This restores register-wide permutation symmetry and gives every member of each CZ pair direct variational control while retaining a single shared parameter per repeated block. Prior feedback shows that full collective coverage reaches perfect validation accuracy, lowers loss, and converges substantially faster without increasing the independent parameter count.
</DESCRIPTION>
