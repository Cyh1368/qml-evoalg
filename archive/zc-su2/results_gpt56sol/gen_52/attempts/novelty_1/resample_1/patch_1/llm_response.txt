<DESCRIPTION>
This ansatz replaces the single-axis collective rotation with a noncommuting RX–RY collective mixer. Both axes share the same parameter, preserving the minimum one trainable degree of freedom per repetition and therefore the existing parameter economy. The additional tied RX layer creates a richer one-dimensional transformation that may improve optimization speed and loss without increasing the reported parameter count, while the proven parallel CZ matching retains stable entanglement and perfect generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
