<DESCRIPTION>
This design preserves the proven collective RY mode and fixed CZ matching, then adds a complementary ring-closing CRZ matching. Unlike simply introducing another independent entangling parameter, the CRZ gates reuse the collective rotation parameter, retaining the same parameter economy while creating register-wide connectivity and a richer one-dimensional optimization trajectory. The tunable phase layer is intended to improve convergence without sacrificing the perfect generalization or increasing the number of independent trainable parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
