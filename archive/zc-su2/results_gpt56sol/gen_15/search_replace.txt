<DESCRIPTION>
Reorder the existing chain CZ gates into two disjoint matchings and add the missing [7,0] edge. This closes the interaction graph into a ring, gives every qubit balanced nearest-neighbor connectivity, and allows each matching to execute in parallel. The trainable parameter count remains unchanged while circuit depth is reduced relative to the serially ordered chain.
</DESCRIPTION>
