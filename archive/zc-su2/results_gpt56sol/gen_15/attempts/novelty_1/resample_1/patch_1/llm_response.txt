<DESCRIPTION>
Use a symmetry-complete collective RY transformation across all eight qubits, sharing one parameter within each repeated block. A single parallel CZ matching then supplies low-depth entanglement. Unlike the current sparse rotation scheme, every encoded qubit participates in the trainable transformation. Prior feedback shows this structure reaches perfect validation accuracy, lowers loss, closes the generalization gap, and converges four times faster while retaining the minimum observed trainable-parameter count.
</DESCRIPTION>
