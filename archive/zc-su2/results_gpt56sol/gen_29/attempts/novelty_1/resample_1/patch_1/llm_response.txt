<DESCRIPTION>
Split the shared RY layer into two register-wide collective modes: one for qubits 0–3 and one for qubits 4–7. This retains strong parameter sharing while offering more flexibility than a single global rotation. The unnecessary trainable XX layer is removed, reducing gate count and depth relative to the current program. A parallel CZ matching preserves economical entanglement across all qubits.
</DESCRIPTION>
