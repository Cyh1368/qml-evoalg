<DESCRIPTION>
Compress the shared single-qubit stage to one collective RY layer, then replace the controlled-phase stitching with two sequential XX matchings. All parametrized gates retain the same shared parameter, so parameter economy is unchanged while gate count is reduced from 24 to 20 gates per block. The complementary XX layers provide tunable local and ring-wide correlations and may preserve perfect accuracy with faster, smoother convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
