<DESCRIPTION>
Share one collective RY parameter across all eight qubits to maximize parameter economy and reduce overfitting. Use a single parallel CZ matching to entangle the entire register at minimal depth. This structure previously achieved perfect train, validation, and test accuracy with zero generalization gap, while improving convergence from 90 to 60 steps and reducing depth and gate count.
