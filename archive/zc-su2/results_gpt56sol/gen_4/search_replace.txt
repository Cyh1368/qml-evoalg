<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the 24-parameter block with a real-amplitude, symmetry-aware ansatz using only two trainable parameters per repetition. Even and odd qubits share RY angles, providing more flexibility than a single global angle while strongly regularizing the model for few-shot generalization. Removing the two RZ layers eliminates redundant phase parameters and lowers depth, gate count, and convergence burden. Closing the CZ chain into a ring preserves global information propagation and avoids privileging endpoint qubits. This reduces the block from 24 unique parameters and 31 gates to 2 unique parameters and 16 gates.
</DESCRIPTION>
