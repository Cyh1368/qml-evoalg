<DESCRIPTION>
Add a second collective rotation stage while preserving the same shared parameter, and place a shifted CZ matching between the two stages. The shifted edges connect the four previously disjoint qubit pairs into an eight-qubit ring, enriching inter-pair correlations without increasing the trainable parameter count. The added shared rotation can also recover the faster convergence observed for two-stage designs while retaining perfect generalization and parameter economy.
</DESCRIPTION>
