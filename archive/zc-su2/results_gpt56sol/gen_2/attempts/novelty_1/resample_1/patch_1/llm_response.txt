<DESCRIPTION>
Compress the ansatz from 24 to 4 trainable parameters per repeated block by sharing rotations across even and odd wires. This symmetry-based regularization should preserve useful heterogeneous behavior while substantially improving parameter economy and few-shot generalization. The second RZ layer is removed because RZ gates commute with the intervening CZ network, making separate pre/post parameter sets unnecessarily redundant. The entangling chain is retained to preserve information propagation and classification capacity while reducing gate count and depth.
</DESCRIPTION>
