split_collective_echo
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Turn the tied rotation/phase sequence into a two-angle RY echo. Each register-wide layer remains strongly regularized through sharing, while the post-entanglement layer gains an independent collective angle. This modest increase in expressivity can improve optimization speed and loss without introducing wire-specific parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
