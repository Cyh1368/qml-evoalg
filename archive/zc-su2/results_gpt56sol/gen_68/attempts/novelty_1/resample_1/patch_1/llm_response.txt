<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the disconnected CZ matching with a full nearest-neighbor ring. This propagates information across all eight qubits while preserving the single shared trainable mode and therefore the same parameter count. The improved connectivity may recover the faster convergence observed in deeper variants without introducing additional trainable parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
