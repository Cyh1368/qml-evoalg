<DESCRIPTION>
This ansatz retains a single shared collective parameter, preserving the strongest parameter economy and regularization observed in prior candidates. Two collective RY echoes are separated by complementary disjoint CZ matchings: the first couples even-odd neighbors, while the shifted second matching connects those pairs into an eight-qubit ring. This expands information propagation across all qubits without introducing additional trainable parameters, while keeping the block shallower and smaller than the earlier three-echo designs. The complementary topology is intended to preserve perfect accuracy and generalization while improving convergence.
