<DESCRIPTION>
This design replaces separate collective rotations and fixed entanglers with a single parallel layer of parametrized XX interactions. Each XX gate simultaneously mixes and entangles its qubit pair, compressing the role of the prior RY–CZ stages into one operation. All four interactions share one parameter, preserving the minimum trainable parameter count while imposing a strong symmetry that should aid few-shot generalization. Because the disjoint gates can execute in parallel, the ansatz substantially reduces gate count and depth and may converge faster without sacrificing register-wide coverage.
</DESCRIPTION>
