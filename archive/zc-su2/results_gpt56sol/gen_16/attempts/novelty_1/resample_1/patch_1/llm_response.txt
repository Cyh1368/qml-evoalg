<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the even/odd rotations into one collective parameter to recover the parameter economy associated with the strongest prior candidates. Replace the serial CZ chain with a disjoint matching to reduce depth and gate count. Add a global RZ layer after entanglement, tied to the same collective parameter, providing a richer repeated RY–CZ–RZ transformation without introducing another independently trainable parameter. This should preserve regularization and accuracy while improving convergence and phase flexibility.
