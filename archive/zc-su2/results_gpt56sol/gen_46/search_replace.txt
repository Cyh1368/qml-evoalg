<DESCRIPTION>
Insert a complementary shifted CZ matching between the first entangling layer and the shared-parameter echo stage. This connects the previously separate qubit pairs into an eight-qubit ring, improving correlation propagation without adding trainable parameters. The unchanged parameter count preserves parameter efficiency and should retain the observed perfect generalization while potentially improving optimization through richer, fixed entanglement.
</DESCRIPTION>
