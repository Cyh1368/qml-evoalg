<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the redundant shared RX layer while retaining the collective RY rotations and connected entangling topology. Prior results show that collective RY dynamics already achieve perfect accuracy, while the extra noncommuting RX gates increase depth and repeatedly apply the same parameter, potentially creating a more oscillatory optimization landscape. This edit preserves the eight effective parameters and expressive ring correlations but should reduce gate count from 144 to 96 and depth from 24 to roughly 18, with a simpler objective that may converge faster.
</DESCRIPTION>
