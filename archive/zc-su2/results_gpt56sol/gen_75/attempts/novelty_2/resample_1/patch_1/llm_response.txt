<DESCRIPTION>
This design retains the proven single shared collective parameter but removes the redundant RX layer. A collective RY mixer is followed by a complete CZ ring, combining the successful parameter-sharing strategy with connected, ring-shifted entanglement. The ring alternates naturally between two disjoint edge matchings, so every qubit communicates with both neighbors while preserving low schedulable depth. Compared with the current ansatz, it reduces the per-block gate count from 20 to 16, keeps the minimum trainable-parameter count, and replaces disconnected qubit pairs with global connectivity that may improve convergence and robustness.
</DESCRIPTION>
