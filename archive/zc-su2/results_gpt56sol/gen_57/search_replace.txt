<DESCRIPTION>
Append a third collective stage using the existing shared parameter. The added RY–CZ–RZ sequence increases nonlinear circuit depth and introduces a complementary rotation axis without increasing the trainable parameter count. Reusing the same four-pair matching preserves parallelism and the symmetry that already achieves perfect generalization, while the extra shared-parameter echo may improve optimization speed and loss.
