<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse each repeated block from 24 trainable parameters to three: separate even/odd RY angles preserve limited spatial flexibility, while one shared RZ angle provides phase mixing with strong regularization. The two original RZ banks are unnecessarily separated because every intervening CZ commutes with RZ; replacing them with one post-entanglement bank removes redundant gates and parameters. This should substantially improve parameter economy, depth, convergence, and small-sample generalization while retaining the expressive RY–CZ–RZ layered structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
