<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the dual-axis pre-entanglement rotations with a streamlined two-echo structure: one collective RY layer, a shifted CZ matching that connects across the previous pair boundaries, then collective RY and RZ echoes. All rotations retain the single shared parameter, preserving minimum parameter count while improving global information mixing and adding modest post-entanglement expressivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
