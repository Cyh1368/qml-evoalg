<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the three consecutive shared-parameter XX matchings into one. For each pair, consecutive XX rotations commute and compose into a single XX rotation with a rescaled angle, so the trainable hypothesis family is preserved while circuit depth and gate count fall by a factor of three. The shorter circuit may also reduce optimization overhead and noise without increasing the parameter count.
