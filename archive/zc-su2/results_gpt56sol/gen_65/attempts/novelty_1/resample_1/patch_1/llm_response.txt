<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the three consecutive shared-parameter XX matchings into one. Because identical XX interactions on the same pair commute, the current sequence only rescales the effective angle and does not add expressivity. A single matching preserves the one-mode hypothesis class while reducing ansatz depth and gate count by threefold, limiting noise and redundant gradient evaluations and potentially improving convergence without increasing the parameter count.
</DESCRIPTION>
