<DESCRIPTION>
Prepend an RX rotation on every wire immediately before its existing RY rotation. Sharing the same phase_stitch parameter across both axes preserves the current eight-parameter economy while adding noncommuting dual-axis expressivity. This may retain perfect generalization while improving optimization speed and loss without introducing independent trainable variables.
