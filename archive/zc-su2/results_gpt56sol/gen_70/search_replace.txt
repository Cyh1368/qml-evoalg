<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the middle repeated XX matching with shared CRZ gates. Repeating identical XX generators mainly rescales one rotation, while interleaving CRZ introduces a noncommuting conditional-phase transformation that can improve expressivity and reduce validation loss. Reusing the existing parameter name preserves the minimal one-parameter-per-repetition economy.
</DESCRIPTION>
