<DESCRIPTION>
Add a second, shifted CZ matching to connect the four previously isolated qubit pairs into a ring. This increases information propagation across the full register without introducing any trainable parameters, preserving parameter economy and the strong observed generalization. The complementary depth-2 entangling topology may improve loss and convergence while retaining parallel execution within each CZ layer.
</DESCRIPTION>
