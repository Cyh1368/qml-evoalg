<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the second CZ matching while retaining one entangling partner for every qubit. The collective RY mode already achieves perfect accuracy with strong parameter sharing, so the additional chain edges may add unnecessary depth and gate cost without increasing expressivity relevant to this task. A disjoint CZ matching executes in one parallel layer, preserves nonlocal feature interactions, and may improve convergence and robustness by shortening each repeated ansatz block without adding parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
