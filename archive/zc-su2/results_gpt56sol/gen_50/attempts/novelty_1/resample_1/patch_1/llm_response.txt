<DESCRIPTION>
This design compresses the successful collective-RY and parallel-CZ pattern into a single stage per ansatz block. Because the fixed architecture already repeats the block with independent parameter copies, duplicating the same shared rotation and matching inside each block is unnecessary. The resulting circuit preserves the one-parameter-per-block economy and proven entangling structure while halving the block gate count and depth relative to the earlier two-stage design. Removing the tied CRZ ring also simplifies the optimization landscape, targeting faster convergence without sacrificing the collective inductive bias that achieved perfect validation accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
