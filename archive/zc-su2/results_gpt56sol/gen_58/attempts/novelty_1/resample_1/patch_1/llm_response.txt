<DESCRIPTION>
Insert a parameter-free CZ ring between the two collective stages. The ring connects the previously isolated qubit pairs into register-wide cyclic connectivity while preserving the existing single shared trainable mode and therefore leaving the parameter count unchanged. This may improve information propagation and convergence without increasing overfitting risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
