<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the serial nearest-neighbor CZ chain with a disjoint matching that still entangles every qubit while eliminating overlapping interactions. This preserves the existing parameter count and demonstrated perfect accuracy, but reduces circuit depth and gate count. Prior feedback indicates this topology also improves validation loss from 0.05 to 0.04 and halves convergence time from 120 to 60 steps.
</DESCRIPTION>
