<sparse_pairwise_rotations>
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Compress the collective rotation layer to one trainable RY per entangled pair, placing it on the even wire while retaining the same shared parameter and CZ matching. This preserves the minimum nonzero parameter count and pairwise feature interactions, while removing four gates per repetition. The sparser layer should reduce circuit cost and overfitting risk without sacrificing the perfect accuracy already achieved by the pairwise architecture.
</DESCRIPTION>
