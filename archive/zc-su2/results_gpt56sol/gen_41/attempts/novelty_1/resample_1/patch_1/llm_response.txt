<DESCRIPTION>
Add a second shared-parameter echo stage while inserting a shifted CZ matching between stages. The shifted matching connects the previously disjoint qubit pairs into an eight-qubit ring, improving inter-pair correlations without adding trainable parameters. Reusing `collective_ry` preserves the existing parameter economy, while the second stage is expected to recover the faster convergence observed for repeated collective layers.
</DESCRIPTION>
