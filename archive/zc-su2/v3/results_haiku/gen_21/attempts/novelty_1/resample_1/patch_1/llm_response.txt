<DESCRIPTION>
The current program achieves good parameter efficiency (5 distinct parameters) but suffers from poor generalization (gap of 0.21) and weak worst-group margin (0.25). The prior high-performing program (score 0.58) used 10 distinct parameters with an alternating RY pattern (ry_0 on even qubits, ry_1 on odd qubits) and achieved much better validation margin (0.57) and worst-group margin (0.28).

The structural redesign:
1. Returns to the proven alternating RY layer strategy (ry_0 and ry_1 parameters) applied on all qubits at the start
2. Adds a mid-circuit alternating RY layer before the final rotations to inject additional learnable heterogeneity at a critical point, following the recommendation
3. Introduces a pre-entanglement RZ layer to set phases before two-qubit interactions
4. Uses a diverse two-qubit entanglement pattern with XX, YY, and ZZ gates to create richer correlations
5. Concludes with a final alternating RY layer and post-RZ layer for readout preparation

This structure:
- Maintains the same input/output interface
- Increases parameter diversity from 5 to 7 distinct parameters (ry_0, ry_1, rz_pre, xx, yy, zz, rz_post)
- Creates three distinct "blocks" of alternating RY applications (initial, mid-circuit, final) to improve expressivity for difficult samples
- Balances parameter economy with robust feature learning
- Should improve worst-group margin by providing better discrimination capacity for hard samples
