3. **From the seed program (score 0.46)**: Inspired by the use of diverse two-qubit gates (XX, YY, ZZ) which provide richer expressiveness for capturing problem structure.

**Key improvements in this hybrid:**
- Reduces parameter count from 12 to 10 distinct parameters (better parameter efficiency)
- Maintains the proven `rz_pre`/`rz_post` separation that delivered the best validation accuracy
- Uses alternating XX gates with parameter sharing for structured entanglement
- Simplifies single-qubit rotations to just 2 shared parameters (ry_even/ry_odd) to reduce overfitting
- Removes the extra RZ layers that add depth without benefit
- Total gate count remains efficient at around 86 gates

This design should generalize better while maintaining good separation on hard samples through the pre/post RZ strategy.
