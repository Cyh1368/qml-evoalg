3. Simplicity and parameter economy are crucial - the best program had the fewest distinct parameters

Algorithmic approach:
- Return to a simpler, proven architecture with minimal depth
- Use only 2 distinct RY parameters (parity-based alternation) for robustness
- Single shared RZ pre-layer for computational universality
- Minimal two-qubit entanglement using mixed Ising gates (XX, YY, ZZ) on strategic pairs
- Single post-layer RZ for final rotation
- Eliminate unnecessary CRX/CRY/CRZ layers that seem to add overfitting risk without proportional benefit
- Target convergence in <150 steps, generalization gap <0.05, and worst_group_margin >0.32

This stripped-down design maximizes parameter efficiency (7-8 distinct params) while maintaining the proven expressivity needed for robust separation on hard samples.
