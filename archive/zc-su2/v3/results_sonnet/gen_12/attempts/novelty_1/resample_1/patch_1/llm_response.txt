skip2_entangling_ring

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed nearest-neighbor CNOT backbone with a "skip-2" double-ring topology (0→2→4→6→0 and 1→3→5→7→1). This creates longer-range entanglement across the register while still using no extra parameters, which prior experiments suggest improves separation/generalization compared to purely local nearest-neighbor connectivity. The parametrized CRZ entangling layer is left on the original nearest-neighbor chain to keep local correlations captured by the trainable part, while the fixed backbone now provides the longer-range mixing.
