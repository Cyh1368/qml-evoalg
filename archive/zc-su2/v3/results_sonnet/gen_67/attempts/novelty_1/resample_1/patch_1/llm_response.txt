<DESCRIPTION>
The duplicated two-block ansatz (current program, score 0.39) doubles depth/gate-count while reusing all parameters, which caused severe underfitting (train_accuracy dropped to 0.56) — the extra depth without new parameters likely pushed the circuit into a barren-plateau-like regime. Revert to the single-block structure that previously scored highest (0.42), which keeps depth/gate-count moderate and retains the CRZ entangling term. On top of that, apply the recommended refinement: split the mid-circuit "rz_a" parity term into "rz_a0" (wires 0,4) and "rz_a1" (wires 2,6) while leaving "rz_b" on the odd wires unchanged. This gives finer phase-alignment granularity in the middle of the circuit at the cost of only one extra distinct parameter (7 total instead of 6), which should improve worst-group margin and validation accuracy without reintroducing the depth-related generalization collapse seen in the duplicated version.
