<DESCRIPTION>
Two combined improvements: (1) reduce trainable parameter count from 6 to 4 distinct names by merging the previously split `ry_a`/`ry_b` and `rz_a`/`rz_b` rotation layers into single shared parameters `ry_ab` and `rz_ab` applied uniformly across all 8 qubits — since parameter economy is explicitly rewarded at every count, this should directly raise the score while keeping the same entangling structure and expressivity per qubit (each qubit still gets an input-encoding-dependent effect from a shared angle). (2) Replace the second fixed entangling layer, which was just the reverse of the first nearest-neighbor CNOT chain (a redundant/adjacent topology), with a "skip-2" ring connecting qubits at distance 2 ([0,2],[2,4],[4,6],[6,0],[1,3],[3,5],[5,7],[7,1]). This creates two independent longer-range mixing cycles that combine with the nearest-neighbor chain and the distance-4 ZZ layer to give richer, more non-local entanglement coverage without adding any parameters, which prior evidence suggests improves robustness/generalization on the hardest validation groups.
</DESCRIPTION>
