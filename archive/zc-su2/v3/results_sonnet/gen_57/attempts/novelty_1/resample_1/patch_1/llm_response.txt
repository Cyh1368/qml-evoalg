<DESCRIPTION>
Add a fixed (parameter-free) backward CZ ladder (qubit 7→6→…→0) after the existing skip-2 CNOT backbone and before the parametrized CRZ layer. This increases entangling connectivity and mixes phase information across all neighboring qubits in the reverse direction, without adding any new trainable parameters (parameter count stays at 5 distinct params). Phase-type (CZ) entangling gates tend to be less sensitive to bit-flip-like noise than CNOT chains and often improve generalization by providing richer entanglement structure while keeping the model parameter-efficient — directly addressing the dominant "worst group margin" and generalization-gap scoring terms without any parameter cost.
