hierarchical_shared_entangler

<DESCRIPTION>
Restructured the ansatz into a clean three-parameter hierarchical block: a shared RY "mixing" layer, a fixed nearest-neighbor entangling ring, a shared RZ "phase" layer, a shared long-range CRZ entangler connecting opposite qubits (0-4,1-5,2-6,3-7) to capture global correlations, a second fixed entangling ring in the reverse direction to spread information differently, and a final reuse of the RY mixing parameter to close the block. This keeps the trainable parameter count minimal (3 distinct names) while combining local nearest-neighbor mixing with long-range entanglement, which should improve robustness on hard validation groups and reduce the train-test gap compared to the previous 4-5 parameter designs, since parameter economy is rewarded and reused rotations tend to generalize better from small training sets.
