The previous candidate split the initial RY layer into three separate parameter groups (ry_a_0, ry_a_1, ry_b), which added two extra trainable parameters without improving the worst-group margin over the simpler 6-parameter seed (0.20 vs 0.13 margin but at the cost of overfitting: gen-gap rose from 0.19 to 0.23, and n_distinct_params rose from 6 to 7). Since parameter economy is scored to matter monotonically and the training set is tiny, we collapse all three initial RY sub-groups into a single shared parameter "ry_a" applied uniformly to all 8 qubits, cutting 2 trainable parameters while keeping the exact same gate topology (nearest-neighbor ZZ-Ising ring, global RZ phase mixing, long-range ZZ coupling across register halves, and a closing shared RY). This preserves the expressive Ising-coupling structure that gave good margins while directly reducing n_distinct_params (7→5), which should improve parameter_efficiency, shrink the train/validation gap (less overfitting on the tiny training set), and keep robust separation strong since the removed asymmetry was not clearly load-bearing (score stayed flat at 0.41 despite the extra params).
