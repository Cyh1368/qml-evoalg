<DESCRIPTION>
Builds on the best-performing prior candidate (score 0.42), which used parity-split initial rotations (ry_a/ry_b), a forward CNOT chain, parity-split RZ phase rotations (rz_a/rz_b), a reversed CNOT chain, a shared CRZ entangling layer on disjoint pairs (ent), and a single shared final RY readout (ry_c). Per the recommendation, we further parity-split the concluding RY readout layer into two parameters — ry_c_e for even wires and ry_c_o for odd wires — instead of a single shared "ry_c". This preserves the same overall structure and entangling topology (which already generalized best among tested variants) while giving the readout layer just enough extra capacity to capture even/odd asymmetries in the encoded features, which prior experiments suggest improves the worst-group margin without requiring extra entangling gates or depth. The cost is one additional distinct parameter (7 total instead of 6), a modest trade against the parameter-count penalty term, but the sharpness of margin captured by allowing the final readout squeeze to differ between even and odd qubits is expected to more than compensate, based on the observed gains from parity-splitting earlier layers (ry_a/ry_b, rz_a/rz_b) versus the fully-merged variant which scored markedly worse (0.39). All other structure — forward/backward CNOT ladders and the shared CRZ entangling block — is kept identical to the best-known configuration to avoid destabilizing what already works.
