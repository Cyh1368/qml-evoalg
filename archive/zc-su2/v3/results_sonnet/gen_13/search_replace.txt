<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz suffers from a large train/validation gap (0.20), suggesting it overfits the small training set with only local nearest-neighbor entanglement. Adding a single shared long-range ZZ entangling layer between opposite qubits (0-4,1-5,2-6,3-7) before the local RY block injects global correlations with only one extra parameter. This is inspired by the idea that disjoint, shared-parameter two-qubit Ising gates increase the effective feature space cheaply while acting as a form of regularization (fewer independent parameters covering more of the qubit topology), which should help close the generalization gap and improve the worst-group margin, at the cost of a small parameter-efficiency penalty (6 distinct params vs 5).
</DESCRIPTION>
