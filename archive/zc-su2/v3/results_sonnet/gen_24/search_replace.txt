<DESCRIPTION>
Split the single shared CRZ entangling parameter "ent1" into two parameters "ent1a" and "ent1b", alternating along the linear chain (even-indexed edges use ent1a, odd-indexed edges use ent1b). This gives the entangling layer more expressive freedom to independently tune "even" vs "odd" bond phases, which can help separate harder-to-classify sample groups that may respond differently to different parts of the chain, at the cost of only one extra trainable parameter (5 distinct total). This mirrors the recommended finer-grained sharing strategy and should improve worst-group margin/robust separation while keeping parameter count low.
</DESCRIPTION>
