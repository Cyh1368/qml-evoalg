<DESCRIPTION>
Restructured the ansatz to use only 4 distinct trainable parameters (down from 5) while preserving strong bidirectional entanglement. The circuit now consists of: (1) a global shared RY rotation on all 8 qubits, (2) a fixed nearest-neighbor CNOT ring for local entanglement, (3) a global shared RZ rotation, (4) a fixed skip-2 "long-hop" CNOT entangler (0→2→4→6→0 and 1→3→5→7→1) that spreads correlations across non-adjacent qubits without adding parameters, (5) a single shared CRZ layer across four disjoint pairs to inject a small amount of parametrized entanglement, and (6) a final global shared RY rotation to consolidate the state before readout. This design reduces parameter count (improving parameter economy, which is weighted heavily), keeps depth in the previously effective ~40-45 range, and relies on fixed (non-parametrized) long-range CNOTs to build robust entangling structure that should generalize better than the deeper, more overfitting-prone variant that had 5 params and 0.20 generalization gap.
