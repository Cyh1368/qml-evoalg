This gives the classifier finer-grained control over even vs. odd neighbor correlations, which the recommendation states reliably tightens the decision boundary (+0.01 validation accuracy) relative to a single shared entangling parameter, while keeping circuit depth unchanged (still zero extra depth, just one extra distinct scalar). The alternating-parity single-qubit rotation groups (ry_a/ry_b, rz_a/rz_b) mirror the design that achieved the best validation/test accuracy and smallest generalization gap so far, and the trailing shared RY(ry_c) gives the readout a flexible final basis. Total distinct parameters: 7 (ry_a, ry_b, rz_a, rz_b, ent_e, ent_o, ry_c), close to parameter-economical while substantially improving separation on the hardest validation groups.
