<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the two parameter-free CNOT ring passes (forward 0→7 and reverse 7→0) with parametrized CRZ (phase-type) entangling rings that share a single new parameter "h". Bit-flip (CNOT) entanglers can be more sensitive to encoding noise and tend to scramble amplitude information; phase-type entanglers (CRZ) preserve more of the encoded amplitude structure while still creating full entanglement across the ring, which should improve generalization to harder validation groups. Although this adds one new distinct parameter, the resulting boost to worst-group margin and robustness is expected to outweigh the small parameter-count penalty, and depth/gate count remain unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
