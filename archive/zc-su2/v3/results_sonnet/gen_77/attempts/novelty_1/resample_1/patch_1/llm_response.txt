The previous edit path (ZZ/CRX long-range coupling with shared params) regressed performance (0.40) compared to the earlier seed using a bit-reversal CNOT ladder with a CRZ entangling layer (0.42). We revert to that stronger-performing seed structure and apply the recommended mixed-axis entangler refinement: instead of four identical CRZ gates, keep CRZ on pairs [0,1] and [4,5] (renamed "ent_z") while switching pairs [2,3] and [6,7] to CRX (new shared parameter "ent_x"). This preserves the depth/gate-count profile of the higher-scoring seed while injecting complementary X-Z entangling couplings that may better capture non-Clifford correlations needed for the harder validation groups, at the cost of one extra distinct parameter (7 vs 6), which should still be a good trade given the substantial score gain of the base structure.
