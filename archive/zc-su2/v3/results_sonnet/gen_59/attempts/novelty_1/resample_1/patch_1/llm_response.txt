<DESCRIPTION>
The duplicated two-block variant (current file) actually hurt generalization (score dropped from 0.42 to 0.39) relative to the single-block version with a CRZ entangling layer. Revert to that better single-block structure, and add a parallel CRX entangling layer immediately after the CRZ layer, reusing the SAME shared parameter name ("ent") instead of introducing a new one. This adds amplitude-mixing (CRX) on top of the existing phase-mixing (CRZ) for extra non-Clifford expressivity and better separation of hard groups, while keeping the distinct parameter count unchanged (6 params total), which strongly favors the score per the parameter-economy criterion. Depth increases only modestly (+4 gates), a low-risk way to boost worst-group margin without growing train-test gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
