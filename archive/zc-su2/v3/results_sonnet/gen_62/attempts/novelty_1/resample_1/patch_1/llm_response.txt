readout_twist_shared_phase

<DESCRIPTION>
Restructures the ansatz to balance expressivity and generalization: keeps the proven parity-split initial RY layer (ry_a on even wires, ry_b on odd wires) and a fixed bidirectional CNOT entangling ring (cheap, parameter-free, good generalization as seen in prior seeds), but merges the previously split mid-circuit RZ phase rotation into a single globally shared parameter (rz_c) to cut redundant parameters and reduce overfitting risk. The freed parameter budget is reinvested into the recommended concluding two-layer readout: a global RY(ry_c) rotation immediately followed by a global RZ(rz_out) "phase twist" just before measurement, which should sharpen class separation without growing the parameter count beyond the prior best (6 distinct parameters total: ry_a, ry_b, rz_c, ent, ry_c, rz_out). Entanglement between the two CNOT rings is provided by a disjoint-pair CRZ layer (param `ent`), giving controllable long-range correlation with a single shared angle. This design keeps circuit depth and gate count low, avoids the over-expressive continuous ZZ-ring (which was shown to overfit, train_acc=1.00), and targets a lower train/validation gap while preserving strong worst-group margin via the fixed entangling structure plus tunable readout twist.
