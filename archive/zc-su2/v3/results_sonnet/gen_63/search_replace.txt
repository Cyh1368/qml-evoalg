merge_zz_params_and_add_shared_phase_align

<DESCRIPTION>
Two complementary changes aimed at (1) reducing parameter count, which the scoring rule rewards unconditionally, and (2) tightening the margin on the hardest validation group by adding a symmetric mid-circuit phase-alignment layer that reuses an existing shared parameter rather than introducing a new one.

1. Merge the long-range `zz_h` coupling into the same shared parameter as the short-range ring coupling `zz_g`. This cuts the number of distinct trainable parameters from 6 to 5 while keeping the same entangling connectivity (nearest-neighbor ring + opposite-half long-range links), letting the optimizer find one consistent entangling strength that generalizes better instead of two independently-tunable ones that can overfit the small training set.

2. Insert a symmetric RZ layer on every qubit using the already-existing `rz_c` parameter, placed right after the long-range ZZ coupling and immediately before the final closing RY rotation. This creates a two-phase phase-alignment (RZ before entangling, RZ after entangling, same parameter) analogous to the balanced RY/RZ sandwiching seen in higher-scoring variants, without adding any new trainable parameter, which should help align phases picked up by the two ZZ layers and tighten the worst-group margin.
</DESCRIPTION>
