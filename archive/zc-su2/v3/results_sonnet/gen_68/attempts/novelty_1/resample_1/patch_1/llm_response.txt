<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz applies four disjoint CRZ gates all sharing parameter "d" for its entangling layer, which only rotates in the Z-basis and may limit the correlations the classifier can express per parameter. Building on the recommendation to mix entangling axes, I convert two of the four CRZ gates (on pairs [2,3] and [6,7]) into CRX gates while keeping them tied to the SAME shared parameter name "d" (rather than introducing a new "ent_x" parameter). This gives the circuit access to both Z-axis and X-axis controlled correlations from a single trainable angle, increasing expressivity per parameter without increasing the distinct parameter count (still 5), which is important since parameter economy is scored strongly. This should help recover generalization/robustness lost in the current variant while respecting the "fewer distinct params always better" guidance.
</DESCRIPTION>
