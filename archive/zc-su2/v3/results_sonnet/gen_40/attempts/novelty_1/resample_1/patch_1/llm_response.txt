split_final_ry_leftright
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Split the concluding shared RY(ry_c) layer into two separately-parametrized halves: RY(ry_c1) on qubits 0-3 and RY(ry_c2) on qubits 4-7. This adds only one extra trainable parameter but lets the ansatz express asymmetric left/right corrections in the final readout rotation, which can improve worst-group margin and validation accuracy without materially increasing depth or overfitting risk, consistent with the seed's observed ~2-4% validation boost from this final rotation layer.
</DESCRIPTION>
