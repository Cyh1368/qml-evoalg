<DESCRIPTION>
Crossover of the two highest-margin seeds. From the first seed we keep the parity-split initial RY encoding (ry_a on even qubits, ry_b on odd qubits) and the continuously-tunable ZZ-Ising entangling ring, which produced the best worst-group margin (0.24) of all candidates. From the second seed we keep the idea of a final shared RY "unmixing" layer that helped push validation/train accuracy higher. To aggressively cut parameter count (which scores higher at every level with no diminishing returns), the long-range ZZ coupling is merged into the *same* shared parameter as the nearest-neighbor ZZ ring (zz_g), and the separate CRZ entangling block from seed 2 is dropped since it added a distinct parameter without improving margin. This yields only 5 distinct trainable parameters (down from 6 in both seeds), while keeping a rich, fully shared-parameter entangling topology (ring + all-to-opposite long range) that lets a single scalar coherently control global entanglement strength — a structure that should generalize better from few training samples and converge faster due to the reduced parameter landscape.

Layout:
1. RY(ry_a) on qubits {0,2,4,6}, RY(ry_b) on qubits {1,3,5,7} — parity-broken encoding.
2. ZZ(zz_g) nearest-neighbor ring over all 8 qubits (0-1,...,7-0) — cheap, tunable entanglement.
3. RZ(rz_c) global shared phase mixing on all qubits.
4. ZZ(zz_g) long-range coupling connecting opposite halves (0-4,1-5,2-6,3-7), reusing the SAME parameter as step 2 to avoid growing the parameter count while still spreading entanglement across the whole register.
5. Final RY(ry_d) global shared closing rotation on all qubits.

Total: 5 distinct parameters, moderate depth, fully shared-parameter Ising-ring backbone.
