<NAME>
merge_shared_params_reduce_overfit

<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz already overfits strongly (train_acc=1.00 vs val_acc=0.78, gap=0.31) despite only 6 distinct parameters, and per the scoring rules fewer distinct trainable parameters always score higher. I merge the two symmetry-broken initial RY rotations (ry_a/ry_b) into a single shared parameter, and reuse the same rotation-angle parameter (zz_g) for the long-range ZZ coupling instead of introducing a separate zz_h. This reduces the parameter count from 6 to 4 distinct names while keeping the same gate structure/depth (so entangling power and expressivity per layer is preserved), which should shrink the train/validation gap, improve robustness on the hardest validation groups, and directly boost the parameter-economy term of the score.

</DESCRIPTION>
