  • One “ent” parameter in a single CRZ on pairs (0,1),(2,3),(4,5),(6,7) injects non-Clifford entanglement cheaply.  
  • Final global RY(c) on all eight qubits sharpens classification margins (validation_margin 0.20, worst_group_margin 0.13).  
- Moderate depth (~54) with 6 distinct parameters balances expressivity and generalization:

  • Outperforms shallower (depth 42–51) or over-deep (>57) circuits that scored ≤0.39.
