add_zz_mid_layer_before_final_rz

<DESCRIPTION>
The current low-parameter ansatz (3 distinct params) underfits badly (train acc 0.62, worst-group margin negative), showing it lacks enough expressivity to separate the hardest samples even on the training set. To add expressive power with minimal parameter cost, we insert a shared ZZ-Ising entangling layer on disjoint qubit pairs right after the CNOT butterfly block and before the final RZ layer. This introduces genuine Z-Z correlations across the register (complementing the existing CRZ chain and CNOT permutation) using a single new shared parameter "zz_mid", raising the distinct parameter count from 3 to only 4 while giving the circuit more capacity to build separable decision boundaries, which should improve both training fit and worst-group margin without a large parameter-efficiency penalty.
</DESCRIPTION>
