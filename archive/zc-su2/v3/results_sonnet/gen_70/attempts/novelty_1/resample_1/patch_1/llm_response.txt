<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses two independent parameters (ry_a_0, ry_a_1) for the diagonal even-qubit subgroups, which raises the distinct-parameter count to 7 without a clear margin benefit (worst_group_margin actually dropped vs. the simpler shared-parameter seed that scored higher). Since parameter economy is weighted heavily and monotonically in the score, merging these two into a single shared parameter "ry_a" (matching the proven high-scoring seed's even-qubit design) reduces the distinct parameter count from 7 to 6 while preserving the overall circuit structure, depth, and entangling topology. This should improve parameter efficiency and likely improve generalization (smaller effective parameter space, less overfitting risk) without harming the expressive entangling ZZ layers that provide the actual separation power.
</DESCRIPTION>
