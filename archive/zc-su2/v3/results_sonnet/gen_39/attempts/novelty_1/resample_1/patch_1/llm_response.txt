split_final_ry_two_groups

<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the current best-performing ansatz (4 distinct parameters, worst_group_margin 0.04), this variant applies the recommended de-sharing of the final RY layer. Instead of a single shared RY(ry_c) across all 8 qubits, we split it into two shared parameters: RY(ry_c1) on qubits 0-3 and RY(ry_c2) on qubits 4-7. This gives the circuit a modest extra degree of freedom (5 distinct parameters total) to model asymmetries between the two qubit halves right before readout, which prior evidence suggests sharpens decision boundaries by a few percent while only mildly increasing parameter count (staying well within the parameter-economy constraints). All other structure (shared RY entry rotation, fixed nearest-neighbor ring, shared RZ layer, fixed skip-2 ring, shared CRZ entangling layer) is kept identical to the current best design, since that combination already achieved the best worst-group margin and validation accuracy trade-off observed so far.
