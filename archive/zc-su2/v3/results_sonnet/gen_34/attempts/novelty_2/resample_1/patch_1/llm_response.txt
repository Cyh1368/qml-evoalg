skip2_longhop_entangler
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the second fixed CNOT chain (currently a reverse nearest-neighbor ring: [7,6],[6,5],[5,4],[4,3],[3,2],[2,1],[1,0]) with a skip-2 "long-hop" entangling pattern: CNOTs on [0,2],[2,4],[4,6],[6,0] and [1,3],[3,5],[5,7],[7,1]. This mirrors the topology already used successfully after the CRZ layer in an earlier seed program (which had a lower generalization gap), and increases qubit-connectivity diversity (each qubit now connects to a partner 2 hops away in both entangling stages) without adding any new trainable parameters. It also slightly reduces gate count (8 vs 7 CNOTs replaced by 8, roughly similar) while changing circuit topology to potentially improve robustness on the worst-case validation group, since long-range correlations get mixed earlier in the circuit rather than purely local nearest-neighbor propagation twice in a row (forward ring then reverse ring is largely redundant).
</DESCRIPTION>
