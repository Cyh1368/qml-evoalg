<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the final fixed reversed-direction nearest-neighbor CNOT chain with a skip-2 "long-hop" entangling layer (CNOTs on [0,2],[2,4],[4,6],[6,0] and [1,3],[3,5],[5,7],[7,1]). The current program's reversed chain (7 sequential CNOTs, depth 63) seems to contribute to a larger generalization gap (0.17) by over-mixing locally without adding useful long-range structure. The skip-2 topology provides better qubit connectivity diversity (each qubit connects to a partner 2 hops away rather than only nearest neighbors), which was associated with lower depth (42) and better generalization gap (0.03) in the second seed program. This is a parameter-free change (no new trainable parameters), so parameter economy is preserved while diversifying entanglement structure, which should help worst-group margin and reduce train-test gap.
