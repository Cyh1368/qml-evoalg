shared_tunable_ring
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce overfitting and circuit depth by removing the globally shared RZ layers, which add two parameters while providing only uniform phase rotations around a diagonal CZ network. Collapse the four RY parameters into the empirically stronger parity-sharing pattern, then replace the fixed nearest-neighbor CZ ring with CRZ gates sharing one entanglement-strength parameter. This preserves ring connectivity while yielding a compact three-parameter model with tunable entanglement and substantially fewer gates.
</DESCRIPTION>
