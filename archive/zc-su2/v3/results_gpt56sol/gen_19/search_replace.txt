Replace the weaker CZ-before-mixer layout with two parity-shared RY-to-CZ feature-extraction cycles. The first cycle adopts the ordering and ring-plus-long-range connectivity of the strongest prior candidate. A second cycle uses fresh even/odd mixer parameters, increasing nonlinear expressivity while retaining strong symmetry and only four distinct parameters. Fixed CZ entanglers preserve parameter economy and may improve worst-group separation without introducing highly flexible trainable couplings.
