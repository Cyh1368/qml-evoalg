<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the second nearest-neighbor CZ brickwork with disjoint distance-two diagonal links. This preserves gate count, depth, and the single-parameter regularization while introducing complementary connectivity before the antipodal layer. The resulting multiscale interaction graph can improve hard-group separation without increasing model capacity or overfitting risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
