<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed antipodal CZ links with CRZ gates sharing one parameter. This preserves the proven multi-scale connectivity while allowing training to tune the strength of all long-range correlations coherently. Sharing keeps the increase to one distinct parameter, regularizes the global interaction pattern, and may improve the worst-group margin without substantially increasing model complexity.
</DESCRIPTION>
