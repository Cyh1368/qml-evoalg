<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed long-range CZ links with CRZ gates sharing one parameter. This preserves the useful antipodal connectivity while allowing training to regulate its strength, potentially suppressing over-entanglement and reducing the large generalization gap. Sharing a single parameter across all four links maintains strong symmetry-based regularization and adds only one distinct parameter.
</DESCRIPTION>
