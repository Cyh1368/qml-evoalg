<DESCRIPTION>
Replace only the diameter-spanning CZ links with CRZ gates tied to the existing shared_mix parameter. This preserves the single distinct parameter and the local parameter-free ring while giving long-range correlations trainable phase control. Sharing the phase with both mixer layers strongly regularizes the added expressivity, which may improve worst-group separation and generalization without increasing parameter count.
</DESCRIPTION>
