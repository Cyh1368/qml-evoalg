<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the shared trainable ZZ coupling with parameter-free CZ entanglers while preserving the full hypercube connectivity, and split the global RY mixer by wire parity. This keeps the distinct parameter count at two, removes a potentially overfitting interaction parameter, and matches the stronger inductive bias seen in the best prior result: fixed multiscale entanglement with parity-shared local rotations. The expected benefits are improved worst-group margin, validation accuracy, and generalization without increasing depth or parameter count.
</DESCRIPTION>
