<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the global RZ layer and the redundant post-entanglement RY layer, which add three parameters yet currently produce no positive worst-group margin. Replace the local CZ ring with a parameter-free multiscale entangling pattern: disjoint nearest-neighbor pairs, shifted nearest-neighbor pairs, and opposite-wire couplings. This retains the useful two-parameter parity-shared RY layer while improving global information propagation, reducing depth and gate count, and strongly improving parameter economy. The prior feedback indicates this compact structure generalizes substantially better and yields a positive worst-group margin.
</DESCRIPTION>
