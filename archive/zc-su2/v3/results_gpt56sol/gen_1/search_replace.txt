shared_ring_ansatz
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Share each rotation type across all qubits, reducing the ansatz from 24 distinct parameters to 3. This symmetry acts as strong regularization for the very small training set, should reduce the large train-validation gap, and greatly improves parameter efficiency while retaining independent pre-entanglement, mixing, and post-entanglement controls. Closing the CZ chain into a ring removes endpoint bias and gives every qubit equal connectivity, supporting more uniform feature propagation and robust worst-group separation.
</DESCRIPTION>
