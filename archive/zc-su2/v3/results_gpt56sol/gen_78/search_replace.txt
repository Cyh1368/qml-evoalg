<DESCRIPTION>
Introduce the stronger alternating RX/RY shared mixer pattern, add a complementary inversion layer, and use staggered ring links for broad information propagation. Replace the antipodal CZ layer with CRZ gates tied to the same shared parameter, adding continuous long-range phase control without increasing the single distinct-parameter budget. This preserves strong regularization while improving noncommuting feature mixing and worst-group separation.
</DESCRIPTION>
