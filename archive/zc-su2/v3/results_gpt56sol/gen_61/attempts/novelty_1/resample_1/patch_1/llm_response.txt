shared_hypercube_mixer
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the circuit to one shared variational angle and use it for both alternating local rotations and every hypercube entangler. Replacing fixed CZ and separate CRZ interactions with shared CRX gates allows the optimizer to continuously tune global correlation strength without increasing parameter count. Removing the redundant RZ layer reduces depth and overfitting, while edge-coloring the ring into disjoint brickwork layers improves parallel depth and convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
