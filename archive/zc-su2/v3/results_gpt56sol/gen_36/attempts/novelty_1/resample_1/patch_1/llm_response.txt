<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses three distinct rotation parameters, 28 gates per block, and an effective depth of 15. Its parity-specific rotations and final mixer provide reasonable validation margin, but parameter efficiency is low. The new configuration regularizes all rotations with one shared parameter while retaining alternating RX/RY axes for parity-sensitive transformations. It adds a parameter-free mid-range CZ layer between local and antipodal connectivity, creating local, medium-range, and global correlation scales. This keeps the effective depth at approximately 15 while reducing the distinct parameter count from three to one, which should improve parameter efficiency, few-shot generalization, and worst-group separation. Training settings such as learning rate and iteration count are fixed externally and therefore unchanged.
