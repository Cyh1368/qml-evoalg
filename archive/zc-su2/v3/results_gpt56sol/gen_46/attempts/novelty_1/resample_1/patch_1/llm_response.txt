<DESCRIPTION>
Swap the alternating mixer axes so even wires use RX and odd wires use RY. This preserves the strong one-parameter regularization, gate count, and parity structure while testing the complementary orientation, which may align better with the hidden encoding and improve worst-group separation without increasing model complexity.
</DESCRIPTION>
