<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the underperforming second entangling cycle and replace its four parity-specific degrees of freedom and repeated CZ network with one globally shared RY mixer. Prior feedback shows this shallower three-parameter structure improves validation accuracy, worst-group margin, and generalization while reducing depth, gate count, and parameter count. The shared mixer still transforms correlations generated by the first CZ network without the optimization difficulty and overparameterization of a second full cycle.
</DESCRIPTION>
