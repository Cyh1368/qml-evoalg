<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the two nearest-neighbor CZ brickwork layers with directed CNOTs while retaining the antipodal CZ links. This preserves the one-parameter regularization, gate count, and connectivity, but adds directional bit-flip propagation that can produce richer local feature mixing and potentially improve worst-group separation without increasing parameter count.
</DESCRIPTION>
