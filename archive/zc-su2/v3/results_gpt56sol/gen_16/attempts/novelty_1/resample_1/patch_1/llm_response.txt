<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the trainable, globally shared ZZ hypercube with the better-performing parameter-free CZ ring-plus-opposite-pair topology. This removes the coupling parameter, reduces overfitting pressure, and uses the connectivity associated with the strongest prior validation and worst-group margins. At the same time, split the uniform RY mixer into four mod-4 shared angles. This adds limited positional flexibility while retaining strong sharing: opposite qubits use the same angle, matching the long-range CZ pairs. The resulting ansatz has four distinct parameters overall, balancing expressivity with parameter economy.
</DESCRIPTION>
