<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed CZ ring with CRZ entanglers governed by one shared parameter. This preserves the full cyclic connectivity while allowing training to tune the global interaction strength. Sharing `crz_entangle` across all eight edges imposes a strong symmetry-based inductive bias and adds only one distinct parameter, limiting overfitting while potentially improving the hardest-group margin.
</DESCRIPTION>
