shared_interleaved_half_mixer

<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse all rotations to one shared parameter to improve parameter efficiency and regularization, while alternating RY on even wires with RX on odd wires to retain parity-sensitive expressivity. Move the existing second mixer directly after the first brickwork CZ layer. Because this noncommuting refresh occurs between entangling stages, it can propagate richer correlations through the staggered and antipodal links without adding gates or distinct parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
