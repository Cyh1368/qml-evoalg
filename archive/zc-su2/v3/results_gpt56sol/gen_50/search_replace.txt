<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the nearest-neighbor CZ brickwork with directed CNOTs while retaining the distance-2 and diameter CZ links. CNOTs introduce bit-flip-mediated information transport and directional feature mixing, which may improve difficult-group separation compared with an entirely phase-based entangling network. This preserves the single shared parameter, gate count, and approximate depth, so parameter efficiency and regularization remain unchanged.
</DESCRIPTION>
