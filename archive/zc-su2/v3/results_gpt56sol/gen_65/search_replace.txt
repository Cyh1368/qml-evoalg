<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the three trainable parameters to one shared alternating RX/RY mixer, matching the more parameter-efficient prior structure. Place antipodal CZ links before the local ring brickwork so global correlations are seeded first and then propagated locally. Removing the redundant final mixer reduces depth, gate count, and overfitting risk while targeting stronger worst-group separation.
</DESCRIPTION>
