global_first_two_phase_mixer
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Seed long-range correlations before local brickwork by moving the antipodal CZ layer first. Add the complementary shared-axis mixer immediately afterward, creating a regularized two-phase inverter while retaining only one distinct parameter. The subsequent local CZ layers can then distribute globally mixed information around the ring, aiming to improve worst-group separation and reduce overfitting without sacrificing parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
