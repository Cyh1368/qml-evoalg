<DESCRIPTION>
The strongest prior configuration used alternating RX/RY mixing before and after the first CZ layer, one globally shared parameter, and achieved the best score with a small generalization gap. This proposal retains that regularized one-parameter structure while replacing the final antipodal matching [(0,4),(1,5),(2,6),(3,7)] with the rotated matching [(0,5),(1,6),(2,7),(3,4)]. The new long-range connectivity preserves parameter count, gate count, and depth while changing global information flow, potentially improving worst-group separation without increasing overfitting. Learning rate and iteration count remain fixed by the external training loop.
