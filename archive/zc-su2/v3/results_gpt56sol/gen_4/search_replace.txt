mirror_shared_nonredundant
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Exploit the reflection symmetry of the linear CZ chain by sharing rotation parameters between mirrored qubits. This reduces the model from 24 distinct ansatz parameters to 8 while retaining position-dependent edge, inner, middle, and central responses. Remove the post-CZ RZ layer because RZ gates commute with CZ gates, making the pre/post RZ parameters functionally redundant within each repeated block. The resulting circuit has fewer flat directions, lower depth, stronger regularization, and should generalize better from the small training set.
</DESCRIPTION>
