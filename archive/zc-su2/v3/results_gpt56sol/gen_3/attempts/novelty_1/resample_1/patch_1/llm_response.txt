staggered_parameter_tying
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Tie rotations by even/odd wire parity and close the CZ chain into a ring. This reduces the ansatz from 24 distinct block parameters to four while retaining two spatial channels and separate pre/post rotation roles. The strong symmetry bias should reduce overfitting and improve worst-group generalization, while the closing CZ removes boundary asymmetry and spreads information across all eight qubits without adding trainable parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
