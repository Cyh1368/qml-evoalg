<DESCRIPTION>
This design replaces the redundant cyclic entanglement graphs with a sparse, multiscale spanning tree. A single shared mixer parameter preserves the strongest regularization observed in prior candidates. Four antipodal links first form global pairs, two distance-two links merge those pairs, and one final bridge connects the entire register. The resulting connected graph uses only seven entanglers per block, reducing unnecessary correlations and overfitting while retaining full-register information flow and low circuit depth.
</DESCRIPTION>

N_QUBITS = 8

    # One globally shared angle strongly regularizes the small-data model.
    # Alternating axes retain parity-sensitive local transformations.
    {"gate": "RY", "wire": 0, "param": "shared_mix"},
    {"gate": "RX", "wire": 1, "param": "shared_mix"},
    {"gate": "RY", "wire": 2, "param": "shared_mix"},
    {"gate": "RX", "wire": 3, "param": "shared_mix"},
    {"gate": "RY", "wire": 4, "param": "shared_mix"},

    # First level: pair opposite regions of the register.
