<DESCRIPTION>
Reorder the existing gates without changing gate count or parameter count. Applying the shared mixer first makes its trainable basis change available to all subsequent entanglers. Placing antipodal CZ links immediately afterward front-loads global communication, while the local brickwork and staggered ring links then refine short-range correlations. This may improve worst-group separation and convergence while retaining the strong one-parameter regularization.
</DESCRIPTION>
