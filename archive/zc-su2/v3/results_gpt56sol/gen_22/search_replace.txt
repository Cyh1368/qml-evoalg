<DESCRIPTION>
Append a second copy of the parity-shared RY and CZ connectivity block while reusing the existing ry_even and ry_odd parameters. This doubles the ansatz depth and allows repeated mixing of local and antipodal correlations without increasing the two-parameter trainable space, preserving parameter efficiency while potentially improving worst-group separation.
</DESCRIPTION>
