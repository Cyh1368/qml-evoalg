frontload_antipodal_links
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Move the long-range antipodal CZ layer directly after the parity mixer, ahead of the local brickwork. This preserves parameter count, gate count, and connectivity while allowing globally separated encoded features to interact earlier in every repeated block. Earlier global communication may improve optimization and worst-group separation without increasing model capacity or overfitting risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
