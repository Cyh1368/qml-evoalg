<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the shallow nearest-neighbor ring and underperforming global RZ layer with the strongest observed parameter-free multiscale CZ pattern. Disjoint local pairs provide robust short-range interactions, shifted links propagate information between pairs, and opposite-wire links capture long-range structure. Removing the global RZ parameter reduces the distinct parameter count from three to two, lowers depth and gate count, and retains the parity-shared RY inductive bias that previously achieved the best combined score.
