<DESCRIPTION>
Adds a nearest-neighbor XX interaction layer immediately after the regularized single-qubit mixer. All four XX gates share one angle, introducing only one additional distinct parameter while capturing pairwise correlations that fixed CZ gates may miss. The existing ring and antipodal CZ topology is retained for broad information propagation and robust separation.
