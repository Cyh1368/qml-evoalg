<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the even/odd RY angles into one shared mixer parameter to reduce model capacity and the train-validation gap while improving parameter efficiency. Replace the staggered nearest-neighbor CZ layer with disjoint next-nearest-neighbor links, preserving gate count, depth, local pair couplings, and antipodal couplings while testing a medium-range correlation topology that may improve worst-group separation.
</DESCRIPTION>
