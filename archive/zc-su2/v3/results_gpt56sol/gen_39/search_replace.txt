<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the final parity-preserving RY layer to the corresponding initial RY parameters, reducing distinct parameters from five to three while retaining even/odd structure. Reorder the commuting nearest-neighbor CZ gates into staggered parallel layers to reduce effective depth, then add a parameter-free mid-range CZ layer before the antipodal links. This introduces an intermediate correlation scale that may improve worst-group separation without increasing trainable complexity.
