<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the four rotation parameters into one strongly regularized shared parameter while alternating RY and RX axes to retain noncommuting transformations across wire parities. Add a fixed distance-2 CZ layer between the local ring and diameter links, introducing an intermediate correlation scale without increasing the trainable parameter count. This targets better held-out separation and worst-group margin while improving parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
