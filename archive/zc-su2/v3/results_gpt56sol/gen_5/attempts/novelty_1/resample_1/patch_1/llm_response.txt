global_rotation_tying
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Tie rotation parameters across all eight qubits while retaining separate parameters for the RY, pre-entanglement RZ, and post-entanglement RZ roles. This reduces the ansatz from 24 distinct parameters to 3, imposing a strong symmetry-based regularizer. The substantially lower capacity should reduce the large train-validation gap, improve convergence and parameter efficiency, and preserve more useful expressivity than collapsing all gate types onto a single parameter.
</DESCRIPTION>
