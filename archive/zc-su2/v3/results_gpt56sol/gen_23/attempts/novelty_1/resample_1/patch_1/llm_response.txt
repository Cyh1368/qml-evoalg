<DESCRIPTION>
Retains the strong two-parameter parity mixers and local CZ brickwork of the current best circuit, while replacing the four fixed antipodal CZ gates with CRZ gates sharing one parameter. This adds only one distinct parameter and allows training to tune the strength of global information exchange, potentially improving worst-group separation without substantially increasing model complexity or overfitting risk.
