<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the parity-specific angles into one shared parameter to recover the stronger parameter efficiency and regularization seen in prior one-parameter candidates. Then replace the staggered CZ layer with directed CNOTs, preserving depth and gate count while introducing bit-flip-mediated information flow that may improve worst-group separation beyond an entirely phase-based entangler network.
</DESCRIPTION>
