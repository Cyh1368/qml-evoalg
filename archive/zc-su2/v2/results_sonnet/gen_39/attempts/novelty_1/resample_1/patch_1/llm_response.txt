alternating_zz_ring_sublayers

<DESCRIPTION>
Applies the recommended depth-compression trick to the entangling ZZ ring: instead of chaining all nearest-neighbor ZZ gates sequentially (which forces full serial depth because each consecutive pair shares a qubit), split the ring into two alternating sublayers of non-overlapping edges — "even" edges ([0,1],[2,3],[4,5],[6,7]) followed by "odd" edges ([1,2],[3,4],[5,6],[7,0]) — all still sharing the single "zz_ring" parameter. Gates within each sublayer act on disjoint qubit pairs and can be scheduled in parallel, cutting the ring's contribution to circuit depth roughly in half (from ~7 sequential layers to ~2) while adding the missing wraparound edge (7,0) to complete the ring topology at no extra parameter cost. This preserves the exact same parameter count and connectivity (now a full ring instead of an open chain), but reduces overall depth, which should help convergence speed and the train/val gap without hurting the worst-group margin, consistent with the reference Gen 28 alternating-sublayer trick.
</DESCRIPTION>
