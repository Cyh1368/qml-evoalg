<DESCRIPTION>
The long-range hypercube couplings ([0,4],[1,5],[2,6],[3,7]) currently share the same "zz_ring" parameter as the nearest-neighbor ring, which may limit the optimizer's ability to independently tune short-range vs. long-range entanglement strength. Splitting these primary diagonals into their own shared parameter "zz_lr" (distinct from zz_ring but still a single shared name across all 4 gates) gives the optimizer one extra degree of freedom to balance local vs. global correlations, which should help separate harder-to-classify samples that depend on longer-range qubit correlations, improving worst-group margin at the cost of only one additional parameter (5 distinct params total instead of 4).
</DESCRIPTION>
