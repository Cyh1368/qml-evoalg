cz_ladder_theta_shared_ansatz

<DESCRIPTION>
Redesigned entangling structure that replaces the previously used CNOT-based
mixing ladders with fixed, parameter-free CZ ladders, while retaining the
highly parameter-efficient "single shared theta" strategy that drives every
RZ single-qubit phase layer and every ZZ two-qubit coupling (ring, hypercube-
diagonal, and skip-2 shells). This keeps the trainable-parameter count at just
2 distinct names (`ry_a` for input encoding rotations, `theta` for all phase
and entangling terms), matching the best-scoring prior architecture's
parameter economy, but swaps the mixing entanglers from CNOT to CZ. CZ gates
are diagonal in the computational basis and pair naturally with the RZ/ZZ
phase-heavy design (which is also diagonal-friendly), so the whole circuit
becomes more "phase-coherent": entangling structure is dominated by
commuting diagonal operations except for the initial/final RY basis change,
which can improve robustness (worst-group margin) and reduce sensitivity to
gate ordering, while keeping identical parameter count and connectivity
richness (ring + long-range + skip-2 shells) as the strongest earlier
candidate. This structural change directly follows the suggested
recommendation of trying CZ ladders in place of CNOT ladders to test whether
phase-based mixing better complements the ZZ/RZ sandwich.
