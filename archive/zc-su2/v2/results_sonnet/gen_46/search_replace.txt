<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce parameter count further and boost expressivity at zero extra parameter cost by merging the previous "zz_even"/"zz_odd" split into a single fully-shared "zz_ring" parameter (dropping distinct params from 4 to 3, matching the best-scoring prior seed), and by adding a complementary XX nearest-neighbor ring reusing the same "zz_ring" parameter name right after the ZZ ring. Mixing ZZ (diagonal) and XX (off-diagonal) two-qubit interactions on the same ring topology increases the entangling power / expressivity of the ansatz without adding any new trainable parameter, which prior evidence suggests improves worst-group margin robustness. This keeps total distinct parameters at 3 (ry, rz, zz_ring), which should improve parameter_efficiency and the combined score similar to (or better than) the earlier 0.72-scoring seed.
