<DESCRIPTION>
The two seed variants show that reducing distinct parameter count consistently raises the combined score (best score 0.70 came from the 4-distinct-param design that merged the RZ pre/post layers into a single shared "rz_p" parameter, versus 0.67 for the current 6-param XX+ZZ design). To combine the benefit of the orthogonal XX/ZZ Ising mixing (recommended for margin robustness) with the parameter economy that empirically wins, I merge the split rz_a/rz_b pre/post-entangling rotation layers into one fully shared "rz_p" parameter (applied to all 8 qubits, before and after the entangling rings), while keeping the ZZ ring followed by an XX ring for richer entangling structure. This drops distinct parameter count from 6 to 5 (ry_a, ry_b, rz_p, zz_ring, xx_ring), which should both increase parameter_efficiency and combined_score, and likely reduce convergence steps back toward the 30-step regime seen in the best seed.
</DESCRIPTION>
