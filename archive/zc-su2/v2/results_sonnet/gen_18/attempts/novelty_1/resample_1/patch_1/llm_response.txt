<DESCRIPTION>
Based on the top-performing seed structure (RY + RZ pre/post layers with a fully-shared ZZ entangling ring, 5 distinct params, score 0.69), I replace the current CZ+CRZ design (which scored lower at 0.65 with 7 distinct params) with the proven ZZ-ring structure, then apply the recommended refinement: split the single shared "zz_ring" parameter into two shared parameters, "zz_even" and "zz_odd", assigned to alternating edges of the ring. This keeps the same ring topology and gate count while giving the entangling layer two degrees of freedom instead of one, potentially improving the worst-group margin without significantly hurting parameter economy (6 distinct params total, still very economical). Removing the extra CZ/CRZ layers also reduces gate count and depth relative to the current underperforming variant, which should help convergence speed and reduce the train-test gap.
