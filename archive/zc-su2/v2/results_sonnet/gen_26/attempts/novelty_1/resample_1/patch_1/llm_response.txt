Builds on the highest-scoring prior design (RY groups → CNOT ladder → shared RZ → shared ZZ ring → CNOT ladder → shared RZ, 4 distinct params, score 0.70) and applies the recommended mid/end-circuit "asymmetric RY remix": after the backward CNOT ladder and final RZ reinforcement, we reuse the same two RY parameter names (ry_a for wires 0-3, ry_b for wires 4-7) once more. This re-emphasizes class-relevant qubit-group asymmetry right before measurement at zero extra parameter cost (still only 4 distinct trainable parameters: ry_a, ry_b, rz_p, zz_ring), aiming to sharpen the decision boundary and improve worst-group margin/robust separation while keeping parameter economy and shallow depth, both of which are explicitly rewarded by the scoring criteria.
