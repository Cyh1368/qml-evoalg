<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the two best-performing seeds (both scoring 0.74 with n_distinct_params=2), this variant pushes parameter economy to its logical extreme by collapsing the encoding rotation parameter ("ry_a") into the same shared name used by every entangling/phase gate ("theta"), yielding a single trainable parameter name (n_distinct_params=1) for the whole ansatz.

Rationale:
- The stated scoring rule is explicit and absolute: "fewer distinct trainable parameter names ALWAYS scores higher, with no point of diminishing returns." Going from 2 to 1 distinct parameter should therefore give a strictly higher score than any 2-parameter variant, as long as accuracy/margins do not collapse.
- Empirically, across all prior seeds, validation/test accuracy stayed pinned near 1.0 and worst-group margin stayed in the 0.34-0.35 range regardless of whether 2 or 4 distinct parameters were used - the entangling topology (ring + skip-4 opposite + skip-2 shell + orthogonal XX ring), not the number of distinct parameter names, is what drives separation quality. This suggests the extra "ry_a" parameter was not pulling much weight and can safely be merged into "theta" without materially hurting accuracy or margin.
- We keep the exact connectivity shells (nearest-neighbor ZZ ring, skip-4 "opposite qubit" ZZ shell, skip-2 ZZ shell, orthogonal XX ring) and the CNOT ladder sandwich + RZ phase sandwich from the highest-margin 0.74-scoring seed (worst_group_margin 0.35, depth 99, gate_count 198), since that topology combination was the best on record for worst-group separation. We deliberately avoid adding a skip-3 shell, since the evidence directly showed that extending to skip-3 increased gate count/depth while *reducing* worst_group_margin (0.34 vs 0.35).
- Net effect: identical circuit depth/gate count/topology as the best 2-parameter seed, but with n_distinct_params reduced from 2 to 1, which per the specified scoring rule should improve the combined score through the parameter-economy term while preserving the proven robust-separation topology.
