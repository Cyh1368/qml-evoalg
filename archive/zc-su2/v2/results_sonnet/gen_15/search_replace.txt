merge_ry_rz_groups_single_param

<DESCRIPTION>
The scoring rule states that fewer distinct trainable parameters always yields a higher score, with no diminishing returns, and the empirical trend across the three prior generations (24→8→5 distinct params, score 0.60→0.65→0.69) confirms this strongly outweighs other metric differences. The current ansatz still uses two separate RY groups (ry_a, ry_b) and two separate RZ groups (rz_a, rz_b) that are symmetric across the 8-qubit register. Since accuracy, margins, and convergence are already saturated (train/val/test accuracy = 1.00, margins ~0.35-0.73) regardless of this split, collapsing each symmetric pair into a single shared parameter (ry_a=ry_b -> "ry_shared", rz_a=rz_b -> "rz_shared") reduces distinct parameter count from 5 to 3 without changing circuit depth, gate count, or entangling structure. This should further improve parameter efficiency and the combined score while preserving the expressivity needed for perfect train/val/test accuracy, since the previous merge (rz pre/post sharing) already showed no accuracy loss.
