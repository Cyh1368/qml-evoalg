<DESCRIPTION>
Crossover of the two best-performing seed variants (both scoring 0.69 with 5 distinct params). Both showed that duplicating the sandwich block inside the spec (doubling depth/gate-count) gives identical score to the single-block version, confirming that raw depth/gate-count don't matter much here and that the backend's own repetition mechanism already provides the necessary expressivity. Since parameter economy is rewarded "at every count", this version keeps the proven sandwich motif (RY pre-rotation -> RZ pre-entangle -> ZZ ring -> RZ post-entangle) but pushes parameter sharing further: the two RZ groups (rz_a/rz_b) used in the top scorer are merged into a single fully-shared "rz" parameter, cutting distinct parameters from 5 down to 4. The RY split into two groups (ry_a/ry_b) is kept, since it is the part most likely tied to different halves of the (unknown) data encoding and dropping it entirely is a bigger structural risk. The ZZ ring is also closed into a full 8-edge ring (adding the wrap-around edge 7-0) at no extra parameter cost, to strengthen entangling connectivity/robustness for the hardest validation groups without touching the parameter budget. Only a single, non-duplicated instance of the block is kept (depth ~20-ish) since duplicating didn't change score but did increase depth/gate-count, which is never beneficial when scores tie.
</DESCRIPTION>
