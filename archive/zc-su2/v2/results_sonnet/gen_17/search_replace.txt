merge_ry_shared_param

<DESCRIPTION>
The historical trend across generations shows that reducing the number of distinct trainable parameters consistently increases the combined score (18→8 params scored 0.61, 10 params scored 0.65, and the current 5-param version scores 0.69, the best so far). This suggests the parameter-economy term dominates and the circuit still has enough expressivity to fit the small training set even with fewer parameters. Building on this trend, I propose merging the two RY pre-rotation parameters (`ry_a`, `ry_b`) into a single shared parameter `ry_all` applied to all 8 qubits. This reduces the distinct parameter count from 5 to 4, likely improving parameter_efficiency and the combined score further while the entangling ZZ ring and shared RZ layers preserve enough non-trivial correlations to maintain accuracy and margins.
</DESCRIPTION>
