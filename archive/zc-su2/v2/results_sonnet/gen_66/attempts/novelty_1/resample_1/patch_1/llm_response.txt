hybrid_shell_theta_crz_yy

<DESCRIPTION>
Builds on the two highest-scoring seeds (0.73 and 0.74), both of which use only two distinct trainable parameter names ("ry_a" for single-qubit rotations and "theta" shared across every entangling/phase gate). The 0.74 seed added an XX nearest-neighbor ring on top of ZZ shells; the 0.73 seed added a directional long-range CRZ shell on the hypercube-diagonal pairs. This version merges both ideas and further enriches the entangling operator basis with a YY nearest-neighbor ring, all while keeping the parameter count fixed at 2 distinct names (4 total slots after CNOT ladders remain zero-parameter). The rationale: richer, multi-basis (ZZ + XX + YY + directional CRZ) entangling structure at zero marginal parameter cost should improve the worst-group margin (robust separation) without hurting parameter economy, while the fixed CNOT ladders (sandwiching the parametrized core) provide additional zero-cost mixing that has empirically correlated with faster/stabler convergence in the prior seeds.

Key insights combined:
1. Aggressive parameter sharing under a single "theta" name across all Ising/CRZ/RZ gates keeps n_distinct_params minimal (dominant score driver).
2. Mixing complementary Pauli-basis couplers (ZZ, XX, YY) plus a directional CRZ shell increases expressivity/reachable operator space without new parameters, which the 0.74 seed's ablation (adding just XX) already showed helps.
3. Long-range (hypercube diagonal) and skip-2 connectivity, verified helpful in the 0.73 seed, is retained to keep information mixing beyond nearest-neighbor.
4. Fixed CNOT ladders before/after entangling core provide free non-parametric mixing/depth.
