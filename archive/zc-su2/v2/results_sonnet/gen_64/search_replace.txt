<DESCRIPTION>
The current candidate uses 4 distinct trainable parameters (ry_a, ry_b, rz, zz_ring) and scored 0.70, while the earlier 2-parameter seed (ry_a shared across all qubits, theta shared across RZ+ZZ layers) scored 0.73 with better parameter economy and comparable/better margins. Since parameter economy is weighted very heavily in the score (fewer distinct params always scores higher, no diminishing returns), I revert to the proven 2-parameter structure (single ry_a pre-rotation, single theta driving RZ layers and all ZZ entangling shells) and extend it with the recommended skip-3 ZZ coupling shell (pairs at hypercube distance 3: [0,3],[1,4],[2,5],[3,6],[4,7],[5,0],[6,1],[7,2]), still reusing "theta". This densifies entanglement connectivity further (potentially improving the worst-group margin, which dominates the score) without adding any new trainable parameter, combining the best of both prior seeds' strengths.
