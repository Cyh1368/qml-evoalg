<NAME>xx_ring_sublayer</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Added an orthogonal XX-coupling ring sublayer (nearest-neighbor pairs [0,1]...[7,0]) sharing a single new parameter "xx_ring", inserted between the ZZ ring (with long-range links) and the backward CNOT ladder. XX and ZZ couplings act on different bases and their combination can enrich the entangling structure, potentially improving the worst-group margin without heavily increasing parameter count (only one new shared parameter added, going from 4 to 5 distinct params).
</DESCRIPTION>
