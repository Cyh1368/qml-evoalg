<DESCRIPTION>
Revert to the previously best-performing single-shared-parameter design (n_distinct_params=1, which scored 0.77 vs the current 5-parameter version's 0.69) and apply the recommended enhancement: insert a mid-block global RZ layer (reusing the same shared "theta" parameter) sandwiched between the ZZ/XX entangling rings and the backward CNOT ladder. This creates a triple RZ sandwich (pre-entangling, mid, post-entangling) around the entangling shells, deepening phase-correlation structure while keeping the parameter count at the theoretical minimum of 1 distinct trainable parameter. Since parameter economy dominates scoring and the single-theta topology already generalized well (worst_group_margin 0.35, validation_margin 0.70), adding the extra RZ layer without new parameters should further improve robust separation without any generalization cost.
