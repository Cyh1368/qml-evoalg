param_efficient_parity_sharing

<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses 24 distinct trainable parameters (one RY and two RZ angles per qubit), which the scoring explicitly penalizes ("fewer distinct trainable parameter names ALWAYS scores higher"). To sharply cut parameter count while preserving expressivity and entangling structure needed for robust worst-group separation, I replace the per-qubit RY/RZ angles with a parity-based sharing scheme (even wires share one parameter, odd wires share another) for both the pre- and post-entangling RZ layers and the initial RY layer. I also replace the fixed CZ entangling ring with a parametrized CRZ ring using a single shared parameter, which adds a trainable entangling degree of freedom (helping correlate qubit pairs, useful for hard/worst-group samples) at the cost of only one extra parameter. This reduces the distinct parameter count from 24 to 7, which should substantially boost parameter_efficiency while retaining a similar circuit depth/structure (same gate count, same entangling topology) so that separation quality and convergence speed are largely preserved.
</DESCRIPTION>
