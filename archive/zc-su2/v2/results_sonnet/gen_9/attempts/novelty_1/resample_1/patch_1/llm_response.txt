share_pre_post_rz_params

<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the number of distinct trainable parameters further by sharing the same RZ parameter between the "pre-entangling" and "post-entangling" rotation layers (instead of using separate rz_pre_* and rz_post_* names). Since the score rewards parameter economy at every count, this cuts the total distinct parameters from 13 down to 9 while keeping the same gate structure, depth, and gate count (only parameter names change), preserving expressivity through the entangling ZZ layer that still separates the two applications of each shared rotation.

</DESCRIPTION>
