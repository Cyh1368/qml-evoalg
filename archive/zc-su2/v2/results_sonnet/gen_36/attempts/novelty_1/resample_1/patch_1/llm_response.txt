<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the even/odd ZZ ring split with a physically motivated distinction between nearest-neighbor ring couplings and long-range cross-link couplings. All 8 ring edges (0-1,1-2,...,7-0) share a single "zz_nn" parameter, and 4 diagonal cross-links (0-4,1-5,2-6,3-7) share a single "zz_lr" parameter. This keeps the same 4 distinct trainable parameter names (ry, rz, zz_nn, zz_lr) but gives the optimizer independent control over local adjacency structure versus long-range entanglement, which should improve the worst-group margin (as previously observed with the full ring + cross-link seed scoring 0.71) while retaining the shallower/parameter-economic circuit style of the current best variant.
