zz_nn_lr_split_ansatz

<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the two prior 0.71-scoring seeds (shared RY-group + shared RZ + fully-shared ZZ ring+cross-link ansatz with 4 distinct params), this version differentiates the two structurally different types of ZZ interactions instead of merging them into one parameter. The nearest-neighbor ring couplings (8 edges, including wrap-around) get their own shared parameter "zz_nn", while the long-range diagonal cross-couplings (4 edges) get a separate shared parameter "zz_lr". This gives the optimizer independent control over local vs. non-local entanglement structure — which is likely important for separating the hardest validation group (worst_group_margin) — while still keeping parameter count low (5 distinct params: ry_a, ry_b, rz, zz_nn, zz_lr). The RY pre-rotation keeps its proven two-group asymmetric split (wires 0-3 vs 4-7), and the RZ phase layer remains fully shared and reused before/after the entangling block to reinforce the encoding at zero extra parameter cost. Fixed CNOT ladders (forward before the ring, backward after) provide additional zero-parameter mixing across the full register, matching the depth/gate-count profile of the strongest prior seed (n_params=6, depth=63, gates=150) but now trading one of the previously-fixed CNOT-only degrees of freedom for genuine control over local-vs-nonlocal coupling strength.
