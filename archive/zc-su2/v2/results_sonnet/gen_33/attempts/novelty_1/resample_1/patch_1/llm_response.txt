ry_even_odd_split

<DESCRIPTION>
The best-performing seed (score 0.71, 4 distinct params) used an RY pre-rotation layer split into wires 0-3 vs 4-7, followed by a fixed CNOT ladder, a shared global RZ phase, a parametrized ZZ entangling ring (with long-range cross couplings sharing the same parameter), a backward CNOT ladder, and a repeated RZ phase. The current program (0.69) diverged from this structure by adding an extra RZ pair of parameters and orthogonal XX gates, which increased parameter count and reduced score.

This edit restores the higher-scoring seed-1 structure (4 distinct params: ry_a, ry_b, rz_p, zz_ring) but tests the recommended alternate partition for the RY layer: even wires [0,2,4,6] share "ry_a" and odd wires [1,3,5,7] share "ry_b", instead of the contiguous 0-3/4-7 split. This preserves the exact parameter economy and topology that achieved perfect train/val/test accuracy while exploring whether an interleaved qubit grouping (which pairs more naturally with the alternating CNOT ladder and ring couplings) can improve the worst-group margin — the dominant term in the combined score — without changing param count.
