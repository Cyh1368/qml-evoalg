merge_param_groups_to_two_halves

<DESCRIPTION>
The best-performing prior variant (score 0.65) used only 7 distinct parameters by splitting the 8 qubits into two halves (0-3 and 4-7) for all single-qubit rotation layers, while the current program (score 0.62) uses four quarter-groups (13 distinct params). Since parameter economy is scored as strictly beneficial and the two-half split already demonstrated superior score with a CRZ-based entangler, we apply the same two-half grouping to the current ZZ-ring-based ansatz. This reduces distinct parameters from 13 to 7 while preserving the ring ZZ-entangling structure, testing whether the ZZ entangler combined with fewer parameters yields even better robustness/margin performance than the CRZ variant.
