add_shared_xx_ring_reuse_param
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Reintroduce the higher-scoring 5-distinct-parameter sandwich ansatz (RY pre-rotation, RZ pre/post, single shared ZZ ring) and, per the recommendation, interleave a ring of XX gates right after the ZZ ring to mix orthogonal Ising interactions for a richer entangling structure. Crucially, to avoid increasing the trainable-parameter count (which is heavily penalized), the XX ring reuses the same shared parameter name "zz_ring" as the ZZ ring rather than introducing a new "xx_ring" parameter. This keeps n_distinct_params at 5 (matching the best-scoring prior program) while adding the orthogonal-interaction structure that reportedly improves worst-group margin and generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
