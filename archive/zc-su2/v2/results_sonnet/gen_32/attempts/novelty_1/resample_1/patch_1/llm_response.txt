<DESCRIPTION>
Two combined zero-parameter-cost structural improvements:
1) Restore the long-range ZZ couplings (0-4, 1-5, 2-6, 3-7) reusing the shared "zz_ring" parameter, which was present in the best-scoring (0.71) prior variant but missing from the current program. These give the ring hypercube-like connectivity, aiding worst-group separation without adding parameters.
2) Interleave the fixed CNOT ladders (forward and backward) with CZ gates on alternating pairs, per the recommendation. This diversifies the entangling structure (mixing controlled-phase and controlled-bit-flip interactions) at zero parameter cost, which can accelerate convergence and enrich the reachable Hilbert space without touching the parameter count.

Both changes keep n_distinct_params at 4, preserving parameter economy while aiming to recover and exceed the 0.71 score.
</DESCRIPTION>
