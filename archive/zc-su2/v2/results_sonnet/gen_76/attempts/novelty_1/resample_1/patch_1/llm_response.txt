<DESCRIPTION>
Add a CRY skip-2 shell immediately after the existing XX ring block, reusing the shared "theta" parameter. This introduces controlled-Y rotations across skip-2 connectivity (i -> i+2 mod 8), broadening the operator basis beyond the current Ising XX/ZZ couplings without adding any new trainable parameter name. Controlled rotations (CRY) are qualitatively different from the Ising-type ZZ/XX couplings already present (they are not simply exponentials of a fixed Pauli tensor product but asymmetric controlled operations), so mixing them in should increase expressivity/entangling variety useful for separating the hardest validation group, while keeping n_distinct_params at 2.
</DESCRIPTION>
