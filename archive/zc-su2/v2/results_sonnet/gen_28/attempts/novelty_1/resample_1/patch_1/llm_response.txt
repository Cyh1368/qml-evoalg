<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the best-performing seed (4 distinct params, score 0.70), this version follows the recommendation to split the ZZ entangling ring into two alternating-parameter sublayers (zz_even on edges 0-1,2-3,4-5,6-7 and zz_odd on edges 1-2,3-4,5-6,7-0) to enrich ring expressivity along even vs odd bonds. To keep the total distinct-parameter count unchanged at 4 (preserving the parameter-economy advantage that drove the best prior score), the two RY pre-rotation groups (ry_a/ry_b) are merged into a single shared "ry" parameter, since group-specific RY expressiveness contributed less than the entangling-ring structure to the observed margins. The RZ pre/post layers keep sharing one fully-shared "rz" parameter as in the best-scoring seed, and a full closed 8-edge ring (with wrap-around 7-0) is retained for full connectivity. This design targets a robustness improvement on the worst-group margin (via more flexible entangling structure) without paying the parameter-count penalty seen when moving to 6 distinct params in the immediately preceding candidate.

Rationale from the metric history:
- Best score (0.70) achieved with 4 distinct params, ry_a/ry_b split, shared rz, single zz_ring — worst_group_margin 0.36, val_margin 0.73.
- Adding rz_a/rz_b split + xx_ring on top (6 distinct params) dropped score to 0.67 despite identical accuracy and margin, confirming parameter economy dominates when margin doesn't improve.
- Therefore any added parameter (e.g., splitting zz_ring into zz_even/zz_odd) must be offset by removing an existing distinct parameter elsewhere (merging ry_a/ry_b) to avoid the same penalty, while still testing the entangling-ring flexibility recommendation.
