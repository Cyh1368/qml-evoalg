zz_ring_split_dual_group

<DESCRIPTION>
Builds on the best-performing prior ansatz (which used two shared parameter groups for RY/RZ pre/post layers plus a single shared CRZ ring, achieving score 0.65 with only 7 distinct parameters) by applying the suggested refinement: splitting the single ring-entangling gate into two shared parameters — one for "even" edges (0-1,2-3,4-5,6-7) and one for "odd" edges (1-2,3-4,5-6,7-0). This preserves the strong parameter-economy of the 2-group RY/RZ structure (4 params total for single-qubit layers) while giving the entangling layer two independent degrees of freedom instead of one, letting the circuit learn slightly richer correlations across the ring without exploding parameter count (going from 7 to 8 distinct parameters). The insight is that entanglement expressivity is often the bottleneck for worst-group margin (hardest samples need richer correlations), whereas single-qubit rotation grouping mainly affects overall accuracy — so we keep single-qubit param count minimal (2 groups) but give entanglement 2 independent params instead of 1, targeting improved robust separation with minimal parameter cost increase.
