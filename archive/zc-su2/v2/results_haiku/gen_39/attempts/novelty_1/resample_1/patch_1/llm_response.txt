<DESCRIPTION>
The prior programs achieved 0.67 combined score with 7 distinct parameters. The current program regressed to 0.66, likely due to the additional CRZ gate and the shift in CZ gate patterns creating redundancy without improved expressivity.

Key insight: The recommendation to decouple CRX into a distinct "ent_crx" parameter was not yet explored. Currently, CRX gates either don't exist or share parameters with other layers. By introducing a dedicated narrow-angle CRX sublayer with its own parameter, we can:

1. **Decouple rotation axes**: CRX (controlled X-rotation) operates fundamentally differently from XX/YY Ising gates and CRZ controlled rotations. Giving it a unique parameter allows the optimizer to learn independent amplitude scales.

2. **Maintain parameter economy**: We stay at 7-8 distinct parameters (similar to winning programs) while gaining fresh entanglement patterns.

3. **Strategic placement**: Insert the CRX layer right after the first XX block to build asymmetric control-target relationships that complement the symmetric Ising gates.

4. **Reduce gate bloat**: Remove the extra CRZ gate and revert to the more selective CZ pattern from the first winning program, reducing depth from 24 and gate count from 111.

The circuit hierarchy becomes:
- Initial RY (symmetry-breaking)
- XX nearest-neighbor (symmetric pair entanglement)
- CRX offset (asymmetric controlled entanglement with dedicated parameter)
- YY offset (complementary symmetric entanglement)
- Intermediate RY (expressivity boost)
- Long-range CRZ (distance-2/3 control structures)
- Fixed CZ skip-3 layer (hard-sample discrimination)
- Final RZ (output rotation)

This design respects the winning structure while testing whether decoupling CRX unlocks better worst-group margins through more diverse entanglement patterns.
