# Analysis

Looking at the current program and prior results:

**Current program metrics (0.66 score):**
- 7 distinct parameters (excellent parameter efficiency)
- 20.0 depth, 93 gate count
- Convergence in 30 steps (fastest)
- worst_group_margin: 0.35 (this is the bottleneck)
- validation_margin: 0.72

**Best prior program (0.65 score):**
- 8 distinct parameters
- 24.0 depth, 108 gate count
- worst_group_margin: 0.35 (same bottleneck)

**Key observation:** The worst_group_margin (0.35) is the critical metric limiting the combined score. The goal states that "the largest score term is the margin of the WORST group," so improving robustness on hard samples is paramount.

**Current issue:** The third entangling block only has 4 CRZ gates (missing the connection [3,6] that was in the prior best version). This reduces expressivity for capturing difficult patterns.

**Proposed improvement:**
1. Add the missing long-range connection [3, 6] to the CRZ block to restore full connectivity pattern
2. This increases expressivity by 1 gate while keeping parameter count at 7 (still using shared "ent_lr")
3. Better coverage of long-range correlations should improve worst_group_margin and overall robustness

The tradeoff is: +1 gate (minimal cost) for better worst-group separation (high value).
