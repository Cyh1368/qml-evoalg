**Key parameters identified:**
- `ry_mid`: only provides single-axis rotation after entanglement

- Convergence slow: suggests circuit lacks expressivity in critical sample regions

**New strategy:**
Following the recommendation to add RZ gates immediately after RY mid gates with a new shared parameter `rz_mid`, I introduce dual-phase modulation on odd wires. This exploits the non-commutativity of RY→RZ bracketing to create richer phase space exploration WITHOUT adding distinct parameters to the bottleneck. By inserting RZ rotations right after the intermediate RY gates on wires [1,3,5,7], the circuit gains geometric expressivity that specifically targets worst-group discrimination.

This approach:
1. Maintains 8 distinct parameters (not 7, but reduces parameter count by removing one less-useful entangling gate)
2. Improves worst_group_margin by leveraging phase-space non-commutativity
3. Accelerates convergence through more direct hard-sample access paths
4. Replaces the slower CRX with fixed single-qubit phase sculpting
