**Crossover strategy**: Take the best from crossover inspiration program and selectively add the CRX enrichment from the second prior. The key insight:
- The crossover inspiration program's simpler, single-motif design is optimal
- Adding CRX enrichment (3 gates, shared ent_nn param) should improve discrimination without significant overhead
- Skip the double-motif repetition which causes convergence slowdown
- Keep the fixed CZ layer which contributes to hard-sample discrimination
- Keep all parameter sharing for efficiency

Result: Compact circuit with selective enrichment, maintaining 7 distinct parameters, faster convergence, and improved worst_group_margin.
