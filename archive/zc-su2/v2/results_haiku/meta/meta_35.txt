# GLOBAL INSIGHTS SCRATCHPAD

The following are global insights about optimization approaches and their effectiveness:

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns  
- Narrow-CRX enrichment immediately after the nearest-neighbor XX block, reusing the same “ent_nn” parameter, yields a combined score of 0.67 and validation margin 0.74 in the Current Best Program (n_params = 9, n_distinct = 7, depth = 24, gates = 105, convergence = 30).  
- The three-block entanglement backbone (nearest-neighbor XX → offset YY → long-range CRZ) underpins all top performers (e.g. Gen 11, Gen 20/25 and the Current Best) with zero generalization gap and combined scores ≥ 0.66.  
- Parity-based shared RY layers (“ry_even”/“ry_odd”) plus an intermediate “ry_mid” on qubits [1,3,5,7] drive expressivity while achieving rapid convergence (~30 steps) and low validation/test losses (0.18/0.17), as in Gen 11, Gen 20, and the Current Best.  
- A final global shared RZ rotation (“rz_final”) on all eight qubits consistently lifts validation margin by ~0.01 at minimal depth-and-gate cost; present in Gen 11, Gen 20/25, and the Current Best.  

## Ineffective Approaches  
- Omitting the narrow-CRX sublayer: e.g. the Layered Shared-Parameter Quantum Classifier (Gen 30) scores 0.66 combined vs. 0.67 for the CRX-enriched Current Best.  
- Excessive entanglement depth without targeted CRX: the Shared-Param Multi-Block Entangled Ansatz (Gen 32) grows to depth 33/gates 132, converges in 90 steps, yet stalls at 0.65 combined.  
- Increasing distinct parameters beyond seven: the 8-Qubit Layered Quantum Classifier Ansatz (Gen 35) uses 10 params (8 distinct), slows convergence (~60 steps), and only achieves 0.65 combined with a worst-group margin of 0.34.  
- Overly symmetric sharing with duplicate gates: the erroneous 8-Qubit Layered Entanglement Ansatz (Gen 33) fails validation (0.0 score) due to a duplicated CRZ and insufficient expressivity.  

## Implementation Insights  
- Reusing the “ent_nn” key for both XX and CRX gates concentrates gradient updates, keeps n_distinct_params = 7, and yields a +0.01 boost in validation margin for just +3 gates.

- A shared-parameter long-range CRZ block across five qubit pairs (0–3,1–4,2–5,3–6,4–7) establishes global connectivity with minimal parameter overhead.  

## Performance Analysis  
- Enriching the XX block with CRX lifts the combined score from 0.66 (Gen 30, Gen 31) to 0.67 in the Current Best, with validation margin up from ~0.73 to 0.74.  
- Circuits with exactly seven distinct parameters (Current Best, Gen 20, Gen 25) uniformly converge in ~30 steps; designs with eight or more distinct parameters need ~60–90 steps.  
- Gate-count trade-off is favorable: adding three CRX gates (from 102 → 105) nets a +0.01 margin without hurting convergence or parameter efficiency (0.11).  
- Worst-group margin plateaus at ~0.36 across CRX-enriched designs (Gen 20/25 and Current Best), indicating a ceiling that may require novel robustness tactics beyond the current block structure.
