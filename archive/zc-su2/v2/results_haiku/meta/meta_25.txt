# GLOBAL INSIGHTS SCRATCHPAD

The following are global insights about optimization approaches and their effectiveness:

## Successful Algorithmic Patterns  
- Parity-based RY sharing with a mid-layer rotation  
  • Splitting the initial RY layer into “ry_even” vs. “ry_odd” plus a shared “ry_mid” on qubits [1,3,5,7], as in Generation 11 (score 0.66) and the current best (Gen 20, score 0.67), consistently cuts convergence to ~30 steps and drives validation accuracy to 0.99.  
- Three-block entanglement (NN XX → OFF YY → LR CRZ) with seven distinct parameters  
  • Both Gen 11 (combined 0.66) and Gen 20 (combined 0.67) use nearest-neighbor XX (“ent_nn”), offset YY (“ent_off”), and long-range CRZ (“ent_lr”) in sequence, balancing expressivity and parsimony to achieve zero generalization gap.  
- Skip-3 non-parametric CZ “margin booster”  
  • Inserting unparameterized CZ gates on qubit pairs spaced by three (wires [0,3], [1,4], [2,5], [3,6], [4,7]) in Gen 20 lifted worst-group margin from 0.35→0.36 and raised the combined score from 0.66→0.67 without adding new parameters.  
- Final global RZ refinement  
  • A single shared “rz_final” applied to all qubits, present in Gen 11, Gen 20, and the current seed, reliably increases validation margins by ~0.01 at minimal depth cost.  

## Ineffective Approaches  
- Deep Ising layering without fresh motifs  
  • Layered 8-Qubit Ising Ansatz (Gen 17) added three Ising layers (depth 46, 129 gates) but plateaued at combined 0.66 and worst-group margin 0.36, matching Gen 11’s margin at far greater resource cost and with no param-efficiency gain (0.11).  
- Heavy parameter sharing but shallow entanglement  
  • Layered Entangled Ansatz (Gen 21) and Shared-Parameter Multi-Block Ansatz (Gen 22) both used 8 distinct parameters in ~23-depth circuits yet only reached combined 0.65, with worst-group margins 0.33/0.34—showing that parameter count alone doesn’t ensure high margins.  
- Replacing ZZ with CRZ in isolation  
  • Gen 16’s swap of long-range ZZ→CRZ retained perfect accuracy but fell to combined 0.65 and worst-group margin 0.35, indicating that gate-type substitution without a new entangling structure underdelivers.  
- Over-parameterization without new entanglers  
  • Expanding to 9 distinct parameters (e.g., Gen 19) increased convergence steps to ~60 and dropped combined score to 0.64, underscoring that extra parameters can hurt robustness if unaccompanied by novel entanglement patterns.  

## Implementation Insights  
- Declarative block naming sharpens optimization  
  • Labeling entangling blocks as “ent_nn,” “ent_off,” “ent_lr” in ANSATZ_SPEC (Gen 11 & Gen 20) halves convergence steps vs. generic θ₁…θₙ by focusing gradients on semantically grouped operations.  
- Skip-3 CZ gating via unlabeled two-qubit entries  
  • Explicit CZ entries with no “param” field produce new entanglement pathways at zero parameter cost, directly boosting worst-group margins (see current best program).  
- Minimal distinct parameters with maximal coverage  
  • The current best ansatz packs 7 distinct parameters—ry_even, ry_odd, ry_mid, ent_nn, ent_off, ent_lr, rz_final—across 9 total parametric gates, marrying low parameter count with broad circuit expressivity.  
- Consistent sharing patterns reduce noise  
  • Reusing the same parameter keys across non-commuting layers (e.g., “ent_nn” in all XX gates) concentrates learning into a few robust directions, as demonstrated by the zero generalization gap in Gen 20.  

## Performance Analysis  
- Score progression tied to entanglement breadth  
  • Circuits with only XX→YY→CRZ (Gen 16/21/22) plateau at combined 0.65; adding a final RZ lifts to 0.66 (Gen 11/24/17); appending skip-3 CZ in Gen 20 achieves 0.67.  
- Depth versus margin trade-off  
  • Gen 11 (depth 21, gates 93) → Gen 20 (depth 26, gates 108) sees +5 depth/+15 gates yield +0.01 combined score and +0.01 worst-group margin with no slowdown in convergence (both ~30 steps).  
- Parameter count correlation with convergence  
  • Seven distinct parameters (Gen 11 & Gen 20) converge in ~30 steps with zero generalization gap, while circuits with 8–9 distinct parameters (Gen 17/21/22/24) need ~60 steps or show degraded margins.  
- Margin metrics differentiate topologies  
  • Validation margin mean rises from 0.71 (8-param designs) → 0.72 (baseline 7-param) → 0.73 (Gen 20), matching combined score increases from 0.65 → 0.66 → 0.67 and underscoring margins as the key leverage point beyond raw accuracy.

# META RECOMMENDATIONS

The following are actionable recommendations for the next program generations:

1. Extend the long-range CRZ block by appending two additional CRZ gates on wires [3,6] and [5,0] using the existing “ent_lr” parameter. This deepens diagonal entanglement along the opposite corners without increasing distinct parameters, sharpening gradient signals across distant qubits.

2. Enrich your entangling motif with a narrow CRX sub-block between the offset YY and long-range CRZ layers: insert CRX gates on pairs [1,2], [3,4], and [5,6] all sharing a new parameter “ent_crx.” Heterogeneous two-qubit interactions (XX→YY→CRX→CRZ) have driven expressivity gains in past generations at minimal overhead.

3. Close the nearest-neighbor ring by adding an XX gate on wires [7,0] parameterized by “ent_nn” at the start of the XX block. Imposing cyclic boundary conditions boosts connectivity and leverages the proven strength of shared NN-XX entanglers without adding extra parameters.

4. After your skip-3 CZ layer, append a second pass of the three-block entanglement sequence (NN XX → OFF YY → LR CRZ), reusing the same “ent_nn,” “ent_off,” and “ent_lr” parameters. Doubling this efficient seven-parameter motif can enrich expressivity further while preserving convergence speed and parameter parsimony.

5. Introduce a mid-layer RZ rotation on the odd wires: immediately after your existing RY mid gates on [1,3,5,7], insert RZ gates with a new shared parameter “rz_mid.” This dual-phase RY→RZ bracketing exploits non-commutativity to potentially lift worst-group margins, analogous to the final global RZ booster, with only one extra distinct parameter.
