- 6 distinct parameters, 8 total params
- Worst_group_margin: 0.36 (this is the bottleneck—robust separation on hard samples)
- Validation_margin: 0.73
- Convergence: 30 steps

**Best prior program (score: 0.69):**
- 5 distinct parameters, 7 total params
- Worst_group_margin: 0.36 (same)
- Validation_margin: 0.73 (same)
- Convergence: 30 steps (same)
- Depth: 30 (higher than current 23)
- Gate count: 123 (higher than current 105)

The key observation is:
1. The best program had **fewer distinct parameters** (5 vs. 6) despite higher depth/gate count
2. The best program achieved the same worst_group_margin (0.36) with slightly better combined score (0.69 vs 0.68)
3. Current program has **more parameters** (6 distinct), which reduces parameter efficiency
4. The recommendation suggests deepening the circuit with the proven XX–CRX–YY motif to enrich expressivity

**Strategy:**
The current program has split the initial rotation into `ry_even` and `ry_odd`, adding an extra parameter without improving separation. The prior best program used a unified `ry` parameter. Additionally, I should add the recommended second XX–CRX–YY block right after the first YY gates (before intermediate rotations) to deepen expressivity while maintaining parameter sharing. This mirrors what worked in the second prior program, but I'll integrate it more carefully into the current structure.

The key is to:
1. Reduce distinct parameters back to 5 by unifying `ry_even` and `ry_odd` to `ry`
2. Insert the second XX–CRX–YY entangling sequence (the recommendation) to improve worst_group_margin
3. Keep the proven long-range CRZ and final RZ layers
