# Analysis of Performance

Looking at the metrics:
- **Current program**: Combined score 0.66, worst_group_margin 0.35, validation_margin 0.72
- **Prior programs**: Combined score 0.67, worst_group_margin 0.36, validation_margin 0.73-0.74

The current program has regressed slightly (score 0.66 vs 0.67), with lower worst_group_margin (0.35 vs 0.36). The main issue appears to be that adding the extra CRZ gate `[5, 0]` didn't help—it may have introduced noise or degraded the separation quality.

The recommendation suggests **hybridizing one CRZ to CRY** under "ent_lr" to add axis diversity. This aligns with the prior observation that axis-diverse parametrized gates (like the CRX in earlier versions) improve worst-group margins.

# Proposed Change

**Strategy**: Replace one CRZ gate in the long-range entangling block with CRY. I'll convert the `[2, 5]` pair since it's in the middle of the CRZ sequence and changing this single rotation axis may help the circuit learn different feature separations for hard samples.

**Rationale**:
1. **Axis diversity**: CRY adds Y-axis rotational encoding alongside the dominant Z-axis (CRZ), enriching the entanglement structure
2. **Worst-group separation**: Prior evolution showed that mixed parametrized gate types (CRX + standard gates) improved worst_group_margin; this continues that pattern
3. **Minimal disruption**: Only one gate changes, maintaining parameter economy (still 7 distinct parameters)
4. **Targeted at long-range**: The ent_lr block governs global entanglement patterns critical for separating difficult samples
