<DESCRIPTION>
Interleave the globally shared RY mixer with the first entangling stage instead of rotating every qubit upfront. Even qubits are mixed first, correlated with their odd partners, and only then are odd qubits mixed. This breaks excessive intermediate symmetry and gives the multiscale CZ network richer data flow. The final RX layer reuses the same single parameter, retaining maximal parameter economy while converting phase correlations into measurable populations.
</DESCRIPTION>
