<DESCRIPTION>
Replace two separated fixed CZ links with parameterized CRZ and CRY interactions while retaining the nearest-neighbor chain. The CRZ link adds tunable phase correlation, while the CRY link introduces a noncommuting conditional rotation that can improve separation of the hardest validation group. Only two parameters are added, limiting the efficiency cost while increasing entangling flexibility where the current worst-group margin has the most room to improve.
