shared_mid_crz
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the even/odd RY angles into one globally shared mixer, removing a parity-specific degree of freedom that did not justify its parameter cost. Reinvest that parameter budget in a shared CRZ angle on every distance-2 link in both recurrent passes. This preserves three distinct parameters while adding trainable medium-range phase coupling, which should improve worst-group separation without weakening parameter economy.
</DESCRIPTION>
