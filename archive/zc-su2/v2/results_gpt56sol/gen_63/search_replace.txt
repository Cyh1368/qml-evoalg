<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the redundant leading RX layer and tie the remaining RY and RX mixers to one global parameter, recovering the stronger one-parameter regularization and parameter efficiency seen in prior results. Then append a parameter-free distance-4 CZ echo after the final RX layer. This preserves the economical multiscale butterfly structure while reinforcing cross-half correlations immediately before readout, which may improve the worst-group margin without adding optimization variables.
</DESCRIPTION>
