<DESCRIPTION>
A compact, regularized ansatz using three distinct trainable parameters. Even and odd qubits share separate RY parameters, preserving limited spatial flexibility while greatly reducing overfitting and optimization cost. A closed CZ ring distributes correlations across every qubit, including the previously disconnected 7-to-0 boundary. The shared RX mixer provides a noncommuting final rotation that remains useful to the readout and across repeated blocks. This reduces the block from 24 distinct parameters and 31 gates to 3 distinct parameters and 24 gates, targeting better parameter efficiency, convergence, and worst-group generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
