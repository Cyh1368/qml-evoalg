<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the even/odd RY angles into one globally shared mixer, removing positional over-specialization while freeing one parameter slot. Reuse that slot as a shared CRZ angle on all distance-2 links. This keeps the current three distinct parameters but reallocates capacity from local degrees of freedom to medium-range phase correlations, which should improve worst-group separation and small-sample generalization without increasing gate count.
</DESCRIPTION>
