<DESCRIPTION>
Restore the stronger fixed-CZ long-range backbone, since the trainable XX links reduced worst-group margin and doubled convergence time. Reallocate that trainable capacity to a mild, structured symmetry break: qubits 0–3 and 4–7 receive separate shared RY angles, while the RX layer remains globally tied. This keeps only three distinct parameters, preserves strong regularization within each half, and may improve hard-group separation by allowing the two circuit halves to adapt differently.
</DESCRIPTION>
