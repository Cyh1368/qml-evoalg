<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed distance-2 CZ couplings in both butterfly stages with CRZ gates tied to the existing “global_mix” parameter. This adds tunable medium-range phase correlations without increasing the distinct parameter count, preserving strong small-data regularization while potentially improving worst-group separation and convergence. Distance-1 and distance-4 CZ gates remain fixed to retain the effective multiscale connectivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
