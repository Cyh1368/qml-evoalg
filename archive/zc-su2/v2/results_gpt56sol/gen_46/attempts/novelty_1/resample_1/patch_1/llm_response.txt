split_ry_mixer
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Partially break the overly restrictive global RY symmetry by assigning qubits 0–3 and 4–7 separate shared mixer parameters while retaining the globally tied RX tilt. This adds only one distinct parameter, preserving strong small-data regularization and parameter economy while allowing the two circuit halves to respond differently before their distance-4 coupling. The added flexibility may improve the worst-group margin without materially increasing optimization difficulty or circuit depth.
</DESCRIPTION>
