<DESCRIPTION>
Replace only the distance-2 fixed CZ couplings with controlled phase rotations tied to one shared parameter, `mid_crz`. This adds a compact trainable medium-range interaction that can adapt the butterfly network to difficult validation groups while retaining strong symmetry and regularization. All four links share one angle, limiting the ansatz to three distinct parameters and avoiding the overfitting risk of independently parameterized couplings.
</DESCRIPTION>
