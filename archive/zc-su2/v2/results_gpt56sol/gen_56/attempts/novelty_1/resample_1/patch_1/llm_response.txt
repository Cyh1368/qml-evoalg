<DESCRIPTION>
Split the final RX mixer into independently shared left- and right-half tilts. This adds only one distinct parameter while preserving strong within-half symmetry and the economical butterfly structure. The extra half-specific freedom may better convert asymmetric cross-half correlations into observable signals, improving the hardest-group margin while retaining substantial parameter sharing and regularization.
</DESCRIPTION>
