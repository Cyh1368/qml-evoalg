<DESCRIPTION>
Uses the efficient single-stage butterfly architecture from the strongest prior candidate while making only the distance-4 links trainable. Four shared CRZ gates introduce one economical nonlocal parameter that can adapt the weakest cross-half correlations, while fixed short-range CZ gates preserve regularization, low depth, and fast convergence.
