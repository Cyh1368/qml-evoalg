<DESCRIPTION>
Prepend a globally shared RX seed layer using the existing global_tilt parameter. This adds an orthogonal mixer before the RY layer, breaking restrictive initial-axis symmetry and potentially improving gradient flow, convergence speed, and worst-group separation. Reusing global_tilt preserves the current distinct-parameter count and its regularization benefit.
</DESCRIPTION>
