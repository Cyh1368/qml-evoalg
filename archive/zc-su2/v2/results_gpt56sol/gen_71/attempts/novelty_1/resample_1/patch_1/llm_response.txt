<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed distance-2 CZ layer with trainable CRZ correlations tied to the existing global_mix angle. This introduces adaptive mesoscopic phase structure without adding a distinct parameter. Also tie the final RX mixer to global_mix, reducing the ansatz from two distinct parameters to one. The stronger sharing should improve parameter efficiency, regularization, and convergence while retaining the staggered multiscale topology that produced the current strong worst-group margin.
</DESCRIPTION>
