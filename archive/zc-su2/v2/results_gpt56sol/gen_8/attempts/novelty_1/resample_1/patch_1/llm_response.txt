<DESCRIPTION>
Replaces the parameter-heavy linear chain with a three-stage butterfly entanglement network. This gives every qubit a short path to every other qubit while reducing circuit depth through parallel pairings. Even and odd wires use tied RY parameters, followed by a globally shared RX mixer, reducing the ansatz from 24 distinct parameters to only 3. The strong parameter sharing acts as regularization for the small training set, while the multiscale connectivity supports robust separation of difficult samples.
</DESCRIPTION>
