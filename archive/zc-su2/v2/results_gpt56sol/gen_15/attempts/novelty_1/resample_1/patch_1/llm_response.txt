<DESCRIPTION>
This design preserves distinct RY parameters so every qubit can learn its own feature-mixing angle, while imposing reflection symmetry only on the phase layers. Qubits in mirror pairs (0,7), (1,6), (2,5), and (3,4) share their pre-entanglement and post-entanglement RZ parameters. This reduces the ansatz from 24 to 16 distinct parameters without enforcing the overly restrictive global or parity sharing seen in the current program. The fixed nearest-neighbor CZ chain retains the strong generalization, margin, and convergence behavior of the original architecture while the structured sharing improves parameter efficiency and regularizes training on the small dataset.
</DESCRIPTION>
