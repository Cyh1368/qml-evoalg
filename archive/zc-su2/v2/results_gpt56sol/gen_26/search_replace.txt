<DESCRIPTION>
Restore the strongly regularized global RY symmetry and fixed CZ butterfly that previously outperformed the current five-parameter design, while adding one globally shared RZ phase-alignment parameter between mixing and entanglement. This reduces distinct parameters from five to three, removes asymmetric trainable links that slowed convergence, and retains limited phase flexibility that may improve the hardest-group margin across repeated blocks.
</DESCRIPTION>
