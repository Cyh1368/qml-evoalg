<DESCRIPTION>
Replace both distance-2 CZ layers with parametrized XX interactions tied to the existing “global_tilt” parameter. This preserves the two-parameter economy while introducing non-Ising-diagonal correlations on medium-range butterfly links. The added noncommuting interaction may improve worst-group separation without increasing the number of distinct trainable parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
