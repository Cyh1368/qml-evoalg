single_block_half_tilts

<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the redundant in-spec repetition, since the fixed architecture already repeats the ansatz externally with tied parameters. This halves ansatz depth and gate count, reducing overprocessing and convergence cost. Reallocate the existing three-parameter budget from parity-specific input rotations to one strongly regularized global RY mixer plus separate left/right RX tilts. The half-specific output basis allows asymmetric phase-to-population conversion across the two four-qubit partitions, potentially improving the hardest-group margin without increasing the current distinct parameter count.
</DESCRIPTION>
