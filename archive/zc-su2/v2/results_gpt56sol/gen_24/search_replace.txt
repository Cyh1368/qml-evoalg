<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single global RY angle with four mirror-pair-shared angles for wires (0,7), (1,6), (2,5), and (3,4). This preserves reflection symmetry and substantial parameter sharing while allowing different spatial pairs to adapt to heterogeneous hidden difficulty groups. The added local flexibility may improve the worst-group margin without resorting to eight independent mixer parameters or changing circuit depth and gate count.
</DESCRIPTION>
