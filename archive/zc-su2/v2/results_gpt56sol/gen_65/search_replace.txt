tied_phase_single_pass

<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the parity-specific and output angles into one globally shared parameter, remove the redundant second pass, and add a tied RZ phase layer between distance-2 and distance-4 entanglers. This preserves the strongest one-parameter regularization seen in prior results while introducing phase control at the multiscale transition. The shallower circuit should reduce optimization redundancy and maintain generalization without paying an additional parameter-efficiency penalty.
</DESCRIPTION>
