<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the four distance-2 controlled-phase rotations with symmetric XX interactions tied to the existing global_tilt parameter. This removes global_phase, reducing the ansatz from three to two distinct trainable parameters while retaining tunable medium-range correlations. The transverse coupling complements the RY mixer and CZ network, may expose hard-sample correlations more directly, and should improve parameter efficiency and convergence without increasing gate count.
