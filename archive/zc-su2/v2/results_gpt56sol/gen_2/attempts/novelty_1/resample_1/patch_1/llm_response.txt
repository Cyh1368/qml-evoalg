parity_shared_ring

<DESCRIPTION>
Tie rotation parameters by even/odd qubit parity, reducing the ansatz from 24 distinct circuit parameters to 6 while retaining more flexibility than fully global sharing. This symmetry-based regularization should improve few-sample generalization, parameter efficiency, and convergence while preserving the alternating structure needed to distinguish encoded features. Closing the CZ chain into a ring removes weakly connected boundary qubits and gives every qubit equal entangling degree, which may improve the worst-group margin.
</DESCRIPTION>
