<DESCRIPTION>
Interleave the shared mixer with the entangling network instead of mixing every qubit before all CZ gates. Mix qubits 0–3 first, immediately apply the four distance-4 CZs, and then mix qubits 4–7 before the distance-2 and distance-1 layers. This injects nonlocal correlations earlier in each repeated block while retaining the same two shared parameters, gate count, and broad connectivity. The reordered hierarchy may improve worst-group separation and convergence without sacrificing parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
