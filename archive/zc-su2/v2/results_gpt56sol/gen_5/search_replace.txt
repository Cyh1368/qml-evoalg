parity_shared_ring
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Share rotation parameters between even and odd qubits, matching the bipartite structure of the entangling chain. This reduces the ansatz from 24 distinct parameters to 6 while retaining more spatial expressivity than fully global sharing. The resulting regularization should improve few-shot generalization, convergence, and parameter efficiency. Closing the CZ chain into a ring removes weakly entangled boundary qubits and gives every qubit equal entangling degree, which may improve the hardest-group margin without adding trainable parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
