<DESCRIPTION>
Insert a globally shared RZ layer between the distance-2 and distance-1 correlation stages. This adds only one distinct parameter while providing a dedicated phase-control degree of freedom at the mesoscopic scale. Placing it after distance-2 entanglement and before the final local CZ stage lets subsequent correlations convert the tied phase adjustment into observable interference, potentially increasing the worst-group margin without sacrificing the circuit’s strong symmetry-based regularization.
</DESCRIPTION>
