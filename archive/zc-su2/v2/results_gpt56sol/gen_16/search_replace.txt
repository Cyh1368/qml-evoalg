<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the deep chain-plus-matching entanglement network with a balanced butterfly topology. The disjoint distance-1, distance-2, and distance-4 layers propagate information across all qubits with substantially lower routing depth. The existing CRZ and CRY parameters are retained on two distance-2 links, so this adds multiscale trainable correlations without increasing the distinct parameter count. This should improve convergence and worst-group separation while preserving strong parameter economy.
</DESCRIPTION>
