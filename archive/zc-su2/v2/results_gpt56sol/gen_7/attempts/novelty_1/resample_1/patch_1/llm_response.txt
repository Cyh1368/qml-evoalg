remove_commuting_rz_redundancy
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the post-entanglement RZ layer. Every CZ commutes with every RZ in the surrounding layers, so each wire’s pre/post RZ pair is equivalent to one RZ whose angle is their sum. Keeping one independently parametrized RZ per wire therefore preserves the realizable block family while eliminating eight redundant parameters and eight gates. This removes flat parameter directions, improves parameter efficiency, reduces depth, and should accelerate convergence without sacrificing the current separation capacity.
</DESCRIPTION>
