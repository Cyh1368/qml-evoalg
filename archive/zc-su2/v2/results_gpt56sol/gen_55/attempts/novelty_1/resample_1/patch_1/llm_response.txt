<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the four distance-2 CZ gates with XX interactions tied to the existing global_tilt parameter. This preserves the two-parameter economy while introducing trainable, non-Ising medium-range correlations that do not commute with the surrounding RY mixing and remaining CZ network. The added transverse expressivity may improve worst-group separation without increasing the distinct parameter count.
