<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the output RX layer to the existing global_mix parameter, reducing the ansatz from two distinct parameters to one. This strengthens regularization, improves parameter efficiency, and follows the prior one-parameter circuit’s faster convergence and higher combined score. Then append a parameter-free echo of the long-range CZ couplings to reinforce cross-half correlations after phase information has been converted by the noncommuting RX layer, potentially improving worst-group separation without adding trainable parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
