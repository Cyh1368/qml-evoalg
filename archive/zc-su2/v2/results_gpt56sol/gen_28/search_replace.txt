<DESCRIPTION>
Append a second complete RY–CZ butterfly–RX stage while strictly reusing the existing global_mix and global_tilt parameters. This increases correlation-processing depth and gives the noncommuting mixers another opportunity to convert global phase structure into measurable populations, potentially improving the hardest-group margin. Because no new parameter names are introduced, the strong two-parameter regularization and parameter-efficiency advantage are preserved.
</DESCRIPTION>
