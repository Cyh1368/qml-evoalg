shared_midrange_regularizer
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Collapse the parity-specific RY angles into one global mixer and replace the heterogeneous distance-2 layer with four CRZ links sharing one parameter. This reduces distinct parameters from five to three, strengthening parameter efficiency and small-data regularization while retaining trainable medium-range phase correlations that may improve worst-group separation. The uniform distance-2 structure also restores butterfly symmetry and should simplify optimization relative to independently trained CRZ/CRY interactions.
</DESCRIPTION>
