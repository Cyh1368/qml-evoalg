<DESCRIPTION>
This crossover retains the current circuit’s noncommuting RX/RY mixing and the inspiration circuit’s globally shared parameter, eliminating the less efficient second trainable angle. It front-loads cross-half CZ connections, then completes distance-2 and distance-1 butterfly correlations before a shared RX readout mixer. This preserves multiscale expressivity while using one distinct parameter, fewer gates, lower depth, and a structure previously associated with faster convergence and stronger validation performance.
