<DESCRIPTION>
This crossover retains the current program’s compact single-stage depth while adopting the inspiration program’s strongest regularization: every trainable gate shares one global parameter. Long-range CZ connections are front-loaded between the two halves, followed by distance-2 and distance-1 butterfly layers. A tied RX output mixer supplies a noncommuting basis change without increasing parameter count. This structure previously showed faster convergence, lower validation loss, and better parameter efficiency than the current three-parameter implementation.
