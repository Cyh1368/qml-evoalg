<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the two trainable XX links with fixed CZ links and tie the output RX layer to the existing global mixer. This restores a complete fixed multiscale butterfly while reducing the ansatz from three distinct parameters to one. The stronger regularization should improve parameter efficiency, convergence, and worst-group generalization; prior feedback also indicates that fixed CZ correlations outperform the added trainable XX interactions.
</DESCRIPTION>
