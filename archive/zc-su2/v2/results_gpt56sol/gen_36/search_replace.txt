<DESCRIPTION>
Reorder the fixed CZ butterfly from short-to-long range into distance-4, distance-2, then distance-1 order. This tests whether front-loading nonlocal correlations improves worst-group separation while preserving the exact gate set, depth profile, parameter count, and strong global parameter tying.
</DESCRIPTION>
