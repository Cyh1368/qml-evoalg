<DESCRIPTION>
The current circuit uses three distinct parameters, two repeated butterfly stages, depth 30, and convergence around 60 steps. Although parity-specific mixing slightly improves the worst-group margin, it reduces parameter efficiency and increases the generalization gap.

The new ansatz returns to one compact multiscale stage and ties all local RY/RX rotations to `global_mix`. A second parameter, `global_link`, is used only by the four distance-4 CRZ gates, concentrating limited extra capacity on nonlocal correlations that are most relevant to worst-group separation. Distance-1 and distance-2 CZ links remain fixed regularizers. This reduces the circuit depth and gate count by roughly half while using only two distinct trainable parameters, targeting faster convergence and better parameter efficiency without removing focused nonlocal adaptability.
