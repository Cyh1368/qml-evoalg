<DESCRIPTION>
This design retains the parameter-efficient global mixing and multiscale butterfly connectivity that performed best, while replacing the fixed distance-2 CZ layer with tunable CRZ interactions. Rather than adding a third parameter, the medium-range phase couplings share `phase_readout` with the final RX layer. This phase-locks correlation generation to its noncommuting readout conversion, yielding richer dynamics while preserving only two distinct trainable parameters. The resulting strongly regularized, low-dimensional optimization should converge reliably and may improve worst-group separation without sacrificing parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
