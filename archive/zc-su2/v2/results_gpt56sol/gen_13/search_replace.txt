<DESCRIPTION>
Prepend each local RY rotation with an RX rotation to introduce a second, noncommuting rotation axis before the RZ and CZ stages. This can improve landscape conditioning and let the repeated block form more useful feature directions, potentially increasing the difficult-group margin. All eight RX gates share one parameter, adding only a single degree of freedom while imposing a strong regularizing symmetry suited to the very small training set.
</DESCRIPTION>
