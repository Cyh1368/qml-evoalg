<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the distance-2 controlled-phase angle to the existing output-mixer angle. This reduces the ansatz from three distinct parameters to two while retaining the trainable medium-range correlations that improved the worst-group margin. The coupling also imposes a strong phase-to-population inductive bias, which should regularize the small-data model and potentially reduce convergence time.
</DESCRIPTION>
