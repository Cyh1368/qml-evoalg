Remove the redundant explicit second copy because the fixed architecture already repeats the entire ansatz with tied parameters. Merge the parity-specific RY angles into one global mixer to reduce the distinct parameter count from three to two and improve small-sample regularization. Finally, replace two long-range CZ links with XX interactions tied to the existing global mixer, adding correlation diversity without adding a trainable degree of freedom.
