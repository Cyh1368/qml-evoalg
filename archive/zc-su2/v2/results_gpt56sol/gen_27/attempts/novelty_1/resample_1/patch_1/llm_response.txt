<DESCRIPTION>
This restores the better-performing globally shared local mixers, reducing the model from five to three distinct parameters. Two distance-4 CZ links are replaced by XX interactions sharing one `global_xx` parameter. This preserves the economical butterfly structure while testing a trainable non-CZ long-range correlation channel that may improve separation of the hardest validation group. The remaining fixed CZ links maintain broad connectivity and limit overfitting.
