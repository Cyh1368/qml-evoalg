<DESCRIPTION>
Improve the ansatz by replacing some fixed CZ gates with parametrized controlled rotations (CRY) to increase expressivity while maintaining the same entanglement structure. This adds more trainable parameters but should improve the model's ability to learn complex patterns in the data.
