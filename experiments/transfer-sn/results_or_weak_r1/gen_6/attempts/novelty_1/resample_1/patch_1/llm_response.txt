<DESCRIPTION>
The previous ansatz was a simple chain of CZ gates with local RY and RZ rotations, which provided basic connectivity but limited expressivity. I have introduced a more robust ansatz structure:
1. **Layered Rotations:** Used both RY and RX rotations for each qubit in every block, increasing the reachable state space volume compared to a single-rotation type.
2. **Enhanced Entanglement:** Replaced the linear CZ chain with a circular chain (0-1, 1-2, ..., 7-0) for better connectivity and introduced a layer of CRZ gates. This allows the circuit to learn entanglement patterns conditional on data features more effectively.
3. **Parameter Efficiency:** The total number of parameters is increased slightly (from 24 to 28 per block) to utilize the increased expressivity of the CRZ gates without overfitting, balancing the model capacity against the dataset size.
</DESCRIPTION>
