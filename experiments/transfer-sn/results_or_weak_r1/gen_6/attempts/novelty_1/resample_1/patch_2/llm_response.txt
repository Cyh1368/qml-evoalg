<DESCRIPTION>
The previous ansatz used a redundant RY-RZ-RZ structure. This version improves the expressivity by utilizing a full rotation set (RX, RY, RZ) on each qubit, allowing the circuit to explore the full Bloch sphere per qubit within each block. This is combined with a circular CZ entangling layer, which provides better connectivity and long-range interaction than a linear chain, without increasing the total parameter count. This should improve convergence and model capacity.
</DESCRIPTION>
