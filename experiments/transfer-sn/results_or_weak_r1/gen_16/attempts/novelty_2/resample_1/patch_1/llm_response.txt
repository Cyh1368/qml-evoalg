<DESCRIPTION>
The previous ansatz was overly complex with 122 parameters, leading to significant overfitting (high training accuracy, larger train-test gap). I am simplifying the ansatz block to reduce the parameter count to 56, employing a more efficient design with shared-parameter rotations (RY, RZ) and a single-parameter shared CRX entangling layer, followed by fixed CZ interactions. This follows the recommendation to introduce shared-parameter entangling layers and prunes redundant rotation layers to improve generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
