<add_learnable_cross_crz>
Make the “opposite-chord” long-range entangling layer learnable by replacing the 4 fixed CZ gates with CRZ gates sharing a single trainable parameter per ansatz block. This keeps parameter efficiency high (adds only 1 parameter per block) while increasing expressivity of cross-chord correlations, which often improves both validation accuracy and convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
