<DESCRIPTION>
This edit enhances the ansatz by introducing more sophisticated entanglement and rotation patterns to improve expressivity and training efficiency. Key changes include:
1. Adding CRZ gates between neighboring qubit pairs to increase entanglement capacity
2. Introducing additional RY rotations to complement existing RZ rotations
3. Using CRX gates in a staggered pattern for more complex parameterized interactions
4. Adding a final layer of RY rotations to ensure full expressivity
These improvements target better feature separation and faster convergence while maintaining the same parameter count and structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
