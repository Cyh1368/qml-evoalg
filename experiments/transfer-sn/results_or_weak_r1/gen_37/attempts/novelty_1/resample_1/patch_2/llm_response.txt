<DESCRIPTION>
In the current EVOLVE block, replace the four long-range “opposite-chord” CZ gates with four CRZ gates that share a single trainable parameter (crz_chord). This keeps the model compact while increasing conditional phase expressivity versus fixed CZ, which often improves classification accuracy and convergence speed.
