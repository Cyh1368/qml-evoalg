<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the four parameter-free long-range “opposite-chord” CZ gates with four CRZ gates sharing a single trainable parameter. This increases the ansatz’s ability to create conditional phase correlations (often more sample-efficient than adding more single-qubit rotations), while keeping parameter count low by removing the RX layer entirely.
</DESCRIPTION>
