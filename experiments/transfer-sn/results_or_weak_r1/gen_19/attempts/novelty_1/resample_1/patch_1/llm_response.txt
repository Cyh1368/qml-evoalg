coarseshare_ry_rx_rz_reduce_params
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce parameter count by using coarse parameter sharing across the two halves of the register (wires 0–3 vs 4–7), while improving expressivity by adding a shared RX layer in addition to the existing shared RY and RZ pre/post layers. Keep the same parameter-free CZ entangling structure (two interleaved rings + opposite chords), so changes are concentrated on trainable single-qubit rotations where the previous variants showed strong performance with aggressive sharing.
</DESCRIPTION>
