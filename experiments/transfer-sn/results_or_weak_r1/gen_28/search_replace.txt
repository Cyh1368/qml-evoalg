<DESCRIPTION>
This modification enhances the ansatz by adding controlled rotations (CRY gates) between neighboring qubit pairs to increase expressivity without significantly increasing parameter count. It also reverts to a proven entanglement structure that showed good performance in previous iterations. The changes include:
1. Adding CRY gates between neighboring qubits to increase expressivity
2. Maintaining the coarse-grained parameter sharing structure for better generalization
3. Keeping the enhanced CZ entanglement structure from previous successful iterations
4. Maintaining the same N_UPLOADS=3 and N_REPEATS=2 structure which showed better convergence
</DESCRIPTION>
