<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed, parameter-free CZ entangling layer with a parameterized CRZ entangling structure (ring + chord) and add separate RZ layers before and after entanglement (as in the best-performing prior architecture). This increases trainable expressivity per block while keeping parameter sharing coarse-grained (few unique parameter keys), which typically improves both validation/test accuracy and the losses, reducing the train-test gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
