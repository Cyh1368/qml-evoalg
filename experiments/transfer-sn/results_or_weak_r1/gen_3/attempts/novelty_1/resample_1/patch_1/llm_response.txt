<AMT_shared_ryrz_params_ring_cz>
<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce parameter count per ansatz block by aggressively sharing single-qubit parameters across qubit pairs (4 distinct RY params + 4 distinct pre-RZ params + 4 distinct post-RZ params = 12 params per block instead of 24), while increasing entangling connectivity via a two-ring CZ pattern (no extra trainable parameters). This should improve the combined score mainly through better parameter efficiency, while maintaining expressive power through repeated feature uploads and two block repetitions per upload.
</DESCRIPTION>
