<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the parity-heavy 50-parameter block with a leaner global bottleneck ansatz. The new design keeps the full ring of CZ entanglers to spread feature-map correlations, but collapses the local mixing into shared all-qubit RX/RY layers and adds only two shared CRZ links across complementary long-range pairs. A final shared RZ layer preserves phase flexibility for the Z-readout. The goal is to reduce overfitting, lower depth and gate count, and improve optimization speed while retaining enough nonlinearity for the binary classification task.
</DESCRIPTION>
