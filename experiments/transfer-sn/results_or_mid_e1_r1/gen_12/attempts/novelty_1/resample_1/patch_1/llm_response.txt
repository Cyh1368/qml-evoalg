<NAME>
shared_residual_rx
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Add a small shared in-circuit residual RX correction at the end of each ansatz block. Reusing one parameter on wires 0 and 4 gives the block a lightweight calibration-like degree of freedom without materially increasing circuit complexity. This tends to improve gradient flow after the post-RZ layer, can reduce underfitting on the validation split, and is consistent with the fixed readout already using a learned gain/bias. The change is minimal, preserves the existing training loop and measurement, and only increases the per-block parameter count by one.
</DESCRIPTION>
