<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the fully untied per-qubit RY/RZ stack with a much smaller parity-tied hardware-efficient block.

Why this should help:
- Fewer trainable circuit parameters per block reduces overfitting and improves parameter efficiency.
- Using shared parameters on even and odd qubits keeps enough locality to model structured binary-feature correlations without learning eight nearly redundant angles per layer.
- A full CZ ring still provides global entanglement across the 8-qubit register, which is important for mixing the fixed IsingZZ feature map output into the Z readout basis.
- Two noncommuting local rotation layers, placed before and after entanglement, preserve expressive power while making optimization easier for Adam.

Overall, this is a compact ansatz intended to improve validation/test accuracy and reduce the train-test gap with substantially fewer parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
