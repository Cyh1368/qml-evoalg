<DESCRIPTION>
This edit compresses the ansatz from a fully independent 24-parameter block to an 8-parameter block by using symmetry-aware parameter sharing.

Design rationale:
- Parity-shared RX/RY/RZ layers reduce overfitting and improve parameter efficiency.
- A full CZ ring preserves local and wrap-around entanglement across all 8 qubits.
- Two shared cross-half controlled-rotation layers (CRZ and CRY) add higher-order nonlinear coupling with only two extra parameters per block.
- The block remains expressive enough for the repeated re-uploading schedule while aiming for better validation/test accuracy per parameter and lower train-test gap.

This keeps the ansatz valid, compact, and compatible with the fixed training loop and measurement pipeline.
</DESCRIPTION>
