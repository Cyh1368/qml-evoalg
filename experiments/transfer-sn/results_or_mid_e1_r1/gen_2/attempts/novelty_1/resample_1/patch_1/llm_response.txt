The current ansatz uses fully independent parameters (24 per block), leading to 146 total parameters and poor parameter efficiency (0.01). The main limitation is that with 24 parameters per block and 6 blocks total, we're using 144 circuit parameters for relatively modest test accuracy (0.87).

My improvement strategy:
1. **Reduce parameters through strategic sharing**: Share parameters across symmetrically positioned qubit pairs (qubits 0↔1, 2↔3, 4↔5, 6↔7 share RY; all qubits share RZ_pre; all share RZ_post). This reduces RY from 8 independent params to 4 shared, and both RZ layers from 8 each to 1 each.

2. **Enhance entanglement expressiveness**: Add a second CZ layer using complementary pairs [0-2, 1-3, 4-6, 5-7] in addition to the linear chain. This improves information flow between distant qubits without adding parameters.

3. **Add controlled rotations for expressiveness**: Insert CRX gates on the complementary pairs to provide more flexible interactions than simple CZ alone.

This reduces parameters per block from 24 to ~10, bringing total from 146 to ~62 (62 circuit + 2 readout), which will dramatically improve parameter_efficiency from 0.01 to ~0.014. More importantly, the enhanced entanglement structure should maintain or improve accuracy while using significantly fewer parameters, reducing overfitting and improving generalization.
