<DESCRIPTION>
This edit introduces a controlled symmetry break in the final layer of the ansatz block. By releasing the parameter sharing for the RZ gates on qubits 2 and 5 (previously shared as 'rz_g2'), we provide the model with a slightly larger hypothesis space to calibrate the readout signals of the central qubits independently. The other three pairs (0/7, 1/6, 3/4) remain mirror-shared to maintain parameter efficiency and regularization. This targeted increase in expressive power aims to reduce the generalization gap and improve validation accuracy by allowing more flexible phase refinement before the final Z-measurement averaging.
</DESCRIPTION>
