<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz utilizes a fixed entangling scaffold composed of four long-range CZ gates and four local CZ gates. To improve the circuit's ability to learn specific correlations between adjacent qubits while maintaining parameter efficiency, I am replacing the four local CZ gates ([0,1], [2,3], [4,5], [6,7]) with two pairs of mirror-shared CRZ rotations. Specifically, one parameter 'crz_loc_0' is shared between the outer local pairs (0,1) and (6,7), and another parameter 'crz_loc_1' is shared between the inner local pairs (2,3) and (4,5). This hybrid approach grants the model tunable Z-axis entanglement at the local level—complementing the global entanglement provided by the fixed long-range CZ gates—potentially improving the validation loss and overall classification accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
