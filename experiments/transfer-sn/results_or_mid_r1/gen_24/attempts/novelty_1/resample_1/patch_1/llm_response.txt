<DESCRIPTION>
The recommendation suggests alternating between two mirror-symmetric CZ mesh patterns to diversify entanglement pathways. While full alternation across blocks cannot be implemented within ANSATZ_SPEC alone, we can incorporate both patterns within each block by adding parametrized CRZ gates on the complementary long-range diagonal [0,5], [1,4], [2,7], [3,6].

This approach:
1. Enriches the entanglement topology by combining the primary long-range diagonal [0,4], [1,5], [2,6], [3,7] with its complementary pattern
2. Uses shared CRZ parameters (crz_comp_outer and crz_comp_inner) to maintain parameter efficiency
3. Introduces only 2 new trainable parameters per block (12 total across all blocks), scaling the total to ~110 parameters
4. Improves expressivity for capturing broader qubit correlations without excessive growth in circuit depth

The placement after local CZ gates and before the middle RZ layer allows the complementary entanglement to interact with the local structure before phase mixing, potentially improving both convergence speed and generalization by providing richer pathways for information flow during data re-uploading.
