<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the parameter-heavy CRX ring with two complementary CZ entangling meshes and a more compact mirrored single-qubit rotation pattern. This follows the recommendation to diversify entanglement pathways while reducing the number of trainable parameters per block from 12 to 8, which should improve parameter efficiency and help generalization without changing the fixed training loop or measurement pipeline. The final reused RY layer adds nonlinearity after the second CZ mesh while keeping the block lightweight.
</DESCRIPTION>
