<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses a linear chain of CZ gates and rotations (RY, RZ) which may limit the expressivity and information flow across the 8-qubit register, especially given that the feature map uses IsingZZ couplings on varied qubit pairs. 

I propose a more expressive Hardware-Efficient Ansatz (HEA) inspired structure:
1.  **Increased Connectivity**: Replacing the linear CZ chain with two layers of CNOT gates: a circular chain (neighboring qubits) and a skip-1 chain (0-2, 1-3, etc.). This ensures that information from any qubit can reach any other qubit much faster, improving the model's ability to capture complex feature correlations.
2.  **Strategic Rotation Layers**: Using two RY layers and one RZ layer (24 parameters total per block). Since the feature map is Z-basis heavy, having multiple RY layers allows the circuit to effectively rotate the state into more expressive regions of the Hilbert space.
3.  **Parameter Parity**: The parameter count per block remains 24, identical to the seed. This ensures that any performance gain is due to the architectural improvement rather than just increasing the parameter budget.
</DESCRIPTION>
