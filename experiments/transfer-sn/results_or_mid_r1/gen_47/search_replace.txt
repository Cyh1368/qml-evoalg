The candidate evolves the ansatz by replacing the hybrid CZ scaffold and mixed-axis controlled rotations with a more structured and symmetric topology.

1.  **Systematic Mirroring**: Maintains the mirror symmetry (mapping qubit $i$ to $7-i$) which was successful in the previous iteration, ensuring parameter efficiency.
2.  **CNOT Ring Scaffold**: Replaces the localized CZ gates with a circular CNOT ring (0-1, 1-2, ..., 7-0). CNOT gates provide stronger basis-state mixing than CZ, and the ring topology ensures no qubit is isolated, facilitating global information flow across the 8-qubit register.
3.  **Expanded Mirrored CRY Core**: Replaces the four-key mixed core with a 4-parameter (8-gate) CRY layer. CRY is particularly effective for binary classification as it directly modulates the amplitudes that determine the PauliZ expectations used in the readout. The chosen pairings (0-4, 1-5, 2-6, 3-7) and their mirrors provide long-range interaction while maintaining the same 12-parameter-per-block efficiency as the previous best candidate.
4.  **Refined Phase Layer**: Retains the RZ layer for final phase alignment before measurement, which is crucial for calibrating the expectations of the Ising-encoded features.
