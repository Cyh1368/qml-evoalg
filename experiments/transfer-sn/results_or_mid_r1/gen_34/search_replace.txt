<DESCRIPTION>
This edit introduces a dual-stage entanglement strategy within each ansatz block. It replaces the simple repeated rotation layers with a more expressive configuration:
1.  **Mirrored RY Pre-rotations**: Compactly lifts features into the Hilbert space (4 parameters).
2.  **Local CZ Scaffold**: Provides initial spatial mixing between adjacent qubits.
3.  **Mixed-Axis Core 1 (CRX-CRY-CRZ-CRY)**: Introduces non-commuting controlled rotations for high expressivity (4 parameters).
4.  **Mirrored RZ Post-rotations**: Refines the phase before the next entanglement stage (4 parameters).
5.  **Mixed-Axis Core 2 (CRZ-CRX-CRY-CRX)**: Cycles the rotation axes over the same qubit pairs to capture complex correlations (4 parameters).
6.  **Global CZ Scaffold**: Ensures long-range entanglement between the two 4-qubit halves of the register.

By using 16 unique parameters per block (98 total for the circuit), this design balances parameter efficiency with a significantly higher entanglement capacity, aiming to improve test accuracy and convergence speed while maintaining a depth well below the limit.
