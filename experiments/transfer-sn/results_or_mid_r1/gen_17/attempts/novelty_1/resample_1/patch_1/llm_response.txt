<DESCRIPTION>
This edit improves the ansatz expressivity by replacing the grouped rotations and CRZ ring with independent single-qubit rotations (RY, RX, RZ) and a robust entanglement structure using CNOT gates. 

1.  **Full Expressivity**: It applies independent RY and RX rotations on all 8 qubits (16 unique parameters). This allows the circuit to better navigate the Hilbert space before and after the entangling layers.
2.  **CNOT Entanglement**: It replaces the CRZ ring with a more standard CNOT ladder (0->1, 1->2, ... 7->0) and a set of "leap" CNOTs (0->4, 1->5, 2->6, 3->7). This combination of local and global connectivity facilitates better information propagation across the 8-qubit register.
3.  **Corrective Phase**: It includes a final layer of independent RZ rotations (8 parameters), as suggested by the performance of prior successful models, which helps calibrate the phase before subsequent feature uploads or measurement.
4.  **Parameter Count**: The total parameters per block increases to 24 (8 RY + 8 RX + 8 RZ), resulting in 146 total parameters. This is well-suited for the Adam optimizer's capacity and provides the necessary degrees of freedom to minimize the L2 loss more effectively than the current grouped configuration.
