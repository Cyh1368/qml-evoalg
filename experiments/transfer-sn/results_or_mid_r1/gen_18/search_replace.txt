1. Removing the redundant individual RZ-pre layer (8 params) that adds little value
2. Removing the individual RX-post layer (8 params) that duplicates functionality
3. Adopting the proven parameter-sharing structure from the second program:
   - Individual RY on each qubit for input flexibility (8 params)
   - Fixed CZ gates for entanglement: diagonal pairs [0,4],[1,5],[2,6],[3,7] plus local adjacent pairs
   - RZ layer with 2-qubit parameter sharing by symmetry: 4 params shared across mirror-symmetric qubit pairs
   - RY layer with 2-qubit parameter sharing: 4 params shared across mirror-symmetric qubit pairs

This reduces to 16 params/block (96 total circuit params) which matches the best-performing second program, reducing overfitting while maintaining expressive power through diverse fixed CZ connectivity and strategic parameter sharing.

   - Proven to balance expressivity with generalization

   - Leverages the natural symmetry of 8-qubit systems
   - Reduces overfitting while preserving model capacity
   - Improves parameter efficiency (score 0.01 in prior programs)

4. **Simplified layer structure**: Removes redundant layers (RZ-pre and RX-post) that don't contribute meaningfully but add 16 extra parameters causing overfitting.

This design directly addresses why the current program (score -0.13) underperforms despite higher parameter count, aligning with the high-scoring second program's architecture while maintaining the fixed training pipeline.
