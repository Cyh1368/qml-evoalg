<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz already performs well, but it still enforces a hard symmetry on the outer qubits by sharing the same `ry_out_0` parameter between wires 0 and 7. That symmetry may be too restrictive for this dataset, especially because the fixed feature map is asymmetric across qubit pairs. 

This edit makes one small, controlled relaxation: it splits the outer `RY` sharing into two independent parameters, `ry_out_0a` and `ry_out_0b`, while keeping the rest of the compact layered structure unchanged. This preserves the efficient topology and low parameter count regime, but gives the model just enough extra expressive power to fit asymmetric decision boundaries and potentially improve validation/test accuracy without materially increasing overfitting risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
