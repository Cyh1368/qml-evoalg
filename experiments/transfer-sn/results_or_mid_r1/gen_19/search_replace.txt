Program 2 succeeded by combining individual parameter assignments in rotations with a structured entangling mesh, creating better expressiveness without excessive parameters.

The key insight from program 2 is the use of intermediate RZ rotations (mid-layer) with individual per-qubit or paired parameters, followed by output RY rotations. This structure adds expressiveness during the optimization while maintaining the efficient CRZ ring from the current program.

The proposed enhancement:
1. Keeps the proven RY input layer with shared group parameters (4 parameters, very efficient)
2. Keeps the CRZ ring topology (8 trainable gates, encouraging smooth entanglement)
3. **Adds an intermediate RZ layer with paired/grouped parameters** (similar to program 2's mid-layer), placing RZ gates with slightly more granularity than the shared groups
4. **Adds an output RY layer with paired parameters** (like program 2's output layer) to refine single-qubit rotations after entanglement

This balances parameter efficiency with increased expressiveness in a controlled way. The extra parameters come from more granular RZ and RY rotations, not dense individual parameterization. Validation accuracy should improve while keeping the test accuracy competitive.
