<DESCRIPTION>
This edit appends a second, compact controlled-rotation core after the mirrored post-rotation shell. It reuses the same four entangling wire pairs as the first mixed-axis core, but cycles the axes through a different order (CRZ→CRX→CRY→CRX) to increase expressivity without changing the fixed training loop or measurement. The change adds only four trainable parameters and four gates per block, which should help the circuit capture residual feature correlations while keeping depth and parameter count within a similar budget.
