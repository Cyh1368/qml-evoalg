<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the current 16-parameter-per-block mirror-symmetric circuit with a more compact 12-parameter ansatz that previously showed stronger efficiency: shared pre-rotations, a fixed entangling CZ scaffold, a small mixed-axis controlled-rotation core, and shared post-phase refinements.

The main goal is to improve the combined score by lowering parameter count and reducing overfitting pressure while keeping expressive nonlocal mixing through the controlled rotations and the existing fixed feature map. This should move the model toward the better-performing, more parameter-efficient regime without changing any of the fixed training or measurement code.
