<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the fully independent per-qubit RY/RZ + line-CZ block with a more compact, higher-bias ansatz that is better suited to the fixed data-uploading pipeline.

Main ideas:
- Keep a strong initial local mixing layer with independent RY on all 8 qubits.
- Replace the shallow nearest-neighbor entangler with a butterfly-style CZ pattern that mixes both long-range and local correlations.
- Use parameter sharing on mirrored qubit pairs for the phase-correction and final readout-adaptation layers. This reduces the number of trainable circuit parameters per block while preserving symmetry and improving generalization.
- End the block with RY rather than RZ so the last operation is directly visible to the Z-basis readout, which usually improves trainability and validation/test accuracy.

Overall this should lower overfitting, improve parameter efficiency, and keep the circuit expressive enough for the 6 repeated blocks induced by the fixed l=3, p=2 layout.
</DESCRIPTION>
