<DESCRIPTION>
The current program achieves 0.91 accuracy but with slower convergence (450 steps) compared to earlier variants (180-270 steps). The issue is excessive parameter sharing in single-qubit rotations combined with a deep circuit.

This edit replaces the heavily-shared single-qubit rotations (ry_init, rz_main, ry_final all shared across 8 qubits) with per-qubit independent parameters. This allows each qubit to learn its own optimal rotation angles rather than forcing all qubits to use the same angle.

Benefits:
- Each qubit can adapt independently to its role in the circuit
- Faster convergence by reducing parameter coupling
- Better expressiveness without significantly increasing total params (trades 3 shared params for 24 independent ones, but removes redundancy)
- Maintains the proven entanglement structure (CZ rings + CRY layers)

The total parameter count increases from 4 unique params to 17 unique params per block (still modest), but the per-qubit adaptation should accelerate learning and improve generalization.
