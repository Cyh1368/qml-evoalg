The current program has depth=126 (between 99 and 135), which suggests it's on the right trajectory but hasn't reached the full potential. By increasing N_REPEATS from 2 to 3, we can:

1. Increase circuit depth from ~126 to ~150+, providing more expressive power
2. Maintain the same parameter efficiency per block (only 4 unique params: ry_0..7 shared, rz_pre/post shared, cry_a)
3. Increase total circuit parameters from 68 to 102 (still reasonable)
4. Test whether the additional depth can push test accuracy closer to 0.90 while maintaining a small generalization gap

This is a conservative but well-motivated change that directly addresses the recommendation and the observed depth-accuracy correlation.
