single_angle_collective_block
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Tie the collective RY, RZ, and RX layers to one angle within each ansatz block. The complete-graph CZ layer and mean-Z readout are permutation symmetric, so three independent collective angles may provide unnecessary capacity. This preserves the expressive sequence of three noncommuting rotation axes while reducing N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK from 3 to 1, N_CIRCUIT_PARAMS from 18 to 6, and total parameters from 20 to 8. The stronger regularization should improve parameter efficiency and generalization, while gradients accumulated across all three rotation layers can accelerate Adam convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
