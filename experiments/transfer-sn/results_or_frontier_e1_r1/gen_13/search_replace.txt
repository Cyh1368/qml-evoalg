<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the overparameterized 146-parameter block with a symmetry-aware dense block using only three parameter keys per block, returning the total to 20 parameters. The complete-graph entanglement aligns with the 28 pairwise feature couplings, while a balanced directed CRZ cycle replaces eight CZ edges. All CRZ gates share the collective phase parameter with the RZ layer, adding tunable two-qubit phase structure without increasing the parameter count. Every qubit appears once as CRZ control and once as target, avoiding wire bias. Placing the shared RX extraction layer last converts the accumulated feature-dependent phases into measurable Z-population differences. This should reduce overfitting and convergence time while retaining the successful dense-connectivity inductive bias.
