<DESCRIPTION>
The previous architectures either suffered from parameter redundancy, poor gate utilization at the boundaries (due to commuting operations), or excessive circuit depth which can hinder gradient flow.

This edit implements a highly optimized hardware-efficient ansatz block:
1. **Initial `RY` layer**: Rotates the state out of the computational (Z) basis immediately after the `IsingZZ` feature map, avoiding commute-merge issues.
2. **Even `CZ` layer**: Interlaces entanglement without adding depth (parallel application on pairs 0-1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-7).
3. **Middle `RZ` layer**: Adds trainable phase shifts while in the intermediate entangled state.
4. **Odd `CZ` layer**: Completes the entanglement ring (pairs 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7-0).
5. **Final `RX` layer**: Ensures that the end of the block does not commute with the `PauliZ` measurements (so no gates are "invisible" at the end of the circuit) and does not commute with the `RY` layer of the next block (preventing adjacent parameter merging).

This gives us 24 parameters per block (same as Prior 2) but drastically reduces the depth per block from 10 to 5, speeding up the optimization landscape traversal and reducing the likelihood of barren plateaus.
</DESCRIPTION>
