<DESCRIPTION>
This edit radically improves the expressiveness of the ansatz by splitting the complete-graph of 28 CZ gates into two non-commuting halves separated by a layer of parameterized RX rotations. In the prior versions, all 28 CZ gates commuted with each other and with the adjacent RZ layer, limiting the generated entanglement to a single basis. 

By grouping the CZ gates into two sets—matchings 1 to 4 (representing stride-1 and stride-2 connections) and matchings 5 to 7 (representing stride-3 and stride-4 connections)—and interleaving an `RX(rx_mid)` rotation between them, we force the subsequent CZ entanglers to act on a fundamentally rotated basis. This creates deeper, more complex correlation between subsets of qubits.

To maintain a minimal parameter count for parameter efficiency (a known strong contributor to the combined score), we employ 4 trainable, collective variables per block (`ry_in`, `rx_mid`, `ry_out`, `rx_out`). The final `RY` and `RX` sequence serves precisely to decode the entangled state back into a measurable Z polarization. Furthermore, the CZ gates are explicitly ordered into their 7 perfect matchings to guarantee minimal circuit depth.
</DESCRIPTION>
