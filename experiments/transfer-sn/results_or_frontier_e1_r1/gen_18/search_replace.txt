<DESCRIPTION>
This edit radically improves the expressiveness of the ansatz by splitting the complete-graph of 28 CZ gates into two non-commuting halves separated by a layer of parameterized RX rotations. In the prior versions, all 28 CZ gates commuted with each other and with the adjacent RZ layer, limiting the generated entanglement to a single basis.

By grouping the CZ gates into two sets—matchings 1 to 4 and matchings 5 to 7—and interleaving an `RX` rotation between them, we force the subsequent CZ entanglers to act on a fundamentally rotated basis. This creates deeper, more complex correlations.

We use 4 trainable, collective variables per block (`c_ry1`, `c_rx1`, `c_ry2`, `c_rx2`). The final `RY` and `RX` sequence serves to decode the entangled state back into a measurable Z polarization. This preserves parameter efficiency while dramatically increasing representation power.
</DESCRIPTION>
