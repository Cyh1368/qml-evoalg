<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the overparameterized wire-specific block with a permutation-aware collective mixer and a dense complete-graph entangler. Four alternating perfect matchings use one shared CRZ parameter, while the other three retain fixed CZ gates. The CRZ directions are balanced so every qubit appears twice as control and twice as target, avoiding readout bias while providing tunable two-body phases. Shared collective RY/RZ/RX layers preserve parameter efficiency and the final RX exposes phase-encoded features to Z readout. This reduces the total parameter count from 146 to 26 while retaining full pair connectivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
