<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the overparameterized, boundary-biased line ansatz with a permutation-equivariant block. All qubits share three collective rotation parameters, matching the pair-based encoding and mean-Z readout while reducing the total parameter count from 146 to 20. A complete-graph CZ layer distributes information without privileging wire positions; its gates are ordered as seven disjoint matchings, so its scheduled depth is only seven—the same depth as the serially listed CZ line. The terminal RX is directly visible to Z measurement and avoids the seed’s redundant pre/post RZ structure. This should improve generalization and parameter efficiency while making the lower-dimensional optimization converge faster.
</DESCRIPTION>
