<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the ring-closing CZ edge, which reduced validation accuracy relative to the open-chain variants, and replace it with a trainable CRZ on the central [3, 4] link. The open CZ chain preserves the stronger prior topology, while the additional near-identity CRZ provides adaptive phase coupling at the chain bottleneck. This adds only one unique parameter per repeated block, retaining parameter efficiency while allowing Adam to tune interaction strength instead of imposing another fixed entangler.
