<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed CZ line with a directed ring of independently parametrized CRY gates. The initial RY/RZ layers provide local basis control, while the CRY ring learns feature-dependent correlations across all eight qubits and closes the missing 7-to-0 connection. A final RY layer improves conversion of the phase-based IsingZZ encoding into the Z readout. The block uses 32 parameters, avoids parameter sharing, and remains compact enough for repeated data re-uploading.
</DESCRIPTION>
