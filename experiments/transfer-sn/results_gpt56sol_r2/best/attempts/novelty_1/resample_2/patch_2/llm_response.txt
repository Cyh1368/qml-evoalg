<DESCRIPTION>
Compress the block by sharing three rotation parameters across all qubits, reducing overfitting and improving parameter efficiency. Replace the CZ line with two trainable CRZ couplings on complementary high-centrality edges, including [2,5], so entanglement strength can adapt to the binary-feature phase encoding. Alternating CRZ directions improves information exchange while retaining a shallow circuit. Parameter sharing is confined to each ansatz block because repeat-level parameter allocation is controlled by the fixed training code.
