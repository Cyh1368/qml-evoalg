<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the overparameterized, qubit-specific ring ansatz with a symmetry-aligned block using only four parameter keys per block. Shared RY/RZ layers regularize the six independent upload/repeat copies and match the permutation-symmetric mean-Z readout. Complete-graph CZ connectivity mirrors all 28 pairwise feature channels, while two central CRZ gates share one parameter to add adaptive phase interactions without extra parameter cost. This reduces total parameters from 146 to 26 and should improve validation accuracy, generalization, L2 loss, and optimization efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
