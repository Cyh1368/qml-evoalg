<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the over-parameterized ring ansatz with a permutation-symmetric complete-graph mixer aligned with the 28 pairwise binary features and mean-Z readout. Sharing parameters across wires reduces the model from 146 to 26 total parameters, improving optimization speed and generalization. The added shared RX layer after the proven shared RY readout mixer supplies a second noncommuting axis, increasing expressivity within the symmetric subspace at only one additional parameter per block.
</DESCRIPTION>
