<DESCRIPTION>
This block uses parameter-efficient, noncommuting mixing to complement the IsingZZ feature map. Independent RY rotations let each qubit adapt to its feature incidence, while parity-shared RZ parameters regularize the model and reduce the train-test gap. A CZ ring provides stable global connectivity, and four parametrized CRY gates across opposing qubits introduce directed, data-dependent mixing without excessive parameter growth. Reversing the direction of alternating CRY links avoids a single control-qubit bottleneck. The block uses only 14 unique parameters, making the six independently parameterized applications comparatively efficient and fast to optimize.
