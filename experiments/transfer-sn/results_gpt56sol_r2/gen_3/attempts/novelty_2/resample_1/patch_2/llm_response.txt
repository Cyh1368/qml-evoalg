<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the measurement-commuting final RZ layer with an RY layer so every block can directly affect Z readout, while retaining RZ phases before entanglement. A closed CZ ring avoids the weakly connected endpoints of a line and adds no trainable parameters. The block remains compact at 24 parameters, supporting faster optimization and limiting overfitting across the six independently parameterized block applications.
