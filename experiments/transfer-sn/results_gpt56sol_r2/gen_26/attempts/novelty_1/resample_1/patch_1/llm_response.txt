<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed middle CZ with a trainable CRZ so the circuit can learn the strength and sign of the central coupling rather than imposing maximal entanglement. Restore the empirically stronger post-entanglement RY basis-mixing layer, while removing the post-RY on wire 3 to offset the new CRZ parameter. This keeps 24 parameters per block and 146 total parameters, improves parameter efficiency relative to simply adding capacity, and reduces the gate count by one per block. The near-zero CRZ initialization also starts as a weak coupling, which should support faster, more stable optimization and reduce overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
