<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the overparameterized, topology-biased skip-chain block with a permutation-symmetric block tailored to the dataset’s 28 pairwise features and mean-Z readout. Sharing each rotation angle across all qubits reduces the circuit from 146 to 20 total parameters, improving optimization speed, parameter efficiency, and regularization. A complete-graph CZ layer exposes every encoded qubit pair to the same entangling structure, avoiding arbitrary locality assumptions and matching the strongest prior result, which achieved 0.95 validation accuracy, 0.26 validation loss, and a 0.94 combined score.
