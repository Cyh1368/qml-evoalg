<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the closing CZ edge between qubits 7 and 0, reverting the entangler from a cycle to an open chain. The added wraparound edge increased depth from 99 to 120 while validation accuracy fell from 0.89 to 0.87, eliminated convergence at the 0.90 threshold, and increased the train-test gap. Because the feature map already couples all 28 qubit pairs, full connectivity is supplied by data encoding; closing the variational ring is redundant and can overmix phase information. The open chain preserves useful entanglement while reducing circuit depth and the observed overfitting tendency, with no increase in parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
