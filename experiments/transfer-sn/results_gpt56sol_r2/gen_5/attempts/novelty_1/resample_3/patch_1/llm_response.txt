<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the terminal RZ layer with RX rotations and close the CZ chain into a ring. Terminal RZ gates commute with the diagonal feature encoding and cannot directly change the final Z readout, especially making the last block's post-RZ parameters ineffective. RX gates instead convert phase-dependent coherences generated by IsingZZ and CZ operations into measurable Z populations, while retaining the same parameter count. The added parameter-free CZ edge [7, 0] removes endpoint asymmetry and gives all eight qubits equal entangling degree, which better matches the complete set of 28 pairwise binary features.
</DESCRIPTION>
