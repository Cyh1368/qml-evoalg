<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the weak line entangler and terminal RZ-only layer with a cyclic trainable CRY mixer. The initial independent RY/RZ rotations retain per-qubit expressivity, while the bidirectional alternating ring couples every qubit and directly changes Z-basis populations relevant to the readout. This keeps the block at 24 parameters, avoiding unnecessary parameter growth while improving gradient-driven mixing and eliminating the open-chain endpoint imbalance.
</DESCRIPTION>
