<DESCRIPTION>
Close the staggered CZ entangling pattern with the missing [7, 0] edge. This removes endpoint bias and gives every qubit two entangling neighbors, which is well matched to the permutation-symmetric mean-Z readout. The added edge is disjoint from the other gates in its staggered sublayer, so it should add useful global mixing with little or no depth increase, no additional trainable parameters, and only six extra gates across the full circuit.
</DESCRIPTION>
