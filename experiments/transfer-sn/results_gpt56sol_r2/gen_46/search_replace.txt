<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the heavily overparameterized ring ansatz with a permutation-shared block aligned to the all-pairs feature map and mean-Z readout. Sharing the pre/post rotations reduces the model from 146 to 26 total parameters, which should improve parameter efficiency and generalization while accelerating Adam optimization. Complete-graph CZ entanglement exposes every encoded pair to subsequent basis mixing. A single controlled phase near the graph center adds a low-cost symmetry-breaking degree of freedom that can capture nonuniform pair relevance without restoring per-qubit overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
