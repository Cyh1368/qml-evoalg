<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the single central bridge with two complementary, parameter-shared CRZ bridges and remove the tied global RX layer. The second bridge exposes an additional long-range phase pathway without increasing the four parameters per block, while shared CRZ angles preserve strong regularization. Removing the global RX layer avoids forcing one parameter to serve incompatible controlled-phase and local-mixing roles, reduces the gate count from 450 to 408, and restores the empirically stronger dual-bridge structure that achieved 0.96 validation accuracy and a 0.95 combined score.
</DESCRIPTION>
