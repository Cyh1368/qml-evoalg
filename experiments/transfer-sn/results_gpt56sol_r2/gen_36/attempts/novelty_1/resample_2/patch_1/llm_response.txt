<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the over-parameterized ring ansatz with a permutation-symmetric block aligned to the mean-Z readout and all 28 pairwise feature couplings. Sharing each local rotation across all qubits reduces the block from 24 to 4 unique parameters, lowering the full model from 146 to 26 parameters and providing strong regularization. Complete-graph CZ entanglement removes the arbitrary ring bias and previously demonstrated substantially better validation accuracy and loss. A trainable CRZ on the central 3-4 link adds a low-cost adaptive two-qubit phase interaction, allowing the circuit to tune entanglement rather than relying exclusively on fixed CZ phases.
</DESCRIPTION>
