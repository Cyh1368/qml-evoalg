<DESCRIPTION>
Remove the globally tied RX layer and retain the four-parameter preparation–phase–entanglement–RY structure. The prior best-performing candidate achieved 0.96 validation accuracy with this structure, while adding the tied RX layer reduced validation accuracy to 0.94, increased the generalization gap, and raised depth and gate count. Because the RX angles are coupled to the adaptive CRZ bridges, they constrain one parameter to serve two unrelated roles and can disturb the phase-to-population conversion produced by the final RY layer. Removing them preserves all 26 trainable parameters and adaptive bridges while reducing circuit depth and improving optimization conditioning.
</DESCRIPTION>
