<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the overparameterized line ansatz with a strongly regularized, permutation-aware block. Sharing each rotation across all wires reduces the model from 24 to 4 parameters per block, lowering the total from 146 to 26 parameters while retaining independent copies across the six fixed blocks. Complete-graph CZ entanglement mirrors all 28 pairwise feature channels, and a trainable CRZ on the 6–7 bridge adds adaptive phase coupling without materially increasing complexity. The final shared RY layer converts the learned phase correlations into the mean-Z readout basis. This combines the strong validation behavior of symmetric complete-graph parents with the proposed localized 6–7 trainable bridge.
</DESCRIPTION>
