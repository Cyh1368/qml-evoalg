<DESCRIPTION>
Add a CZ connection between qubits 7 and 0 to turn the open entangling chain into a ring. The current chain gives endpoint qubits a weaker correlation pathway even though the feature map treats all qubits as participants in pairwise phase features and the readout weights every qubit equally. Closing the ring removes this boundary bias, improves global phase-information propagation, and adds no trainable parameters, preserving parameter efficiency while potentially improving validation accuracy and convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
