<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the symmetric CZ chain with an alternating-direction CNOT chain. This preserves gate count and parameter count while introducing directional population mixing between neighboring qubits. Alternating controls avoids a one-way information bottleneck and can improve feature propagation, optimization speed, and expressivity without increasing overfitting risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
