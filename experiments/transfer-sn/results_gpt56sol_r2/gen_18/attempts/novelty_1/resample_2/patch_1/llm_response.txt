Replace the redundant split RY/RZ and post-RY layers with an independent RZ–RY–RZ Euler block on every qubit. This provides full local SU(2) expressivity around the entanglers while retaining exactly 24 parameters per block and 146 total parameters. Reorder the CZ line into an even/odd brickwork schedule and remove the underperforming ring-closing CZ; this preserves connected entanglement while reducing circuit depth. The richer local basis changes should better transform the diagonal IsingZZ feature phases into measurable Z-population differences without sacrificing parameter efficiency.
