<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the heavily over-parameterized ring ansatz with a permutation-shared complete-graph block, then add one adaptive CRZ on the suggested critical 6–7 bridge. Shared pre/post rotations regularize the six independent block copies and align with mean-Z pooling, while complete-graph CZ coupling mirrors all 28 pairwise feature channels. The shared RX layer supplies noncommuting basis mixing, and the localized CRZ adds trainable two-qubit expressivity without sacrificing parameter efficiency. This reduces the model from 146 to 32 total parameters and builds directly on the strongest prior complete-graph candidate.
</DESCRIPTION>
