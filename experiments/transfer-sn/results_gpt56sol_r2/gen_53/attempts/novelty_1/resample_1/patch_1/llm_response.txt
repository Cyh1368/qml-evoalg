<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the overparameterized pair-specific block with a permutation-symmetric, three-parameter block. Globally shared pre-RY, phase-RZ, and post-RY angles align with the mean-Z readout and regularize the six independent block copies. A fixed complete CZ graph mirrors all 28 pairwise feature channels, while removing the trainable CRZ avoids redundant phase capacity. This reduces the model from 13 to 3 parameters per block and from 80 to 20 total parameters, improving parameter efficiency and reducing overfitting while restoring the stronger full-connectivity inductive bias demonstrated by prior candidates.
</DESCRIPTION>
