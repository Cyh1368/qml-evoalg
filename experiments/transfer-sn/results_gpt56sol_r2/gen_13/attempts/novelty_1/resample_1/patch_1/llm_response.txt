<DESCRIPTION>
Reorder the existing CZ chain into two disjoint-edge layers. Because all CZ gates commute, this preserves the ansatz unitary, parameterization, initialization behavior, and observed predictive performance while allowing substantially more parallel execution. The first layer acts on (0,1), (2,3), (4,5), and (6,7); the second acts on (1,2), (3,4), and (5,6). This should reduce circuit depth without adding gates or parameters, improving architectural efficiency and potentially the combined score.
</DESCRIPTION>
