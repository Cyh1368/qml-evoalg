<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the highly overparameterized ring block with a permutation-shared mixer and complete-graph entanglement aligned to all 28 pairwise feature channels. Sharing the local angles reduces the model from 146 to 26 total parameters, improving optimization and generalization under the symmetric mean-Z readout. A single trainable CRZ on the central edge introduces a low-cost symmetry-breaking interaction that can capture nonuniform task structure without sacrificing parameter efficiency; this architecture has substantially stronger validation accuracy and lower L2 loss than the current ring.
</DESCRIPTION>
