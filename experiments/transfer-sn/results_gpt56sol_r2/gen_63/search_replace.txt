<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the overparameterized local ring ansatz with the strongest prior symmetric complete-graph design, augmented by a third shared central CRZ bridge on wires [1, 6]. Global parameter sharing aligns with the permutation-symmetric mean-Z readout and reduces the model from 146 to 26 total parameters, improving parameter efficiency and regularization. The complete CZ graph mirrors all 28 pairwise feature channels, while three complementary CRZ bridges reuse one parameter to add adaptive phase interactions without increasing N_PARAMS. The final shared RY layer converts those learned phases into Z populations. This targets the prior 0.95 score while testing the recommended extra bridge for higher accuracy or lower loss.
