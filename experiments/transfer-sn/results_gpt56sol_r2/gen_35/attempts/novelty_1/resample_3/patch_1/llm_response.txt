<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the overparameterized, qubit-specific ring ansatz with a permutation-symmetric block whose three rotation parameters are shared across all qubits. Because the 28 binary features correspond to all qubit pairs, complete-graph CZ entanglement aligns the circuit topology with the feature structure and avoids privileging an arbitrary ordering. The shared pre-mixing, phase, and post-mixing angles reduce the circuit parameter count from 146 to 20, improving parameter efficiency and regularization while retaining noncommuting transformations around the diagonal feature map. This structure previously achieved substantially better validation accuracy, lower loss, a smaller generalization gap, and faster convergence.
</DESCRIPTION>
