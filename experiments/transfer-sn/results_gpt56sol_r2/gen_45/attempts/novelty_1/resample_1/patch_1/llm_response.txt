<DESCRIPTION>
Aggressively share each local rotation across all qubits, reducing the ansatz from 24 to 3 local parameters per block and total model size from 146 parameters to 26 after adding one controlled-rotation parameter. This permutation-equivariant regularization matches the complete pairwise feature map and mean-Z readout, directly addressing the current generalization gap and poor parameter efficiency. Replace the sparse ring with complete-graph CZ entanglement, which previously performed substantially better by coupling every encoded feature pair. A single central CRY adds a parameter-efficient, noncommuting symmetry-breaking channel that can convert learned phase correlations into measurable populations more directly than CRZ, while avoiding a large increase in optimization dimension.
</DESCRIPTION>
