<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the linear CZ chain with a seven-edge next-nearest-neighbor pattern. This preserves the entangling-gate count and parameter count while coupling qubits that were not directly connected in the seed block. Across the independently parameterized repeated blocks, the longer-range connectivity should improve feature mixing and reduce reliance on deep propagation through adjacent qubits.
</DESCRIPTION>
