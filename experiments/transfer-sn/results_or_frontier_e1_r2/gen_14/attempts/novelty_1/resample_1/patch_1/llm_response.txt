<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the underfitting two-parameter global block with a parameter-shared hypercube mixer based on the strongest prior architecture, augmented by one globally shared RZ phase. The RZ layer turns the real RY preparation into complex amplitudes, improving interference with the ZZ feature phases and subsequent RX mixers. Three interleaved hypercube matchings propagate correlations across the register, while two-color sharing regularizes the model. Setting the new phase to zero recovers the prior high-performing block, so this is a controlled expressivity increase requiring only eight parameters per block (50 total). The final RX layer remains directly observable under Z readout.
