Furthermore, the linear CZ topology takes 7 depth layers per block and lacks full connectivity.

We propose a much more efficient Hardware-Efficient Ansatz (HEA) block:
1. **Fewer parameters**: Replaced the two `RZ` layers and one `RY` layer with just one `RY` and one `RZ` layer. This drops the parameter count from 24 to 16 per block (a 33% reduction), leading to 98 total parameters. This will boost parameter efficiency and mitigate overfitting (reducing the generalization gap).
2. **Stronger, shallower entanglement**: We replaced the linear `CZ` gates with a 2-step alternating ring of `CNOT` gates (Evens-Odds, then Odds-Evens). `CNOT` is highly entangling (mixing X and Z bases), and the alternating ring guarantees full connectivity across all 8 qubits with a circuit depth of only 2 for the entangling layers.
3. **Interleaved rotations**: Placing the `RZ` layer between the two `CNOT` steps ensures that the local phase rotations don't trivially commute with the entangling operations, maximizing expressivity while maintaining a compact circuit depth.
