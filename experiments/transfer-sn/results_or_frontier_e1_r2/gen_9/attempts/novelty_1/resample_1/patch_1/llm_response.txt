<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the over-entangling ring-and-cross circuit with the empirically stronger seven-edge CZ line, ordered as commuting brickwork to reduce circuit depth. The RZ layers surrounding CZ share parameters because local RZ gates commute with every CZ; therefore each pair is equivalent to one unrestricted effective rotation of angle 2θ without redundant optimizer coordinates. This preserves per-qubit phase flexibility while reducing the block from 24 to 16 independent parameters, lowering the full model from 146 to 98 parameters. The sparse entanglement and tied phase coordinates should improve generalization and parameter efficiency while retaining the favorable optimization rate of two phase applications.
</DESCRIPTION>
