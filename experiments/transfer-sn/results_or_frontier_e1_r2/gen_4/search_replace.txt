<DESCRIPTION>
The original ansatz uses CZ gates which commute with the pre- and post-RZ rotations, resulting in redundant parameters within a block (reducing the effective expressivity). Also, it has 24 parameters per block, leading to low parameter efficiency and potential overfitting.

This proposed edit changes the ansatz to use a "brick-layer" CNOT architecture interspersed with alternating RY and RZ rotations. CNOT gates do not commute with RZ on their target qubits, thus maximizing expressivity and entanglement generation. By structuring the CNOTs into two staggered layers `(0-1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-7)` and `(1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7-0)`, the ansatz fully connects the 8-qubit register in each block while retaining a minimal depth. This reduces the parameter count from 24 to 16 per block, improving parameter efficiency while enhancing the actual entanglement capacity and generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
