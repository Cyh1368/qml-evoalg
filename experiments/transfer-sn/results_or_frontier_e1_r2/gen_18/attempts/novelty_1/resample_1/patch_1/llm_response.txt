<DESCRIPTION>
Drawing inspiration from the highly successful all-to-all CZ network in the highest-scoring prior program, this ansatz maximizes entanglement by retaining all 28 possible CZ pairings (which perfectly matches the 28-dimensional all-to-all feature map). However, to prevent the 28 commuting CZ gates from collapsing into a single diagonal operator, the network is fundamentally restructured. 

We split the 28 CZ gates into two distinct, non-commuting entanglement layers:
- Layer A contains distance 1 and distance 4 couplings (12 gates).
- Layer B contains distance 2 and distance 3 couplings (16 gates).

These two layers are separated by a global non-commuting RX bridge. This completely shatters the commutativity of the dense CZ block, dramatically increasing the expressivity of the ansatz while maintaining the exact same gate count. 

The ansatz uses only three globally shared parameters per block (`ry1`, `rx1`, `ry2`). This massive parameter sharing provides strong regularization, enabling rapid convergence, outstanding parameter efficiency, and robust generalization. The initial RY creates the necessary superposition from the zero state, the RX bridge enforces the non-commuting split, and the final RY ensures full visibility for the terminal Z measurement.
</DESCRIPTION>
