<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the persistent parity-based channel assignment with three axis-aligned hypercube slicings. Preparation uses the dimension-0 partition, phase-to-population mixing uses dimension 1, and decoding uses dimension 2. Each CZ matching therefore interacts with a different balanced channel decomposition, removing the old two-sublattice bottleneck and exposing more diverse feature correlations. The parameter count, gate count, and depth remain unchanged, preserving the current regularization and efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
