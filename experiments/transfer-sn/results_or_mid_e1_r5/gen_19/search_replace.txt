<DESCRIPTION>
This update introduces a more expressive symmetric ansatz by incorporating RZ rotations to complement the IsingZZ feature map's phase-based encoding. It replaces the local CRY layers with a fixed circular CNOT ladder for global connectivity and long-range parametrized CRY gates to better capture correlations across the qubit topology. The parameter sharing is maintained in symmetric pairs (qubits k and k+4) to keep the total parameter count efficient (80 parameters including readout) while increasing the circuit's ability to model complex axis-mixing and phase interactions.
</DESCRIPTION>
