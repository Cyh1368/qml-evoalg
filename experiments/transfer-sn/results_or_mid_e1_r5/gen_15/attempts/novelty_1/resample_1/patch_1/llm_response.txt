<DESCRIPTION>
This edit enhances the ansatz by introducing cross-ring fixed entanglement and diversifying the post-entanglement rotation axes. 

1. **Fixed CZ Cross-Entanglement**: We add fixed CZ gates between the mirrored qubit groups (0-4, 1-5, 2-6, 3-7). This increases the connectivity of the 8-qubit graph without increasing the parameter count, facilitating better information flow between features.
2. **Post-Entanglement Axis Mixing**: Following the recommendation, we replace the final RZ layer with a heterogeneous layer of RX and RY rotations. By alternating axes (RX for wires 0, 4, 2, 6 and RY for 1, 5, 3, 7) while maintaining symmetric parameter sharing, we improve the circuit's expressivity and its ability to steer expectations toward the measurement basis.

These changes aim to reduce the generalization gap and improve convergence speed while maintaining a highly efficient parameter count (74 total parameters).
</DESCRIPTION>
