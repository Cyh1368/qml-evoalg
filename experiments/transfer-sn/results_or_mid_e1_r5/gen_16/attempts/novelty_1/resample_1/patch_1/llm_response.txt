<DESCRIPTION>
This edit compresses the ansatz into a more parameter-efficient symmetric mixer: mirrored single-qubit RY/RZ layers stabilize the mean-Z readout, while a sparse CZ ring plus cross-half CRY couplings preserves expressive nonlinearity with far fewer trainable weights. The result is designed to lower overfitting, reduce depth/gate count, and improve accuracy-per-parameter under the fixed re-uploading schedule.
