<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the narrow RY-only/CRY-ladder block with a more expressive but still parameter-efficient symmetric ansatz. The new block keeps the useful qubit-wise rotations, adds a full phase layer, and broadens entanglement using two complementary two-qubit patterns: a CZ ring for strong nonparametric mixing and a small shifted CRY set for trainable correlations. Reusing the same RY/RZ parameter names in the second half of the block creates a palindromic structure that tends to stabilize optimization and reduce overfitting, while the added topology diversity should improve validation/test accuracy and help the model reach the convergence threshold earlier.
</DESCRIPTION>
