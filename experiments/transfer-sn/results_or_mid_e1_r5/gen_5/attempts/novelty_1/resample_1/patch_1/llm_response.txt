# Analysis

Looking at the current performance:
- **Test accuracy**: 0.87 (room for improvement)
- **Validation accuracy**: 0.89
- **Generalization gap**: 0.07 (reasonable but could be tighter)
- **Current n_params**: 146
- **Current depth**: 99

The seed architecture uses:
1. Independent RY rotations on all qubits
2. Independent RZ rotations (pre-entanglement)
3. A fixed line topology of CZ gates
4. Independent RZ rotations (post-entanglement)

**Proposed improvement**: Enhance expressivity by:
1. **Adding RX gates** for complete SU(2) single-qubit coverage (rotation around x-axis was missing)
2. **Introducing parametrized CRY gates** on alternating qubit pairs to create learnable, bidirectional entanglement instead of purely passive CZ gates
3. **Keeping CZ gates** on complementary pairs to maintain structural diversity

This targeted approach will:
- Increase expressivity through better single-qubit freedom (RX) and parametrized two-qubit gates (CRY)
- Balance expressivity with parameter efficiency (increase from 146 to ~218 params, still reasonable)
- Improve the circuit's ability to learn non-trivial quantum correlations
- Reduce the generalization gap by better utilizing the parameter budget
