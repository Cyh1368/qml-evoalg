<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the overparameterized block with a leaner, symmetry-aware ansatz.

Key ideas:
- keep mirrored parameter sharing across qubit pairs (0,4), (1,5), (2,6), (3,7) to reduce variance and improve generalization;
- remove the trainable controlled-rotation layer, which was adding parameters without a clear validation benefit;
- insert two fixed CZ entangling patterns (local neighbors plus cross-ladder links) to preserve nontrivial multi-qubit mixing at zero parameter cost;
- diversify the post-entanglement single-qubit layer by alternating RX and RY across mirrored pairs, giving the circuit axis heterogeneity while keeping the parameter count low.

Overall, this reduces the per-block parameter budget to 12 while keeping enough entangling structure for the re-uploaded feature map, which should help training reach good accuracy faster and with a smaller train-test gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
