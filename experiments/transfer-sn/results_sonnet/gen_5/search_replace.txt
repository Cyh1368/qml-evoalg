ring_crz_entangler_with_ry_mixing

<DESCRIPTION>
The seed ansatz uses a simple linear chain of fixed CZ gates plus RY/RZ rotations, giving limited entanglement structure and no parametrized two-qubit interactions. This limits expressivity per block and requires more depth/uploads to fit the data, contributing to the observed train-test gap (0.94 vs 0.87) and moderate validation/test loss.

I propose replacing the fixed CZ line with a ring of parametrized CRZ gates (closing the loop from qubit 7 back to qubit 0), which gives richer, trainable entanglement while keeping gate count modest. I also reduce the pre-entanglement RZ layer to save parameters (since RY+CRZ ring already provides sufficient degrees of freedom), and add a final RY layer instead of RZ to mix in a different basis before readout Z-measurement, which should help since the readout measures PauliZ — having a final RY (rather than RZ, which commutes with Z-measurement and is therefore partially wasted in terms of readout influence within a single block before the next upload) actually contributes more meaningfully to the final observable if it's the last block. This should improve trainability, expressivity per parameter, and generalization.

Parameter count: 8 (RY) + 8 (CRZ ring) + 8 (final RY) = 24 per block, vs previous 24 (8 RY + 8 RZ_pre + 8 RZ_post) — same parameter budget, but with entangling parametrized gates instead of fixed CZ, likely giving better use of the training capacity.
