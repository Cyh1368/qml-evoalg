hybrid_cz_crz_single_stage

<DESCRIPTION>
The current candidate uses two full CRZ entangling stages (16 CRZ gates, 16 params/block → 96 circuit params total plus readout = 194 params), giving a larger generalization gap (0.10) and lower combined score (0.84) than the simpler seed variants. Following the recommendation, I replace the second offset CRZ stage with a mix of fixed CZ and trainable CRZ gates (alternating every other coupling), cutting the second stage's parameter count from 8 to 4 while retaining some of its expressivity. This reduces total circuit parameters (16→12 per block, saving 4*6=24 params overall), narrowing the train/test gap and improving parameter efficiency while keeping enough entangling expressivity from the trainable gates that remain. This directly implements the suggested "hybrid CZ/CRZ" pattern but applied to the second (offset) entangling stage which was the most param-heavy addition versus the seed.
