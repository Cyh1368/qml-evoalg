lightweight_trainable_ring_entangler

<DESCRIPTION>
The current seed uses fixed CZ gates (maximal, non-trainable entanglement) plus a redundant pre/post RZ split, achieving a good gap (0.07) but leaving room for improvement in absolute accuracy. Prior CRZ-heavy variants improved train/val accuracy but increased the generalization gap and parameter count. This edit keeps the single RY+RZ per qubit (16 params, avoiding the seed's redundant double-RZ split), but replaces the fixed CZ ring with a lightweight *trainable* CRZ ring (8 params) that starts near-identity (small random init already handles this) so it can smoothly learn the right amount of entanglement instead of committing to maximal entanglement from step one. This should reduce the generalization gap seen with fixed CZ while giving more flexibility than fully fixed gates, without ballooning parameter count. Total per-block params: 16 (RY+RZ) + 8 (CRZ ring) = 24, keeping the total moderate (24*6+2=146, same budget as current), preserving parameter efficiency while trading gate rigidity for trainability.
