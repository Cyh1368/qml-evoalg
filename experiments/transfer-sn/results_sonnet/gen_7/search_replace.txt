add_trainable_entangling_ring_with_shared_params

<DESCRIPTION>
The current spec uses fixed (non-trainable) CZ gates for entanglement, which limits the optimizer's ability to control coupling strength and can contribute to the train/test gap seen in the seed. I replace the fixed CZ chain with a ring of trainable CRZ gates (closing the loop with a 7-0 connection for richer mixing), while sharing a single parameter across all ring couplings to keep parameter count low (only +1 param per block vs the seed's 0, since CZ had no params). This should let the model learn appropriate entanglement strength for better generalization without much added parameter overhead, while adding negligible depth. I also slightly reduce single-qubit gate redundancy is already done; here we focus purely on making entanglement trainable and cyclic instead of a fixed open chain.
