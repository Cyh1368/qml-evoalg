<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the rigid complete-graph CZ layer with seven trainable CRZ perfect matchings. All four disjoint edges in a matching share one angle, adding only seven parameters per ansatz block rather than one per edge. This preserves complete pair coverage and edge-coloring depth efficiency while allowing Adam to strengthen, weaken, or effectively disable different interaction channels. Near-identity initialization should also improve early optimization conditioning, while independent parameter copies across re-upload blocks permit stage-dependent entanglement.
</DESCRIPTION>
