<DESCRIPTION>
Restore the proven collective RX input axis, then insert one independently trainable collective RY/RX mixer after the fourth perfect matching. In the current block, all CZ gates commute and effectively form one diagonal K8 layer; the midpoint non-diagonal mixer separates that layer into two interacting stages so partial correlations can be transformed before the remaining entanglers. Sharing each angle across all qubits preserves parameter efficiency and regularization. The midpoint parameters are independent, so setting them to zero recovers the strong prior two-axis architecture as a subspace. This adds only three parameter keys per block, resulting in 38 total parameters while targeting faster convergence and lower validation loss.
</DESCRIPTION>
