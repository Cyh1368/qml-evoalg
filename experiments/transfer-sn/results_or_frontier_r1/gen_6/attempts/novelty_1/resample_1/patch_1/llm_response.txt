Looking at the current design, I see several opportunities for improvement:

1. **Linear CZ chain (0-1, 1-2, ..., 6-7)** means qubits 0 and 7 only interact indirectly through 6 intermediate gates. Adding ring closure (7→0) and cross connections (0-4, 1-5, 2-6, 3-7) dramatically improves qubit connectivity.

2. **Only one rotation layer before entanglement** limits the circuit's ability to process entangled states. Adding a second RY layer between two entangling stages (ring + cross) allows the circuit to better exploit the correlations created by ring entanglement before applying cross connections.

3. The feature map uses IsingZZ (Z-diagonal). The current ansatz's CZ + RZ layers after entanglement are also Z-diagonal—adding a post-ring RY layer breaks this Z-diagonal chain and increases expressivity.

The combined score appears to heavily weight validation accuracy, so pushing accuracy higher with a more expressive block should improve the score despite slightly more parameters.
