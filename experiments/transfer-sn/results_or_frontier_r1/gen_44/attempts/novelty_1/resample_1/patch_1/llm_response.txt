Looking at the current program's architecture, I see that CZ gates commute with each other, so all 28 CZ gates in a consecutive sequence effectively collapse into a single entangling layer regardless of ordering. The current design has one mid-mixer splitting them into 2 stages (4 matchings + 3 matchings). By adding a second mid-mixer and splitting into 3 stages (3+2+2 matchings), we create three genuinely distinct entangling stages with non-commuting barriers between them, increasing effective circuit depth with only 2 additional shared parameters per block.

<DESCRIPTION>
Split the 7 perfect matchings of K8 into three non-commuting stages separated by two mid-circuit RY+RX mixers instead of one. This creates three genuinely independent entangling layers (matchings 1-3, 4-5, 6-7) with non-commuting single-qubit rotations between them. The key insight is that consecutive CZ gates commute, so splitting them with parametrized rotations effectively increases the circuit's expressive power. This adds only 2 shared parameters per block (from 6 to 8), going from 38 to 50 total parameters, while significantly increasing the effective circuit depth and expressivity. The 3-2-2 split ensures a good balance of short-range (matchings 1-2), long-range (matching 3), cross (4-5), and diagonal (6-7) entanglement in each stage.
