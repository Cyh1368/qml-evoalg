<DESCRIPTION>
The previous bipartite K4,4 entangler severely bottlenecked intra-partition mixing, resulting in a significant accuracy drop compared to the complete graph. Meanwhile, using a complete K8 graph (28 CZ gates) per block results in high gate count and depth. 

This edit constructs a highly expressive and parameter-efficient symmetric block:
1. **4-Regular Circulant Graph**: We replace the K4,4 graph with a combination of distance-1 (nearest-neighbor) and distance-3 (rapid-mixing) ring connections. This requires only 16 CZ gates (same as K4,4) but guarantees uniform and fast correlation spread across all 8 qubits.
2. **Commutativity Breaking**: In earlier highly parameterized programs, breaking the commutativity of CZ gates was key to high accuracy. We insert a collective `RX` layer (`mid_rx`) *between* the distance-1 and distance-3 CZ stages. This transforms the 16 commuting CZs into two highly non-commuting entanglement stages, boosting expressivity.
3. **Parameter Efficiency**: We use exactly 4 parameters per block (`in_ry`, `mid_rx`, `out_rz`, `out_rx`). This ensures the model converges rapidly with minimal parameters (only 26 total), substantially increasing the efficiency component of the combined score while achieving top-tier generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
