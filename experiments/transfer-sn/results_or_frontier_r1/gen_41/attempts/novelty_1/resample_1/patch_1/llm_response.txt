<DESCRIPTION>
The previous best configurations successfully utilized the full K8 entanglement graph to map perfectly to the 28 dataset features, but broke the commuting `CZ` sequences into two halves to increase expressivity. 

This new algorithm pushes that concept further by maximally interleaving the entangling gates and the local mixers without increasing the parameter count. By splitting the 7 perfect matchings of K8 into four shallow chunks (M1+M2, M3+M4, M5+M6, M7) and inserting local `RY` and `RX` rotations between them, we prevent the state from lingering in the computational basis and rapidly accelerate entanglement spread. 

To maintain high parameter efficiency (which heavily influences the overall score), we tie the parameters of the mixing layers in an alternating A-B-A-B pattern. Thus, the ansatz requires only 6 parameters per block (`ry_a`, `rx_a`, `ry_b`, `rx_b`, `rz_out`, `rx_out`), providing the expressiveness of a much deeper parametrized network while tightly constraining the optimization space to improve the train-test generalization gap.
</DESCRIPTION>
