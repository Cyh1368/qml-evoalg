<DESCRIPTION>
Add a shared collective RX layer immediately after the input RY layer. The noncommuting RY–RX mixer creates richer complex amplitudes before the diagonal CZ interactions, improving access to the uploaded phase information while preserving permutation symmetry. It adds only one parameter per block—six circuit parameters overall—and prior results indicate this structure improves validation accuracy, loss, and convergence speed. Retaining the current perfect-matching CZ order should provide those expressivity gains at lower depth than the earlier lexicographic implementation.
