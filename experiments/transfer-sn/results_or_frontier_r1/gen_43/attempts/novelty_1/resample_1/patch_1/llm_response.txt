<DESCRIPTION>
This edit combines two improvements:
1. The mid-circuit RY+RX mixer from the best-performing program (score 1.07), which breaks the commuting diagonal CZ sequence into two non-commuting interaction stages.
2. Replacing fixed CZ gates with parameterized CRZ gates, sharing one trainable parameter per perfect matching (7 total). This lets the optimizer learn the ideal entanglement strength for each matching independently.

CRZ gates are diagonal in the computational basis (like CZ), so consecutive matchings still commute—the mid-circuit mixer remains essential for breaking this commutativity. The seven perfect matchings cover all 28 K8 edges at minimum depth.

Parameters per block: 2 (input RY+RX) + 4 (CRZ matchings 1-4) + 2 (mid RY+RX) + 3 (CRZ matchings 5-7) + 2 (output RZ+RX) = 13.
Total: 13 × 6 blocks + 2 readout = 80 parameters.
