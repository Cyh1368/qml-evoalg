<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the rigid `CZ` interactions with parameterized `CRZ` gates, leveraging the existing one-factorization of the K8 complete graph (7 perfect matchings). Each matching is assigned a shared parameter, `crz_match_0` through `crz_match_6`. This provides tunable entanglement strength per matching, which is a powerful enhancement when working with highly symmetric and parallelizable topologies.

Additionally, this change restores the two-axis input and output collective mixers (`RY` + `RX` for input, and `RZ` + `RX` for output) that yielded exceptional test accuracy (~0.98) in earlier iterations, effectively balancing expressivity and parameter-efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
