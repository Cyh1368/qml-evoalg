<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the symmetry-breaking cube with a permutation-invariant complete-graph CZ layer covering all 28 feature pairs. Ordering the edges as seven perfect matchings minimizes entangling depth. The input RY-RX mixer and output RZ-RX mixer provide complementary noncommuting axes while retaining only four parameters per block. This combines the strongest prior mixer arrangement with lower-depth complete connectivity, targeting faster convergence and improved validation loss without reducing parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
