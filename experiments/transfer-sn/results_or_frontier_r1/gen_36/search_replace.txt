<DESCRIPTION>
The previous best architecture achieved higher generalization and accuracy by inserting a mid-circuit mixer to break up the 28 commuting CZ gates, allowing the circuit to process the phases introduced by the initial entangling edges before applying the rest.
Here, we take that logic a step further by factoring the complete graph K8 into its 7 perfect matchings and distributing them across three separate entangling blocks (Matchings 1-2, Matchings 3-4, Matchings 5-7).
Between each block, we introduce parameter-efficient collective single-qubit rotations (alternating pairs of RY, RX, and RZ). This deeply interleaves trainable non-commuting operations with the fixed entanglement, giving the ansatz significantly greater expressivity while maintaining full permutation equivariance and remaining well within the parameter budget.
</DESCRIPTION>
