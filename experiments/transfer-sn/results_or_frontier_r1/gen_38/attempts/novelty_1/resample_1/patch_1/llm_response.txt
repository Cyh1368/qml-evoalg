<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed, maximally entangling CZ network with trainable CRZ interactions, sharing one angle across each perfect matching. The seven matching-level parameters let Adam suppress redundant couplings or strengthen useful interaction classes while preserving complete K8 coverage and the disjoint-edge schedule. Because CRZ gates initialize near identity, they also provide a stable residual-like path early in training; the surrounding RX/RY mixers convert their learned phases into the mean-Z signal. Sharing within each matching regularizes the added expressivity compared with assigning a separate parameter to every edge.
</DESCRIPTION>
