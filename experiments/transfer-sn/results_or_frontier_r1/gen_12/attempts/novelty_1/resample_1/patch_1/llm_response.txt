<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the overparameterized, symmetry-breaking block with a permutation-symmetric collective mixer using only three parameters per block, reducing the total parameter count from 194 to 20. A complete-graph CZ entangler matches the pairwise structure of the 28-feature encoding and allows information from every feature pair to influence the mean-Z readout. The collective RZ–RX output sequence converts learned phase correlations into measurable populations. Because all CZ gates commute, they are ordered as seven disjoint perfect matchings: this implements the same complete-graph unitary while substantially reducing circuit depth relative to a lexicographic all-pairs schedule. The resulting symmetry, lower parameter count, and reinforced shared-parameter gradients should reduce overfitting and accelerate convergence.
