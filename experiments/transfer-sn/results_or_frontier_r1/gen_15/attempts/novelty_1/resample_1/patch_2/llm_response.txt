<DESCRIPTION>
A fundamentally different ansatz topology using a butterfly (FFT-inspired) entanglement pattern instead of a simple linear chain. The key innovations are:

1. **Butterfly entanglement topology**: Three layers of CZ gates with different strides (even pairs, odd pairs with ring closure, and stride-4 cross connections). This creates all-to-all connectivity in just 3 entanglement layers, whereas the seed's linear chain requires information to propagate sequentially.

2. **Tri-axis rotation strategy**: Alternates RY → RX → RZ → RY rotation layers between entanglement stages. This provides full SU(2) coverage and is specifically designed to complement the ZZ feature encoding: RX rotations are maximally non-commuting with the Z-basis feature encoding, enabling richer information extraction.

3. **Ring + cross structure**: Even-pair CZ, odd-pair CZ (completing a ring), plus stride-4 CZ (crossing the ring). This ensures every qubit pair is connected through at most 2 intermediate entanglement layers, dramatically improving information flow compared to the linear topology.

32 parameters per block × 6 block applications = 192 total ansatz parameters, a modest increase over the seed's 144 that adds significant expressibility through topology rather than raw parameter count.
</DESCRIPTION>
