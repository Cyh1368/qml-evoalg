An excellent way to improve expressivity without adding new two-qubit gate layers is to parameterize the existing entangling structure. By replacing the fixed `CZ` gates with parameter-shared `CRZ` gates (one parameter per matching round), the optimizer can independently control the interaction strength for different subgraphs of the complete feature connectivity. This provides fine-grained control over entanglement scaling, giving the ansatz more capacity to suppress unhelpful feature cross-terms or amplify useful ones while maintaining its optimal shallow depth structure.
