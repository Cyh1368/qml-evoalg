<interleave_mixers>
Interleave two-axis collective mixers between entanglement layers to boost expressivity and nonlocality while preserving parameter efficiency and equivariance.
</interleave_mixers>

<DESCRIPTION>
Following the provided recommendation, this edit inserts parameter-efficient collective `RY` and `RX` layers between each of the perfect-matching `CZ` entanglement rounds. Previously, the sequence of commuting `CZ` gates was completely localized and didn't mix states until the end of the matching sequence. By interleaving two-axis collective mixers (adding merely 2 new parameters per insertion), we allow continuous mixing of phase relationships generated by both the feature map and the entanglers. This significantly boosts the non-local expressiveness of the circuit block without meaningfully impacting parameter efficiency, improving performance and test accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
