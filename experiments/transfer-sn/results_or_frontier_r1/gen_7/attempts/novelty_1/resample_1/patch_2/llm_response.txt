<DESCRIPTION>
This ansatz introduces a "telescoping" multi-scale approach to quantum entanglement, acting across three different spatial topologies within the 8-qubit register. It draws inspiration from strongly entangling layers but replaces uniform connectivity with a nested structure. 
1. It begins with dense single-qubit local preparations (RY, RZ). 
2. It applies a nearest-neighbor ring of CZ gates for tight local entanglement (stride 1). 
3. After intermediate local RX rotations to tilt the Bloch vector, it applies long-range CNOT gates (stride 4) to instantly share information between the two distant halves of the register. 
4. Medium-range CRY parameterized entangling gates (stride 2) follow, smoothly fine-tuning the interactions and increasing expressivity. 
5. An output layer of independent RY gates correctly adjusts the axis prior to the subsequent data uploading or final Z-measurements.
By mixing rigid unparameterized topological gates (CZ, CNOT) with parameterized ones (CRY) across multiple distances, this architecture significantly boosts expressiveness while maintaining strong gradient flows for the Adam optimizer.
