<DESCRIPTION>
Split the 28 CZ edges of K8 into three non-commuting stages separated by two mid-circuit single-axis mixers (RY after stage 1, RX after stage 2). Since CZ gates commute, a single block of all 28 is effectively shallow. Three interleaved non-commuting stages create much deeper effective entanglement. Uses 6 shared parameters per block (same as best prior program with 38 total params). The orthogonal mid-circuit axes (RY, RX) maximize non-commutativity between stages.
