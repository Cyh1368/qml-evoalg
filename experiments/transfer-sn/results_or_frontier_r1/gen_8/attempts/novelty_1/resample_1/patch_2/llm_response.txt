<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current ansatz (RY+RZ layers with CZ line topology) with a richer design:
1. RY layer for Y-rotations on all qubits
2. RZ layer for Z-rotations on all qubits  
3. Staggered ring CZ (even pairs: 0-1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-7 then odd pairs: 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7-0)
4. RX layer between entanglement stages for full SU(2) coverage
5. Cross CZ connections (0-4, 2-6) for long-range entanglement reducing graph diameter
6. Final RZ layer after entanglement

This gives better qubit connectivity (ring+cross vs line), all three rotation axes, and should improve both accuracy and generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
