<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the CRX-heavy, odd/even shared block with a more structured hardware-efficient ansatz. The new block uses four shared parameter groups aligned to adjacent qubit pairs for RY, RX, and RZ, which increases expressivity over the current pair-sharing while still keeping parameters tied and regularized. I also replace the controlled-rotation layer with two CZ entangling patterns: a local ring to propagate information across neighbors and a long-range cross layer to mix the two halves of the register.  

The intent is to improve validation/test accuracy, lower the train-test gap, and make optimization easier for Adam by removing the more brittle controlled rotations and using symmetry-aligned parameter sharing.
</DESCRIPTION>
