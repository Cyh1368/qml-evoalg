<DESCRIPTION>
This edit keeps the block size and overall gate budget essentially the same, but changes the inductive bias to be much less over-parameterized. Instead of giving every qubit its own independent rotation angles, the ansatz now ties parameters by wire parity (even/odd), cutting the trainable degrees of freedom per block from 24 down to 6. That should reduce overfitting and improve validation/test accuracy under the fixed Adam training loop.

I also replace the final linear-chain CZ endpoint with a wrap-around CZ to give the block a ring-like connectivity, which improves information flow across the 8 qubits without increasing gate count. Finally, I mix RX/RY/RZ layers rather than using only RY/RZ, giving a more expressive noncommuting local basis while staying parameter-efficient.
</DESCRIPTION>
