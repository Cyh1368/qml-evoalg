<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the current high-parameter CRY-heavy block with a more parameter-efficient four-group rotation ansatz.

Key ideas:
- Use shared RY, RX, and RZ parameters on contiguous qubit pairs: [0,1], [2,3], [4,5], [6,7].
- Replace the parameterized controlled rotations with a compact fixed CZ mixer across the two halves of the register.
- Keep the block expressive enough for the 6 repeated applications in the fixed training layout, while reducing the number of learnable parameters substantially.

Why this should help:
- The current program overfits slightly: train accuracy is higher than validation/test, and validation loss is relatively poor.
- Shared pairwise rotations reduce redundant degrees of freedom and improve parameter efficiency.
- The cross-half CZ couplings help propagate information globally without adding trainable parameters, which should improve generalization and convergence stability.
</DESCRIPTION>
