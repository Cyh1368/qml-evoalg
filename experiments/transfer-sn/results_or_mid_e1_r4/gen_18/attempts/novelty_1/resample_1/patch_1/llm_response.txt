<DESCRIPTION>
This edit improves the expressivity and entanglement of the ansatz while maintaining a low parameter count by:
1. Completing the nearest-neighbor entanglement ring by adding the missing CZ gate between qubits 6 and 7.
2. Introducing a layer of parametrized controlled-RX (CRX) gates on parity pairs (0-1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-7).
3. Using parameter sharing (a single "crx_parity" key) for the entire CRX layer within each block to improve parameter efficiency and regularization.

This approach follows the recommendation to boost combined_score_raw by increasing model capacity slightly (from 38 to 44 total parameters) while preserving the low generalization gap observed in the current version.
</DESCRIPTION>
