<DESCRIPTION>
The previous ansatz relied heavily on RZ and CZ gates, both of which commute with the IsingZZ feature map used for data encoding. This limits the non-trivial interference between the circuit and the feature-encoded data.

I am proposing a "strongly entangling layer" style structure that uses RY rotations and CNOT gates. Since RY and the target of a CNOT do not commute with the Z-basis phase shifts of the feature map, this should significantly improve the expressivity and ability of the circuit to capture complex correlations between features.

The new ANSATZ_SPEC follows a RY-CNOT(even)-RZ-CNOT(odd)-RY pattern:
1. A layer of parametrized RY gates for initial basis mixing.
2. An even-layered CNOT entanglement block ([0,1], [2,3], [4,5], [6,7]).
3. A layer of parametrized RZ gates to allow for phase adjustments between entanglers.
4. An odd-layered circular CNOT entanglement block ([1,2], [3,4], [5,6], [7,0]).
5. A final layer of parametrized RY gates to ensure full single-qubit rotation coverage (Euler Y-Z-Y decomposition) and to prepare the state for Z-basis measurement.

This design maintains the same parameter count (24 per block, 146 total) as the current version but focuses on gates that actively interact with the data encoding.
</DESCRIPTION>
