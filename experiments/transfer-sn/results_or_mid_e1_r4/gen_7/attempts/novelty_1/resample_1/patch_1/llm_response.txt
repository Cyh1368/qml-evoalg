I propose a hybrid approach that combines:
1. Full RY rotation layer (8 parameters)
2. CRY on even pairs (4 parameters) for controlled entanglement
3. A linear chain of CZ gates (0 parameters) for bidirectional fixed entanglement, similar to Program 2's successful structure
4. RZ rotation layer (8 parameters) for phase calibration
5. CRY on odd pairs (3 parameters) for additional controlled interactions

This retains the controlled rotation structure from Program 3 while adding the proven CZ chain connectivity from Program 2, improving overall connectivity coverage and expressivity. The CZ gates work well as evidenced by Program 2's superior performance and should boost generalization while maintaining reasonable depth.

Expected improvement: validation accuracy should increase from 0.88 to 0.89 (matching Program 2), and test accuracy should reach 0.87 or higher.
