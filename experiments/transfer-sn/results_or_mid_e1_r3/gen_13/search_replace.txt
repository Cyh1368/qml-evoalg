The previous results show that parameter sharing and Z-basis controlled rotations are highly effective for this binary feature classification task. Prior successful models utilized shared parameters to improve efficiency and convergence speed.

This new ANSATZ_SPEC design improves upon the 'Prior 2' success (0.69 score) by:
1.  Maintaining low parameter count (5 unique parameters per block) to maximize parameter efficiency.
2.  Using a hybrid entanglement strategy: one layer of CRZ gates (compatible with the IsingZZ feature map) and one layer of CRY gates (providing non-linear basis transformations) in a cyclic pattern to ensure full qubit connectivity.
3.  Sandwiching the entanglement between RY and RZ rotation layers to allow the model to explore the full Bloch sphere effectively.
4.  Total circuit parameters: (5 params/block * 3 uploads * 2 repeats) + 2 readout params = 32 parameters.
