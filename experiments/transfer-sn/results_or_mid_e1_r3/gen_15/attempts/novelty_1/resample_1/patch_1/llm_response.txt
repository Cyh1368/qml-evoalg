<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz ends with a static CZ ring after the final RY layer. Because the classifier reads out only Z expectations, a terminal diagonal CZ layer cannot change measurement probabilities by itself; its phase information is effectively stranded at the end of the block. This edit moves the ring entanglement before the final RY layer so the last non-commuting single-qubit rotation can convert the entangled phase structure into measurable population differences.

I keep the parameter budget unchanged and preserve the symmetric, low-depth design. This should improve optimization signal, reduce underfitting from the current over-regularized terminal entangler placement, and retain the efficiency benefits of the compact 6-parameter-per-block ansatz.
</DESCRIPTION>
