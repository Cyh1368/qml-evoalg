<DESCRIPTION>
This edit compresses the ansatz to a much smaller, more reusable parameter set while increasing global mixing.

Key ideas:
- Share parameters across parity classes to cut the block from 24 unique angles to 5.
- Keep a lightweight nonlinear entangler using shared CRY gates on long-range pairs.
- Append a static CZ ring at the end of each block to propagate correlations across all 8 qubits without adding parameters.

This should improve parameter efficiency, reduce overfitting, and speed up convergence under the fixed Adam training loop.
</DESCRIPTION>
