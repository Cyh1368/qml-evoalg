<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the fixed CNOT/CZ entanglers with two trainable CRZ ring entangling layers, while keeping the ansatz compact and symmetry-aware.

Why this should help:
- The current block is expressive but partly rigid; the validation gap suggests the model is learning dataset-specific quirks rather than a stable decision boundary.
- CRZ entanglers let the circuit learn the strength of correlations induced by the feature-map uploads, which is especially useful in a re-uploading pipeline.
- Using shared parameters across all qubits in each stage keeps the block parameter-efficient, reduces optimization noise, and usually converges faster with Adam.
- The alternating nearest-neighbor and shifted-ring patterns still allow information to propagate across the full 8-qubit register without making the circuit too deep.

Overall, this aims to improve validation/test accuracy and generalization while preserving a small, trainable ansatz.
</DESCRIPTION>
