<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the dense per-qubit RY/RZ stack with a more parameter-efficient parity-shared brickwork block.

Why this should help:
- Fewer trainable parameters per block lowers overfitting pressure and should reduce the train/validation gap.
- Alternating CNOT and CRY entanglers create non-commuting mixing around the ring, which is a better fit for the fixed ZZ feature map than a purely diagonal CZ ladder.
- Parity sharing keeps the ansatz compact while still allowing distinct behavior on even/odd sublattices.
- The overall circuit remains shallow enough to train well with Adam under the fixed re-uploading schedule.

Net effect: same 8-qubit / 6-block pipeline, but with a more regularized and expressive block that should improve generalization and parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
