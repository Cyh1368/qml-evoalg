I restore the RZ pre-rotation layer (adds phase-encoding flexibility before entangling), keep CRZ on alternating pairs but replace every other CRZ with a plain CZ (as in the top-scoring seed) to add fixed non-parametric entanglement (encouraging efficient parameter use), and close the entangling chain with a wraparound CZ(7,0) to make the entangling graph a full connected ring rather than an open chain. This combination reintroduces the elements that empirically worked best while keeping the parameter count essentially the same as the current candidate (going from 4 CRZ + 4 RZ_pre-less to a full alternating chain of CRZ/CZ plus RZ pre/post layers), directly targeting improved validation/test accuracy and reduced generalization gap.
