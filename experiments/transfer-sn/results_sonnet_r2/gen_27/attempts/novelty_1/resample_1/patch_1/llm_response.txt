<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses a shared-parameter ring of CRZ gates on nearest-neighbor pairs (crz_a, crz_b alternating), which efficiently reuses parameters but only captures local entanglement patterns within the ring topology. Since the ring wraps around (7->0), there is already some long-range connectivity, but it's limited to adjacent pairs in the ring ordering. To help the circuit capture more complex nonlocal correlations that may exist in the feature-pair encoding (which maps 28 features to fixed qubit pairs), I introduce a single additional long-range CRZ gate between qubits 0 and 4 (opposite sides of the ring) that is only applied during blocks corresponding to odd upload indices. This adds just one new parameter ("long_crz") but only activates in half of the reuploading stages, giving the model a chance to build non-local structure without significantly increasing parameter count or circuit depth in every block. This is a small, targeted, low-risk architectural change consistent with the recommendation to test sparse long-range links.
