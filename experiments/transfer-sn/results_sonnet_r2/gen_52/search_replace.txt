Restore the higher-performing dual-ring (distance-1 + distance-2 CRZ) ansatz from the best prior program (score 0.91) as the base, then extend it with a lightweight distance-3 CRZ entanglement ring using two new shared parameters ("crz_e", "crz_f") on qubit pairs (0,3), (1,4), (2,5), (3,6), (4,7), (5,0), (6,1), (7,2). This explores longer-range correlations beyond nearest and next-nearest neighbors with minimal parameter overhead (+2 params per block), building on the empirically stronger dual-ring baseline rather than the CZ-interleaved single-ring variant that scored lower (0.86).
