add_ring_crx_entanglers

<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses a mix of CRZ and CZ gates in a ring topology with pre/post RZ layers, giving good entanglement but only phase-type couplings for the parametrized two-qubit gates (CRZ) combined with fixed CZ. To increase expressivity while keeping parameter count modest, I propose replacing the fixed CZ gates in the ring with parametrized CRX gates. This gives full-ring parametrized entanglement (8 CRZ+CRX gates instead of 4 CRZ + 4 CZ), allowing the ansatz to learn both phase and amplitude-mixing correlations between neighboring qubits, which should improve trainability and reduce the train-test gap by providing more flexible, richer interactions without adding excessive depth. Since CRX and CRZ act on the same wire pairs (offset ring), this adds only 4 extra parameters per block (going from 4 to 8 parametrized 2-qubit gates), which multiplied by N_UPLOADS*N_REPEATS=6 blocks yields 24 more parameters total — a modest increase that should still be efficient while boosting representational power especially for capturing feature correlations beyond simple ZZ phase kicks.
