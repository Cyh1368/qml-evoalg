<NAME>
add_ry_mixer_post_rz

<DESCRIPTION>
Adds a lightweight local mixing layer of RY rotations immediately after the existing `rz_post` layer, reusing the already-shared `ry_p0…ry_p3` parameter keys from the pre-rotation layer. Since these parameters are shared (same string key), this adds zero new trainable parameters while injecting an additional non-commuting rotation axis right before the next feature upload. This can help the model re-express information encoded in the Z-basis (from IsingZZ feature map and RZ layers) into the Y-basis, improving expressivity of each block without any parameter-count cost, potentially speeding convergence and improving accuracy without harming the train-test gap.
