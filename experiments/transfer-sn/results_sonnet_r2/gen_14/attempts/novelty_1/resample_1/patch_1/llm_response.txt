shared_crz_ring_entangler

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the mixed CRZ/CZ entangling chain with a full ring of CRZ gates that share a single trainable angle per block (one param instead of four). This reduces parameter count per block (from 4 entangler params to 1), lowering total N_PARAMS while giving every edge in the ring a tunable phase (versus half of them being fixed CZ). Sharing one parameter across the ring acts as a regularizer, reducing overfitting risk (helping close the generalization gap) while still allowing the entangling strength to be learned jointly, unlike the fixed-angle CZ edges in the current spec. This also increases entangling connectivity (7 CRZ edges forming a full ring across all 8 qubits: 0-1,1-2,...,7-0) versus the current 4 CRZ + 4 CZ chain, which should improve mixing of qubit information for the readout mean.
