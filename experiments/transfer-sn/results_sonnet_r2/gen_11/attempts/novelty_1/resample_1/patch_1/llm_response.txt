per_qubit_gain_readout

<DESCRIPTION>
The current readout uses a single scalar gain applied to the mean of all qubit Z expectations, discarding information about which qubits are individually more predictive of the label. Extending this to a per-qubit gain vector (8 extra parameters) allows the model to learn a weighted combination of the qubit measurements before averaging, effectively acting as a linear readout layer. This is a lightweight, gradient-friendly enhancement that keeps the ansatz depth and gate count fixed while giving the classifier more flexibility to combine quantum features, which should reduce validation/test loss and improve accuracy without meaningfully increasing overfitting risk (only 7 extra parameters vs 170 total).
