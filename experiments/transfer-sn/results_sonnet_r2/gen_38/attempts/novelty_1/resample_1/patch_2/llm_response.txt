<DESCRIPTION>
Since only ANSATZ_SPEC is editable and the offset computation (block_index * N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK) is fixed, I cannot change the sharing granularity across repeats directly. However, I can achieve equivalent parameter tying by using a wire-parity based sharing scheme combined with fewer distinct parameter names, and more importantly, by ensuring params used in repeat 0 and repeat 1 of the same upload are structurally similar but distinct only where needed. Since true cross-repeat sharing requires changing the offset formula (immutable code), the next best lever within the ansatz spec itself is to further reduce N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK by merging RZ pre/post into a single fully shared name set and reducing distinct RY groups from 4 to 2, cutting per-block params from 6 to 4. This continues the empirically successful trend (fewer, more shared parameters improved gap and accuracy from seed to current) while staying within the editable region.

Concretely: merge the 4 RY groups into 2 groups (wires 0,1,4,5 share one param; wires 2,3,6,7 share another), keeping RZ pre/post structure at 4 groups but merging pre and post into the same param keys (as already done in a middle candidate), and keep the 2-angle CRZ ring. This reduces N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK from 6 to 4, giving N_CIRCUIT_PARAMS = 4*6=24, total N_PARAMS=26, a large reduction from 86, testing whether even stronger tying keeps or improves accuracy while shrinking the generalization gap further.
