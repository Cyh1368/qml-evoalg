<DESCRIPTION>
The current ANSATZ_SPEC (68 params, distance-1 ring only) underperforms the prior best (98 params, dual distance-1/distance-2 ring) which achieved a higher combined score (0.91 vs 0.89) with better train/val/test accuracy despite more parameters. To recover that improved entanglement structure while keeping some parameter efficiency, I restore the dual-ring (distance-1 + distance-2) entangling structure with shared angles, but keep the mirrored RY sharing from the current candidate to save some single-qubit parameters relative to the fully independent seed. This adds richer connectivity per block (each qubit couples to both its neighbor and next-nearest neighbor) which the historical results show meaningfully improves accuracy and reduces validation/test loss, at a modest parameter cost (added crz_c/crz_d shared angles), replacing the single long-range CRZ that likely under-entangles the circuit.
