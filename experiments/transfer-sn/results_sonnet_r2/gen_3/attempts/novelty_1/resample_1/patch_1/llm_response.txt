add_ring_entanglement_and_crz_couplings

<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz block only has a linear chain of fixed CZ gates plus per-qubit RY/RZ rotations, with no parametrized entangling gates. This limits the model's ability to learn correlations between distant qubits (recall feature map couples arbitrary qubit pairs via FEATURE_PAIRS) and gives entangling gates no trainable weight, reducing expressivity.

I propose:
1. Close the entangling chain into a ring by adding a CZ between qubit 7 and qubit 0, improving connectivity so information can flow around the whole register rather than just linearly.
2. Replace half of the fixed CZ gates with parametrized CRZ gates (using new independent params), giving the model trainable control over entanglement strength between neighboring qubits. This adds only 8 new parameters per block (small increase from 146 total, within budget) while significantly increasing expressive power, since CRZ interpolates between no entanglement and full CZ-like entanglement depending on the learned angle.
3. Keep the pre/post RZ single-qubit layers intact since they already showed benefit.

This should help reduce the train-test gap by allowing the optimizer to tune entanglement strength (potentially reducing overfitting by shrinking angles toward zero when unnecessary) while still allowing rich entangling patterns when needed for hard-to-separate feature correlations.
