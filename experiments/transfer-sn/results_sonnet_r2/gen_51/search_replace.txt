add_distance3_crz_ring

<DESCRIPTION>
Building on the dual-ring (distance-1 + distance-2 CRZ) ansatz that improved the score from 0.85 to 0.91, I add a third lightweight entanglement ring at graph-distance 3 around the 8-qubit cycle. This introduces longer-range correlations (each qubit now couples to its neighbor 3 steps away) at a minimal cost of only 2 additional shared parameters ("crz_e"/"crz_f"), following the same alternating-parameter-sharing pattern used successfully in the distance-1 and distance-2 rings. Since distance-3 on an 8-cycle produces 4 distinct edges before wrapping (0-3,1-4,2-5,3-6,4-7,5-0,6-1,7-2 — note this is 8 edges, each qubit appearing exactly twice, forming another 8-cycle at higher "stride"), this adds structured non-local entanglement that may help capture higher-order feature interactions not reachable by the shorter-range rings, while keeping the parameter count increase very small (only 2 new params per block, i.e. +12 total given 3 uploads x 2 repeats) relative to the existing 98 params, preserving parameter efficiency while potentially improving accuracy and reducing the train-test gap.
