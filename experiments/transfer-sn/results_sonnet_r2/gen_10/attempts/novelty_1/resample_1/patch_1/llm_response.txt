<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz mixes fixed CZ gates and trainable CRZ gates in an alternating chain, plus full pre/post RZ layers on all 8 qubits. Given the feature map already applies IsingZZ (Z-Z coupling) and the block already has RZ pre-rotations, the pre-RZ layer is partially redundant with the phase information from the previous block's post-RZ and the next upload's feature map. To improve parameter efficiency and entangling power per parameter, I: (1) remove the redundant pre-RZ layer (halving single-qubit Z rotations, which are largely absorbed by the feature map phases), and (2) make all entangling links trainable CRZ instead of a fixed CZ / CRZ alternation, closing the ring into a full 8-gate CRZ ring. This increases entangling expressivity (from 4 to 8 trainable entanglers) while removing 8 redundant single-qubit parameters (pre-RZ), keeping total parameter count comparable but shifting capacity from redundant single-qubit phases to more expressive trainable entanglement, which should improve generalization and reduce the train-test gap.
