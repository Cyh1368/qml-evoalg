The current spec (train 0.98, val 0.91, test 0.86, gap 0.12) shows overfitting relative to the earlier seed variants. The generalization gap is larger than the seed's 0.07-0.08. To improve generalization while keeping entangling expressivity, I reduce the number of independent CRZ parameters by sharing across more pairs (already partially done with crz_a/crz_b alternating), but the deeper issue is likely too much freedom in the RZ pre/post layers duplicating capacity redundantly with CRZ parameters. Instead, I propose merging the pre-RZ and post-RZ layers into a single shared-parameter set per wire (reduces params from 16 RZ per block to 8), which cuts total parameter count substantially, encourages simpler/more regularized circuits, and should reduce the train-test gap while retaining accuracy. This follows the parameter-efficiency recommendation and typical regularization intuition in variational circuits: fewer redundant free single-qubit phases before/after the entangling layer reduces overfitting risk without lowering the entangling capacity that captures feature correlations.
