<DESCRIPTION>
Since I cannot edit the offset logic outside the EVOLVE-BLOCK, I achieve equivalent parameter sharing across the two repeats within an upload by exploiting the existing per-block parameter-key mechanism differently: I restructure ANSATZ_SPEC to keep the same gate structure but this doesn't directly let repeats share params since offset changes per block_index regardless of param names (each block_index gets its own offset window, so identical param names across blocks would still map to different absolute indices due to the offset).

Given the constraint that offset = block_index * N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK is fixed, the only way to reduce effective free parameters within the EVOLVE-BLOCK is to reduce N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK itself (i.e., increase intra-block parameter sharing) so that even though blocks still get independent copies, each copy has fewer unique knobs, reducing total capacity and thus mitigating overfitting while retaining the repeat structure. I do this by merging the two independent CRZ ring angles into one shared angle, and merging the pre/post RZ pair-groupings into fewer groups (2 groups of 4 qubits instead of 4 groups of 2), cutting N_PARAMS_PER_BLOCK from 18 to 12 (8 RY stays independent for expressivity on encoding features, but RZ pre/post pairs are now grouped by half-register, and CRZ ring angle is fully shared). This reduces total circuit parameters from 108 to 72, directly attacking the overfitting gap while keeping the same gate topology/depth.
