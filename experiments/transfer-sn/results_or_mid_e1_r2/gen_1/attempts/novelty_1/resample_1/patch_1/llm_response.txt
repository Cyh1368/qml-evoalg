<DESCRIPTION>
The seed ansatz uses a simple line of CZ gates and heavy RZ rotations (16 out of 24 parameters per block). In binary classification with IsingZZ feature encoding, RZ gates are often redundant as the data re-uploading already introduces phase shifts. 

This improvement:
1. Replaces the linear CZ chain with a circular CNOT chain (8 gates), providing better connectivity and state mixing.
2. Diversifies the rotation types by using RY, RX, and RZ layers (8 params each), ensuring the ansatz can explore the full Bloch sphere and effectively transform the phase-encoded features into basis-state differences for the Z-expectation readout.
3. Maintains the parameter budget (24 per block) while significantly increasing the expressivity and entangling power of the circuit.
</DESCRIPTION>
