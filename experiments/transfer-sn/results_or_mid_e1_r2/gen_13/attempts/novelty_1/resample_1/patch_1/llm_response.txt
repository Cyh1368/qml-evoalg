<DESCRIPTION>
This edit reduces over-parameterization while keeping a strong inductive bias for the re-uploading classifier. The current block uses fully independent local rotations on every qubit plus a large number of parameters, which appears to fit the training set well but leaves a noticeable validation/test gap. I replace it with a more structured hybrid ansatz:

- parity-shared RX layers to keep a small amount of global mixing,
- independent RY rotations on each qubit to preserve per-qubit expressivity,
- a fixed CZ chain for stable entanglement propagation,
- long-range CRY couplings with shared parameters to capture nonlocal feature interactions,
- parity-shared final RZ layers for compact phase refinement.

This should lower the parameter count substantially, improve parameter efficiency, and often generalize better under the fixed data-reuploading training loop while still retaining enough expressivity for the 3-upload, 2-repeat pipeline.
</DESCRIPTION>
