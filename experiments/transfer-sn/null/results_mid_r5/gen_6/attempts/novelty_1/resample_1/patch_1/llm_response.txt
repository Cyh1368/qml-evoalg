<DESCRIPTION>
This update optimizes the ansatz for better parameter efficiency and expressivity. 
1.  **Replaced Linear CZ Chain with Circular CNOT Chain**: The previous design used a linear nearest-neighbor `CZ` chain (7 gates). A circular `CNOT` chain (8 gates, including the [7, 0] wrap-around) provides stronger entanglement and better information flow across all qubits, which is crucial for high-dimensional feature classification.
2.  **Reduced Parameter Count**: The current ansatz (24 params/block) used two separate `RZ` layers. By condensing the rotations into a more standard `RY-CNOT-RZ` structure (16 params/block), we reduce the total circuit parameters from 144 to 96 (a ~33% reduction). This significantly boosts the `parameter_efficiency` metric while maintaining enough capacity (6 total applications of the block) to keep accuracy high.
3.  **Optimization Path**: The circular CNOT connectivity helps the model converge in fewer steps by providing more direct paths for gradient flow between all qubit pairs across the multiple re-uploading stages.
</DESCRIPTION>
