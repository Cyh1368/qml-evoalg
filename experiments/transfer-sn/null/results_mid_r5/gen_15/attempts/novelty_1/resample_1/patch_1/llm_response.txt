<DESCRIPTION>
This edit compresses the block to four trainable angles per repeated ansatz block while keeping strong data-driven mixing.

Why this should help:
- Parameter efficiency improves materially: 4 shared circuit parameters per block instead of 5, dropping total trainable parameters from 32 to 26.
- The entangler pattern becomes a small-world graph: local CZ pairs preserve the useful inductive bias from the binary feature map, while two long-range bridges connect the two halves of the register and help information flow across the whole 8-qubit system.
- Using shared parity-based RY and half-register RZ layers reduces overfitting pressure and usually converges faster under Adam on re-uploading classifiers.
- Gate count also falls slightly, which should help the combined score without sacrificing the proven re-uploading structure.

The change stays fully within the allowed ANSATZ_SPEC schema and preserves the fixed training loop and readout.
</DESCRIPTION>
