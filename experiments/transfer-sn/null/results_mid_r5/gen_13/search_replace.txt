<DESCRIPTION>
Improve the ansatz expressivity and validation loss by introducing parametrized two-qubit gates (CRX) and ensuring full Bloch sphere coverage for each qubit. The new block consists of an RY rotation layer, followed by a circular entanglement pattern of CRX gates, and a final RZ rotation layer. This increases the parameter count per block from 16 to 24 (total 146), which is expected to lower the validation loss significantly (aiming for &lt;0.12) based on prior performance trends where higher-parameter models achieved better loss and overall scores.
</DESCRIPTION>
