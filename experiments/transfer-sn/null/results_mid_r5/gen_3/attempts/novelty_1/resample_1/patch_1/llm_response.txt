<DESCRIPTION>
I’m using a multiscale, parity-split quantum CNN-style block instead of independent per-qubit rotations plus a nearest-neighbor CZ chain.

The idea is to:
- regularize strongly by sharing parameters across symmetry classes of qubits,
- mix information locally with nearest-neighbor CNOTs,
- inject long-range feature interactions directly with shared CRZ couplings,
- and finish with a mirrored CZ pattern that couples opposite ends of the register.

This creates a hierarchical butterfly-like entangling structure that is better matched to the fixed ZZ feature map: local correlations are captured first, then long-range phase information is propagated across the full 8-qubit register with far fewer effective degrees of freedom. That should improve generalization, reduce overfitting, and train efficiently under the fixed Adam loop.
</DESCRIPTION>
