<DESCRIPTION>
The current program achieves excellent accuracy (0.99) but has slow convergence (390 steps) due to aggressive parameter sharing. Prior program 2 achieved better convergence (180 steps) with individual RY and RZ gates.

The key insight: the trade-off between parameter count and convergence speed is non-linear. The current design (86 params, 390 steps) has worse overall efficiency than prior 2 (146 params, 180 steps).

**Proposed improvement:**
1. Restore individual RY and RZ pre gates for per-qubit expressiveness (16 params per block)
2. Add strategically placed CRZ (controlled-RZ) parametrized gates on non-adjacent qubits (0-2, 2-4, 4-6) to enhance mid-range entanglement without creating a fully-connected entangling layer
3. Keep individual RZ post gates (8 params per block)

This design:
- Provides 27 unique params per block (vs 24 in prior 2's successful configuration)
- Enables finer per-qubit control while maintaining controlled two-qubit interactions
- Should reduce convergence steps significantly (back toward ~200-250) while preserving high accuracy
- Total params ≈ 164 (reasonable middle ground between 86 and 146)
</DESCRIPTION>
