The current ansatz (Score 1.24, 86 params) uses a linear CZ chain and limited rotation variety. While accurate, it shows slower convergence (390 steps) and higher training loss (0.16) compared to more expressive designs. 

To improve the `combined_score`, I propose a "Strongly Entangling" block structure that utilizes all three rotation axes (RX, RY, RZ) and a two-stage CNOT entanglement pattern. 

1. **Gate Diversity**: Adding RX rotations alongside RY and RZ ensures the ansatz can reach any point on the Bloch sphere more efficiently, facilitating faster convergence.
2. **Entanglement**: Replacing the linear CZ chain with a two-layer circular CNOT pattern (even pairs then odd pairs) improves the propagation of quantum correlations across the 8-qubit register.
3. **Parameter Allocation**: I've assigned 8 independent parameters each to RX and RY layers to maximize expressivity, while using a shared 4-parameter scheme for the RZ layer. This results in 20 parameters per block (122 total), providing a balance between the capacity of Seed 2 (146 params) and the efficiency of the current model.

This configuration aims to lower the training loss and accelerate convergence while maintaining high generalization.
