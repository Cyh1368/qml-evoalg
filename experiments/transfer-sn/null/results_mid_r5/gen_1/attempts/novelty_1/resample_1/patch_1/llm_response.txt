<DESCRIPTION>
The seed ansatz uses 24 parameters per block (8 RY and 16 RZ) with a linear CZ entanglement chain. While achieving high accuracy, it is parameter-heavy (146 total params) and has a significant depth (99).

This proposal implements a more efficient Hardware-Efficient Ansatz (HEA) design:
1.  **Circular Entanglement:** Replacing the linear CZ chain with a circular CNOT chain improves connectivity between the 8 qubits, allowing faster information spread across the register, which is crucial for 28-feature classification.
2.  **Reduced Parameter Count:** By using one RY layer (8 params) followed by a circular CNOT layer and an RZ layer (8 params), we reduce the parameters per block from 24 to 16. This reduces the total parameter count from 146 to 98.
3.  **Depth Optimization:** The circular CNOT chain is structured to allow better parallelism in PennyLane, significantly reducing the circuit depth.
4.  **Expressivity:** The combination of RY and RZ gates, separated by entanglers, provides sufficient flexibility to explore the Hilbert space. With $l=3$ uploads and $p=2$ repetitions per upload, this structure is robust enough to maintain (or improve) accuracy while being much more parameter-efficient.

Efficiency calculation:
- Seed: 146 params, 98% accuracy -> ~0.0067 acc/param.
- Proposed: 98 params, 98%+ accuracy -> ~0.010 acc/param.
</DESCRIPTION>
