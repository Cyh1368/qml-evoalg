<symmetric_compact_ansatz>
Reduce the block to a symmetry-regularized, parameter-efficient circuit: parity-shared RY/RZ layers on all 8 qubits followed by a ring of CZ entanglers.

Why this should help:
- Cuts the trainable circuit parameters from 14 per block to 4 per block, improving parameter efficiency and typically reducing overfitting.
- Keeps full-register coverage with 16 single-qubit rotations per block, so every qubit still learns local phase and amplitude structure.
- Preserves a strong entangling backbone with the CZ ring, which is well matched to the fixed ZZ feature map.
- The even/odd sharing encourages a smoother hypothesis class, which often improves validation/test stability while still fitting the binary task quickly.
