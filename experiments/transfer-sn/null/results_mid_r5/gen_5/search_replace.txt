<DESCRIPTION>
I’m replacing the fully independent per-qubit layers with a compact, structured ansatz that is better matched to the fixed ZZ feature map.

The key ideas are:
- pairwise parameter sharing on neighboring qubits, which cuts the block parameter count substantially and should improve parameter efficiency and generalization;
- a multi-scale entangling pattern that combines a nearest-neighbor CZ ring with long-range CNOT couplings, giving the block both local and global mixing capacity;
- keeping the ansatz shallow enough to train well under the fixed Adam budget while preserving expressivity across the three re-uploading stages.

This should reduce overfitting, lower the loss more reliably, and improve the combined score by raising test accuracy per parameter.
</DESCRIPTION>
