<DESCRIPTION>
To improve the model's ability to learn complex decision boundaries, I am increasing the expressivity of the ansatz by adding an extra layer of RX gates and expanding the entanglement structure. Specifically, I am:
1. Adding an RX rotation layer to provide additional SU(2) coverage alongside the existing RY and RZ layers.
2. Expanding the CRZ long-range correlation layer to include jump-2 connections across all qubits, increasing connectivity.
3. Consolidating the CZ entangler backbone into a more standard full-cycle entanglement (0-1, 1-2, ..., 7-0) for better qubit-to-qubit interaction.

These changes increase the parameter count per block, which typically improves convergence for classification tasks on 8-qubit circuits by offering a richer hypothesis space.
</DESCRIPTION>
