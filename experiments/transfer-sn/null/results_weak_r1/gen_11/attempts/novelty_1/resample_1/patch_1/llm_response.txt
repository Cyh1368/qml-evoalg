</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
This change implements the recommended strategy of mixing controlled-rotation axes in the entangler layer. We replace every second CRY gate with a CRZ gate to introduce controlled-Z rotations alongside the existing controlled-Y rotations. This preserves the depth and gate count while potentially improving convergence and robustness by diversifying the types of controlled rotations in the ansatz. The parameter names are updated to reflect the gate change (cry_* to crz_*), maintaining the same parameter structure and count.
</DESCRIPTION>
