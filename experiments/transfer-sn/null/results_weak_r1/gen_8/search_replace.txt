<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses 140 parameters, which leads to high redundancy given we have already achieved 100% accuracy. I am modifying the `ANSATZ_SPEC` to share single-qubit rotation parameters across adjacent qubit pairs (0-1, 2-3, etc.). This reduces the number of independent RY parameters per block from 16 to 8, while keeping the rich CRZ entanglement structure. This reduction in parameters will directly improve the `parameter_efficiency` metric and is expected to maintain our high classification accuracy by enforcing useful structural symmetries.
</DESCRIPTION>
