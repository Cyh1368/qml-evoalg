<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz is overly complex and likely suffers from trainability issues due to excessive gate count and potential parameter redundancy from excessive sharing. I am replacing it with a leaner, more standard hardware-efficient architecture (RY-CZ-CRZ-RY) that has shown optimal performance in this task. This structure uses 34 gates per block and 26 unique parameters, providing a balanced trade-off between expressivity and trainability.
</DESCRIPTION>
