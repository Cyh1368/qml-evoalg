<reduce_shared_ry_crz_plus_cry_bridge>
Make the ansatz more parameter-efficient by (1) strongly sharing the RY parameters within each of the two RY layers using low-half vs high-half groups, (2) sharing CRZ parameters across multiple “adjacent” and “chain” pairs, and (3) adding a single extra controlled-rotation bridge (CRY on [0,3]) to preserve expressivity after parameter reduction. This should lower L2 loss and improve the parameter-efficiency term while keeping the same overall connectivity style.
