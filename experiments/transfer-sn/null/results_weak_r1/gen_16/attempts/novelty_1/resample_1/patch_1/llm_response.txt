Adding a second long-range CRZ layer to increase mid-range correlation capture. The new CRZ gates target anti-diagonal pairs (0,7), (1,2), (4,3), (5,6) with unique parameters. This change aims to improve parameter efficiency and reduce convergence steps while maintaining high accuracy.
