**Key observations:**

2. The linear CZ chain (0-1, 1-2, ..., 6-7) has depth 7 since each gate shares a qubit with the next. A brick-wall CZ ring has depth 2 with better connectivity.
3. Removing the redundant RZ layer and using brick-wall CZ reduces from 24→16 params/block (146→98 total) and dramatically reduces depth.
