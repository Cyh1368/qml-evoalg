collective_phase_mixers
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the overparameterized, qubit-specific block with a permutation-consistent collective mixer. Each rotation angle is shared across all eight qubits, reducing the circuit from 146 to 20 total trainable parameters while retaining independent parameters for each repeated block. The fixed all-pairs IsingZZ feature map already supplies data-dependent entanglement, so the additional line of CZ gates is unnecessary and imposes an artificial ordering on the qubits. The RY layer creates interference paths, the RZ layer controls their relative phases, and the terminal RX converts phase information into the measured Z populations. Unlike the seed's terminal RZ layer, the final RX remains directly observable by the readout. This should improve parameter efficiency, reduce overfitting and circuit depth, and accelerate Adam convergence while preserving the symmetry naturally matched by the mean-Z readout.
</DESCRIPTION>
