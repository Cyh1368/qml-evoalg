<DESCRIPTION>
Tie each post-entangler RZ angle to the corresponding pre-entangler angle. Every RZ commutes with the CZ line, so the original block depends only on the sum of each wire's pre/post angles; keeping them independent wastes one parameter per qubit without adding expressivity. Reusing the parameter keys preserves the same reachable effective rotations while reducing parameters per block from 24 to 16 and total parameters from 146 to 98. Applying the shared angle twice also retains the favorable effective update scale of the redundant parametrization, helping preserve convergence speed while improving parameter efficiency and reducing overfitting risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
