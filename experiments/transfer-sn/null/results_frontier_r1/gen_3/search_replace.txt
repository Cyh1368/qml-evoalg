<DESCRIPTION>
To improve parameter efficiency and convergence speed, we optimize the ansatz block by reducing redundant parameterized gates and enhancing the entanglement topology.

1. **Parameter Reduction**: The original block uses three layers of parameterized rotations (`RY`, `RZ_pre`, `RZ_post`) requiring 24 parameters per block (146 total). By removing the `RZ_post` layer, we reduce the block's parameter count to 16 (98 total). Because the ansatz is repeatedly applied across multiple uploads, the rotation operations cyclicly combine, so the single `RZ` layer per block remains sufficiently expressive to span the necessary Hilbert space.
2. **Entanglement Topology**: The previous linear chain of `CZ` gates (`[0, 1], [1, 2], ..., [6, 7]`) causes a maximum entanglement distance of 7 and requires 7 layers of execution. We replace this with a ring of `CNOT` gates connecting nearest neighbors with periodic boundary conditions (connecting wire 7 back to 0). By grouping them into non-overlapping pairs (`[0, 1], [2, 3], ...` followed by `[1, 2], [3, 4], ...`), the entanglement phase completes in just 2 depth layers.
3. **CNOT vs CZ**: While `CZ` only adds phases, `CNOT` directly mixes computational basis states, which accelerates the generation of complex multi-qubit entanglement when alternated with our IsingZZ (phase-coupling) data encoding layer.
</DESCRIPTION>
