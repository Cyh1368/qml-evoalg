<DESCRIPTION>
Redesigned ansatz using a brick-layer entanglement pattern with ring CNOT connectivity. The block alternates between even-pair CNOTs (0→1, 2→3, 4→5, 6→7) and odd-pair CNOTs (1→2, 3→4, 5→6, 7→0), sandwiching RY and RZ rotation layers between them. This creates a highly expressive circuit with efficient parameter usage (24 params per block) and strong entanglement coverage across all qubits via the ring topology. The brick-layer pattern avoids barren plateaus better than fully random entanglement while ensuring information flows between all qubit pairs within two entangling layers.
</DESCRIPTION>
