<DESCRIPTION>
The current butterfly-based ansatz achieves a combined score of 1.21, significantly underperforming both prior programs (1.40 and 1.44). Analysis reveals:

**Current Issues:**
- Test accuracy drops to 0.99 (vs 1.00 in priors)
- Validation/test loss worsen (0.14/0.12 vs 0.05/0.05)
- Convergence slows (240 steps vs 150)
- Despite only 62 params, gate_count explodes to 396 (vs 228 in Gen1)
- Architecture complexity does not translate to better expressiveness

**Root Cause:**
The butterfly multi-scale entanglement with 38 parametrized/fixed gates per block creates excessive circuit depth and coupling complexity without sufficient parameter control. The extreme parameter sharing (only 2 unique RY parameters across 4 qubits, etc.) limits adaptability during training.

**Proposed Fix:**
Return to the proven Gen1 architecture (score 1.44) which demonstrated:
- Clean parameter efficiency: 16 parameters per block (8 RY + 8 CRX)
- Balanced gate count: 228 total (manageable depth)
- Perfect test accuracy: 1.00
- Fast convergence: 150 steps

The Gen1 structure uses:
1. Independent RY rotations on all 8 qubits (individual parameter per wire)
2. A ring of CRX gates connecting consecutive qubits (8 unique CRX parameters)
3. A cross-pattern CZ layer for multi-scale entanglement
4. No excessive parameter sharing or butterfly complexity

This approach combines direct parameter control with proven entanglement patterns.
