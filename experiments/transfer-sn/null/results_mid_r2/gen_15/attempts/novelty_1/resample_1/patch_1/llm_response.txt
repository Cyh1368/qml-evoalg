<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses heavy parameter sharing (only 8 parameters per block) and a restricted gate set (RY, RX, CZ), which results in slower convergence (step 750) and higher validation loss (0.11) compared to earlier successful seeds. 

This improvement evolves the ansatz by:
1.  **Increasing expressivity**: Removing parameter sharing for single-qubit rotations, giving each qubit its own degrees of freedom.
2.  **Enhancing entanglement**: Utilizing a combination of controlled-rotations (CRX/CRY) in a circular chain and fixed long-range CZ gates, as seen in the high-performing (1.45 score) previous iterations.
3.  **Applying the RZ-post-entanglement recommendation**: Adding a layer of 8 independent RZ rotations at the end of each block to optimize phase interference following the entanglement layers.

This configuration results in 24 parameters per block, striking a balance between the high expressivity required for fast convergence (targeting the ~150 step range) and the parameter efficiency needed for a high overall score.
