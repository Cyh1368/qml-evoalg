<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current RZ/CRZ-heavy block with a compact butterfly-style hardware-efficient ansatz.

Why this should help:
- Keeps the total trainable budget at 16 parameters per block, matching the previously strongest parameter regime.
- Removes redundant phase-only layers that add depth but little direct value for Z readout.
- Uses two noncommuting single-qubit layers (RY then RX) to convert the feature-map phases into measurable amplitude differences.
- Uses two sparse CZ butterfly stages to spread information globally across the 8 qubits with lower depth than the current ring-plus-cross pattern.
- This should improve convergence speed, reduce gate count/depth, and preserve the strong validation/test performance seen in the best prior runs.

Overall, this is a targeted efficiency upgrade: same parameter count class, better connectivity structure, and less circuit overhead.
</DESCRIPTION>
