The previous candidate achieved a perfect 1.0 accuracy on both train and test sets but used 50 total parameters. To improve the `combined_score`, we can significantly increase the `parameter_efficiency` by reducing the number of unique parameters per block while maintaining the same expressive circuit structure.

I have updated the `ANSATZ_SPEC` to share parameters across qubits (even/odd wires) instead of more granular groups. This reduces the number of unique parameters per block from 8 to 4. With $l=3$ re-uploads and $p=2$ repeats, the total circuit parameters decrease from 48 to 24, bringing the total parameter count (including gain and bias) to 26. This increases the theoretical `parameter_efficiency` from 0.02 to approximately 0.038. I also maintained the periodic CZ ring structure and the alternating RY/RX layers, which have already proven effective for this binary classification task.
