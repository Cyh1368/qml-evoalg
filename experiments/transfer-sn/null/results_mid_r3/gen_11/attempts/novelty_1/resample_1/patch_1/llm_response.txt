<DESCRIPTION>
This proposal aims to improve the convergence speed and maintain high accuracy by introducing long-distance entanglement and parameter sharing within the ansatz block. 

The current program uses a ring of CRY gates which provides nearest-neighbor connectivity. While effective, it can lead to slower information propagation across the 8-qubit register. 

My modification:
1. Retains the initial RY rotation layer on all qubits for local expressivity.
2. Introduces a fixed CNOT ring to establish baseline entanglement without increasing parameter count.
3. Adds long-distance CRY gates (connecting qubits 0-4, 1-5, 2-6, and 3-7) to facilitate faster global correlation development.
4. Adds a final RZ layer with shared parameters between opposite qubits (e.g., qubit 0 and 4 share a parameter) to maintain the 16-parameter-per-block budget while adding phase flexibility.

This structure maintains the efficient 98-parameter total but should accelerate convergence due to the improved connectivity graph.
</DESCRIPTION>
