<DESCRIPTION>
This evolution proposes a more parameter-efficient and faster-converging ansatz by combining fixed two-qubit gates (CZ) with a reduced number of parametrized entanglers (CRY).

The previous 1.49 score was achieved using 8 RY gates and a ring of 8 CRY gates, totaling 16 parameters per block (98 total parameters). My proposed block uses:
1. A full layer of independent RY rotations (8 parameters) to maintain high expressive power for basis transformation.
2. A fixed CZ ring to provide rapid entanglement without increasing the parameter count, which is expected to speed up Adam convergence (drawing inspiration from Seed 1's faster convergence).
3. A set of 4 long-range CRY cross-links (0-4, 1-5, 2-6, 3-7) to facilitate global information flow across the 8-qubit register. These use 4 independent parameters.

Total parameters per block are reduced from 16 to 12. This decreases the total model parameters from 98 to 74, significantly improving the `parameter_efficiency` metric. The combination of fixed circular entanglement and parametrized global bridges is designed to maintain 100% accuracy while lowering the final loss and improving training speed.
</DESCRIPTION>
