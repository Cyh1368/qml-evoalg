<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the broader seed block with a more parameter-efficient mirrored ansatz. It keeps strong local mixing via shared RY/RZ layers, retains a small set of CZ entanglers for short-range correlation, and adds the recommended shared CRZ diagonal coupler on long-range pairs ([0,7], [1,6], [2,5], [3,4]) to capture global feature interactions with very few extra degrees of freedom.

The key idea is to reduce depth and parameter count while preserving expressivity through parameter sharing and a lightweight long-range entangling path. This should improve optimization speed, reduce overfitting risk, and raise the combined score by improving efficiency without sacrificing accuracy.
