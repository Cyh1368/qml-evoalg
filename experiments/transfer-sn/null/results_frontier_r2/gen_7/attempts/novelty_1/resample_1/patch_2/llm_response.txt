<DESCRIPTION>
The previous candidate programs showed that using a pure `RY` + `CNOT` ansatz underfits the dataset, while a highly parameterized ansatz (like Prior 2 with 146 parameters) achieves near-perfect accuracy (0.98 test accuracy) but is relatively inefficient and contains redundant parameters. 

Specifically, in Prior 2, the ansatz block ended with `RZ` and `CZ` gates. Because the final readout is a `PauliZ` expectation value, any gates at the very end of the circuit that commute with `Z` (such as `RZ` and `CZ`) have zero effect on the final measurement. This means the last layers in the final block application were entirely wasted, limiting gradient flow and expressivity.

To solve this and maximize the combined score (which favors both high accuracy and parameter efficiency), we introduce a highly expressive 16-parameter block with the following layout:
1. `RY` rotation layer: Rotates the state out of the computational basis (useful after the Z-diagonal `IsingZZ` feature maps).
2. `CZ` entangling ring: Creates maximum entanglement efficiently without adding parameters.
3. `RX` rotation layer: Further rotates the state. Crucially, because it is placed *after* the `CZ` gates, it ensures that the `CZ` gates in the final block application do not commute with the final `Z` measurements, making every single gate in the circuit actively contribute to the output.

This reduces the parameter count from 146 (in the best prior) to 98, massively boosting parameter efficiency, while the improved non-commuting placement of gates and Euler-like alternating rotations (RY-CZ-RX) should allow the model to comfortably reach ~1.0 accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
