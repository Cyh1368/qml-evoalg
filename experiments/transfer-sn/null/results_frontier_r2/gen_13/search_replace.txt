<rz_ry_rz_sandwich>
Replaces the single RY and RZ gates with an arbitrary single-qubit rotation parameterized via Euler angles (RZ-RY-RZ). This increases the single-qubit expressivity before the entangling layer, providing a wider set of local transformations to better orient the states relative to the fixed feature map couplings. It also shifts the two-qubit block to a ring topology for more symmetric entanglement spread.
</rz_ry_rz_sandwich>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replacing the RY and RZ gates with a full RZ-RY-RZ sequence implements an arbitrary local single-qubit rotation (U3) via Euler angles. This provides maximum expressiveness per qubit within the ansatz before the entangling CZ operations. Additionally, by modifying the entanglement map from a line (or disjoint pairs) to a full contiguous ring (i.e., nearest neighbor CZ gates with periodic boundary conditions), entanglement is symmetrically distributed across all 8 qubits. This approach quadruples the number of trainable Z-rotations relative to the Y-rotations in the block without significantly increasing depth.
</DESCRIPTION>
