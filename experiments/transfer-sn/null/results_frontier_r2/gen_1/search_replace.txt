<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the overparameterized line ansatz with a permutation-equivariant, QAOA-style collective mixer. The original two RZ layers commute through the CZ layer and are therefore partly redundant; the final RZ layer is also invisible to a Z-basis readout in the last block. Shared RY/RZ rotations, a complete-graph CZ interaction matching the all-pairs feature geometry, and a final shared RX rotation preserve phase sensitivity while making every rotation relevant to the readout. Complete-graph CZ and collective rotations remove arbitrary endpoint bias from the line topology and align with the mean-Z readout. Parameter sharing reduces the block from 24 parameters to 3, lowering the full model from 146 to 20 parameters, which should improve generalization, parameter efficiency, and Adam convergence while retaining nonlinear all-qubit expressivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
