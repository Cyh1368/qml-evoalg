<DESCRIPTION>
This edit implements a hybrid entangling strategy that interleaves parameterized CRX gates with fixed CZ gates in a ring topology. The approach combines the parameter efficiency of CZ gates with the expressivity of CRX gates. By alternating between even and odd indexed CRX/CZ pairs, we create a more flexible entangling structure while maintaining a relatively low parameter count. This should improve the model's ability to learn complex patterns in the data while keeping the circuit depth manageable.
</DESCRIPTION>
