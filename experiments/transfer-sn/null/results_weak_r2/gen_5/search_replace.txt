<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the seed block’s mostly 1D CZ chain with a higher-connectivity CZ entangling scaffold (more non-neighbor interactions, no extra parameters), and reduce parameter count by sharing the final RZ across all qubits (one parameter instead of eight). This should improve representational power while boosting parameter efficiency and reducing the train–test gap.
