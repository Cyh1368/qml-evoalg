<DESCRIPTION>
The current model uses independent parameters for every gate, leading to 146 parameters and potential overfitting, as evidenced by the high train accuracy (1.00) and slightly lower test accuracy (0.98). I will implement a parameter-sharing scheme (grouping by qubit parity) that reduces the number of unique parameters per ansatz block from 24 to 6, significantly improving parameter efficiency. Additionally, I will replace the linear CZ-chain entanglement with a more expressive connectivity pattern (inspired by the seed program's structure), which should maintain high accuracy while potentially allowing for faster convergence and better generalization by utilizing circuit depth more effectively.
</DESCRIPTION>
