<name>
shared_ring_entangling_reduced_params
</name>

<description>
Reduce parameter count per block by reusing (sharing) rotation parameters across qubit subsets (even/odd). Increase effective expressivity with a denser but fixed CZ entangling pattern: two staggered ring layers plus a cross/ladder layer. Remove controlled CRZ parameters (often redundant with CZ + local rotations) to lower circuit/optimization complexity while preserving nonlinearity for the classifier.

This should improve the combined score by (1) lowering N_PARAMS (better parameter_efficiency) and (2) reducing train-test gap/optimization difficulty while retaining high expressivity via CZ connectivity and re-uploads.
</description>
