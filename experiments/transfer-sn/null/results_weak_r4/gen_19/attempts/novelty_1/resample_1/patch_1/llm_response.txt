<DESCRIPTION>
This edit enhances the expressivity and parameter efficiency of the circuit. 
1. **Trainable Entanglement**: I replaced the second layer of fixed `CZ` gates with a `CRZ` layer. This adds trainable entangling gates, which significantly improves the model's capacity to learn complex feature correlations while maintaining similar depth.
2. **Parameter Sharing**: I introduced parameter sharing in the input `RY` layer (grouping into 4 pairs) and kept the `RX` layer sharing. This reduces the parameter count from 16 to 12 per block, matching the parameter-efficient configuration of the highest-performing previous model (1.44 score). By reducing parameter count, we promote better generalization and reduce the risk of overfitting, while the new trainable entanglement layers ensure we don't sacrifice capacity.
</DESCRIPTION>
