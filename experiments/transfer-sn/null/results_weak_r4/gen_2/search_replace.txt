<DESCRIPTION>
This edit enhances the ansatz by replacing the simple CZ chain with a more sophisticated entangling strategy that includes additional controlled rotations and better parameter distribution. The new architecture includes:
1. More expressive single-qubit rotations alternating RY and RZ layers
2. Strategic CRZ gates for stronger entanglement between distant qubits
3. Additional CZ connections for improved connectivity
4. Better parameter distribution for efficient learning
This should improve both training convergence and final accuracy while maintaining parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
