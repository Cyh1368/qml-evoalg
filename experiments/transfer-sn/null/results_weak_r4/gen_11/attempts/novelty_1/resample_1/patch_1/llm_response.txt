<REDUCED_rx_param_sharing>
<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce trainable parameters per ansatz block by sharing the RX parameters across qubit pairs (0&1, 2&3, 4&5, 6&7).  
This keeps the same gate structure (RY layer + CZ two-layer ring + RX output shaping), but cuts unique RX parameters from 8 to 4, reducing total trainable circuit parameters from 96 to 72 and total QML parameters from 98 to 74 (readout calibration unchanged).
This should improve parameter efficiency while preserving (already saturated) classification accuracy, and may also speed up convergence due to fewer degrees of freedom.
</DESCRIPTION>
