<DESCRIPTION>
The original ansatz used 24 parameters per block (RY, RZ, CZ-chain, RZ) which, when repeated across 3 uploads and 2 repeats (6 blocks total), resulted in 146 trainable parameters. While accurate, this is relatively parameter-heavy for an 8-qubit system.

This evolution optimizes the ansatz by:
1.  **Reducing Parameters:** Moving to a 16-parameter-per-block structure (RY, CZ-circular, RY). This reduces the total parameter count from 146 to 98, significantly boosting the `parameter_efficiency` metric.
2.  **Circular Entanglement:** Replacing the linear CZ chain with a staggered circular CZ arrangement. This ensures better connectivity and information flow across all qubits, especially important for the 28-feature IsingZZ map.
3.  **Basis Flexibility:** Using two layers of RY rotations separated by entanglers. This allows the model to rotate the phase-encoded features into the Z-basis more effectively than the original RZ-heavy configuration, which is often redundant when followed by phase-coupling (IsingZZ) or phase-gates (CZ).

This refinement targets a higher combined score by improving parameter efficiency and reducing the gate count/depth while maintaining the high classification accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
