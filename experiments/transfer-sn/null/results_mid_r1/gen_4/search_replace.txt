<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses a relatively large parameter set (146 total) with separate RZ layers before and after a simple CZ line of entanglement. To improve parameter efficiency while maintaining accuracy, I propose replacing the post-entanglement RZ layer with parametrized CRZ (controlled rotation Z) gates in a line pattern. This consolidates expressiveness: instead of having parameters in separate RZ gates and no parameters in CZ gates, we integrate the rotation parameters directly into the entanglement layer via CRZ.

Key benefits:
1. **Parameter reduction**: Reduces from 24 params/block (8 RY + 8 RZ pre + 8 RZ post) to 23 params/block (8 RY + 8 RZ + 7 CRZ), saving ~1.4% of circuit parameters per block, or ~4% overall (146 → 140).
2. **Better parameter utilization**: CRZ gates parametrically couple qubits, allowing rotation angles to directly influence entanglement strength, which can improve trainability and generalization.
3. **Maintained expressiveness**: Two layers of single-qubit rotations plus parametrized two-qubit entanglement preserve the model's capability to learn complex decision boundaries.
4. **Faster convergence potential**: Fewer redundant parameters can lead to faster convergence (evidenced by improved parameter efficiency helping optimization dynamics).

The structure becomes:
- RY rotations (8 params, independent)
- RZ rotations (8 params, independent)
- CRZ line: each of the 7 qubit-pairs (0-1, 1-2, ..., 6-7) has a parametrized controlled-Z rotation (7 params, independent)

This removes the post-entanglement RZ layer, which was somewhat redundant given the flexible pre-entanglement RZ and the now-parametrized entanglement layer.
</DESCRIPTION>
