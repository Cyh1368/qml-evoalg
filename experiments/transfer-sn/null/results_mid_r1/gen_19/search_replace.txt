<DESCRIPTION>
The recommendation suggests appending a final shared-parameter RZ phase layer to the ansatz block. This adds a global phase adjustment before the Z-expectation measurement, leveraging the success of simple single-qubit rotations with minimal overhead.

The insight is that:
1. All 8 qubits share the same RZ parameter (1 parameter for 8 gates within a block)
2. This acts as a global fine-tuning mechanism for the measurement basis
3. Single-qubit rotations have proven effective in this task
4. The gate overhead is modest (8 gates per block) while providing additional degrees of freedom

This should help:
- Reduce convergence time (currently 210 steps, could approach 150)
- Maintain perfect accuracy already achieved
- Add expressiveness with minimal parameter increase (6 total, from 140 to 146)
</DESCRIPTION>
