<DESCRIPTION>
The hypercube evolution ansatz is designed to maximize information propagation across an 8-qubit register by utilizing a topology inspired by a 3D hypercube (since $2^3 = 8$). 

The binary feature map (IsingZZ) creates phase correlations between specific qubit pairs. To decode these effectively, the ansatz performs the following:
1. **Basis Transformation (RY):** Converts the phase-encoded feature information into the population basis, allowing for interference and state differentiation.
2. **Logarithmic Entanglement (Hypercube):** Instead of a linear chain, we use a three-stage connectivity pattern:
   - Stage 1 (Distance 1): CNOTs on pairs (0,1), (2,3), (4,5), (6,7).
   - Stage 2 (Distance 2): CNOTs on pairs (0,2), (1,3), (4,6), (5,7).
   - Stage 3 (Distance 4): Parametrized CRY gates on pairs (0,4), (1,5), (2,6), (3,7).
This ensures that any qubit's state can influence any other qubit in at most 3 steps, matching the diameter of the feature space connectivity.
3. **Phase Refinement (RZ):** A secondary rotation layer between entanglement stages allows the model to fine-tune the relative phases of the entangled states before the final readout.
4. **Parameter Balance:** We use 20 unique parameters per block. With 6 total blocks (3 uploads x 2 repeats), the total parameter count is 122. This strikes a better balance between the seed's 98 and the current implementation's 146, aiming for higher accuracy with superior parameter efficiency and faster convergence.
