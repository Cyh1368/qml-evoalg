<DESCRIPTION>
This approach implements a hardware-efficient ansatz (HEA) with an alternating "brickwork" pattern of parametrized controlled rotations. Specifically, it uses independent RY rotations on all qubits followed by two layers of CRX gates (even and odd pairs). 

The use of RY as the base rotation layer allows for optimal population transfer between $|0\rangle$ and $|1\rangle$, while the CRX gates provide entanglement via an orthogonal rotation axis (X), creating a more expressive Hilbert space representation for binary classification. The brickwork connectivity (even pairs, then odd pairs) ensures global entanglement across the 8-qubit register within each block. 

By using 16 parameters per block (8 RY + 8 CRX), we maintain the same parameter count as the highest-performing seed (Seed 2) while potentially improving the loss landscape and generalization by mixing Y and X-axis operations. This architecture is designed to reach high accuracy quickly while keeping the circuit depth manageable.
</DESCRIPTION>
