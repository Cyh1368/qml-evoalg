<DESCRIPTION>
The previous candidate (Program 3) achieved 100% accuracy with a 1.46 score using a double-RY and double-CZ brick-wall structure (16 unique parameters per block). This proposed architecture aims to further minimize the validation loss and improve convergence speed by:
1.  **Diversifying Rotation Axes**: Replacing the second RY layer with an RZ layer. Alternating RY and RZ rotations is a common strategy in hardware-efficient ansatze to ensure the circuit can explore the entire Bloch sphere more effectively.
2.  **Stronger Entanglement**: Replacing the CZ gates with CNOT gates. CNOTs provide a more direct basis transformation (X-flip) compared to the phase-only interaction of CZ, which can lead to faster training on binary feature tasks.
3.  **Circular Connectivity**: Maintaining the brick-wall pattern (even and odd pairs) including the (7,0) wrap-around to ensure high connectivity across all 8 qubits, which is critical for the global mean-Z readout.

This maintains the 16-parameter count (98 total parameters including gain/bias) while improving expressivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
