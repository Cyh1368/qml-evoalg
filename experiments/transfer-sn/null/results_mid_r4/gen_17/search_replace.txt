This edit overhauls the `ANSATZ_SPEC` to improve expressivity and global connectivity, drawing inspiration from the successful Seed 2 design (score 1.51) while incorporating the recommended skip-link strategy.

The new block structure consists of:
1.  **Independent RY rotations** on all 8 qubits (8 parameters) to provide a flexible starting point for basis transformation.
2.  **Circular CNOT entanglement** (7 ladder gates + 1 wrap-around) to ensure full local connectivity across the qubit ring.
3.  **Four symmetric CRZ skip links** ([0,4], [1,5], [2,6], [3,7]) with unique parameters (4 parameters). These non-local links are crucial for capturing long-range correlations between binary features that the local feature map might not bridge effectively.
4.  **A final layer of RZ rotations** with shared parameters across qubit pairs (4 parameters) to refine the phases before the next feature upload or measurement.

Total unique parameters per block: 16 (RY: 8, CRZ_skip: 4, RZ_final: 4).
Total circuit parameters: 16 * 3 * 2 = 96. Total parameters: 96 + 2 = 98.
This design maintains the parameter count of the best-performing prior candidate (1.51) but improves the entanglement structure and adds global connectivity.
