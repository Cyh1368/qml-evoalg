<DESCRIPTION>
This update replaces the fully per-qubit rotation stack with a more structured, lower-parameter ansatz that is better aligned with the fixed feature map.

Key ideas:
- Use mirrored parameter sharing across qubit pairs to cut the per-block parameter count from 24 to 16, improving parameter efficiency and helping reduce overfitting.
- Keep strong expressivity by applying two rotation stages, with CZ entanglers on both a nearest-neighbor ring and long-range mirror pairs.
- The long-range CZs encourage global mixing across the 8-qubit register, which is useful because the feature map already injects pairwise correlations through IsingZZ couplings.
- The result should preserve accuracy while reducing train-test gap and converging more cleanly in the repeated re-uploading pipeline.
</DESCRIPTION>
