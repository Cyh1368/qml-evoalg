<DESCRIPTION>
The seed ansatz (24 parameters per block) contains functional redundancies: an RZ layer followed by a diagonal CZ layer and another RZ layer is mathematically equivalent to an RZ-CZ-Identity sequence on each wire, as RZ gates commute with CZ.

This improvement:
1.  Reduces parameters from 24 to 16 per block by removing the redundant RZ layer.
2.  Enhances entanglement by replacing the linear CZ chain with a circular CNOT chain, ensuring better information flow between all qubits (0-7 and 7-0) within each block.
3.  Improves parameter efficiency (a key component of the combined score) while maintaining the expressivity needed to reach high accuracy. Total parameters are reduced from 146 to 98.
</DESCRIPTION>
