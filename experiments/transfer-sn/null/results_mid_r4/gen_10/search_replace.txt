<DESCRIPTION>
This edit replaces the high-parameter block with a shallower, more parameter-efficient ansatz that still mixes all 8 qubits each repetition. The new block keeps per-qubit amplitude control via unique RY rotations, uses a ring plus shifted CZ mesh for entanglement, and compresses the final phase layer into two shared RZ parameters (even/odd parity). This should reduce total circuit parameters, lower depth/gate count, and improve convergence speed while preserving enough expressivity for the feature-map-heavy classifier.
</DESCRIPTION>
