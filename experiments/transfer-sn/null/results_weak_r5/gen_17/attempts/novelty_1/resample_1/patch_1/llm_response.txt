<DESCRIPTION>
Make the final phase-rotation layer parameter-efficient by replacing the two symmetry-shared RZ keys (“rz_final_even” on {0,2,4,6} and “rz_final_odd” on {1,3,5,7}) with a single shared key applied to all 8 qubits.

This reduces the number of trainable circuit parameters per ansatz block (from 14 → 13), improving parameter efficiency while keeping the overall circuit structure (same uploads/repeats, same entanglers, same mixing RY layer) and the expressivity largely intact. Since the previous variants already achieved perfect accuracy, this targets the remaining objective: lower loss and faster convergence with fewer parameters.
</DESCRIPTION>
