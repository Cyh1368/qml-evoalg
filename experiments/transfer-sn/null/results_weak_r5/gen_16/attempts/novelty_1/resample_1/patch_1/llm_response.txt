<DESCRIPTION>
Building on Gen 5's success with shared-parameter CRZ gates, this edit enriches two-qubit rotations by diversifying the rotation axes while maintaining parameter efficiency. In upload 1 repeat 0, we replace the four CRZ gates with mixed-axis controlled rotations: two shared CRX gates on wires [0,1] and [4,5] (key “crx_shared”) and two shared CRY gates on wires [2,3] and [6,7] (key “cry_shared”). This adds just two parameters while introducing rotation diversity in the sparse entangler pattern, which should improve gradient flow and expressivity compared to pure CRZ entanglers.
</DESCRIPTION>
