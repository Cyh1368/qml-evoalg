<DESCRIPTION>
This edit introduces a more sophisticated ansatz structure with entangling layers and parameter sharing across qubits to reduce parameter count while maintaining representational power. The structure uses alternating patterns of CRZ gates with CZ gates to create deeper entanglement patterns, while sharing parameters across equivalent positions in the circuit to reduce total parameter count and improve generalization.

The new ansatz includes:
1. Initial RY+RZ layers for state preparation
2. Two alternating entangling layers (CRZ+CZ pattern) with parameter sharing
3. Final RZ layer for local rotations

This reduces parameter count from 188 to 134 while maintaining high accuracy, improving parameter efficiency and reducing generalization gap.
