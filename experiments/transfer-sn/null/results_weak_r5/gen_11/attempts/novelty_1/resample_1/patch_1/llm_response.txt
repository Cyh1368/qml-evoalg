crz_to_crx_entangling_layers
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the fixed CRZ entangling layers with shared-parameter CRX gates to improve gradient flow and convergence. This builds on the success of parametrized two-qubit gates while adding an extra controllable rotation axis. The CRX gates use the same wiring patterns as the original CRZ gates but with a new shared parameter key "crx_shared" to maintain parameter efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
