<description>
This edits only the candidate ansatz block to improve training stability and generalization while reducing parameter count.

Key changes:
- Keep expressive single-qubit rotations but move to a more standard basis: RY (8 params) + RZ (8 params).
- Replace the current 6 distinct controlled-phase parameters with only 2 shared CRZ parameters (odd-chain and even-chain). This reduces parameters per block and encourages smoother optimization across re-uploading blocks.
- Use fixed CZ entanglers aligned with the CRZ connectivity to preserve circuit expressivity while not increasing trainable parameters.
</description>
