<DESCRIPTION>
Refactored the ansatz to replace the linear CZ-chain with a denser, more expressive CRZ-based entanglement structure. By utilizing parameter sharing for the two-qubit CRZ gates (grouped into 'even' and 'odd' pairings), I increased entanglement connectivity while simultaneously reducing the total parameter count per block. Additionally, I replaced one of the RZ rotation layers with an RX layer, which improves Bloch sphere coverage and model expressivity. This significantly improves parameter efficiency while maintaining high classification accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
