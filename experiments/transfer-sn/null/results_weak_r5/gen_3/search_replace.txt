<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce ansatz parameter count per block by reusing rotation parameters across qubits with even/odd wire parity (parameter sharing via identical `param` strings). Concretely:
- Replace per-qubit RY with two shared parameters: `ry_e` (even wires) and `ry_o` (odd wires).
- Replace per-qubit RZ layers (pre and post) with two shared parameters each: `rz_pre_e/rz_pre_o` and `rz_post_e/rz_post_o`.
- Keep the fixed CZ entangling chain unchanged.
This dramatically lowers `n_params` (thus optimizer work and overfitting risk) while preserving enough variational freedom for the 3× reuploading and 2× block repetition to learn decision boundaries.
</DESCRIPTION>
