<DESCRIPTION>
Based on the recommendation, I'm augmenting the architecture with a fixed IsingXX re-injection layer between uploads. This adds no trainable parameters but increases expressivity by introducing nonlinearity across uploads. The IsingXX gates are applied between upload 1 and upload 2, mirroring the existing ZZ encoding but along the XX axis. This follows the multi-upload insight that expressivity derives more from interleaved couplings than sheer parameter count.
