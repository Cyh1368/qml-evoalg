<DESCRIPTION>
Replaces the original RY-RZ-CZ-RZ ansatz block with a more parameter-efficient hardware-efficient ansatz.
The new block uses `RX` and `RY` rotations followed by alternating `CNOT` layers to form a 1D ring entanglement.
This reduces the parameter count per block from 24 to 16, eliminating redundant parameters that commute with the final Z measurement (the final `RZ` layer in the original code had zero gradient in the last block).
The alternating `CNOT`s also significantly reduce the circuit depth compared to the previous linear `CZ` chain. This improves the parameter efficiency of the model and reduces the chance of overfitting, aiming to close the generalization gap while maintaining high expressivity and training accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
