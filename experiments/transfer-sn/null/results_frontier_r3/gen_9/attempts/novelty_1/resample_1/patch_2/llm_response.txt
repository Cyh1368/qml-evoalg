<DESCRIPTION>
This ansatz uses a brick-layer architecture with three rotation layers interleaved with two CZ entangling layers. The first CZ layer connects even pairs (0-1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-7), the second CZ layer connects odd pairs (1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7-0) forming a ring topology. An additional set of long-range CZ connections (0-4, 2-6) bridges distant qubits to improve information flow. The rotation layers alternate between RY and RZ for full Bloch sphere coverage, totaling 32 parameters per block. With p=2 and l=3, this gives 192 trainable parameters - expressive enough for classification but not so large as to overfit.
