3. **Reduced depth/gate count**: Fewer gates per block means faster training

My proposed design uses a **brick-layer CZ topology** (even pairs, then odd pairs) forming a ring, with RY+RZ rotations. This gives:

- Ring connectivity instead of linear chain (better information flow)
- Parallel CZ layers reduce effective depth
- Same single-qubit expressivity (RY+RZ covers full Bloch sphere)
