shared_rotations_param_cut
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Reduce the number of trainable ansatz parameters per block by aggressively sharing single-qubit rotation parameters across qubit pairs, while keeping the same CZ entangling ladder. Concretely:
- Replace the 8 distinct RY + 16 distinct RZ parameters (24 params/block) with:
  - 4 shared RY parameters for qubit pairs (0,1),(2,3),(4,5),(6,7)
  - 4 shared post-entangling RZ parameters for the same qubit pairs
- Keep the existing 7-qubit CZ chain to preserve expressivity.

This targets higher `parameter_efficiency` (smaller `n_params`) while retaining high accuracy/generalization by maintaining entanglement and leaving re-uploading/repeating (fixed by the training loop) to recover representational power.
</DESCRIPTION>
