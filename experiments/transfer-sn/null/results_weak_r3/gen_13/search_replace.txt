<DESCRIPTION>
This modification aims to improve the expressivity and training efficiency of the ansatz by:
1. Adding cross-layer connections to increase entanglement between distant qubits
2. Introducing parameter sharing in controlled rotations to reduce parameter count
3. Adding additional RY rotations after entangling layers to increase expressivity
The changes should help achieve faster convergence while maintaining high accuracy with fewer parameters than previous versions.
</DESCRIPTION>
