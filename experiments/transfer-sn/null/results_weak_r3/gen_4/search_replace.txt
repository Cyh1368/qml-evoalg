<DESCRIPTION>
The original ansatz uses a simple linear chain of CZ gates for entanglement, which may not be optimal for complex feature interactions in the 8-qubit system. This change introduces a more sophisticated entanglement structure by:

1. Replacing the linear CZ chain with a multi-layered entanglement pattern that includes both nearest-neighbor and cross-connections
2. Adding parametrized controlled rotations (CRY) to increase expressivity
3. Implementing a more hardware-efficient entanglement strategy that better utilizes all qubits

This approach should improve:
- Accuracy by enabling more complex feature correlations
- Generalization by reducing overfitting through better entanglement
- Parameter efficiency by making each parameter more impactful
- Convergence speed by providing better gradient flow
</DESCRIPTION>
