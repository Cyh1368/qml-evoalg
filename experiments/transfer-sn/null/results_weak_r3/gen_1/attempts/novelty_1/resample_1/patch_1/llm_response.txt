<DESCRIPTION>
I am optimizing the ansatz structure to improve both parameter efficiency and state-space exploration. The original ansatz used three layers of single-qubit rotations (RY-RZ-RZ) leading to 24 parameters per block and 146 total parameters. 

My proposal replaces this with two layers of distinct single-qubit rotations (RY and RX) separated by two layers of CZ entanglement, creating a more entangled, compact structure with only 16 parameters per block (total 98 parameters). This maintains the overall connectivity (a full 0-1-2-3-4-5-6-7 chain) while significantly increasing parameter efficiency, which is a key component of the `combined_score`. By using RY and RX rotations, we ensure better exploration of the Bloch sphere compared to just RY/RZ.
</DESCRIPTION>
