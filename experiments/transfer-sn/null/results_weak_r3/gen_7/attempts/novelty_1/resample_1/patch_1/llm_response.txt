<DESCRIPTION>
This edit introduces a new entangling layer using controlled rotation gates (CRZ) to improve the expressivity of the ansatz while maintaining parameter efficiency. The controlled rotations create stronger correlations between qubits compared to the simple CZ ladder, which should lead to better learning of complex patterns in the binary classification task. The CRZ gates are applied in a staggered pattern to ensure full coverage of qubit interactions while keeping the circuit depth manageable. This should help improve convergence speed and final accuracy while maintaining good generalization.
</DESCRIPTION>
