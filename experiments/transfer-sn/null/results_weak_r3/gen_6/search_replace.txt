<DESCRIPTION>
To improve the parameter efficiency and potential generalization of the model, I am introducing parameter sharing across qubit pairs for all single-qubit rotation layers (RY and both RZ layers). The current implementation uses 24 unique parameters per ansatz block. By pairing qubits (0,1), (2,3), (4,5), and (6,7) and sharing parameters between them, we reduce the number of unique parameters per block to 12. This significantly reduces the total parameter count while maintaining the same circuit architecture, which typically improves training stability and parameter efficiency without degrading accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
