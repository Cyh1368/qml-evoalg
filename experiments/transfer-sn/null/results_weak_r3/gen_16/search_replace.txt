<DESCRIPTION>
This edit implements parameter sharing for single-qubit rotations across symmetric qubit pairs and reallocates the saved parameters to add more CRZ gates. This follows the recommendation to improve parameter efficiency and circuit expressivity. The changes:
1. Share RY and RZ parameters across qubit pairs (0,1), (2,3), (4,5), (6,7) - reducing 16 parameters to 8
2. Add 4 new CRZ gates on next-nearest neighbors to utilize the saved parameters
3. Maintain the same overall structure but with better parameter efficiency
This should improve the combined score by reducing the train-test gap and accelerating convergence while maintaining high accuracy.
