even_odd_parameter_sharing

<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses highly constrained "collective" parameters, forcing all qubits to share the same weights. This limits expressivity. I propose using even/odd parameter sharing (two independent sets of parameters for qubits 0/2/4/6 and 1/3/5/7), which maintains beneficial symmetries and parameter efficiency while allowing the circuit to learn parity-dependent features, potentially improving performance without risking overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
