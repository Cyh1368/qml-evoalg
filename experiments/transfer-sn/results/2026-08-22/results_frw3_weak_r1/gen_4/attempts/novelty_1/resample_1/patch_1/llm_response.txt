I am replacing the linear chain with a Hierarchical Entanglement Ansatz (HEA). This design utilizes a "divide-and-conquer" entanglement pattern (recursive pairing), which constructs a complete communication graph across all 8 qubits in just log2(N)=3 layers of CZ gates. This structure ensures that any single-qubit information can influence any other qubit with minimal circuit depth. I have also replaced the simple linear gates with alternating rotation layers (RY followed by RZ) to create a more versatile parameter space for the optimizer to navigate, while keeping the total parameter count efficient.
