<DESCRIPTION>
I am upgrading the ansatz from a fully collective (all-qubit shared) parameterization to a parity-split parameterization. By assigning distinct parameters for even and odd wires (even/odd qubit indices), we introduce spatial flexibility into the variational circuit, allowing for more expressive transformations without a massive increase in parameter count or circuit depth. This targeted increase in expressivity is expected to improve the model's ability to classify the features.
</DESCRIPTION>
