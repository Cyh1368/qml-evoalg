To improve the performance of the 8-qubit classifier, I will refine the ansatz to break some of the strict collective symmetry while maintaining parameter efficiency.

The current design uses collective `RY` and `RZ` rotations, which limits the expressive power of the circuit by forcing all qubits to share the same parameters. By introducing spatially varying rotations (partitioning the qubits into two groups, e.g., evens and odds), we can increase the representational capacity of the model with only a small increase in the parameter count. This allows the circuit to learn distinct features for different parts of the Hilbert space.

I will also modify the `RY` layer slightly to act on even/odd qubits separately.
