<entanglers_crz_param_shared>
<DESCRIPTION>
Switch the fixed CZ hypercube entangler layer to a parameterized CRZ entangling layer (shared single parameter across all entangling gates within the block). This increases circuit expressivity while keeping parameter count low (still shared/efficient), and introduces controlled, non-commuting entangling behavior that often improves separability after the fixed ZZ feature map + re-uploading.
</DESCRIPTION>
