<DESCRIPTION>
I am increasing the circuit depth and expressivity by inserting a layer of controlled-phase (CRZ) gates in the middle of the entanglement structure. This adds learnable entanglement strength without significantly increasing the parameter count (only 1 additional parameter per block). By placing this layer symmetrically within the existing CZ hypercube-connectivity layers, we maintain the structural symmetry of the ansatz while allowing the optimizer to adjust the entanglement phase. This should improve accuracy and decrease loss.
