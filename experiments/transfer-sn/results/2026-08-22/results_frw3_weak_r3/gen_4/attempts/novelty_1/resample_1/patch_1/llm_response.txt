<DESCRIPTION>
This approach moves away from collective parameter sharing, which constrains the circuit to symmetric transformations. Instead, it implements a hierarchical, conditional rotation ansatz. By using controlled-Z rotations (CRZ) as the primary entangling mechanism, the circuit becomes capable of modeling complex, non-linear dependencies between data features. This conditional structure allows the entanglement generated in the circuit to be dynamically shaped by the input state itself, rather than relying on static, uniform rotations. This is significantly more expressive for classification tasks with 28-dimensional feature spaces, enabling higher accuracy while maintaining parameter efficiency through structured weight sharing across qubit pairs.
</DESCRIPTION>
