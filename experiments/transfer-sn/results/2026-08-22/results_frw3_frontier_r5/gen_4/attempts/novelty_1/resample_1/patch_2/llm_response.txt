Looking at this problem, I need to improve the ansatz block for an 8-qubit classifier. The seed uses a simple line of CZ gates which limits entanglement propagation. I'll design a brick-layer entanglement pattern with long-range connections and an additional rotation layer for better expressibility.

<NAME>
brick_entangle_longrange_ry
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The seed ansatz uses a linear chain of CZ gates, which requires many layers for information to propagate across all qubits. This redesign uses:

1. RY+RZ rotations on all qubits (expressible single-qubit unitaries)
2. Brick-layer entanglement: even pairs (0-1, 2-3, 4-5, 6-7) then odd pairs (1-2, 3-4, 5-6) - this creates faster entanglement spread than a linear chain
3. Long-range CZ connections (0-4, 3-7) to bridge distant qubits, matching the fact that the IsingZZ feature map uses various qubit pairs
4. A middle RY rotation layer between entangling layers for more expressibility
5. Final RY layer to give the circuit enough freedom to prepare good measurement states

This gives 32 unique parameters per block (vs 24 in seed). With p=2 and l=3, that's 192 total trainable ansatz parameters - expressive enough without overfitting risk.
</DESCRIPTION>
