wagner_graph_ansatz

<DESCRIPTION>
Replaces the baseline linear CZ-chain ansatz with a highly efficient "Wagner graph" (Möbius ladder) CNOT topology.
The dataset encodes 28 features via all-to-all `IsingZZ` interactions (which are phase/Z-basis transformations). To effectively complement this, the new ansatz uses `RY` and `RX` rotations to mix amplitudes into the X and Y bases.
By using a combination of even, odd, and cross CNOT layers, the maximum distance between any two qubits becomes 2 (diameter 2 graph). This guarantees extremely rapid entanglement spread across the entire circuit.
Additionally, this reduces the number of parameters per block from 24 to 16, halves the circuit depth per block, and lowers the overall gate count, resulting in a significantly higher parameter efficiency and reducing the likelihood of overfitting without sacrificing expressivity.
</DESCRIPTION>
