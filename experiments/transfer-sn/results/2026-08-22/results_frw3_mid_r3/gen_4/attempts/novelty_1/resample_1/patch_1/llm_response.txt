# Analysis

Looking at the performance metrics:

**Current program (score: 0.45)** - Best performer:
- test_accuracy: 0.90, validation_accuracy: 0.90, train_accuracy: 0.91
- n_params: 26 (highly efficient!)
- generalization_gap: 0.01 (excellent)
- convergence_step: 270 (reasonable)

The current design achieves excellent generalization through aggressive parameter sharing via collective gates. However, there's still room for improvement: the model could benefit from slight asymmetry to increase expressivity while maintaining the excellent generalization properties.

**Proposed improvement:**

Add per-qubit individual RZ gates positioned strategically to allow fine-tuned phase adjustments after entanglement but before the output mixing stage. This breaks symmetry slightly while keeping the core symmetric structure that enables good generalization. The added 8 parameters (one per qubit) would increase total parameters from 26 to 34 - still very parameter-efficient - while providing each qubit independent control over its phase state before measurement basis rotation.
