<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz achieves excellent performance (test_accuracy=0.90, generalization_gap=0.01) with heavy parameter sharing using collective gates. To push accuracy higher while maintaining generalization, I propose adding selective per-qubit phase parameters after the collective input mixer.

The key insight: the model has strong symmetry which aids generalization, but per-qubit RZ gates inserted after the collective RY input allow fine-grained control over individual qubit states before entanglement, without breaking the overall efficiency.

This modification:
1. Adds 8 per-qubit RZ parameters (per_rz_0 through per_rz_7) for individual phase calibration
2. Maintains the highly efficient collective parameter structure (only modest increase from 26 to 34 params per block)
3. Improves expressiveness while preserving the small generalization gap through controlled parameter growth
4. Preserves convergence speed by keeping the fundamental circuit structure

This is inspired by the success of hybrid architectures that use both collective and per-qubit parameters to balance expressiveness and efficiency.
</DESCRIPTION>
