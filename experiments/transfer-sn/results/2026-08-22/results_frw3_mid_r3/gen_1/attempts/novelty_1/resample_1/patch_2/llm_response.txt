<DESCRIPTION>
This ansatz uses a 3-dimensional hypercube entangling topology instead of a linear chain. For 8 qubits (labeled as 3-bit binary strings 000-111), the hypercube connects qubit pairs that differ in exactly one bit, organized into 3 parallel layers:
- Dim 0: (0,1),(2,3),(4,5),(6,7) — pairs differing in bit 0
- Dim 1: (0,2),(1,3),(4,6),(5,7) — pairs differing in bit 1  
- Dim 2: (0,4),(1,5),(2,6),(3,7) — pairs differing in bit 2

This ensures every qubit pair is connected by at most log2(8)=3 CZ gates — optimal diameter. Between each entangling layer, RY rotations convert accumulated phases into amplitude differences observable in Z measurements. A final RZ layer provides phase tuning. This replaces the seed's linear CZ chain (diameter 7) with a log-depth structure that propagates correlations exponentially faster, critical for the IsingZZ-encoded features which create qubit-pair correlations across the full register. The RY-dominated rotation basis is specifically chosen because RY gates are the only real single-qubit rotations that don't commute with Z, making them maximally effective at extracting phase information into Z-measurement outcomes.
