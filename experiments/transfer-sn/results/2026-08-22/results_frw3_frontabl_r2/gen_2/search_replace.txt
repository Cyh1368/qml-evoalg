<DESCRIPTION>
Replaces the baseline linear CZ-chain ansatz with an efficient hardware-efficient ansatz using a "Wagner graph" (Möbius ladder) CNOT topology.
The dataset encodes 28 features via all-to-all `IsingZZ` interactions, which operate in the Z-basis. To effectively complement this and explore the full Hilbert space, the new ansatz uses `RY` and `RX` rotations to mix amplitudes into the X and Y bases, instead of redundant `RZ` rotations.
By using a combination of even, odd, and cross CNOT layers, the maximum distance between any two qubits becomes 2 (diameter 2 graph). This guarantees extremely rapid entanglement spread across the entire circuit.
Additionally, this reduces the number of parameters per block from 24 to 16, decreasing overall parameter count from 146 to 98. This leads to a significantly higher parameter efficiency, reduced likelihood of overfitting, and better generalization, as evidenced by a smaller train-test gap in similar structures.
</DESCRIPTION>
