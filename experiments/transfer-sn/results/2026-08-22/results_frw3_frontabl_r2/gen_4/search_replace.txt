<DESCRIPTION>
The previous ansatz achieved high parameter efficiency (26 parameters) via fully collective parameter sharing across all qubits, but limited its expressiveness. Furthermore, all its `CZ` gates were placed consecutively, meaning they commuted and effectively collapsed into a single diagonal operation, significantly reducing entangling depth.

This edit introduces an **interleaved bipartite hypercube** block:
1. **Bipartite Parameter Sharing:** We map the 8 qubits to a hypercube, which is naturally bipartite. Qubits with even parity (0, 3, 5, 6) share one parameter (set 'A'), and odd parity (1, 2, 4, 7) share another (set 'B'). This breaks the complete symmetry while elegantly mirroring the hypercube's exact entangling edges (every CZ connects an 'A' to a 'B').
2. **Interleaved Non-Commuting Rotations:** We place non-diagonal rotation layers (`RX` or `RY`) *between* the three disjoint `CZ` perfect matchings. This prevents the `CZ` layers from commuting, forcing the creation of highly complex, deep entanglement.
3. **Trailing Non-Diagonal Gate:** The block concludes with an `RX` rotation, ensuring that the final `CZ` matching does not commute with the subsequent `IsingZZ` feature map or the final `PauliZ` measurements.
</DESCRIPTION>
