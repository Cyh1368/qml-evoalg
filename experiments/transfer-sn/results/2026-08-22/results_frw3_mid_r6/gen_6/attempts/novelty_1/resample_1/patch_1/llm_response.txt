<DESCRIPTION>
The current design achieves excellent performance (test accuracy 0.90, generalization gap 0.01) through heavy parameter sharing with only 4 unique parameters per ansatz block. While highly efficient, this aggressive symmetry may limit the model's expressiveness.

To improve further, I propose adding individual per-qubit RX rotations after the CZ entangler layer. This breaks the collective symmetry in a controlled way, allowing the optimizer to learn qubit-specific corrections while maintaining the efficient collective structure for the main mixing layers.

The new structure will be:
1. Collective RY input mixer (shared across all 8 qubits)
2. Collective RZ phase (shared across all 8 qubits)
3. CZ entangler (fixed structure)
4. **Individual RX rotations on each qubit** (NEW - 8 per block)
5. Collective RY output mixer (shared across all 8 qubits)
6. Collective RX output mixer (shared across all 8 qubits)

This increases parameters from 26 to ~74 total (12 unique per block × 6 blocks + 2 readout), a conservative increase that should improve expressiveness while maintaining reasonable parameter efficiency and low generalization gap.

The motivation: individual rotations after entanglement can capture qubit-specific phase corrections without breaking the efficient symmetry-preserving design of the main layers.
</DESCRIPTION>
