<DESCRIPTION>
I am replacing the existing linear ansatz with a more parameter-efficient and structurally robust hypercube design. The previous ansatz used 146 parameters, which is potentially overfitting given the task complexity. By introducing parameter sharing across all qubits (using the same parameter string for all gates in a layer), I reduce the per-block parameter count to 4 (RY1, RZ1, RY2, RX2). This design employs a hypercube entanglement strategy (CZ gates across three connectivity dimensions) which is known to be superior for 8-qubit systems to ensure global information mixing.
