<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current mostly-local “chain CZ + individual RY/RZ” block with a more expressive-but-parameter-efficient structure:

- Use <collective> single-qubit rotations (parameter sharing across all 8 wires) to improve trainability under data re-uploading.
- Replace the linear CZ chain with a <degree-3 cube> entangler implemented as three disjoint perfect matchings (same pattern as a strong prior result): this increases effective entangling connectivity while keeping the gate set fixed-size.
- Keep the ansatz block compact (4 trainable parameters per block), which reduces total circuit parameters and helps the optimizer reach higher validation/test accuracy faster with lower train-test gap.
