<DESCRIPTION>
The current program achieves excellent performance (score 0.45) with a highly parameter-efficient collective parameter sharing strategy. However, there is room to improve test accuracy and expressiveness while maintaining efficiency.

**Key observation from metrics:**
- Current: 26 params, test_acc=0.90, generalization_gap=0.01
- The parameter sharing yields excellent generalization, but may limit expressiveness

**Proposed enhancement:**
Add individual RX rotations after the collective RY input mixer and before the collective RZ phase. This allows per-qubit fine-tuning early in the circuit without sacrificing the parameter-sharing benefits of the later stages.

**Rationale:**
1. Adds only 8 new parameters per block (individually tuned RX on each qubit), bringing total to ~74 params
2. Provides expressiveness boost by allowing asymmetric contributions early in the ansatz
3. Maintains collective phases and output stages which were working well
4. Early individual tuning can help the circuit adapt to dataset asymmetries while collective later stages preserve good generalization
5. Expected to improve test accuracy from 0.90 toward 0.92-0.94 range while keeping generalization gap low

This follows the pattern seen in prior program 2 which achieved test_acc=0.87 with 146 params; we're finding a better middle ground.
