<DESCRIPTION>
The previous collective parameter sharing (one parameter for all 8 qubits) was highly efficient but potentially under-parametrized for the 28-feature IsingZZ map, which couples all possible qubit pairs.

This update introduces a bipartite sharing scheme: qubits are divided into two groups of four (Group A: 0-3, Group B: 4-7). Each layer of rotations now uses two independent parameters (one per group) rather than one. This respects the topology of the hypercube entangler, where the first two sets of CZ gates (Matchings 1 and 2) act internally within these groups, while the third set (Matching 3) provides the necessary communication between them.

This change increases the parameters per block from 4 to 8, bringing the total parameter count to 50. This remains highly efficient but doubles the expressive power of the model, allowing it to better differentiate and process the information from the 28 different feature couplings.
</DESCRIPTION>

for q in range(8):
    group = "a" if q < 4 else "b"
    ANSATZ_SPEC.append({"gate": "RY", "wire": q, "param": f"ry_in_{group}"})
for q in range(8):
    group = "a" if q < 4 else "b"
    ANSATZ_SPEC.append({"gate": "RZ", "wire": q, "param": f"rz_in_{group}"})

# Hypercube Entangler: Matching 1 & 2 (internal to groups)
