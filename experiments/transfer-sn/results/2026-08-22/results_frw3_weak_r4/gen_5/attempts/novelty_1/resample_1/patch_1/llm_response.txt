<DESCRIPTION>
The current ansatz uses fully collective (shared) parameters for the rotation layers, which preserves symmetry but limits expressivity. I am upgrading this to an even/odd splitting scheme for all rotation layers. This doubles the number of trainable parameters per layer (4 layers of 2 params each = 8 per block, versus 4 layers of 1 param each = 4 per block), allowing for more flexible state transformations while still maintaining a degree of symmetry and parameter efficiency. The entanglement layer remains unchanged to preserve the hypercube structure.
</DESCRIPTION>
