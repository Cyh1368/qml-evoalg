<DESCRIPTION>
I am replacing the collective (shared parameter) output layers with per-qubit output layers. This introduces individual degrees of freedom for each wire during the final readout rotation stage. This allows the classifier to learn different feature importance weights for each qubit, which should improve the representational capacity of the model without significantly increasing the risk of overfitting, as the overall parameter count remains small.
</DESCRIPTION>
