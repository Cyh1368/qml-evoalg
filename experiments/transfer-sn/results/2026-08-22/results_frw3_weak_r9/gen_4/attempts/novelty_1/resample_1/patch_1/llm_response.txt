<AMPLIFIED_entangler_with_crz_and_fewer_output_mixer_params>
<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the current highly-symmetric “collective RX/RY output mixer” with a slightly more expressive, still parameter-efficient structure:
- Keep the input collective RY and collective RZ.
- Keep the cube entangling CZ network (12 CZs).
- Add one additional parameterized controlled-phase layer (CRZ) on a disjoint matching of 4 pairs (reuses a single shared parameter for all four CRZ gates).
- Use a single collective Y-basis mixer for all qubits (to rotate into the measured Z basis) plus one collective final Z rotation, instead of both collective RY and collective RX.

This increases ansatz expressivity (non-diagonal + additional entangling non-commutativity via CRZ) while increasing parameter count only from 4→5 per block, which should improve validation/test accuracy and reduce the train-test gap without overfitting.
</DESCRIPTION>
