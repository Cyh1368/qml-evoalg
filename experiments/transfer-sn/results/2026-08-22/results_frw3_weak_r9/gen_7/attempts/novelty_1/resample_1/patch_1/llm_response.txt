<DESCRIPTION>
The previous implementation used independent parameters for every gate, resulting in a large number of parameters (146) which likely contributed to overfitting and a poor generalization gap. I am replacing the `ANSATZ_SPEC` with a highly symmetric and parameter-efficient design using parameter sharing (collective rotations) and a cube-topology entanglement layer. This setup has proven successful in previous iterations, significantly reducing parameter count to 26 and enforcing structure that helps the optimizer, leading to better generalization and accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
