<DESCRIPTION>
I am replacing the current long, wire-specific chain with a compact symmetry-preserving block: shared collective RY/RZ mixers, a 3D cube-style CZ entangler, then shared collective RY/RX output mixers. This cuts the learnable circuit parameters per block sharply while preserving rich noncommuting structure and improving gradient sharing across qubits. The cube entangler spreads the fixed feature-map correlations more effectively than a linear CZ chain, and the final RX layer is better aligned with the Z-basis readout than a trailing RZ. Overall this should reduce overfitting, improve validation/test accuracy, and converge faster under Adam.
</DESCRIPTION>
