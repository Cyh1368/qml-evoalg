<DESCRIPTION>
Replace the cube entangler with the complete eight-vertex graph, scheduled as seven disjoint perfect matchings. The current cube gives every qubit equal degree but still introduces an arbitrary coordinate system, whereas the binary pair features naturally describe edges between all qubit pairs. The complete CZ layer is invariant under every qubit permutation, so the full model better matches permutation-invariant record properties and should reduce sensitivity to vertex ordering. It also supplies global nonlinear interactions without adding trainable parameters, keeping the total at 26 and preserving the current parameter efficiency and low overfitting risk.
